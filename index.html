1. Core Technical Approach

Pillar I · Spectral-Free Matrix Proofs

Operator Identities Over Jordan Forms

Instead of brittle eigenvalue or matrix-square-root arguments, we prove detectable DARE Schur stability via forced-observer zero-step escape (DAREDetectable.lean), discrete ISS via a square-root-free matrix Peter–Paul identity (ISS.lean), and H∞ attenuation via indefinite minimax completion of squares (BoundedRealHInf.lean).

Pillar II · Closed-Form Conic Duality

Zero-Solver 1–10 kHz Safety Shields

Online QP/SOCP solvers introduce non-convergence risk and WCET jitter. We prove analytical conic-duality solutions in Lean 4 (LorentzConeCBF.lean, BacksteppingCLF.lean) and emit recursion-free C99 & IEC 61131-3 ST kernels (ctrl_lorentz_cbf_filter_3d, ctrl_sontag_clf) executing in <100 ns.

Pillar III · Finite Group Parseval Projections

Non-Convex Matrix Variety Decomposition

For open problems like the Byers–Nash n=6 conjecture (MF-13), we act on 15D skew space 𝕂6 with the 8-element (F,G)-Klein involution group and 3 plane-swap involutions W12, W13, W23 to obtain an exact 15D orthogonal Parseval decomposition (ByersNashOffDiagComplete.lean).
