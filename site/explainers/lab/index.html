Where the ideas come from

Tao’s work informs the methods: finite countermodels, additive structure, and visible proof dependencies. OpenAI’s math repository supplies two precisely scoped starting points: a tropical counterexample and a unit-job scheduling specification. The control applications and small experiments here are our adaptations.

Terence Tao: The equational theories project, a brief tour

Method inspiration: search finite countermodels before trusting an algebraic rewrite.

Green, Manners and Tao: Sumsets and entropy revisited

Method inspiration: inspect additive support growth and distinguish support from entropy. No application of PFR is claimed.

Terence Tao: Formalizing PFR in Lean4 using Blueprint

Presentation inspiration: expose dependencies and missing obligations.

OpenAI/math: TruffetModel and TruffetCounterexample

Recompute the displayed feasible-point cost gap. The substitution algorithm and upstream Lean proof are not replayed by this suite.
