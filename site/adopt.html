How much improvement is enough?

Use 30% in a whole-task metric as a starting ambition for a substantial migration. A cheap, reversible adapter can justify a smaller gain. A new capability—such as detecting an unsupported command—can be valuable without being faster.

Compare the complete bundle, not the sum of its best component percentages. These controls illustrate a serial latency model; they are not measured ctrl or YOLO results.

Illustrative: 5.00% less total latency.

New time / old time = 1 − stage share × stage reduction + overhead. Parallel pipelines, bottleneck shifts and accuracy trade-offs require measurement.

For every claimed win, publish baseline → result, repeated-run variation, important regressions and integration cost. Lower jerk, lower electrical energy and lower temperature are three different measurements.

Help with one useful test

Bring a trajectory, a detector profile, a compiler boundary case, a counterexample, or a domain we missed. One reproducible example is enough to start. Failed hypotheses stay in the pipeline so others can learn from them.
