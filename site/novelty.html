Current assessment

Much of the underlying mathematics is established, often decades old. The strongest supported contribution today is formalization, precise specialization, independent checking and careful integration. Some narrower ideas merit a novelty investigation. The reviewed evidence does not yet establish a major new mathematical result.

This assessment covers seven representative claims, not every theorem or all 60 research questions. “We derived it” does not establish “nobody knew it.” Neither theorem counts nor GO decisions measure originality.

Three paths to a significant contribution

Formal methods / systems

Close the implementation gap

One useful theorem, one actual execution target, a complete correctness argument and a downstream user. Demonstrate a capability missing from the closest existing system.

Robotics / controls

Beat a strong matched baseline

Show a new mechanism or justified specialization under realistic constraints. Separate simulations from physical measurements and report failed cases.

Mathematics

Resolve a precise open obstacle

Prove the original statement, produce an exact counterexample, or establish a structural theorem that materially changes the problem. Seek independent specialist scrutiny.

Recognition follows originality, significance and independent validation. An award cannot be inferred from proof volume, compute spent or a promising idea. A known theorem can still support an important engineering contribution when it makes a previously difficult capability reliable and usable.
