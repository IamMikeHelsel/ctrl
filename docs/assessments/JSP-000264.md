## Problem and known solution
Erdős 318 (ErGr80 p.42): for A an infinite AP and nonconstant f : A → {±1}, must some finite nonempty S ⊆ A have Σ f(n)/n = 0? Also: arbitrary positive-density A? Squares excluding 1?
- A = ℕ: Erdős–Straus 1975; odd numbers: Sattler 1975; every infinite AP: Sattler 1982 (the "original question", answer yes).
- Positive density: false — any set containing exactly one even number fails (Erdős, per Sattler 1982). Proof is a 2-adic one-liner: put f = +1 on odds, −1 on the even e. Subsets avoiding e have positive sum; subsets containing e would need Σ_{odd} 1/n = 1/e, impossible because clearing denominators by the odd product P gives e·N = P with P odd.
- Squares \ {1}: Larsen 2026 (circle-method paper, ~470 lines TeX, heavy analytic number theory: Bloom-style methods, smooth/rough numbers, circle method). Formalization would be a multi-month effort.

The JSP record (JSP-000264) lists status Solved, Lean proof No, cites ErSt75/Sa75/Sa82/Sa82b/ErGr80 — i.e. the classical results, not Larsen. Scope ambiguous, but the most natural reading is the AP result (Sa82b), which is already formalized.

## Proof plan
(Only relevant if we wanted to duplicate; not recommended.)
1. Lemma: T finset of odd naturals, e even positive ⇒ Σ_{n∈T} 1/n ≠ 1/e (multiply by P = ∏T, cast to ℕ, parity).
2. contain_single_even: get unique even e from ncard = 1; get odd o from `hA.exists_gt`/infinitude minus {0,e}; f := if Even n then −1 else 1; case split e ∈ S.
3. parts.i: A = {n | Odd n} ∪ {2}; density 1/2 via counting odds below N and `Tendsto`; apply step 2.
No finite computation; no `decide`/`native_decide` needed.
parts.ii: XL, would require formalizing Larsen's full circle-method argument.
