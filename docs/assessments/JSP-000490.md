## Problem and known solution
Komjáth's question (Er87): for a family of countably infinite sets $(A_i)$ with $|A_i\cap A_j|\neq 2$ for $i\neq j$, what is the least cardinal $C$ such that $\bigcup A_i$ can always be coloured with $C$ colours so that no $A_i$ is monochromatic? GPT-5.4 Pro (prompted by Chojecki) showed that no such cardinal exists. Let $\kappa=(2^C)^+$. By Erdős–Rado, $\kappa\to(\omega_1)^2_C$, so in particular every $C$-colouring of the edges of $K_\kappa$ has a monochromatic countably infinite clique. Take the ground set to be the edges of $K_\kappa$ and take the family to be the edge sets of all countably infinite cliques. Two such edge sets meet in either infinitely many edges or $\binom{n}{2}$ edges, and $\binom{n}{2}\neq 2$ for every $n$. The proof is about one paragraph, given Erdős–Rado. No publication is listed in the JSP record.

## What exactly must be proved in Lean
`∀ C : Cardinal.{0}, ∃ V F, (∀ A ∈ F, #A = ℵ₀) ∧ F.Pairwise (#(A∩B) ≠ 2) ∧ ⋃₀ F = univ ∧ ∀ c : V → C.out, ∃ A ∈ F, c constant on A`.
Only the weak form of Erdős–Rado is needed: $(2^C)^+ \to (\omega)^2_C$, meaning a countably infinite monochromatic clique, not an $\omega_1$-sized one.

## Mathlib readiness (exists / missing)
- Exists: `Cardinal.infinite_pigeonhole*` and `exists_infinite_fiber` in `SetTheory/Cardinal/Pigeonhole.lean`; `Cardinal.mk`, `succ`, `2^C`, cofinality and regularity of successor cardinals; `Set.ncard` and `Finset.card` for $\binom{n}{2}$.
- Missing: Erdős–Rado. A grep for Erdős–Rado or Ramsey-type partition calculus in Mathlib finds nothing relevant (only Hales–Jewett and Hindman). The plby proof builds it from scratch using the standard end-homogeneous-set / tree argument (`erPred` defined by well-founded recursion).

## Proof plan
(Moot because of the priority risk.) Define `erPred c x` by well-founded recursion as the predecessors that are compatible with all earlier ones. Prove that it is transitive and forms a chain. Show that some $x<\kappa$ has an infinite `erPred` chain, using a counting bound of at most $2^C$ possible types at each level together with regularity of $(2^C)^+$. Pigeonhole the colours $c(z, \cdot)$ along the chain to get an infinite monochromatic clique. Then handle the intersection arithmetic ($\binom{n}{2}\ne 2$) and coverage. This should take roughly 600–700 lines, which matches plby's 673-line file. No finite computation is involved; `decide`/`native_decide` are not needed.
