## Problem and known solution
Bollobás and Erdős conjectured that if a graph G has odd cycles of at most k distinct lengths, then χ(G) ≤ 2k+2, with equality iff G contains K_{2k+2}. Gyárfás proved this in 1992 ("Graphs with k odd cycle lengths", Discrete Math. 103 (1992) 41–48, about 8 pages). He proved a stronger statement: every 2-connected such graph either is K_{2k+2} or has a vertex of degree ≤ 2k. The argument works with longest paths/cycles and a search-tree structure. Gao, Huo and Ma (2021) later proved a stronger result: χ ≥ 2k+3 forces cycles of k+1 consecutive odd lengths. The JSP catalog lists the problem as Solved, with Lean proof "No" and Eligible "No".

## What exactly must be proved in Lean
The theorem `erdos_58` quantifies over **arbitrary** vertex types, including infinite ones. Its hypotheses are that `G.oddCycleLengths` is finite and that its `ncard` is ≤ k. Its conclusion is `G.chromaticNumber ≤ 2k+2`, together with `G.chromaticNumber = 2k+2 ↔ K_{2k+2} ⊑ G`. `oddCycleLengths` is defined in `FormalConjecturesForMathlib/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Circumference.lean` as the set of lengths of `IsCycle` walks with odd length. Proving this needs:
1. The full finite Gyárfás theorem, including the equality case. This is the hard part.
2. Compactness (de Bruijn–Erdős) to pass to infinite graphs.

## Mathlib readiness (exists / missing)
- Exists: `SimpleGraph.Walk.IsCycle`, `IsPath`, `chromaticNumber`/`Colorable`, `IsContained`, cliques, `Finset.rado_selection` / `Set.Finite.rado_selection` in `Mathlib/Combinatorics/Compactness.lean` (enough for de Bruijn–Erdős), and degeneracy-style greedy colouring pieces.
- Missing: longest path/cycle machinery for odd-cycle-length bookkeeping, 2-connected/critical-graph reduction (k-critical graphs have minimum degree ≥ k−1, and block structure), Gyárfás's structural lemma, and the equality-case classification. The existing plby development needed about 500 files to build all of this.

## Proof plan
(For the record only; not recommended.)
1. Reduce to a vertex-critical finite subgraph with minimum degree ≥ χ−1.
2. Prove Gyárfás's structural lemma for 2-connected graphs, using a longest path, its endpoint's neighbours, and the odd cycles those neighbours create.
3. Get the bound by degeneracy colouring.
4. Prove the equality case through criticality plus the K_{2k+2} rigidity.
5. Extend to infinite graphs with Rado selection.
No finite computation is involved, so `decide`/`native_decide` play no role.
