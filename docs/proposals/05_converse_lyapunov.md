## 1. Abstract

We present a complete, machine-checked formalization of the constructive algebraic and analytic foundations of the **Converse Discrete-Time Lyapunov Theorem** in Lean 4. While the direct discrete-time Lyapunov theorem (`ControlsLean.Controls.DiscreteLyapunov`) establishes that the existence of a positive-definite quadratic energy function $V(x) = x^\top P x$ with strictly positive step dissipation $x^\top (P - A^\top P A) x > 0$ implies geometric trajectory decay $A^k x_0 \to 0$, it leaves open the fundamental converse question of mathematical systems theory: *how is the Lyapunov certificate $P$ constructed from the system dynamics $A$ and a prescribed stage cost $Q \succ 0$?*

For a discrete-time linear time-invariant (LTI) system $x_{k+1} = A x_k$ ($A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$) and symmetric stage cost $Q \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$, the canonical Lyapunov candidate is generated by the finite-horizon discrete Lyapunov sum:
$$P_N = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^k)^\top Q A^k = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^\top)^k Q A^k.$$

We establish machine-checked proofs in `ControlsLean.Controls.ConverseLyapunov` that:
1. **Matrix Power Transpose & Dual Recurrences**: Matrix transposition commutes with natural powers ($(A^k)^\top = (A^\top)^k$, `transpose_pow`), and $P_N$ satisfies both the upper-index accumulation $P_{N+1} = P_N + (A^N)^\top Q A^N$ (`lyapunovSum_succ`) and the congruence-shifted Bellman recurrence $P_{N+1} = A^\top P_N A + Q$ (`lyapunovSum_succ'`).
2. **Exact Telescoping Discrete Stein / Lyapunov Identity**: Equating the two recurrences yields the exact algebraic Stein telescoping identity without requiring eigenvalue decompositions or Jordan canonical forms:
   $$A^\top P_N A - P_N = (A^N)^\top Q A^N - Q \qquad (\text{`stein\_telescoping` / `lyapunovSum\_step`}),$$
   and at the quadratic form level along trajectories:
   $$x^\top (A^\top P_N A - P_N) x = (A^N x)^\top Q (A^N x) - x^\top Q x \qquad (\text{`quad\_stein\_telescoping` / `dotProduct\_stein\_telescoping`}).$$
3. **Symmetry & Positive-Definiteness Preservation**: If $Q^\top = Q$, then $P_N^\top = P_N$ (`lyapunovSum_transpose`). For $N \ge 1$ and positive semidefinite $Q$ ($\forall v, 0 \le v^\top Q v$), $P_N$ dominates the initial stage cost $x^\top P_N x \ge x^\top Q x$ (`quad_lyapunovSum_ge`). Consequently, if $Q \succ 0$ (`Q.PosDef`) and $N \ge 1$, then $P_N \succ 0$ (`posDef_lyapunovSum`).
4. **Nilpotent / Deadbeat Exact Converse Lyapunov Certificate**: Whenever $A^N = 0$ (as achieved by deadbeat state-feedback pole placement in `ControlsLean.Controls.PolePlacement`) and $Q \succ 0$, the finite sum $P_N$ is strictly positive definite ($P_N \succ 0$) and satisfies the exact discrete-time Stein/Lyapunov equation $A^\top P_N A - P_N = -Q$ (`converse_lyapunov_nilpotent`).
5. **Infinite-Horizon Limit Converse Lyapunov Certificate & Bidirectional Bridge**: If $\lim_{N \to \infty} P_N = P$ and $\lim_{N \to \infty} (A^N)^\top Q A^N = 0$ in the matrix topology, then $P$ satisfies the exact Stein equation $A^\top P A - P = -Q$ (`stein_eq_of_tendsto`), preserves symmetry $P^\top = P$ (`transpose_of_tendsto_lyapunovSum`), dominates $Q$ ($x^\top P x \ge x^\top Q x$, `quad_ge_of_tendsto_lyapunovSum`), and satisfies $P \succ 0$ whenever $Q \succ 0$ (`converse_lyapunov_of_tendsto`), thereby directly certifying global asymptotic stability $A^k x_0 \to 0$ via `DiscreteLyapunov` (`tendsto_pow_mulVec_zero_of_converse_lyapunov`).

## 2. Mathematical Formulation

### 2.1 Finite-Horizon Lyapunov Sum and Cost-to-Go Interpretation
Consider the autonomous discrete-time linear dynamical system:
$$x_{k+1} = A x_k, \qquad x_0 \in \mathbb{R}^n, \quad A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}.$$
Given a stage weighting matrix $Q \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$, the cumulative quadratic cost incurred by the trajectory $\{x_k = A^k x_0\}_{k=0}^{N-1}$ over a finite horizon of $N \in \mathbb{N}$ steps is:
$$J_N(x_0) = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} x_k^\top Q x_k = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^k x_0)^\top Q (A^k x_0) = x_0^\top \left( \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^k)^\top Q A^k \right) x_0.$$
This motivates defining the $N$-horizon discrete Lyapunov matrix sum:
$$P_N = \mathrm{lyapunovSum}(A, Q, N) := \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^k)^\top Q A^k.$$
By induction on $k \in \mathbb{N}$ using the anti-homomorphism $(M N)^\top = N^\top M^\top$ and commutativity of powers $A^k A = A A^k$, transposition commutes with matrix exponentiation:
$$(A^k)^\top = (A^\top)^k,$$
so $P_N = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^\top)^k Q A^k$.

### 2.2 Dual Recurrences and the Stein Telescoping Identity
The finite sum $P_{N+1} = \sum_{k=0}^N (A^k)^\top Q A^k$ admits two distinct structural decompositions:

1. **Terminal-Term Splitting** (`Finset.sum_range_succ`):
   Isolating the $k = N$ term from the upper end of the sum gives:
   $$P_{N+1} = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^k)^\top Q A^k + (A^N)^\top Q A^N = P_N + (A^N)^\top Q A^N.$$

2. **Initial-Term Splitting / Bellman Shift** (`Finset.sum_range_succ'`):
   Isolating the $k = 0$ term $(A^0)^\top Q A^0 = I^\top Q I = Q$ and shifting the index $k \mapsto k + 1$ gives:
   $$P_{N+1} = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^{k+1})^\top Q A^{k+1} + Q.$$
   Since $A^{k+1} = A^k A$, its transpose is $(A^{k+1})^\top = (A^k A)^\top = A^\top (A^k)^\top$. By associativity and distributivity of matrix multiplication over finite sums:
   $$\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^{k+1})^\top Q A^{k+1} = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} A^\top (A^k)^\top Q A^k A = A^\top \left( \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^k)^\top Q A^k \right) A = A^\top P_N A.$$
   Hence:
   $$P_{N+1} = A^\top P_N A + Q.$$

Equating the right-hand sides of both recurrences yields:
$$A^\top P_N A + Q = P_N + (A^N)^\top Q A^N,$$
which rearranges to the **Exact Telescoping Discrete Stein / Lyapunov Identity**:
$$A^\top P_N A - P_N = (A^N)^\top Q A^N - Q.$$
Evaluating the associated quadratic form at any state $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ yields the trajectory energy balance:
$$x^\top (A^\top P_N A - P_N) x = (A^N x)^\top Q (A^N x) - x^\top Q x.$$

### 2.3 Symmetry and Positive-Definiteness Preservation
Suppose $Q$ is symmetric ($Q^\top = Q$). Then each stage summand satisfies:
$$\left( (A^k)^\top Q A^k \right)^\top = (A^k)^\top Q^\top ((A^k)^\top)^\top = (A^k)^\top Q A^k,$$
so by linearity of transposition over finite sums, $P_N^\top = P_N$.

Suppose further that $Q$ is positive semidefinite ($\forall v \in \mathbb{R}^n, v^\top Q v \ge 0$). Then for every $M \in \mathbb{N}$ and every vector $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$:
$$v^\top P_M v = \sum_{k=0}^{M-1} (A^k v)^\top Q (A^k v) \ge 0.$$
For any horizon $N \ge 1$, writing $N = M + 1$ and invoking the Bellman recurrence $P_{M+1} = A^\top P_M A + Q$:
$$x^\top P_N x = (A x)^\top P_M (A x) + x^\top Q x \ge x^\top Q x.$$
Consequently, if $Q$ is strictly positive definite ($Q^\top = Q$ and $\forall x \neq 0, x^\top Q x > 0$), then for every $N \ge 1$ and $x \neq 0$:
$$x^\top P_N x \ge x^\top Q x > 0,$$
certifying that $P_N$ is symmetric positive definite ($P_N \succ 0$).

### 2.4 Nilpotent / Deadbeat Converse Lyapunov Certificate
If the system matrix $A$ is nilpotent of index $N$ ($A^N = 0$, such as a deadbeat closed-loop system $A_{cl} = A - B K_{db}$), the terminal remainder $(A^N)^\top Q A^N$ vanishes identically:
$$(A^N)^\top Q A^N = 0^\top Q 0 = 0.$$
The Stein telescoping identity then collapses at finite horizon $N$ to the exact discrete-time Lyapunov equation:
$$A^\top P_N A - P_N = -Q,$$
with $P_N \succ 0$ whenever $Q \succ 0$.

### 2.5 Infinite-Horizon Limit Converse Lyapunov Certificate
In the general asymptotically stable (Schur) regime where $(A^N)^\top Q A^N \to 0$ and the partial sums $P_N$ converge to a limit matrix $P = \sum_{k=0}^\infty (A^k)^\top Q A^k$ in the finite-dimensional matrix topology on $\mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$:
1. By continuity of the linear Stein operator $X \mapsto A^\top X A - X$:
   $$A^\top P A - P = \lim_{N \to \infty} (A^\top P_N A - P_N) = \lim_{N \to \infty} \left( (A^N)^\top Q A^N - Q \right) = 0 - Q = -Q.$$
2. By continuity of matrix transposition $X \mapsto X^\top$ and Hausdorff uniqueness of limits (`tendsto_nhds_unique`):
   $$P^\top = \lim_{N \to \infty} P_N^\top = \lim_{N \to \infty} P_N = P.$$
3. By continuity of the quadratic evaluation functional $X \mapsto x^\top X x$ and closedness of the upper-bounded ray $[x^\top Q x, \infty)$ (`ge_of_tendsto`), the uniform lower bound $x^\top P_N x \ge x^\top Q x$ ($N \ge 1$) passes to the limit:
   $$x^\top P x \ge x^\top Q x \qquad \forall x \in \mathbb{R}^n.$$
   Hence $Q \succ 0 \implies P \succ 0$, completing the constructive converse Lyapunov certificate.

## 3. Formalization Architecture in Lean 4

The implementation is contained in `ControlsLean/Controls/ConverseLyapunov.lean` and builds directly upon `ControlsLean.Controls.DiscreteLyapunov` and `ControlsLean.Tactics.ControlTactics`.

### 3.1 Design Decisions
1. **Index-Type Generality**:
   Matching `DiscreteLyapunov.lean`, all definitions and theorems are parameterized over an arbitrary finite index type `{n : Type*} [Fintype n] [DecidableEq n]` rather than restricting to `Fin m`. This allows immediate instantiation on both canonical `Fin n` state spaces and block-sum state spaces `l ⊕ m` used in observer and LQR synthesis.
2. **Dual Recurrence Proof of Stein Telescoping**:
   Rather than proving `stein_telescoping` by induction and manual matrix ring expansion at each step, we derive it algebraically from `lyapunovSum_succ` (`Finset.sum_range_succ`) and `lyapunovSum_succ'` (`Finset.sum_range_succ'`). This isolates the matrix-power transpose algebra into a single four-line congruence proof (`lyapunovSum_succ'`), after which `stein_telescoping` and `lyapunovSum_dissipation` follow purely by `abel` in the additive group of matrices.
3. **Topological Limit Passage via `fun_prop`**:
   Because `Matrix n n ℝ` is definitionally the finite Pi type `n → n → ℝ`, Lean 4's `fun_prop` tactic automatically discharges continuity of matrix transposition (`X ↦ Xᵀ`), quadratic form evaluation (`X ↦ x ⬝ᵥ (X *ᵥ x)`), and the Stein operator (`X ↦ Aᵀ * X * A - X`). Combining these with `tendsto_nhds_unique` and `ge_of_tendsto` yields concise, elementary proofs of all infinite-horizon limit properties.
4. **Unconditional Nilpotent Certificate**:
   In `converse_lyapunov_nilpotent`, we avoid even requiring `1 ≤ N` as an explicit hypothesis: when $N = 0$, $A^0 = 1 = 0$ implies that the underlying vector space `n → ℝ` is subsingleton ($x = 1 x = 0 x = 0$), so positive definiteness of $P_0 = 0$ holds vacuously while $A^\top P_0 A - P_0 = 0 = -Q$ continues to hold by `stein_telescoping`.

### 3.2 Formal Lean 4 Declarations

#### Definitions
```lean
namespace ControlsLean.Controls

variable {n : Type*} [Fintype n] [DecidableEq n]

/-- The `k`-th stage Lyapunov summand `(A ^ k)ᵀ * Q * (A ^ k)`. -/
def lyapunovTerm (A Q : Matrix n n ℝ) (k : ℕ) : Matrix n n ℝ :=
  (A ^ k)ᵀ * Q * (A ^ k)

/-- The finite-horizon discrete Lyapunov sum:
`P_N = ∑ k ∈ Finset.range N, (A ^ k)ᵀ * Q * (A ^ k)`. -/
def lyapunovSum (A Q : Matrix n n ℝ) (N : ℕ) : Matrix n n ℝ :=
  ∑ k ∈ Finset.range N, (A ^ k)ᵀ * Q * (A ^ k)
```

#### Headline Theorems
```lean
/-- Matrix transposition commutes with natural number powers: `(A ^ k)ᵀ = (Aᵀ) ^ k`. -/
@[simp]
theorem transpose_pow (A : Matrix n n ℝ) (k : ℕ) : (A ^ k)ᵀ = (Aᵀ) ^ k

/-- Congruence-shifted recurrence: `P_{N+1} = Aᵀ * P_N * A + Q`. -/
theorem lyapunovSum_succ' (A Q : Matrix n n ℝ) (N : ℕ) :
    lyapunovSum A Q (N + 1) = Aᵀ * lyapunovSum A Q N * A + Q

/-- **Exact Telescoping Discrete Stein / Lyapunov Identity**:
`Aᵀ * P_N * A - P_N = (A ^ N)ᵀ * Q * (A ^ N) - Q`. -/
theorem stein_telescoping (A Q : Matrix n n ℝ) (N : ℕ) :
    Aᵀ * lyapunovSum A Q N * A - lyapunovSum A Q N = (A ^ N)ᵀ * Q * (A ^ N) - Q

/-- **Quadratic-Form Stein Telescoping Identity** (`dotProduct` version):
`x ⬝ᵥ ((Aᵀ * P_N * A - P_N) *ᵥ x) = ((A ^ N) *ᵥ x) ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ ((A ^ N) *ᵥ x)) - x ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ x)`. -/
theorem dotProduct_stein_telescoping (A Q : Matrix n n ℝ) (N : ℕ) (x : n → ℝ) :
    x ⬝ᵥ ((Aᵀ * lyapunovSum A Q N * A - lyapunovSum A Q N) *ᵥ x) =
      ((A ^ N) *ᵥ x) ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ ((A ^ N) *ᵥ x)) - x ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ x)

/-- **Symmetry Preservation**: If `Qᵀ = Q`, then `(lyapunovSum A Q N)ᵀ = lyapunovSum A Q N`. -/
theorem lyapunovSum_transpose (A Q : Matrix n n ℝ) (hQ : Qᵀ = Q) (N : ℕ) :
    (lyapunovSum A Q N)ᵀ = lyapunovSum A Q N

/-- **Lower Bound by Stage Cost `Q`**:
For `N ≥ 1` and positive semidefinite `Q` (`∀ v, 0 ≤ v ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ v)`),
`x ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ x) ≤ x ⬝ᵥ (lyapunovSum A Q N *ᵥ x)` for all `x`. -/
theorem quad_lyapunovSum_ge (A Q : Matrix n n ℝ)
    (hQ : ∀ v : n → ℝ, 0 ≤ v ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ v)) {N : ℕ} (hN : 1 ≤ N) (x : n → ℝ) :
    x ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ x) ≤ x ⬝ᵥ (lyapunovSum A Q N *ᵥ x)

/-- **Positive-Definiteness Preservation**:
If `Q` is `Matrix.PosDef` and `1 ≤ N`, then `lyapunovSum A Q N` is `Matrix.PosDef`. -/
theorem posDef_lyapunovSum (A Q : Matrix n n ℝ) (hQ : Q.PosDef) {N : ℕ} (hN : 1 ≤ N) :
    (lyapunovSum A Q N).PosDef

/-- **Nilpotent / Deadbeat Converse Discrete-Time Lyapunov Theorem**:
If `A ^ N = 0` (e.g., a deadbeat closed-loop system) and `Q` is `Matrix.PosDef`,
then `P_N = lyapunovSum A Q N` is `Matrix.PosDef` and satisfies the exact discrete-time
Lyapunov equation `Aᵀ * P_N * A - P_N = -Q`. -/
theorem converse_lyapunov_nilpotent (A Q : Matrix n n ℝ) {N : ℕ}
    (hA : A ^ N = 0) (hQ : Q.PosDef) :
    (lyapunovSum A Q N).PosDef ∧ Aᵀ * lyapunovSum A Q N * A - lyapunovSum A Q N = -Q

/-- **Infinite-Horizon Converse Discrete-Time Lyapunov Theorem** (`PosDef` certificate form):
If `lyapunovSum A Q N → P` and `(A ^ N)ᵀ * Q * (A ^ N) → 0` as `N → ∞`,
and `Q` is `Matrix.PosDef`, then `P` is `Matrix.PosDef`, satisfies the exact discrete-time
Lyapunov equation `Aᵀ * P * A - P = -Q`, and dominates `Q` (`∀ x, x ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ x) ≤ x ⬝ᵥ (P *ᵥ x)`). -/
theorem converse_lyapunov_of_tendsto (A Q P : Matrix n n ℝ)
    (hP : Tendsto (lyapunovSum A Q) atTop (𝓝 P))
    (hA : Tendsto (fun N => (A ^ N)ᵀ * Q * (A ^ N)) atTop (𝓝 0))
    (hQ : Q.PosDef) :
    P.PosDef ∧ Aᵀ * P * A - P = -Q ∧ (∀ x : n → ℝ, x ⬝ᵥ (Q *ᵥ x) ≤ x ⬝ᵥ (P *ᵥ x))

/-- **Bidirectional Bridge (Direct + Converse Lyapunov Equivalence)**:
When `P` is obtained as the infinite-horizon Lyapunov limit for `Q.PosDef`, `P` directly
certifies global asymptotic stability `A ^ k *ᵥ x₀ → 0` via `PosDef.tendsto_pow_mulVec_zero`. -/
theorem tendsto_pow_mulVec_zero_of_converse_lyapunov (A Q P : Matrix n n ℝ)
    (hP : Tendsto (lyapunovSum A Q) atTop (𝓝 P))
    (hA : Tendsto (fun N => (A ^ N)ᵀ * Q * (A ^ N)) atTop (𝓝 0))
    (hQ : Q.PosDef) (x₀ : n → ℝ) :
    Tendsto (fun k : ℕ => (A ^ k) *ᵥ x₀) atTop (𝓝 0)
```

## 4. Novelty Analysis & Gap Positioning

### 4.1 Comparison against Seed Codebase (`ControlsLean.Controls.DiscreteLyapunov`)
- **Seed Scope**: `DiscreteLyapunov.lean` proves the *direct* Lyapunov theorem (`tendsto_pow_mulVec_zero` and `PosDef.tendsto_pow_mulVec_zero`): assuming a matrix $P \succ 0$ with $P - A^\top P A \succ 0$ is already given as an external hypothesis, every trajectory satisfies $A^k x_0 \to 0$.
- **Gap Resolved**: `DiscreteLyapunov.lean` contains no construction of $P$, no finite-horizon Lyapunov sums $P_N = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (A^k)^\top Q A^k$, no Stein telescoping identity $A^\top P_N A - P_N = (A^N)^\top Q A^N - Q$, and no converse stability certificates. `ConverseLyapunov.lean` closes this exact gap and connects back to `PosDef.tendsto_pow_mulVec_zero` via `tendsto_pow_mulVec_zero_of_converse_lyapunov`.

### 4.2 Synergy with Deadbeat Pole Placement (`ControlsLean.Controls.PolePlacement`)
- In `PolePlacement.lean`, deadbeat state-feedback control renders the closed-loop companion matrix nilpotent ($A_{cl}^2 = 0$). Combining `deadbeat_nilpotent` with `converse_lyapunov_nilpotent` immediately yields an explicit, closed-form quadratic Lyapunov certificate $P_2 = Q + A_{cl}^\top Q A_{cl} \succ 0$ satisfying $A_{cl}^\top P_2 A_{cl} - P_2 = -Q$ for any $Q \succ 0$.

### 4.3 Comparison against Mathlib
- Mathlib provides general `Matrix.PosDef` and `Filter.Tendsto` machinery, but contains zero formalizations of the Stein equation $A^\top P A - P = -Q$, finite-horizon Lyapunov sums, or discrete-time converse Lyapunov theory.

## 5. Literature Grounding & Citations

1. **Stein, P.** (1952). "Some General Theorems on Iterants." *Journal of Research of the National Bureau of Standards*, 48(1): 82–87.
   - *Historical Significance*: Introduced the discrete-time matrix equation $A^\top P A - P = -Q$ (now universally termed the **Stein equation** or discrete-time Lyapunov equation) and established the congruence series characterization of contractions.
   - *Lean Formalization*: Formalized in `stein_telescoping`, `quad_stein_telescoping`, and `stein_eq_of_tendsto`.

2. **Kalman, R. E., & Bertram, J. E.** (1960). "Control System Analysis and Design Via the 'Second Method' of Lyapunov: II. Discrete-Time Systems." *Journal of Basic Engineering (Transactions of the ASME)*, 82(2): 394–400.
   - *Historical Significance*: Established the direct and converse Lyapunov theorems for discrete-time linear systems via the infinite-horizon quadratic cost series $P = \sum_{k=0}^\infty (A^\top)^k Q A^k$.
   - *Lean Formalization*: Formalized in `lyapunovSum`, `posDef_lyapunovSum`, `converse_lyapunov_of_tendsto`, and `tendsto_pow_mulVec_zero_of_converse_lyapunov`.

3. **Hespanha, J. P.** (2018). *Linear Systems Theory* (2nd ed.). Princeton University Press.

     Derives the telescoping identity $A^\top P_N A - P_N = (A^\top)^N Q A^N - Q$ and proves that $P = \lim_{N \to \infty} P_N$ is symmetric positive definite with $x^\top P x \ge x^\top Q x$ whenever $Q \succ 0$. Formalized in `stein_telescoping`, `quad_lyapunovSum_ge`, and `converse_lyapunov_of_tendsto`.

4. **Horn, R. A., & Johnson, C. R.** (1991). *Topics in Matrix Analysis*. Cambridge University Press.
   - *Chapter 2 (Stable Matrices and Inertia)*, Section 2.2 ("The Stein Equation"), Lemma 2.2.1 & Theorem 2.2.2, pp. 99–103:
