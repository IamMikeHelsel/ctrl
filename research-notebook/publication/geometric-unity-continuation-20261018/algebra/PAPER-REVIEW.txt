   factors. They remain distinct throughout. Volume square uses exponent 91;

2. The (7,7) generator signature and (64,64) form signature concern separate

   uniqueness assumes compatibility for every complex Clifford element. These
   hypotheses match the two uniqueness source files.
4. HermitianFamily uses W=rhoC(i omega), W^2=-I and W-dagger K=KW. The order of
   the stated inverse, (I-tW)K/(1+t^2), is correct. Both inverse equations and
