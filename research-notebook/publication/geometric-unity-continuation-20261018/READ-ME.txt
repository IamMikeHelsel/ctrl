The question
Can selected split Clifford data used in Geometric Unity be made explicit and
checked, and what becomes clear when their assumptions are separated?

Verified baseline
Actual exterior-algebra representation, full real and complex matrix algebra
equivalences, actual adjoint, and explicit (64,64) spinor signature. These are
standard algebraic results, with a concrete Lean construction and replay record.

New research directions in this round
- Classification of all fully compatible real and complex forms.
- Nonproportional nondegenerate Spin-invariant Hermitian forms.
- Actual skew locus, commutator closure, and split-coordinate transport.
- Full fourteen-generator volume and independent skew invariant candidates.
- Group conjugation formulas for the exact mathlib spinGroup definition.
- Exact quadratic normalization condition for vector twists.
- Explicit inner equivalence for nontrivial normalized choices.

Important boundaries
K is the product of seven negative generators. The full volume omega is the
product of all fourteen generators. They are different objects.
Full Clifford-vector compatibility is stronger than Spin invariance.
The complex skew locus is a REAL vector space. Multiplication by i need not
preserve it.
Distinct tensors need not give distinct operators or physics.
The source displays an action; its precise operator choices and variation data
still need to be fixed before source-specific field equations can be formalized.
No proof of the whole theory, new physical prediction, or publication novelty
is asserted. See ACCEPTANCE.json and the final acceptance report for exact completed proof gates.
