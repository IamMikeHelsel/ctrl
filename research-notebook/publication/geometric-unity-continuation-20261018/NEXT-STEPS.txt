Next research gates

1. Finish the representation-theory interface.
Bundle the real vector maps as equivariant linear maps for the exact Spin action.
Descend the alternating pair map to Lambda²V. Formalize the invariant-tensor/dual
identification, then prove the Hom-space lower bounds as Lean finrank results.
Do not replace the existing hand-proof label until those interfaces compile and
pass independent review. Existing raw identities and independence lemmas provide
the algebraic core, but they are not that bundled theorem.

2. Determine which raw choices remain after quotienting by allowed symmetries.
The normalized vector family is inner-equivalent in the Clifford algebra.
The intended gauge transformations may impose a stricter equivalence. Prove the
positive/negative branch distinction for the specified group and how every field
transforms before assigning physical meaning to a parameter. Similarly, the
Spin-invariant H_t family is nonproportional; its classification under coordinate
changes has not been proved here.

3. Define the source-specific operator before proving its properties.
Required inputs: normalized Phi tensors or free coefficients; each product,
bracket and projection; domain of curvature tensors; orientation/Hodge and
curvature conventions; the selected Bianchi identity and torsion assumptions.
Then test whether different candidate directions survive or vanish on that domain.
The raw tensor lower bounds do not establish distinct Shiab operators.

4. Define the variational problem.
The 2021 draft displays an action. Fix independent fields, induced metric and
connection variations, integration domain, support/boundary conditions, and when
pullback occurs. State and prove a bounded Euler-Lagrange identity only after
those choices are explicit.
