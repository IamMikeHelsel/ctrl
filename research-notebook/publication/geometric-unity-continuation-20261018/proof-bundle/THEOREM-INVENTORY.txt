Theorem inventory and exact scope

All unqualified names below are in HyperbolicSpinor unless noted otherwise.
Exact Lean declarations remain authoritative; source line numbers can drift only
if a future copy changes them. source-hashes.json fixes this copy.

1. CliffordModel.lean, namespace GUClifford
Q is weightedSumSquares on Fin 14 -> real, with first seven signs positive and
last seven negative. Cl is mathlib's CliffordAlgebra Q. gamma_relation proves
{gamma_a,gamma_b}=2 eps_a delta_ab. This is the real (7,7) generator convention.

2. HyperbolicSpinor.lean, SplitSpinor.lean, SpinorDimensions.lean
S7 is the exterior algebra on a seven-dimensional real space. Its actual ordered
exterior basis has 128 subset labels. splitAction is the algebra action built
from creation and contraction. splitMatrix is that action in spinBasis.
clifford_finrank proves 16384. These dimensions alone are not faithfulness.

3. BasisActions, FockOperators, FockImage, WordActions, VacuumProjector,
MatrixUnits, FullImage, RealEquivalence
Matrix units arise in the image through actual operator words and the vacuum
projector. splitMatrix_surjective plus the dimension argument gives
splitMatrix_injective. splitMatrixEquiv is Cl ≃ₐ[real] Matrix (Finset (Fin 7)) _ real.
This is an actual full-matrix algebra equivalence, not an assumed representation.

4. TransposeBridge, VolumeForm, RealAdjoint
K is the ordered product of seven negative generators in the same actual basis.
K_transpose, K_square prove Kᵀ=K and K²=I. realAdjoint is A -> K Aᵀ K.
splitMatrixEquiv_star intertwines Clifford conjugation and this form adjoint.
This fixes every vector to minus itself under conjugation.

5. ComplexAdjoint, ComplexEquivalence, ComplexBridge
GUComplex.CCl is complex ⊗[real] Cl. complexMatrixEquiv is an algebra equivalence
over complex to the full 128 by 128 complex matrix algebra. bar conjugates the
complex scalar AND applies real Clifford conjugation. complexMatrixEquiv_bar
proves rho(bar x)=Kc rho(x)ᴴ Kc, where Kc is the entrywise extension of actual K.
skew_iff_matrix identifies the skew locus exactly. bar(i)=-i is essential.
This is a declared scalar-conjugation convention; the source GU paper's intended
convention must be determined separately. No assertion resolves that source issue.

6. ComplementAction, SplitSignature, ComplexSignature
K sends e_T to (-1)^(sum over i in T of (7-i)) e_complement(T).
D has diagonal +1 without index zero, -1 with index zero. U=I+KD.
U_congruence: Uᵀ K U=2D. U_orthogonal_scaled: UᵀU=2I.
Uinverse_left/right give both inverses, Uinverse=(1/2)Uᵀ.
positive_half_count and negative_half_count each prove 64.
Uc_congruence and Ucinverse_left/right transport the same result to conjugate
transpose over complex. This is an explicit (64,64) spinor form. It is distinct
from the (7,7) form on the 14-dimensional generator space. The formal diagonal
is still subset-indexed; no separate Fin 64 reindexing is claimed.

7. AdjointControls, OrderControls, ComplexControls, SignatureControls,
ReviewerClient, CounterWitnesses
Concrete witnesses reject omitted complement/sign, positive-only signature,
wrong scale, mistaken inverse, wrong half-count, fixed imaginary scalar and the
singular I+K substitution. Nine false statements are both rejected and negated.

What is not established
A differential-geometric model of GU; the Phi operators or projected brackets;
Bianchi assumptions; variational field equations; gravity/Standard Model recovery;
physical predictions; uniqueness from Spin invariance alone; literature novelty.
The full-generator adjoint condition is stronger than Spin invariance. The matrix
commutant argument under the former must not be advertised as the latter.
