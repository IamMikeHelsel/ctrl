Independent review: actual Spin action and rational inner change

SpinEquivariance is tied to the pinned mathlib definition of spinGroup Q. That
definition intersects the unitary group with the even part of the algebraic
subgroup generated by invertible Clifford vectors. It is not an independently
formalized connected Lie group, double cover or identification with a named
classical group. These distinctions matter in a formal theorem statement.

The even-induction argument establishes actual full-volume commutation with all
even elements. Mathlib's conjugation-in-range theorem supplies the actual vector
w transformed from v; both original and volume-twisted choices use the same w.
The skew-conjugation lemma needs no group assumption (any s*x*star(s) preserves
skewness), which is stronger and harmless. The group premise is genuinely used
for the vector range result and even commutation. The identity is a proved group
member. A reviewer proves that the full volume is not central in the whole
Clifford algebra; dropping the even restriction is false.

RationalBoost fixes T=2+omega, inverse=(2-omega)/3. Both inverse identities,
the vector conjugation formula, commutation with Spin and conformal K-preservation
are exact actual-algebra statements. star(T)*T=3I, so this T has not been shown to
be a Spin element and in fact fails unit norm. A reviewer proves omitting /3
from the inverse instead gives product 3I. Both false claims are rejected with
mathematical goals, with exact negations proved independently.
