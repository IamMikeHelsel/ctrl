Independent review of added uniqueness modules

FormUniqueness
The statement quantifies over every real Clifford vector. It assumes neither
symmetry nor invertibility. The zero form is therefore correctly admitted.
Clifford induction extends the intertwiner identity to all algebra elements.
The proved surjective matrix representation turns H*K into a full-matrix
centralizer, so it is scalar. This yields exactly H=cK, including c=0.
Normalization at any nonzero K entry fixes c=1.

Independent clients prove 2K is compatible and differs from K, and exhibit a
nonzero coordinate suitable for normalization. The diagonal K(empty,empty)=0
provides a counterexample to normalization without the nonzero-entry assumption.
The false scale and false zero-coordinate uniqueness claims are both negated in
Lean and rejected by separate false clients with unsolved mathematical goals.

ComplexFormUniqueness
ComplexCompatible quantifies over every element of the complex Clifford algebra
and uses the declared conjugate-linear bar. It is a full intertwiner premise;
it is not merely Spin invariance and is stronger in its statement than a vector
condition. The centralizer argument yields H=cKc for complex c. Hermitian symmetry
forces c to be real, using Kc nonzero. No nondegeneracy assumption excludes zero.

An independent client proves i*Kc is compatible but not Hermitian and not a real
multiple of Kc. This verifies that the Hermitian premise cannot be dropped from
the real-scalar conclusion. A false real-multiple client is also rejected.
