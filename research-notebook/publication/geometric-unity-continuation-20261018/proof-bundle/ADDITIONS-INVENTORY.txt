Accepted additions and exact scope

FormUniqueness
VectorCompatible H iff H=cK for real c. The premise is compatibility with every
real Clifford vector. No nondegeneracy or symmetry premise is hidden. c=0 is
allowed. Equality at a nonzero K entry fixes c=1. A zero entry cannot fix scale.

ComplexFormUniqueness
ComplexCompatible H iff H=cKc for complex c, using the full intertwiner premise
for every complex Clifford element and the declared conjugate-linear bar. If H
is Hermitian, c is real. i*Kc shows the Hermitian premise is needed. These are
full-adjoint compatibility statements, not uniqueness under Spin invariance.

SkewLie
The actual Clifford skew locus corresponds bijectively to matrices satisfying
A^T K+KA=0. Commutators stay in the locus. A -> Uinverse*A*U transports the
condition to D. It proves a subtype equivalence and bracket identities, not a
bundled mathlib LieEquiv. Ordinary multiplication does not preserve this locus.

Chirality
omega is the actual ordered product gamma0...gamma13, with omega²=1 and
star(omega)=-omega. It anticommutes with vectors and commutes with their even
products. omega is not scalar. Twisted vectors remain skew. Its real matrix is
actual; no separate parity matrix is assumed. The twist changes a vector square
by a minus sign and hence does not alone preserve Q.

VolumeTwist
Eight generic conditional lemmas about skewness, equivariance and independence.
Theorems with assumptions remain conditional. Do not count them as a GU-specific
operator construction. Their later concrete instantiations are listed next.

VolumeTwistActual
The actual gamma0 is a unit. gamma0 and omega*gamma0 are real-linearly independent.
Their generator-indexed functions are independent and their affine family is
injective and skew. This is not a definition or dimension computation of a
space of Spin-equivariant tensors.

QuadraticTwist
F(a,b)(v)=a*iota(v)+b*omega*iota(v) squares to (a²-b²)Q(v)I.
It preserves all vector squares iff a²-b²=1. A normalized F lifts through the
Clifford universal property to an algebra endomorphism. The rational parameters
(5/3,4/3) give a nontrivial normalized choice. The theorem does not yet package
all such lifts as algebra equivalences; RationalBoost supplies the concrete
inner realization for that rational choice.

ComplexChoices
The actual alternating map B(v,w)=(iota(v)iota(w)-iota(w)iota(v))/2 and
its i*omega-twisted complex counterpart are skew under conjugate-linear bar.
As functions on V×V they are real-linearly independent, witnessed on (e0,e1).
The file does not descend the map through exterior power, construct an invariant
Hom space or compute its full dimension. Complex scalar conjugation matters.

SpinEquivariance
omega commutes with all actual even elements and therefore the pinned mathlib
spinGroup. Both vector choices transform with the same actual conjugated vector.
The group is defined through algebraic closure of invertible vectors, unitary
condition and evenness. No Lie-group double-cover theorem is proved here.

RationalBoost
T=2+omega is an actual unit with inverse (2-omega)/3. Its inner conjugation sends
vectors to F(5/3,4/3), and T commutes with mathlib Spin. star(T)T=3I and its matrix
satisfies rho(T)^T K rho(T)=3K. Thus the rational alternative is inner-equivalent
to the original realization. These are not physically distinct models, and T
itself is not a unit-norm Spin element.

Global boundary
None of these statements fixes the source GU Phi operator, builds its proposed
field equations, proves physical predictions or establishes research novelty.
The reusable result is a checked algebraic specification with explicit choices,
proof dependencies, counterexamples and equivalence relations.

Final extension: LiftEquivalence
The rational normalized lift is exactly conjugation by T on ALL Clifford elements,
not only on generators. It is bijective. This does not make it the identity:
the reviewer proves the rational lift moves gamma0.

Final extension: GeneralBoost
For every a²-b²=1 in the displayed family, an actual unit commuting with mathlib
Spin conjugates vectors to F(a,b). For a != -1 use x=a+1,y=b and denominator
x²-y²=2(a+1). The exceptional point a=-1 forces b=0; omega implements it.
The inverse formula requires its nonzero denominator; a reviewer rejects using
x=y=1. This is algebraic inner equivalence of this two-parameter family, not a
classification of all tensors, or equivalence under a prescribed unitary gauge
group. omega has star norm -1 and cannot be normalized to +1 by real scaling.

Final extension: HermitianFamily
W=rho(i*omega), H_t=Kc(I+tW), real t. Every H_t is Hermitian, has a proved
explicit two-sided inverse, and is invariant under the actual spinMatrix action
of mathlib spinGroup. H_1 is not any complex scalar multiple of Kc.
An independent client combines this with ComplexFormUniqueness to prove H_1 is
not fully Clifford-compatible. This is a concrete distinction between Spin
invariance and full-adjoint compatibility, without relying on a generic warning.
No inertia or complete classification of this Hermitian family is asserted.
