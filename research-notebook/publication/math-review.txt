Independent mathematical review — 2026-10-17

Disposition

The reviewed Bernstein/LDL soundness layer, positive energy bounds, arbitrary
sample-index jitter bound, and quadratic affine robustness layer are sound in
their written formal scope. I found no false theorem in these Lean sources and
no producer route that turns an unchecked certificate into a checked theorem.
There are three manuscript-to-formalization gaps that should be resolved before
submission, one of which hides a necessary sign hypothesis.

This is a source-level mathematical review. I did not repeat the parent's full
build or all benchmark tests. Successful builds and axiom audits remain separate
evidence. Source hashes at the review snapshot are listed below.

Findings requiring action

1. Necessary actuator sign hypothesis is not explicit in the proposition.
   main.tex lines 105–113 give qR+b<=R and 10R<=U^2, then conclude that the
   rounded request has magnitude strictly below U under the "full feasible
   budget." This phrase is not a defined list of assumptions. In particular,
   U>=0 is essential, and does not follow from the displayed squared condition.
   The Lean theorem correctly assumes hU: 0<=U.

   Concrete exact counterexample to a reading that omits U>=0:
     theta=1/240, hmin=1/20, delta=1/10000, W=1/4000,
     R=1/10, U=-1, x=v=0.
   Then q=28679/28800, b=6507/50000000, and
     R-qR-b=260999/900000000>0; 10R=U^2.
   All other displayed conditions hold, while the rounded request is zero and
   the alleged conclusion 0<-1 is false.

   Fix: explicitly list theta>0, delta>0, W>=0, U>=0, hmin<=1/2, V<=R,
   qR+b<=R, and 10R<=U^2 in the proposition or a precisely defined preceding
   certificate predicate. The Lean theorem does not require hmin>=0 for this
   particular conclusion; the trajectory theorems do require it. Do not silently
   interchange the hypothesis lists of the one-step, invariant, and limitation
   theorems.

2. A model bridge is claimed for every segment but is missing for the generated
   two-state examples at this snapshot.
   main.tex line 84 says the equality of each segment to the generated transition
   is checked. HeldModels.Jerk.segment_eq_transition and
   HeldModels.Planar.segment_eq_transition establish this for their generated
   benchmarks. SampledPD proves initial conditions and derivatives for its own
   two-state formulas, but a source search found no equality connecting those
   formulas to Generated.DoubleIntegrator.transition or
   Generated.DoubleIntegratorWide.transition.

   Their polynomials visibly agree, so this is an easy missing formal bridge,
   not a suspected mathematical discrepancy. Add explicit equality theorems (and
   the vector energy correspondence if claiming the same P-energy interface),
   or restrict line 84 to the jerk and planar examples. Reusing a mathematically
   identical polynomial by human inspection is not yet the claimed Lean bridge.

3. Robust invariance and ultimate bounds must be identified as sample-time
   guarantees; the manuscript should not imply the same R-bound at all real times.
   Certificate.pd_quantized_invariant and pd_quantized_guarantee quantify natural
   sample indices. They do not assert V(segment(t,z_n))<=R for every
   0<=t<=h_n. In particular, substituting an intermediate t<hmin into the
   trajectory certificate's hmin-based budget is not justified.

   HeldModels provides stronger nominal statements for jerk/planar: every segment
   offset 0<=t<=h_n is bounded by the same sample-index geometric envelope. These
   are sound and should be reported with those quantifiers. The new robustness
   instance has no corresponding theorem. The older disturbed intersample result
   at h<=1/6 with different budgets does not establish the newer h<=1/2 claim.

   Fix: state explicitly after the robust recurrence/ultimate bound that those
   results hold at samples, and state the separate nominal segment guarantee.
   Keep global real-time assembly, no-Zeno coverage, and robust intersample bounds
   as separate obligations unless proved. This is a formal coverage limitation,
   not a claim that the desired intersample statement is false.

Positive-form and interval-certificate checks

- The interval substitution t=(h-a)/(b-a) uses a<b and correctly obtains
  0<=t<=1. All interval points are quantified; no finite sample grid is used.
- The sum of PSD Bernstein coefficients with nonnegative weights is PSD. The
  independently proved sum-of-weights identity is unnecessary for this cone
  argument, which is fine. Coefficients need only be semidefinite.
- The LDL soundness theorem does not need triangular or nonsingular L. Those
  properties are useful for finding certificates, but C=L diag(d)L^T and d>=0
  already imply PSD. Zero diagonal entries in a coefficient witness are safe.
- The exact identity multiplied by h connects the producer's divided residual
  to the actual matrix P-A(h)^T P A(h)-hcI. Nonnegative h is essential when
  scaling PSD; the generated decrease proof derives it from the partition.
- A(0)=I makes symbolic division polynomial. At h=0 the checked identity is
  still meaningful and the decrease becomes equality/nonexpansion; no division
  by zero appears in the accepted theorem.
- P is now checked positively through lowerBound>0 and the PSD witness for
  P-lowerBound I, in addition to the producer's strict rational LDL check.
  Thus positivity is not merely a Python assertion. Likewise the upper energy
  witness is checked in Lean. These address the earlier negative-P loophole.
- The choices lowerBound=1/tr(P^{-1}) and upperBound=tr(P) are conservative valid
  bounds for positive-definite P; acceptance depends on direct checked matrix
  witnesses, not on trusting this spectral fact or numerical eigenvalues.
- The generic energy_bounds theorem itself permits nonpositive constants. It
  correctly promises only the inequalities. The generated lower_pos and
  upper_pos declarations supply the positivity needed for state control.
- The finite coverage implementation uses ordered partition endpoints and exact
  case splits. The generic covered_psd theorem actually permits any index type;
  its conclusion needs only the explicit cover, not finiteness.

Geometric rate, jitter, and ultimate-bound checks

- contract_of_decrease uses h>=0, c>=0, upper>0, and V<=upper||x||^2. Its sign
  manipulation is correct: subtracting hc||x||^2 gives at least the advertised
  energy contraction. It does not by itself require V positive.
- jitter_energy DOES require P PSD because it compares the h_n-dependent factor
  to the hmin-dependent factor using V>=0. It also requires the final factor
  q>=0. Generated examples prove that numerical factor condition explicitly.
- The bound is pathwise for every sequence satisfying the interval and recurrence
  hypotheses. No independence, distribution, smoothness of the jitter, or fixed
  sampling period is assumed.
- With hmin>0, c>0, upper>0 and 0<=q=1-hmin*c/upper, q<1. Combining lower energy
  coercivity gives ||x_n||^2 <= (upper/lower) q^n ||x_0||^2. The manuscript's
  geometric conclusion is mathematically justified. With hmin=0, only the
  printed nonexpansion envelope follows; there is no positive decay rate.
- Even hmin>0 is not by itself a formal global real-time theorem in the current
  artifact. One needs sample times t_n=sum_{k<n}h_k, divergence/coverage, and
  gluing. Upper sampling bounds and sample-index decay can then give a real-time
  envelope, but that additional conclusion is not presently claimed in Lean.
- The quadratic Young inequality uses nonnegativity of Q and theta>0 correctly.
  The local induction does not assume the future invariant it is proving: the
  current-step bound is used under the inductive sublevel hypothesis.
- The affine transient formula is valid for 0<=q<1 even when the initial energy
  lies below the eventual floor. The eventual epsilon statement is correctly
  weaker than convergence to the floor, and much weaker than convergence to zero
  with persistent disturbances. No such stronger claim should be inferred.
- A nonnegative generic Q can be degenerate, including Q=0. Therefore the generic
  sublevel invariant is not automatically a bounded physical state region. The
  concrete positive PD form has separate coercivity; applications of the generic
  API need their own state-to-energy relation.
- Arbitrary disturbance sequences are allowed from one sample to the next, but
  each concrete w_n is held constant over that interval. No arbitrary measurable
  disturbance theorem or floating-point refinement is present.

Producer review

The producer accepts only rational literals, checks shape and P symmetry,
rejects nonpositive energy pivots, forms exact Bernstein coefficients, and emits
Lean reconstructions. The power-to-Bernstein formula is correct. Python assertions
are outside the proof trust boundary; removing them would not make false emitted
Lean identities pass. The generic mathematical theorem assumes certified PSD
residuals, so a producer rejection only means this sufficient certificate attempt
failed. It does not establish instability or maximality of the accepted interval.

The producer consumes the rational P/A/partition/margin fields, not every descriptive
field in the input JSON. Model prose and auxiliary metadata are not themselves
verified plant semantics. The separately proved generated-transition equalities
are therefore important. No source edits were made during this review.

Fresh skeptical review of the provisional rank-four MF-13 argument

I re-expanded the argument from its matrix definitions and did not find a sign,
factor-of-two, or outside-support coupling error. It remains a hand derivation,
not a completed Lean theorem and not a novelty claim.

Key details that survived review:
1. For S^T=S, K^T=-K, S^2=-K^2, the first two norm terms cancel, leaving
     ||L_A(S)||_F^2-||L_A(K)||_F^2
       =2 tr((AS)^2+(AK)^2).
   The sign on the K cross term is opposite because K^T=-K.
2. For K=diag(lambda_i J,0) and S=diag(lambda_i R_i,0), each diagonal block
   contributes 2 lambda_i^2[(tr(B_ii R_i))^2+(tr(B_ii J))^2]. This uses the exact
   2x2 trace/determinant identity and det(R_i)=-1, det(J)=1.
3. With two active blocks, a signed SVD of B_12 using U,V in SO(2), not arbitrary
   O(2), makes that block diagonal while preserving each canonical J block.
   Choosing both reflections X=[[0,1],[1,0]] cancels the sole cross term for ANY
   opposing B_21. Rank-one or zero B_12 is not an exception to signed SVD.
4. For A=[[B,C],[D,E]] and supported K, the outside contribution is
   2||D K||_F^2. The same-square S gives the same contribution. C/E are absent.
5. The published K7 witness's leading 6x6 minor has exact determinant
   47114175364 (independently computed with Fraction elimination). A real odd
   skew matrix has zero determinant, so this proves rank(K7)=6. Therefore the
   proposed universal rank-six same-square extension is genuinely obstructed by
   the known 7D example; it is not merely a numerical warning.

Formalization/review obligations before using rank-four exclusion in a paper:
- Orthogonal real skew canonical form with rank determining the number of active
  2x2 blocks; include rank zero and lower ambient dimensions.
- The signed 2x2 SVD existence theorem with BOTH factors in SO(2); allowing an
  orientation reversal without tracking the resulting J sign is a real pitfall.
- Exact Frobenius trace identities, block decomposition, and the same-square norm
  equality. Do not rely only on the 400 floating-point construction checks.
- Construction of the symmetric witness under the inverse orthogonal transform,
  preserving its norm and its gain comparison.
- To conclude that BOTH members of a strict 6D singular pair are invertible:
  even-rank parity for real skew matrices, existence/normalization of the top skew
  singular pair, identification of the restricted adjoint with L_(A^T), and equality
  of corresponding restricted operator norms. Rank<=5 alone is not rank<=4; parity
  is an essential missing link.
- Literature verification against the full Chen–Tian proof before any novelty
  statement. The full proof was not accessible during this review.

No MF-13 claim should be added to the controls manuscript on the strength of this
hand derivation. Keeping the mathematical exploration separate is appropriate.
