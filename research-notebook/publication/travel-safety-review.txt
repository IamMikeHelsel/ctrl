1. THE ACTUAL MAIN STATEMENT

TravelSafety.travel_limit states that, for any initial z0 with V(z0)<=12,
any hmin>0, every sampling sequence hmin<=h_n<=1/5, any held disturbance
sequence |w_n|<=1/100, and every real t>=0, the defined state satisfies
  |x(t)|<=2.
The state consists of position, velocity, and acceleration. Its P is exactly
  [[38/5,257/25,5],
   [257/25,1319/50,769/50],
   [5,769/50,278/25]].
The source defines the sample recurrence rather than assuming that an
unproved feasible recurrence happens to exist. A constant h_n=1/10 with
hmin=1/10 and w_n=0 demonstrates consistency of the timing/error assumptions.
The active initial state below demonstrates a nonzero admissible initial set.

2. EXACT MODEL, COMMAND, AND DISTURBANCE

SaturatedLag.segment is the exact exponential held solution with input
  saturate 1 (-x0-3*v0-2*a0)+w,
where both the sampled command and w are constant through the segment.
The component derivative lemmas and segment_ode check
  x'=v, v'=a, a'=-a+saturate 1 (-x0-3*v0-2*a0)+w.
They preserve the initial sample in the feedback law. There is no silent
replacement by continuously evaluated state feedback or an Euler update.
The exponential response is retained exactly and only enclosed for energy
analysis. The added disturbance is after clipping, not a disturbance applied
directly to the acceleration equation v'=a+w.

The command clamp is max(-1,min(1,u)). SaturationSector.clamp_abs_le proves
the command bound. It does not bound the total actuator drive u+w by one.
Nor does it impose a hard rate bound on a' or directly bound acceleration by
one. The normalized lag constant is one; these are not hardware units.

3. SATURATION SECTOR AND COMMON ENERGY

TravelGeometry.request_energy proves input(z)^2<=V(z)/2. On V<=12 this
gives an adequate request bound for the [1/4,1] sector. The bound is a
sufficient rational certificate, not the exact support function of the region.
region_sector chooses one alpha before quantifying over all t,w, correctly
fixing the same gain for the entire held interval. Zero requests and the
clipping boundaries are handled in exists_sector_gain.

SaturatedLag.nominal_enclosed uses one common exponential interpolation
parameter across all coordinates and one common gain interpolation parameter.
It includes the alpha*exp(-t) term through successive affine identities.
Correlation between alpha and the state, or exp(-t) and t, does not invalidate
this conservative rectangle enclosure. The four vertex equalities connect
the explicitly derived dynamics to the generated transitions. All four P
matrices coincide, and nominal_energy_decrease uses that equality explicitly.

The four generated certificates cover every 0<=t<=1/5, including zero;
nominal decrease is V(next)<=V(z)-t*||z||^2. The generated upper bound
M=451/10 yields the stated rate 10/451=2*(5/451).

4. DISTURBANCE AND THE ZERO-TIME ENDPOINT

TravelDisturbance.response_enclosed correctly represents the exact input
response using t times [t/2,0,1] and [-t/2,t,1-t]. Positive quadratic
convexity supplies a uniform response energy bound 13*t^2. The four endpoint
energies are independently consistent: 278/25, 3049/250, 278/25, 7474/625.
Homogeneity and |w|<=W give error energy <=13*t^2*W^2.

StepScaledRobustness applies Young's inequality with theta=t*eta, eta=5/451.
The zero-time case is explicit; no division by zero or minimum local elapsed
time is assumed. Its scalar invariant argument uses a nonnegative remaining
factor and the exact budget
  13*(1/5+451/5)*(1/100)^2 =1469/12500 <=60/451=eta*12.
The rational slack is 87481/5637500>0. The resulting invariant applies to
every segment offset; it is not merely a sample-endpoint theorem.

5. TRAVEL, RECURSION, AND REAL-TIME COVERAGE

TravelGeometry.position_energy uses the checked sufficient bound x^2<=V/3;
therefore V<=12 implies |x|<=2. It does not rely on floating-point inversion
of P. The independently calculated sharper support 1.95626 is not the
published theorem in this module; report the formal travel limit as two.

samples_invariant is ordinary induction on the defined exact held updates.
state chooses the unique containing half-open sample interval. The positive
minimum hmin supplies unbounded sample times and coverage through SamplingClock.
state_invariant bounds its selected offset by h_n<=1/5, then invokes the
universal segment invariant. At a sample boundary, state_at_sample agrees
with the recursively defined new sample. This resolves the previous
sample-only versus all-real-time scope gap for the defined execution.

The main claim is about this explicitly assembled execution. TravelExecution
now proves state_on_closed_segment, including the endpoint, so sample-boundary
selection agrees with the preceding completed segment. It proves continuity
on each closed interval and joins adjacent intervals at positive sample
boundaries. At time zero it proves the appropriate one-sided continuity.
state_continuousOn_nonnegative therefore covers the entire [0,infinity) state,
including acceleration. state_ode_interior uses local equality with the held
segment to prove the ODE for every coordinate on every open sampling interval.
All these statements need only positive dwell time, not the safety bounds.
No derivative at command-switching boundaries is claimed. A general uniqueness
theorem for every independently specified ODE solution is still outside this
artifact, and the manuscript explicitly says so.

6. ACTUAL CLIPPING IS NOT ASSUMED AWAY

TravelSafety.active_initial proves all of the following for z0=(0,0,1):
  V(z0)=278/25<12, requested input=-2, applied command=-1.
Thus the model actually attenuates a request by 50 percent at the initial
sample. The disturbance remains a separate post-clamp contribution.
active_initial_travel instantiates the SAME all-real-time theorem for this
state, for every admitted timing and held disturbance sequence. The witness
is not from an unrelated region or a different controller.

Do not claim all initial states clip, that clipping persists forever, or that
the region realizes alpha=1/4. Exact support arithmetic gives a maximum
request about 2.11089 and true minimum alpha about 0.473735 for V<=12.
The verified sector is deliberately broader than the particular region uses.

7. CLAIM BOUNDARIES FOR THE PAPER AND USER SUMMARY

Appropriate: a checked normalized position-travel envelope for an exact held
first-order actuator-lag model, with active command clipping, bounded held
post-clamp disturbance, and arbitrary sampling intervals in a positive range.

Do not extend it to arbitrary measurable intra-interval disturbances, direct
mechanical loads, sensor quantization, floating-point controller execution,
uncertain lag parameters, sample/compute delays, hard-stop contact, or physical
calibration. The initial condition is the full three-state ellipsoid, not
just |x0|<=2 with unrestricted velocity and acceleration. Persistent error
does not imply convergence to the origin. This result proves no globally
maximal safe region, optimal sampling bound, or novel general control theory.

No required correction was identified in the reviewed main statement or the
new TravelExecution bridge. The manuscript's travel subsection agrees with
the source: defined exact execution, continuous state, ODE only on interval
interiors, post-clamp held disturbance, full-state ellipsoid, and active
initial clipping. It does not inflate this into hardware safety or uniqueness.
Minor wording improvement only: "a fixed positive Young parameter would not
yield this uniform sublevel bound" is more precise than saying the parameter
"would not preserve the proposed sublevel"; the limitation is of that estimate.
