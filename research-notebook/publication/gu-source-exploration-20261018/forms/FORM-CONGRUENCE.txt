The displayed Hermitian family is one orbit under Spin-commuting coordinate changes
18 October 2026 — hand derivation, with an exact polynomial check; NOT yet Lean-certified.

Input: existing accepted algebra/HermitianFamily.lean proves W²=-I,
W†K=KW, K†=K, and W commutes with every represented mathlib Spin element.
H_t=K(I+tW), t real. These identities use complex matrices and real parameters.

Set S=aI+bW with a,b real. Then
  S†KS = K S² = K((a²-b²)I+2abW).
For any real t, set
  q=sqrt(1+t²), a=sqrt((q+1)/2), b=t/(2a).
Here a>0, a²-b²=1, 2ab=t, and a²+b²=q>0. Thus
  S†KS=H_t,   S^(-1)=(aI-bW)/q.
Since S is a polynomial in W, it commutes with every Spin matrix.
This establishes congruence of every displayed H_t to K, while preserving the
represented Spin action under the corresponding coordinate transformation.
It does NOT classify all invariant Hermitian forms.

Interpretation: the previous nonproportionality result is correct, but it is not
an inequivalence result. This new hand derivation resolves that specific open
coordinate-congruence question for the displayed family. There is no modulus
in this family under arbitrary invertible complex Spin-commuting changes of
basis. Such changes need not preserve fixed Clifford generator matrices.
For t nonzero, S is not K-unitary: its congruence changes K to H_t. It therefore
does not follow that S belongs to GU's fixed gauge group, or that a full action
with all other structures held fixed is unchanged.
