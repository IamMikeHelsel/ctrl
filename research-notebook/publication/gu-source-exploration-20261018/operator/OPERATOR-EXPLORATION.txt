A bounded reconstruction of the Einstein-like contraction
18 October 2026. Hand derivation plus exact executable examples; NOT Lean-certified.

PRIMARY SOURCE
Eric Weinstein, Geometric Unity, Author's Working Draft v1.0, April 1 2021:
https://geometricunity.nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/Geometric_Unity-Draft-April-1st-2021.pdf
Printed p41 (8.1): a family built from invariant Lie-algebra-valued forms,
gauge conjugation, Hodge stars, and commutator or i-anticommutator products.

displaced-torsion derivative and quadratic terms.

part of the source's construction; this experiment is not a discovery that
there are multiple choices or that the author forgot to define any action.

ADDED CONVENTIONS AND RESTRICTIONS
Work at one point, epsilon=identity, with V=R^(7,7), orthogonal generators
 e_i e_j+e_j e_i=2 s_i delta_ij, s=(+1 seven times,-1 seven times).
Use the canonical vector and bivector inclusions. Assume an algebraic
Riemann tensor R_ijkl: antisymmetric within each pair, symmetric between
pairs, and satisfying R_ijkl+R_iklj+R_iljk=0. Define
 B_ij=e_i e_j (i<j),
 rho_ij=sum_(k<l) s_k s_l R_ijkl e_k e_l,
 Ric_il=sum_j s_j R_jijl,  scal=sum_i s_i Ric_ii,
 J(x,y)=i(xy+yx).
This fixes an explicit spin-curvature normalization; another normalization
rescales all formulas. Use component contractions below rather than assuming
unverified exterior-wedge factorials or pseudo-Riemannian Hodge signs. The
source's output is degree13; ours is its proposed pointwise degree1 analogue
before any identification via Hodge star. The exact (9.3) correspondence is
not yet proved. In particular this does not cover general u(64,64)-valued
curvature or the displaced-torsion contributions in (9.4).

HAND DERIVATION
Let C_i=sum_j s_j [e_j,rho_ji]. The Clifford relation gives
 [e_j,e_k e_l]=2 s_j(delta_jk e_l-delta_jl e_k),
so
 C_i=2 sum_l s_l Ric_il e_l.                                  (A)
Let A_i=sum_j s_j (e_j rho_ji+rho_ji e_j). Repeated-index terms
cancel. The remaining distinct-index trivectors have coefficients given
by cyclic curvature sums; pair symmetry and Bianchi give A_i=0. (B)
Thus the Jordan product here gives zero on Riemann curvature, while the
commutator recovers Ricci. Bianchi, rather than mere equivariance, matters.

For the bivector contraction set
 Q=sum_(i<j) s_i s_j J(B_ij,rho_ij).
The scalar part before multiplying by i is -scal. The remaining degree4
part is the total antisymmetrization of R and vanishes by Bianchi. Hence
 Q=-i scal 1.                                                (C)
The same double contraction with a commutator is zero already by pair
symmetry: exchange the two bivector index pairs to reverse the bracket.
Finally define D_i=J(e_i,Q)=2 scal e_i. Combining these formulas,
 E_i=C_i-(1/2)D_i
    =2 sum_l s_l (Ric_il-(1/2)scal g_il)e_l.                   (D)
This is twice the ordinary Einstein contraction, in our normalization.
It uses a commutator in the Ricci term and TWO Jordan products in the
scalar composition. Substituting commutators everywhere would not yield
this scalar correction on this domain. This concrete product assignment
is a useful candidate to compare with the author's intended (9.3).

Multiplying Phi1 by the full Clifford volume omega gives omega*C_i and
omega*A_i because omega commutes with all bivectors. Thus the previous
volume-twisted candidate does not revive the Bianchi-killed Jordan term
on this curvature domain. No classification or equivalence of complete
operators follows from that statement.

WHAT THIS CHANGES
There is now a concrete constructive target beyond generic ambiguity:
formalize (A)-(D) with algebraic-curvature assumptions, then prove an exact
translation into a convention-fixed version of the source's (9.3).
This supports the source's claimed Weyl-annihilation mechanism for a
bounded candidate. It also demonstrates why importing algebraic Bianchi
automatically into the full gauge/displaced-torsion curvature would be an
extra assumption: the negative control is visible to different products.
The differential gauge Bianchi identity d_A F_A=0 is not the algebraic
cyclic identity used here, and must not be substituted for it.

FOCUSED QUESTIONS INFORMED BY THE EXPERIMENT

products at both scalar contractions, with what normalization/Hodge signs?
2. Which input sector is required to obey algebraic Riemann symmetries:
only F_B, or also any displaced-torsion combination inside (9.4)?
3. What happens to the non-Riemannian grade3/grade4 channels detected by
the negative control: retained dynamics, projected away, or constrained?
4. Which Bianchi condition selects the desired operator on the full domain,
and is there a written symbol or operator calculation we can reproduce?

REVIEW BOUNDARY
Existing accepted Lean algebra supplies the Clifford/adjoint setting and
volume properties. The curvature tensor and contractions in this note
have not yet been added to that library. This is a new local exploration
and should not be folded into the previous 48-module certification count.
