Purpose
Examine the author's available mathematics and associated computation before
contacting him. This round acquires sources, audits third-party software, and
performs bounded exact calculations. It does not extend the frozen Lean proof
bundle or establish a new physical result.

What changed
1. The thesis has a concrete computational antecedent: its 448x448 symbol matrix
and six determinant fixtures. It credits Mathematica code to Dror Bar-Natan and
algebra to Weinstein. This is not a standalone 14D GU implementation.
2. Under explicit Riemann-curvature assumptions, a commutator supplies Ricci,
and two Jordan products supply the scalar correction. This gives a bounded
Einstein-like candidate that annihilates Weyl. Exact correspondence with the
source's Hodge-star formula and its full gauge-curvature domain remains open.
3. The H_t family in our earlier paper is congruent to K under invertible complex
coordinate changes commuting with Spin. Fixed-coordinate nonproportionality
remains true. The new result removes an apparent modulus under that equivalence;
it does not prove equivalence under GU's specified gauge group.
4. Later speech distinguishes several constructions and describes a separate
unreleased complex. Transcript formulas need verification against the recording.

Best next research milestone
A. Reproduce the thesis symbol calculation from the scanned source: exact exterior
normalizations, representation projections, all six 10-coefficient fixtures,
then 448x448 determinants or exact rank/nonzero certificates. Verify the five
nonzero cases and the singular control before claiming reproduction.
B. Formalize the bounded curvature identities in Lean, and explicitly translate
their factorial/sign conventions into draft (9.3). Test the torsion-containing
curvature separately instead of assuming Riemann algebraic Bianchi.
C. Formalize H_t congruence and state precisely which transformations are allowed.
D. Use the resulting source-matched successes and discrepancies for a short note
to Weinstein. These tasks are concrete enough to do before asking him to supply
missing definitions. A source ambiguity discovered during reproduction would
itself be a useful, narrow question.

Evidence boundaries
The earlier 48-module Lean acceptance stays unchanged. This round's new
contraction and congruence results are hand derivations plus exact executable
checks. Community repository claims are statically inspected, not accepted as
proof or author endorsement. Downloads are a selected research corpus, not a
complete collection of all Weinstein writings. Raw third-party material remains
local; preservation retains our analyses, code, URLs and hashes. No contact,
public upload, or submission was made.
