Accepted scope

- Human reaching: minimum jerk is described as a task-specific predictive model.
  Optimal feedback is described as a useful principle. The robotic interpretation
  is explicitly a hypothesis, with no neural-filter or tremor-cause claim.
- Yaskawa: the buffered/native INFORM distinction, dense-point warning and
  separate incremental interface agree with the official API guide. SMOOTH's
  timing/torque and corner-rounding descriptions are supported. For maximal
  precision, the corner-rounding clause could say "the guide also warns of more
  inward corner rounding"; this is optional wording, not a blocker.
- Joins: low within-plan jerk cost does not control joins, and a snap penalty
  needs explicit treatment of a jerk jump. No unsupported higher regularity or
  proprietary industrial optimization objective is claimed.
- Energy: the work identity is correct for the stated ideal mass and ordinary
  admissible force/velocity trajectories. Rest-to-rest net work can be zero
  without smoothness. The draft correctly separates this from motor losses,
  electrical energy, squared torque and squared jerk. No measured savings claim.
- The new task contract is not presented as a proved full endpoint result.
