VERDICT
The core mathematical claim and its stated limitations match the sources.
No new theorem/model mismatch was found. Physical conversions and experiment
numbers are correct. The paper consistently makes proof-domain extension its
contribution and explicitly reports the benign direct-controller baseline.
This is a credible bounded formal-verification case-study draft, not evidence
of a novel backup principle or a superior controller. The corrections below
are needed before describing the paper and artifact as submission-ready.

SCOPE CLARIFICATION RECOMMENDED

Line 69 combines operation on S with recovery on B. This is a mathematically
valid informal consequence of two existing contracts, since B is disjoint
from S. However, the inspected Lean artifact does not define or verify a
unified mode-selection implementation that recognizes S union B. Prefer:
"Together with the existing invariant theorem, this permits a one-time choice
of direct control on S or recovery on B." Explicitly leave initial-state
measurement, admission decisions and an implemented selector outside scope.
The current prohibition on repeated switching/general runtime shielding is
correct. This is a wording clarification, not a missing proof of the stated
concrete B theorem.

MATHEMATICAL AND SEMANTIC CHECKS THAT PASSED

* Equation (1) and the error placement agree with the source. The same fixed
  rate is used in both phases. The tail command is clipped feedback plus d;
  d enters inside r, so physical actuation need not stay inside [-U,U]. This
  is accurately disclosed. The backup has exactly zero such errors.
* The box, quadratic form, 23.498378 lower bound, T=5/4, tail h<=1/5 and 1/90
  combined error budget match the concrete proofs. The terminal conclusion
  is V<=12, with no terminal-safety premise left in the concrete theorem.
* The state is continuous in every coordinate; feedback evaluates the same
  trajectory at shifted times T+t_n. Derivatives are required on open hold
  intervals only. Commands may jump at the switch/samples while state does
  not. This distinction is correctly expressed. Positive minimum dwell
  prevents Zeno accumulation. No velocity or acceleration reset is hidden.
* The held-flow formulas, independent long-backup position bounds, 32 cells
  of width 1/160 and 33 endpoint bounds agree with the code. The exponential
  bound is for the degree-nine half-argument polynomial, followed by positive
  squaring. Direct whole-box interval checks replace the exploratory corner
  reduction. The LDL identity is exact. The separate tighter diagnostic
  number is correctly excluded from the formal theorem's exported bound.
* The old nominal-decrease coefficient 3/2, sector [2/5,1], 12 matrix vertices,
  response coefficient 16 and 1/90 invariant budget agree with the frozen
  UncertainLag and UncertainTravel sources. These are model facts, not new
  evidence that the backup tolerates disturbances.
* Both generic and concrete execution existence are present, and uniqueness
  is for the same initial state, rate, durations and held error sequences.
  The execution predicate does not contain safety or equality to a selected
  curve. Thus the theorem is not vacuous.

PHYSICAL SCALE AND LINEAR-MODEL CHECK

Scaling by (U*tau0^2,U*tau0,U) and t=s/tau0 gives r=tau0/tau.
The controller remains -X/tau0^2-3V/tau0-2A, so it is not retuned to actual tau.
The physical switch is 5*tau0/4, post-handoff hold cap tau0/5, error budget U/90,
and envelope 2*U*tau0^2. Physical existence, uniqueness and actual endpoint
normalization are formalized.

For tau0=.1 s and U=2 m/s^2, position scale=.02 m, velocity scale=.2 m/s,
acceleration scale=2 m/s^2. All table entries check:
  initial X=1.98--2.02 cm; V=.098--.102 m/s; A=+/-.02 m/s^2;
  T=125 ms; envelope=+/-4 cm; later hold<=20 ms;
  path=(657/800)*.02 to (5297/3200)*.02 m
      =1.6425--3.310625 cm.
This is a dimensionally consistent assumed linear actuator model. There is
no derivation or measured error bound showing that a real robot linearizes
to this model. The manuscript correctly labels the numbers illustrative and
uncalibrated; retain that boundary in any animation or presentation.

EXPERIMENT CHECKS THAT PASSED

The table matches experiment-results.json after the stated rounding. Recounted
paired terminal-energy signs: 13 improvements, 11 degradations. Command total
variation increases in every pair. Direct feedback reaches V<=12 before T in
all tested cases; both methods show zero observed position violations. The
text reports these adverse results rather than implying the baseline is unsafe.
The .05 aligned-clock case and .1/.2 clock-phase differences are accurately
explained. Peak travel is obtained numerically from held-segment extrema;
energy is sampled. Neither supports whole-box or all-time inference. Zero
errors, three rates, corners and finite horizon are disclosed.

Calling the runs "matched pairs" means same initial state, rate, period and
horizon, not identical update clocks. The manuscript immediately gives the
clock distinction, which is sufficient. Keep the experimental baseline separate
from a formal direct-controller prefix: that prefix is not proved here.

NOVELTY AND PUBLICATION ASSESSMENT

The three related-work paragraphs make bounded claims and explicitly concede
established backup control and earlier proof pipelines. Live primary metadata
checks on 17 October 2026 confirmed the titles/authors/year for Chen et al.
(arXiv 2104.11332) and van Wijk et al. (arXiv 2409.07700v2, with the cited IEEE
DOI). The abstract descriptions support the brief related-work characterizations:

https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.07700
The earlier source review inspected the Hellwig primary paper at
https://logic.kastel.kit.edu/ap/pub/zonotope-proofs.pdf
No new systematic novelty search or competing-tool evaluation was performed.

The manuscript now has a specific demonstrable capability: a complete checked
route from a nondegenerate box outside an earlier certificate through a long
backup segment into a robust sampled invariant, for arbitrary actual continuous
ODE executions. That is stronger than a conditional composition lemma.
Nevertheless the selected box is small, the backup is exact, the baseline
already succeeds empirically, and no independent user or matched verification
comparison demonstrates broader value. Another proof assistant and theorem
counts alone are not novelty. The current case-study framing is appropriate;
strong control-research novelty or deployment readiness would be unsupported.
