THE TWO FORMAL CLAIMS
A. A fixed uncertain actuator rate, saturated sampled controller, bounded held
   errors, a full-state initial ellipsoid, and bounded positive sample durations
   imply a position envelope for all nonnegative real times. Existence and
   uniqueness are proved for the same prescribed sample and error sequences.
B. A supplied classical trajectory, continuous tracking feedback, time-varying
   bounded response/disturbance and one initial error cube imply tracking and
   terminal residual bands through the supplied horizon. No state is reset.
There is no theorem here combining B's varying response with A's sampled and
saturated controller. Neither theorem establishes PL d/e or robot safety.
