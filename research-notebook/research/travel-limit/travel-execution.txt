Execution semantics for the assembled travel-safety trajectory
=============================================================

Source: lean-controls/LeanControls/TravelExecution.lean
Namespace: LeanControls.TravelExecution

The module imports TravelSafety and uses its existing recursive samples and
real-time state definitions. No alternative execution is introduced.

state_on_segment proves, for every n and 0<=s<h(n),
  state(T_n+s)=SaturatedLag.segment(s,samples(n),w(n)).
The positive minimum dwell time gives coverage; uniqueness of the containing
half-open interval identifies the selector's index with n.

state_on_closed_segment extends the equality to s=h(n). Although the selector
uses the next interval at that endpoint, samples_succ says its initial state
is exactly the completed previous segment. state_on_interval gives the same
identity in absolute-time coordinates throughout [T_n,T_(n+1)].

segment_continuous follows coordinatewise from the exact local ODE derivative
theorems. state_continuousOn_interval transfers it to each closed interval.
At a positive sample boundary, gluing the adjacent closed intervals yields a
neighborhood of that boundary. At zero, the first interval gives right
continuity. Thus state_continuousOn_nonnegative proves continuity of the
entire state vector on [0,infinity), including all sample boundaries.

state_ode_interior proves the actual assembled trajectory satisfies
  x'=v, v'=a, a'=-a+clip[-1,1](-x_n-3v_n-2a_n)+w_n
at every real time strictly between T_n and T_(n+1). An open neighborhood
stays in that same interval; local equality and the derivative chain rule
transfer the exact held-segment ODE to the global execution.

Only hmin>0 and h(n)>=hmin are required. These are execution-semantics
results for arbitrary initial state and arbitrary held disturbance sequence.
The travel bound still requires TravelSafety's additional initial-region,
maximum timing, and disturbance magnitude hypotheses. No bound or continuity
of the disturbance sequence is assumed here. Its per-interval hold is part
of the defined model. The state is continuous even if the held input changes
discontinuously at a boundary; no derivative claim at boundaries is made.
