PROOF
ActuatorLag.exp_interpolation provides theta in [0,1] satisfying
  exp(-t)=(1-theta)(1-t)+theta(1-t+t^2).
Substitution into the exact response gives
  response(t)=(1-theta) • (t • lowerNormalized(t))
                +theta • (t • upperNormalized(t)),
  lowerNormalized(t)=[t/2,0,1],
  upperNormalized(t)=[-t/2,t,1-t].
No division by t is performed, so this exact identity works at t=0.

Both normalized vectors are affine in t. On [0,1/5], each is the convex
combination of its values at 0 and 1/5 with weight 5*t. Exact rational
endpoint energies are:
