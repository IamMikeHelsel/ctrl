ASSESSMENT

The proposed proof architecture is sound if it preserves the sampled/held
semantics below. Four vertices suffice despite the correlation between the
exponential and the saturation coefficient. A useful application result would
be a checked position envelope with positive disturbance tolerance, arbitrary
admissible sampling, and an explicit initial state that actually clips.

The control-theoretic ingredients are established. The credible prospective
contribution is their exact, replayable composition in Lean and an honest
application benchmark. It is not a new saturation theorem, a newly solved open
problem, or a hardware safety validation.

1. STATE THE ACTUAL SYSTEM BEFORE THE CERTIFICATE

State z=(x,v,a), normalized lag time constant 1, command limit U>0,
K=[1,3,2]. On sample interval k, freeze
  u_k = clip_U(-K z_k),   w_k constant,   |w_k| <= W.
The continuous dynamics on that interval are
  x'=v, v'=a, a'=-a+u_k+w_k.
This is different from continuously evaluating clip_U(-K z(t)). It is also
different from putting a load disturbance in v'=a+w. The held disturbance
enters after command clipping: U bounds u_k, not u_k+w_k or acceleration a.
First-order lag is not itself a hard actuator rate limit.

For offset s and e=exp(-s), the exact solution is
  a(s) = e a0 + (1-e)(u_k+w_k),
  v(s) = v0 + (1-e)a0 + (s-1+e)(u_k+w_k),
  x(s) = x0+s v0+(s-1+e)a0+(s^2/2-s+1-e)(u_k+w_k).
Equivalently z(s)=F(s,e,alpha)z_k+g(s,e)w_k, where
  F0 = [[1,s,s-1+e],[0,1,1-e],[0,0,e]],
  g  = [s^2/2-s+1-e, s-1+e, 1-e]^T,
  F  = F0-alpha*g*K.
Check initial values and all three component derivatives. The explicit
solution establishes a model realization; any claim about every solution of
an independently defined ODE additionally needs the appropriate uniqueness
bridge or must quantify over these explicit held executions.

Arbitrarily varying measurable w(t) cannot be replaced by a single held scalar
w_k in this formula. It needs a convolution/reachable-set bound and separate
regularity semantics. Keep the initial result explicitly piecewise constant.

2. SECTOR AND FOUR-VERTEX ENCLOSURE

For 0<kappa<=1, if |K z_k|<=U/kappa, there is a fixed alpha in [kappa,1]
with clip_U(-K z_k)=-alpha K z_k. Use alpha=1 when K z_k=0; otherwise
alpha=min(1,U/|K z_k|). This alpha stays fixed throughout the segment.

For every offset 0<=s<=H<=1,
  1-s <= exp(-s) <= 1-s+s^2.
F is affine in e holding alpha fixed and affine in alpha holding e fixed;
the alpha*e term is allowed. Interpolating the two e endpoints and the two
alpha endpoints yields four nonnegative product weights summing to one.
Use the same e in every matrix entry. The true e and alpha need not vary
independently: proving all points of the enclosing rectangle is conservative
and sound. It does not assert all four corners are physically realizable.

At s=0 the e interval collapses. Handle this case directly, without dividing
by s^2. At s>0, theta=(exp(-s)-(1-s))/s^2 is in [0,1]. For intermediate
offsets use exp(-s) and vertices evaluated at s, not exp(-h_k) or vertices at
the eventual sample duration. Common P across all four corners is essential
for the proposed convex-energy step. Four different Lyapunov matrices cannot
be substituted into the same argument.

3. ROBUST INVARIANCE MUST WORK AS s TENDS TO ZERO

Let E(z)=z^T P z, P positive definite, E(z)<=M||z||^2, and suppose the
four checked vertices imply the nominal bound
  E(Fz) <= E(z)-s*c*||z||^2 <= (1-lambda*s)E(z), lambda=c/M.
Assume E(gw)<=B*s^2*W^2. A fixed positive Young parameter creates an
energy multiplier greater than one near s=0 and does not prove the desired
whole-segment invariant set. Choose theta=eta*s, with 0<eta<lambda.
For s>0, quadratic Young gives
  E(Fz+gw)
    <= (1+eta*s)(1-lambda*s)E(z)+B*(s^2+s/eta)*W^2
    <= [1-(lambda-eta)*s]E(z)+B*s*(H+1/eta)*W^2.
Require lambda*H<=1 to keep the substituted nominal factor nonnegative.
Then E(z)<=R implies E(z(s))<=R whenever
  B*(H+1/eta)*W^2 <= (lambda-eta)*R.
At s=0 the result is the exact initial identity. A sharper calculation can
retain the negative eta*lambda*s^2 term; the displayed sufficient bound is
easy to inspect and avoids claiming an optimal disturbance margin.

A convenient coarse bound follows from the same exponential enclosure:
  |g_x|<=s^2/2, |g_v|<=s^2, |g_a|<=s, for 0<=s<=H<=1.
Thus B=M*(1+5*H^2/4) works. This deliberately does not identify the
polynomial endpoint responses with the actual positive exponential response.
Sharper integral bounds on g may improve the disturbance budget later.

The selected application uses M=451/10, c=1, H=1/5, R=12, W=1/100,
eta=5/451. The coarse B=9471/200 above does NOT prove this disturbance
budget. The sharper B=13 used by the proposed calculation is independently
supported by exact arithmetic. At the exponential endpoints,
  g/s = r0=(s/2,0,1), or r1=(-s/2,s,1-s),
  E(r0)=19*s^2/10+5*s+278/25 <=3049/250=12.196,
  E(r1)=84*s^2/25+88*s/25+278/25 <=7474/625=11.9584.
Each polynomial has positive coefficients and is increasing on [0,1/5].
Convexity of E handles the actual exponential interpolation. This yields
E(gw)<=13*s^2*w^2 after the corresponding equalities are checked in Lean.
Then the selected positive-disturbance budget is
  D=13*(H+1/eta)*W^2=1469/12500 <60/451=(lambda-eta)*R,
with exact slack 87481/5637500. These expressions were independently
checked with exact rational arithmetic; formal response and composition
proofs were still being constructed when this review was written.

4. NONVACUITY, CLIPPING DEPTH, AND TRAVEL GEOMETRY

For rho_K=K P^-1 K^T and rho_x=(P^-1)00, support of the ellipsoid gives
  |Kz|^2<=rho_K E(z),  |x|^2<=rho_x E(z).
All required budgets must hold together:
  U^2/rho_K < R <= U^2/(kappa^2*rho_K),
  rho_x*R <= L^2,
  the positive-disturbance invariance budget in section 3.
The strict lower bound is what distinguishes active clipping from a theorem
whose entire invariant set has clipping inactive. It guarantees that some
admitted states clip, not that every trajectory clips or continues clipping.
If tight support constants are used, L^2>U^2*rho_x/rho_K is necessary for
this particular ellipsoid family to include an actively clipped state.

For Lean, exact PSD certificates for rho_K*P-K^T*K and
rho_x*P-e1*e1^T may be simpler than a general inverse-matrix theorem.
An explicit rational witness should accompany the existential argument.
Rescaling P and R together changes notation, not the physical safe set.

  kappa=1/4, H=1/5, c=1,

  rho_K*12=12400425/2782958=4.455843... <16=U^2/kappa^2,

  z=(0,0,1), E(z)=278/25=11.12<12, Kz=2>1,

The largest request over this ellipsoid is
  sqrt(12400425/2782958)=2.1108868720...,

For comparison, the earlier travel-one R=3 candidate admitted only mild

initial state or every interval clips.

The old jerk/lag P is not a useful route to strong attenuation at c=1:
its continuous-limit residual Q_alpha-I has determinant
  -3*(227*alpha^2-1918*alpha+1547)/16,
with smaller zero approximately 0.9030953856. Shrinking H cannot repair a
negative determinant at s=0. This symbolic diagnostic motivated changing P;
it does not establish instability of the actual saturated system.

5. EXECUTION AND CLAIM QUANTIFIERS

Recommended theorem shape: for every initial state E(z0)<=R, every sequence
h_k in [hmin,H] with hmin>0, and every held disturbance sequence |w_k|<=W,
define the clipped held executions explicitly; then for all k and all
0<=s<=h_k, E(z_k(s))<=R and |x_k(s)|<=L, with |u_k|<=U.
Induction uses the endpoint as the next initial state. Common P and the sector
condition are re-established at each sample by invariance, rather than being
assumed for the entire future without proof.

For a claim quantified over every real time t>=0, connect these intervals
to a sampling clock and prove coverage. An upper bound on h_k alone allows
Zeno behavior. Local certificates can allow s=0 even though the global clock
requires positive progress. At sample boundaries define the input convention;
state continuity is enough, and differentiability of acceleration need not
hold across a command jump.

The initial ellipsoid constrains position, velocity, and acceleration jointly.
This is not safety for every state satisfying merely |x|<=L. The travel limit
is symmetric around the regulation origin; a physical track needs a coordinate
shift and dimensions. Hard-stop contact, friction, sensor errors, unmodeled
delays, machine arithmetic, and parameter uncertainty are outside this model.
With persistent nonzero disturbances, invariant travel is a reasonable claim;
convergence to the origin is not established by this argument.

7. CONCRETE PUBLICATION TARGET AND REJECTION CRITERIA

Defensible prospective result: a fully replayable Lean application certificate
for position travel of a sampled, genuinely clipping, first-order actuator-lag
system under specified held disturbances and sampling bounds. Numerical design
remains untrusted; exact rational certificates, exponential enclosure, saturation
semantics, clock coverage, and the final quantified travel statement are checked.

Strengthen the artifact with: an assumption-to-theorem map; positive rational W;
the active witness above; one same-object numerical comparator with identical
P, K, H, R, disturbance channel and initial set; replay time/certificate size;
and a conservatism report. Physical calibration is a later application step.
A simulated trajectory can illustrate the witness but cannot prove the bound.

Reject or narrow the proposed claim if any of these happens:
  * The final admissible set forces |Kz|<=U, or the clipping witness lies outside it.
  * Only sample endpoints are safe; no universal offset or clock bridge exists.
  * Polynomial vertices silently replace the true exponential plant.
  * The theorem assumes arbitrary varying disturbances but proves only held ones.
  * The invariant set or disturbance budget is empty/zero despite broader prose.
  * Different P matrices are mixed without a valid multiple-Lyapunov argument.
  * A failed common-P/Bernstein search is presented as physical infeasibility.
  * Routine control ingredients are presented as new theory or first formal safety.

Readiness: the selected design is coherent and merits implementation. Its exact
initial witness exercises 50 percent command attenuation, with positive held
disturbance tolerance and a strict position margin. No comparative or physical
advantage has been established. Publication potential rests on a clean formal
integration and measured evidence, not on this controller's mathematical novelty.
