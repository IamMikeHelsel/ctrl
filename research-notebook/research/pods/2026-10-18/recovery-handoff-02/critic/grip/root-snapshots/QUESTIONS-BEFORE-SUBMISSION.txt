DECISIONS BEFORE A COMPLETE APPLICATION OR PAPER SUBMISSION
18 October 2026. No answer is needed to preserve this bounded proof milestone.

1. What physical operation should the full theorem cover?
   Specify a complete initial state set and the actual input bounds during
   braking loss, recovery and handoff. The existing first-stop theorem gives
   an upper position limit. It has no lower initial-position or acceleration
   bound and cannot by itself support a two-sided travel claim. A new admission
   theorem must cover a declared operating set, not silently strengthen that
   older theorem's assumptions.

2. What does successful holding mean for that operation?
   The present result permits an 111.05 mm position interval, speed up to
   0.4442 m/s and acceleration up to 3.5536 m/s². Choose useful position,
   speed, acceleration and settling-time limits. Decide whether the final
   target stays fixed or can depend on the observed stop. The timing contract
   must include sensor age, computation, transport and time held by the drive.

3. What evidence would establish value beyond this checked case study?
   Use the same plant, initial set, errors, history, timing and authority in an
   independent reachable-set comparison, or have an independent consumer use
   the proof interface on a different plant. Record the certificate's tightness,
   manual work and trust boundary. Existing theory already covers delay,
   pending input history and formal intersample safety. A new proof count is
   not evidence of novelty or practical benefit.
