Accepted scope
A conditional scalar theorem proves a first zero of forward velocity and an
upper position limit for restored effective braking with lag. It derives the
loss-phase acceleration envelope from the physical ODE, retains a symbolic
exponential upper flow, and then derives a simple conservative budget. One
continuous physical state spans the two phases. The result requires sustained
negative effective target and a positive response-rate floor after recovery.

The exact assumptions, conclusion and limitations are in SEMANTIC-REVIEW.txt.
For V=v0+A*T+(A+b)/k, the horizon is T+V/b and the position limit is
X+v0*T+A*T^2/2+V^2/(2*b). Parameters satisfy v0,A,T>=0 and b,k>0. Initial
bounds are x<=X, 0<=v<=v0 and a<=A. Before T, r>=0 and q<=A. After T,
r>=k and q<=-b must persist through the full computed horizon. Rates are
continuous within each phase. Each phase has a classical ODE on its open
interval. Position, velocity and acceleration remain continuous across T.
The theorem assumes a supplied execution; it does not prove general existence.

The exact witness really coasts for one second with q=0, then has q=-1 and
r=1 from the same state [1,1,0]. The no-recovery example satisfies the actual
ODE while moving forward forever with q=0 and r=1. These establish nonvacuity
and reject response-rate-only recovery. They are not hardware simulations.

Limits that matter
The guarantee ends at first zero. It supplies no permanent rest, lower travel
limit, detector, hold composition or full operating region. Continued negative
target can reverse the witness: v(3)=-exp(-2)<0. The model excludes instantaneous
acceleration jumps and has no contact/friction mechanics. Motor effort alone
does not establish the effective target assumption. General existence and
physical application need separate evidence.

The simple budget is not a tighter replacement for the old certificate. Its
old-contract reference-position bound is exactly 0.1605859375 m > 0.15 m.
At v0=.01,A=T=0,k=8, changing b from 1 to 2 raises this simple budget from
0.0091125 m to 0.0169 m. That is conservatism from a dropped exponential term,
not worse physical braking. The symbolic exponential envelope is proved
separately and does not rely on the usefulness of the relaxed formula.

This is an extension and reuse of the local ActualStop/EventStop work. The
new delta derives the loss acceleration bound, generalizes constants and drops
the recovered rate ceiling. Novelty, practical advantage, implementation and
publication readiness remain unestablished. The next full-operation result
needs declared lower state/input bounds and an admission/detection contract.
