Statement and hypothesis review
Each delayed_sampled_hold requires 0<=D<=.0005 s, D<=H, one supplied Execution,
an IsSchedule, and first measured state exactly (.14,-.01,-1.17) in SI units.
It does not take an endpoint cube or aggregate mismatch premise. Sensor errors
from the physical state at the recorded timestamp are (.001,.01,.05).
The fixed center is .088125 m; cube radius is .055525 m; gains are unchanged.
Rates are in [8,12]/s and target error has magnitude at most .01 m/s^2.

The primary case fixes the pending command at -1 m/s^2. The separate braking
case fixes it at -1.21 m/s^2. These are two proved cases, not an interval of
permitted pending commands. The latter gives effective target in [-1.22,-1.20]
under the stated error, which matches one inequality of the old braking model.
This scalar compatibility does not prove reachability of the admission box.

Startup and history are explicit
One global clock starts at the initial reading. One continuous physical state
spans the pending interval and every later update. The pending command acts
before D and on its left side; clipped measured feedback acts on the right at D.
Physical target error is added after command clipping. The ODE has separate
right and left derivatives, so a command change need not have a classical
acceleration derivative at the switching time.

observation_admission derives the initial true-state box from the error and
schedule premises. startup_acceleration derives |a|<=1.22 from the pending ODE.
The velocity and measurement envelopes then give startup cube membership.
There is no assumed safe endpoint or supplied startup trajectory enclosure.

On a bounded prefix, the age estimate integrates the ACTUAL mixed-mode feedback
slope. It includes the pending interval when a read timestamp precedes D.
Startup slope is bounded separately by 7 or 8. The coarse digital refinement
then bounds the slope across the whole prefix by the same constant. The final
digital mismatch bound is .110 in the -1 case and .111 in the -1.21 case.
Their exact margins below 2R=.11105 are .00105 and .00005 m/s^2 respectively.
First contact at or before D is excluded by the derived startup cube. After D,
the barrier uses the derived mismatch bound. The prefix assumption is discharged.
No continuity of mismatch or future state lookup is used.

Clock review
Both channels require 0<=stamp<=t and total age t-stamp<=.001 s. Dispatches are
strictly ordered and locally finite; samples precede dispatches; cell indices
select held readings. The initial stamp remains 0 at dispatch D.
first_cell_age proves that this read can be held only while global t<=.001.
no_first_hold_after_full_age rejects a positive first cell when D>=.001.
A delay of .0005 therefore leaves at most another .0005 s for the first hold.
The old 5 ms capture allowance is not imported into this contract.
At the final horizon the right channel is algebraic endpoint data, with no
right ODE. Its next dispatch is not evidence of an execution beyond that horizon.

Nonvacuity and conclusions
Both exact witnesses use D=.0005 s, H=.001 s, r=8, d=0, and the nominal initial
state. Their exponential pending and holding phases join at the same full state.
Acceleration at dispatch differs from its initially read value. The command
jumps to .23 m/s^2 while the reading remains stamped 0. The second scheduled
dispatch is at H. The witnesses construct no evolution after H.
These are nonzero-delay, nonconstant physical executions, not zero-delay or
reset-state demonstrations. They do not establish general trajectory existence.

The theorem covers the whole interval [0,H], including startup. It gives
x in [.0326,.14365] m, |v|<=.4442 m/s, |a|<=3.5536 m/s^2, and effective target
magnitude<=1.4356 m/s^2. Clipping inactivity follows in the proof. The wide
residual bands establish containment, not precision standstill or convergence.

Remaining decision
Accept this delay/history bridge at the fixed admission. To claim a full stop
and hold, derive a guard that covers the stopping outcomes and prove that a
sampled detector reaches it under the same timing and uncertainty assumptions.
Retain the existing distinction between first velocity zero, admission, delayed
handoff and bounded holding. No publication novelty or measured benefit is
accepted by this proof review.
