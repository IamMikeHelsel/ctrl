INTERNAL OVERLAP ADDENDUM
18 October 2026. Statement inspection only; no old source was rerun or changed.

ActualStop.delay_coordinate_upper already derives delay travel and velocity
bounds from a supplied acceleration bound. delayed_actual_first_stop already
proves first zero velocity and forward travel safety after a bounded delay,
with persistent effective target <=-1.2, rate in[8,12], and fixed initial bounds.
It assumes acceleration<=.1 throughout the delay. Its concrete delay bound is
.05 s, total stop deadline .55 s, and forward position limit .15 m.

EventStop.delayed_event_first_stop already weakens the post-delay physical
requirements to the part of the path before the first zero event. It retains
the supplied delay acceleration envelope and fixed constants. Its before-stop
guard is not an eventual digital detector or a permanent-rest guarantee.

The new loss/recovery route must expose its actual delta. Candidate useful
extensions are an acceleration envelope derived from pre-recovery ODE dynamics,
symbolic rather than fixed parameters, one-sided recovery transitions, and
explicit impossibility examples. Merely renaming the previous delay as grip
loss is not a new physical model or control theorem. Neither old nor new scalar
lag equations alone establish mechanical contact or friction behavior.

Inspect the new theorem's assumptions against these old statements. Report
reuse and generalization separately from the new bound. Root retains the
usefulness/novelty decision. This critic will not convert a successful Lean
replay into a claim of new control theory.
