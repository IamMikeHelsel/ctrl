C1. Conditional delayed holding, two fixed pending commands.
LeanControls.DelayedHold.delayed_sampled_hold uses pending q=-1.
LeanControls.BrakingDelayedHold.delayed_sampled_hold uses pending q=-121/100.
Each theorem assumes a supplied Execution and IsSchedule on [0,H], with
0<=D<=1/2000 seconds and D<=H. The first measured state is exactly
(7/50,-1/100,-117/100), stamped at 0. Its true-state errors are at most
(1/1000,1/100,1/20). Rates satisfy 8<=r<=12; physical target error is at most
1/100. No endpoint cube, aggregate mismatch, or ongoing invariant is assumed.

The physical state is continuous. The physical equations are x'=v, v'=a,
a'=r*(u+d-a). Right and left derivatives are required separately. Pending q
acts before D and on the left at D. The clipped measured-feedback command
acts on the right at D and thereafter. Physical error d is added AFTER clip.
Feedback is -8*(x-c)-6*v-a/2; c=141/1600, clip range [-2,2].

The conclusion covers every real t in [0,H], including startup and dispatch:
|x-c|, |x-c+v/4|, |x-c+v/2+a/16| <= R=2221/40000.
Thus .0326<=x<=.14365, |v|<=8R, |a|<=64R, and |u+d|<=1.4356.
The first digital command uses the ORIGINAL reading at stamp 0.

C3. The clock contract preserves total information age.
Execution requires 0<=stamp<=t and t-stamp<=.001 seconds on the same global
clock, on both derivative channels. Samples precede their dispatches.
Dispatch times strictly increase and are locally finite. Each command uses

proves that D>=.001 leaves no positive-duration first cell before the next

C4. There are exact nonconstant delayed execution witnesses for both cases.
Each Witness.positive_delay_witness constructs the physical solution with

The pending phase and following phase are exact exponential ODE solutions,

has dispatch 0 at .0005 and dispatch 1 at .001. The witness proves safety

C5. Limited connection to the older braking condition.

Neither case proves that the old stopping family reaches this fixed reading

C6. Explicit rejected cases.
The earlier 5 ms capture allowance is not the 1 ms information-age contract.
The new startup cap is 0.5 ms. Relaxing either age or startup cap to 5 ms
breaks the submitted certificate; the failed Lean mutants are preserved.
This is a certificate rejection, not a claim that every such motion is unsafe.
An exact q=-2 example has |feedback slope|=12.08>8 and >7.
An admitted sensor-box endpoint with q=-1.21 has slope 7.48>7, so the original
slope-7 budget cannot simply be copied to the braking-compatible case.
The assertion that an original stamp-0 reading has age<=1 ms at t=1.5 ms is
also rejected. No quantitative maximum safe startup delay is claimed.
