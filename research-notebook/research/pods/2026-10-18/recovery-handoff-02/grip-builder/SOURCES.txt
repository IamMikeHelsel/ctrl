TARGETED PRIMARY-SOURCE OVERLAP CHECK
18 October 2026. This is a narrow scope check, not a novelty search or a
reproduced comparison. No paper artifact was executed.

1. Shalev-Shwartz, Shammah, Shashua, On a Formal Model of Safe and Scalable
Self-driving Cars, arXiv:1708.06374v6, Section 3.1, Definition 1 and Lemma 2.
https://arxiv.org/html/1708.06374v6
Inspected the response-time assumption and longitudinal distance formula.
It combines bounded acceleration during response with a positive minimum
braking rate afterward. The rear-vehicle distance includes v*T+A*T^2/2 and
(v+A*T)^2/(2b). Thus the delay-then-brake structure is established prior work.
Boundary: the inspected formula bounds actual braking acceleration after
response; it does not provide this acceleration-lag transient or a grip-recovery
theorem. Our C=(A+b)/lambda term is derived from the stated ODE, not attributed
to RSS. No legal or implementation claim is taken from this source.

2. Gacsi, Kiss, Molnar, Braking within Barriers: Constructive Safety-Critical
Control for Input-Constrained Vehicles via the Backup Set Method,
arXiv:2510.15797v1, Sections I and IV-B, equations 55-56.
https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15797v1
Inspected the split-friction motivation and force constraints. The paper
models braking force as limited by available friction and uses backup sets
to constrain lateral motion during braking. It is direct precedent for
separating physical braking capacity from a desired control input.
Boundary: its inspected four-wheel problem is not the present scalar recovery
deadline/lag contract. No claim of identical theorem assumptions or direct
substitution is made. No numerical or proof-effort comparison was run.

3. Friction-Aware Force-Realizable Safety Control for Four-Wheel Independently
Steered and Driven Mobile Robots, International Journal of Robust and
Nonlinear Control, DOI 10.1002/rnc.70772, Sections 5.1 and 6, equations 44-49
and Remark 7. Publisher full text inspected.
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/rnc.70772
The inspected section derives a conservative acceleration reserve from a
friction-limited force set and requires a positive certification floor for
closing obstacles. It uses a speed-squared/braking-reserve safety margin.
Below the floor, it withholds that collision-avoidance guarantee rather than
clipping the denominator to an invented positive value. This is relevant
overlap for broad friction-aware recoverability or safe-distance claims.
Boundary: this is a different robot, force model and sampled guarantee. The
inspection does not establish a fixed loss-duration theorem with our lag ODE.
No full proof or artifact audit was performed.

Local overlap
variable-response/ComparisonEnvelope.lean already proves scalar variable-rate
comparison and physical x/v/a envelopes. FirstStop.lean gives earliest-zero
existence from a proved endpoint sign. ActualStop/EventStop already compose a
bounded delay with braking, but their delay acceleration bound is assumed.
The proposed narrow delta derives that bound from a loss-phase ODE and adds
an explicit parameterized recovery-deadline/authority contract. Formal
composition and clear limits can be useful; these sources establish no
publication novelty or advantage over existing stopping methods.
