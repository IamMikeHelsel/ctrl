DRAFT — delayed handoff and recovery review
This is a working root outline. Acceptance is pending independent review.

Question answered by the handoff route
If a reading is already old when its command reaches the drive, can we keep
the axis inside the stated holding region without pretending time restarted?
The candidate theorem includes the pending command, the true state at dispatch,
and both sides of the command change. No endpoint safe set is assumed.

Fixed admission, in SI units
The first reading is (x,v,a)=(.14,-.01,-1.17). Its true-state errors are at most
(.001,.01,.05). The holding target is c=.088125 m. The admitted transformed
cube has radius .055525 m. The first dispatch delay is at most .5 ms. Every
reading retains its original timestamp, and its total age must stay<=1 ms.
Thus a reading sampled at0 and dispatched at.5 ms has at most.5 ms left for
its first hold. A later dispatch does not restore its age allowance.

Two pending-command cases are built separately
The first uses -1 m/s². The stronger case uses -1.21 m/s². Physical target
error is added after the command and has magnitude at most.01 m/s².
The stronger case therefore keeps the effective pending target<=-1.2 m/s²,
which matches one condition in the old braking proof. It uses more of the
proof error budget. It does not show that braking reaches the admission box.

Why this still is not a completed stop
The accepted old stop family admits response rates8..12/s. A numerical screen
of three constant-rate members puts their peak positions at140.711,132.525,
and126.761 mm. The new fixed admission requires139..141 mm. Two members never
reach it. This is a numerical screen of an integration gap, not a formal
impossibility theorem or a claim that the motions are unsafe.

Even after handoff, the fixed invariant allows speed up to444.2 mm/s and
acceleration up to3.5536 m/s². It guarantees containment, not a quiet arm or
precision standstill. The team must define the useful stop and hold limits.

Recovery route: pending
The candidate uses a known deadline for restoration of effective braking,
a positive braking floor, and a positive response-rate floor. It models an
acceleration target and its lag. Mechanical contact, tire slip and sensor
estimation of available grip are not supplied by that equation.
The simple symbolic bound fails our existing150 mm clearance case and can
be very loose near rest. Retain the sharper old certificate where it applies.

Next decisions already identified
1. Use an admission guard that covers the actual stopping outcomes, then prove
   a detector reaches and accepts that guard with real sensor and clock limits.
2. Define acceptable position, speed, acceleration and settling time before
   claiming an application or deciding whether a wider safe set is useful.
3. Compare the same complete contract against an independent reachability
   method. Separate theoretical novelty, formalization value and hardware use.
