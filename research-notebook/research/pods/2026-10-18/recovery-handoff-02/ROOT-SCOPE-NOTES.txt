ROOT SCOPE AND USEFULNESS NOTES — provisional
18 October 2026. Root analysis; no new result is accepted by this note.

A complete operation still needs three distinct steps.
1. Reach a measured state that the handoff guard accepts.
2. Keep the same physical state and original reading time through dispatch.
3. Remain inside the holding set afterward.
The delayed-hold construction addresses steps2 and3 for a fixed admission box.
Even if the pending target meets the earlier braking inequality, that alone
cannot show step1 or connect the full stopping operation. The admitted velocity
box is [-.02,0]m/s, so it already reaches or passes first zero velocity.
An eventual detector and the relation to the stop trajectory remain open.

The restored-braking model uses effective acceleration target q. A command
sent to a motor is insufficient evidence for that physical target. Contact,
slip, force capacity and their measurement need a separate model or validated
bounds. A deadline T, authority b and rate lambda are supplied conditions.
We have not inferred them from a robot, shoe or tire.

The simple candidate formula is
  U=v0+A*T; Z=U+A/lambda;
  L=v0*T+A*T^2/2 + (Z+b/lambda)^2/(2*b).
Its relaxed braking part equals Z^2/(2*b)+Z/lambda+b/(2*lambda^2).
It therefore grows with b when b>lambda*U+A. This is a loss from dropping a
negative exponential term, not evidence that stronger braking harms safety.
Do not optimize a physical brake using this artifact of the bound. One may
apply the contract with a weaker certified b, but proving or implementing that
optimization is outside this milestone. The near-rest and old-contract failures
must appear in the final review packet, not only in an appendix.

Publication decision
Proof acceptance, engineering value and novelty need separate verdicts.
A useful next full comparison must share the physical plant, disturbances,
sensor errors, total clock age, initial set, and command authority. Compare
certified clearance and timing and review effort. A method tested under easier
assumptions is not an equal comparison. No new theory or hardware benefit is
established by these notes.

Nearest sampled-delay and formal-envelope overlap checked by root
Singletary, Chen and Ames, Control Barrier Functions for Sampled-Data Systems
with Input Delays, arXiv:2005.06418v1. Section IV assumes delay equal to an
integer number of sample periods and a safe zero-input initialization. It
carries an input-history buffer into the predicted dispatch state. That is
prior work on command history and initialization. Our fixed pending-command
proof derives its startup safety box, but this inspection does not establish
a novel delay method or an equal-assumption implementation comparison.
https://arxiv.org/html/2005.06418v1

Hellwig et al., From Zonotopes to Proof Certificates, arXiv:2509.20301v1,
Definition 2 and Section 4. It separates next-step invariance, safety between
steps and admissible commands, then connects numerical enclosures to deductive
checks. These obligations and the broad certificate-to-safety narrative are
prior work. Our possible value still requires a useful implementation contract,
measured reuse or a matched comparison. No artifact from either paper was run.
