ROOT PROGRESS AND VALUE GRADE
18 October 2026. Grade of this bounded milestone, not publication odds.
FINAL: both bounded proof milestones accepted after independent review.

Proof scope — useful, bounded progress
The handoff closes a real premise gap in the earlier sampled hold: a command
can arrive after its reading was taken. The proof carries one physical state
and the old timestamp through that interval. It derives startup containment
and feedback mismatch. The independent critic accepted both fixed cases.
The narrow .00005 m/s² margin in the braking-compatible case is explicit.

The recovery construction derives the loss-phase acceleration bound and gives
an exponential upper flow with only a recovered rate floor. The closed-form
consequence has symbolic parameters, a genuine coast-then-brake execution and
an exact no-recovery counterexample. Root inspected the actual statements and
the frozen evidence. Independent review accepted this conditional scope with
no builder repair. Its 87 accepted checks exclude one preserved critic-only
tactic failure, which was repaired and rerun separately.

Engineering benefit — partial
These results specify assumptions that a caller must meet. The age contract
includes sensing, transport and holding; a nominal 1 ms sampling rate does not
by itself meet a 1 ms age budget. The recovery model distinguishes physical
braking authority from actuator response speed. That distinction is useful
when requirements or model assumptions are reviewed.

The complete operation remains unproved. The old stopping theorem lacks the
lower state and input bounds needed for a two-sided handoff contract. Its
numerical examples also do not all enter the present fixed admission box.
The holding theorem permits wide speed and acceleration ranges. It is not a
precision-rest result. Neither proof is a demonstrated shoe, tire, robot-arm or safety-rated
machine result. Contact-force jumps are outside the recovery ODE.

Comparison — no demonstrated advantage
The simple recovery formula fails the old 150 mm limit: it returns
160.5859375 mm. It can become looser near rest or with stronger braking.
Keep the exact exponential helper and the sharper old certificate. The
relaxed formula is a sufficient screen, not an optimizer for brake design.
No external implementation was tested under a matched full contract.

Novelty and submission readiness — not established
The source check finds overlap in delayed braking, input history and formal
intersample safety. Formal verification of this particular interface is not
by itself a new control result. The current packet is suitable for internal
technical review. It does not yet answer Mike's concern about practical value.

Next decision
Choose a full operating set and a useful terminal requirement. Then test one
complete chain: braking loss, recovery, detector admission, delayed dispatch
and holding. The detector must cover the stopping outcomes under the same
uncertainty and time assumptions. A fixed target and a target selected from
the observed stop are different tasks; declare which one is wanted.
The recovery theorem currently keeps braking conditions through a full computed
horizon. A controller that switches at first zero needs an event-based version
or a proved compatible continuation before it can use the holding theorem.

After that interface exists, compare certified clearance, terminal accuracy,
timing allowance and proof effort against an independent reachable-set method
under the same assumptions. A failed comparison is an outcome to preserve.
More isolated scalar bounds are not the next useful milestone.
