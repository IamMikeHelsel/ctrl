E9: ACTUAL FINITE-GRAPH FRACTIONAL HALF

All coefficients, profile values and costs below are rational. Let V be a
nonempty finite type and G a triangle-free simple graph. Let C be the existing
OrientedBCLCut G object. Its small side X has size at most the complement Y.
The structure also carries e(X)+e(Y)<=(2/47)n^2. The new proof does not use
that numerical budget, a maximum-cut premise, or a global edge-density bound.

Set n=|V|, k=|Y|, y=k/n, j=e(Y)/n^2, m=2j/y and b=y-1/2. Require y>1/2.
Let R,L be rational with R>=0. Define
  K = 4R+1,
  C0 = j-4R(y-m)^2-m^2,
  L0 = 8R(y-m)-2m,
  A(L) = C0+(L-L0)^2/(4K),
  H(L) = ((j-R)/b-L)^2/4+L*m.
If A(L)<=0 and H(L)<=0, then there is an actual function z:V->Q such that
  0<=z_v<=1 for every v,
  sum_v z_v=n/2,
  edgeCost G z / n^2 <= R.
edgeCost counts each unordered edge once. This is exactly the existing
predicate HasNormalizedFractionalHalfBoundQ G R. The Lean endpoint is
Erdos128.FiniteProfileBridge.half_from_squared_certificates.

The only new arithmetic premises are R>=0, y>1/2 and the two square tests.
No feasible profile, graph moment identity or objective bound is a premise.
The theorem applies to odd orders as a fractional-half statement. It has no
integer-half or floor-half conclusion. It does not assert existence of C.

Derived normalization
The graph on Y is the actual induced graph, with uniform probability weights
1/k. Its edge mass is mu=j/y^2. Its local target mass is alpha=n/(2k)=1/(2y),
which is strictly between zero and one. With ambient side degree defined as
the induced neighbor count divided by n, its local weighted degree is d/y.
The local target cost is T=R/y^2 and the local affine slope is L/y.

The exact universal identities are
  ambient_phi_low(d)-L(d-m)
    =A(L)-K*(d-m-(L0-L)/(2K))^2,
  ambient_phi_high(d)-L(d-m)
    =H(L)-(d-((j-R)/b-L)/2)^2,
where ambient_phi_low(d)=j-4R(y-d)^2-d^2 and
ambient_phi_high(d)=d(j-R)/b-d^2. Since K>0, both square tests give global
pointwise majorants. Thus they cover the restricted E8 degree domains too.

At a local degree t, the ambient degree is y*t. Each ambient difference
equals y^2 times its E8 normalized difference. The proof derives this by
exact rational identities, including slope L/y. The threshold scales as
y*(1-alpha)=y-1/2, with y positive. No branch direction is reversed.

E8 then supplies a unit profile pi on Y, with local mean alpha and local
objective at most R/y^2. Extend pi by zero outside Y. The reused attributed
helpers derive ambient mass k*alpha=n/2 and normalized edge cost
y^2*objective. The new wrapper proves that this cost is at most R.

The balanced case y=1/2 is excluded: alpha=1 and b=0. Lean's total division
does not supply a valid positive-denominator instance there. The balanced
endpoint stays separate. No .0251 cover, BCL all-order argument, blowup,
rounding or novelty conclusion is part of this theorem.
