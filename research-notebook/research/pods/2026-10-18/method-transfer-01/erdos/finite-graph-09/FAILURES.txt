E9 PRESERVED FAILURES

Every development invocation records its source bytes, compiler log and exit
status in an attempt directory. development-attempts.json indexes them.
These attempts are not the final fresh-source evidence.

CutHelpers: the first extracted build failed at the induced edge cardinality
identity. Printed terms looked identical, but their synthesized finite-type
instances differed under Lean 4.35. Attempts using a narrower simp set and an
explicit set-to-finset equality did not resolve it. The final proof uses
convert, simplification and congruence, with the same statement. One later
attempt only failed because an extra tactic ran after the goal was closed.
The exact extraction/compatibility patch is preserved.

SquareCertificate: the first build used an unavailable multiplication lemma
name and had an extra ring after field_simp had closed a goal. A repair then
let default simp rewrite a rational inverse inside one inequality; linarith
treated the two forms as different atoms. Restricting simplification to
mul_zero and applying sub_nonpos resolves this without changing the statement.
The added threshold identity also initially had a redundant final ring.

FiniteGraphBridge: the main endpoint compiled before the explicit degree
normalization lemma was added. That new lemma exposed another finite-set
instance mismatch after clearing denominators. The final proof uses two
congruence steps and membership extensionality. Its statement is unchanged.

FiniteControls: initial failures concerned the Std.Symm/Std.Irrefl constructor
wrappers, absence of a direct decidability instance for CliqueFree, an exact
finite intersection simplification and rewriting a function-valued profile.
The repairs give the explicit triangle-free proof, exact finite counts,
an arithmetic looplessness proof and an extensional profile equality.
No numerical claim or theorem premise was weakened.

Four expected false mathematical controls are separate from these compiler
development failures. They test wrong cost scaling, wrong mass, wrong slope
scaling and the excluded balanced alpha endpoint. They must fail for their
stated mathematical reason; see CONTROLS.txt and the fresh rejection logs.
