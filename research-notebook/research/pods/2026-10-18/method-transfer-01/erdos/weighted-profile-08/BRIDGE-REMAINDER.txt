E8 STOP BOUNDARY AND SMALLEST NEXT BRIDGE

E8 supplies a rational weighted profile on an actual finite graph. It does
not instantiate the E5 scalar cover or establish the full .0251 theorem.
No new numerical optimization was run.

For a cut whose larger side Y has ambient weight y>1/2, the next wrapper
must form the induced graph on Y and divide its positive weights by y.
It must derive, with the existing finite-graph objective conventions,
  alpha = 1/(2*y),
  ambient degree D = y*d,
  ambient second degree S = y^2*s,
  j = y^2*mu,
  T = R/y^2,
  L_normalized = L_ambient/y.
Extend the resulting unit profile by zero outside Y. Prove that its ambient
mass is 1/2 and its ambient cost is y^2 times the normalized objective.
This is the smallest useful next interface. These scale facts are not
assumed to be formalized merely because they are written here.

Then the two complete-square inequalities must imply the exact pointwise
premises of exists_affine_profile. The exact E5 cover, old uniform and
completion branches, BCL input, all-order transfer, balanced blowup and
floor-half rounding still need formal composition. The balanced y=1/2
case gives alpha=1 and must use the separate endpoint argument; E8 requires
strict alpha<1. Keep all original BCL, parity and rounding conditions.

The accepted written .0251 bound is separate evidence. E8 does not promote
it to a Lean endpoint. The external .02491 comparison has an unaccepted
max-cut premise and is not imported. No best-known or novelty claim follows.
