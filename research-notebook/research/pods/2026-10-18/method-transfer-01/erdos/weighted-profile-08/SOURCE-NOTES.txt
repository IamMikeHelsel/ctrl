Source modules: Erdos128/WeightedGraph.lean, CubicNeighborhood.lean,
OneStepSaving.lean. All three are copied byte-for-byte from the E3 input.
Their hashes, imports and preserved package pins are recorded in INPUTS.json.

The graph API, symmetric sum lemma and neighborhood endpoint construction
are existing work. The latter is the negative feasible endpoint of the
upstream onestep construction, as recorded in E1/comparison-02. The E8
bridge exposes exact profile costs and graph moments, then proves the
accepted affine criterion. This packet makes no novelty claim for the
construction, the averaging method or the numerical constants.

Import chain:
  Mathlib -> WeightedGraph -> CubicNeighborhood -> OneStepSaving
  -> WeightedProfiles -> ProfileControls -> Audit.
WeightedProfiles uses the existing graph definitions, degree bounds,
two_mul_edgeMass and weighted_neighbor_sum_swap. Its endpoint does not
invoke exists_cubic_neighborhood or exists_oneStepSaving. Those sources
remain in the declared import closure and are rebuilt and audited too.
The K3 lower-bound proof is reused from the E3 semantic control, with its
exact minimizer added here. This reuse is also marked in the Lean source.

replay.py copies all five local source modules to a new empty directory,
then builds them and Audit. Its LEAN_PATH includes only that fresh copy
and the recorded package dependency directories. It does not import E8
development products or prior research modules. Each of the six resulting
modules is separately checked with leanchecker. All public theorem axiom
closures are printed and checked against propext, Classical.choice and
Quot.sound. This does not replace independent statement review.

proof shortcuts in the accepted source set. Earlier failed source attempts
and their compiler diagnostics remain in their own attempt directories.
Build binaries are excluded from the frozen artifact manifest; their hashes
are retained in the successful verification record. Source and log files
remain preserved. Root owns repository sync, commits and pushes.
