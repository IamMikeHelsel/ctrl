E10 PROOF MAP

1. RationalIntervals.lean proves interval membership for constants, addition,
negation, subtraction, four-corner multiplication, square and positive
reciprocal. A square interval that straddles zero has lower endpoint zero.
A positive reciprocal reverses the endpoints. Membership itself implies
ordered endpoints. Every reciprocal in an expression requires a checked
strictly positive lower endpoint. Expr.sound proves the complete recursive
evaluator sound for every rational parameter point in the input intervals.

2. SelectorSemantics.lean encodes A and H as expressions. A_eq and H_eq prove
exact equality with the unchanged E9 certificates at
  L=L0+theta*(L1-L0), L1=(j-R)/(y-1/2)-2mu.
Both identities use that SAME L. moment_sound turns checked expression
upper bounds into the M disjunct. uniform_sound and completion_sound prove
the other leaf implications. x_mono, x_bounds and Q_antitone establish
completion monotonicity on its actual valid domain, including the join.

3. split_c and split_j prove closed-child coverage. At a split, a point on
the shared boundary is in both children; the proof may choose the left
child. Neither area equality nor sampled points establish coverage.

4. CheckedTree.lean contains 1743 explicit node theorems generated from the
unchanged E5 certificate. All 872 leaf tests are reduced in the Lean kernel
using decide +kernel: 53 uniform, 250 completion, 569 moment. Moment leaves
select theta 0 or 1, while their slopes are evaluated at the actual point.
The 871 internal node theorems instantiate the generic closed split rules.
Their types check all child endpoints against their parent. The root proves
coverage of [767/1000,22/25] x [0,I]. The generator and JSON are not trusted
proof engines: the source theorem remains valid only if its arithmetic and
all node applications typecheck. No supplied stored enclosure is accepted
as a premise. All expression enclosures are recomputed in the kernel.

5. UniversalSelector.lean adds analytic exteriors. Below 767/1000, uniform
cost is bounded by I*(767/1000)^2<R. Above 22/25, it proves exactly
 (1+c^2)R-c^2(completion(c)+I)
 = 54123/293750000 + (201409/5875000)(c-22/25)
   + (109297/470000)(c-22/25)^2 > 0.
If U fails there, this identity implies Q. The balanced boundary c=1 is
covered without dividing by y-1/2. The lower boundary c=1/2 is covered by
U. Both middle/exterior boundaries are closed. universal_selector joins
these regions; it retains only the parameter-bound hypotheses.
