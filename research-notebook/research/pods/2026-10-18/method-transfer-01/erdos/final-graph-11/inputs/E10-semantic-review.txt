E10 SOURCE AND MATHEMATICAL REVIEW
18 October 2026. Final execution verdict is in REPORT.txt when frozen.

Exact statement read in source and fresh Audit output
E10.universal_selector c j assumes only
 1/2<=c, c<=1, 0<=j, j<=E10.I,
with rational variables, E10.R=251/10000 and E10.I=2/47. It returns:
 U: c^2*j<=R; or
 Q: 3/4<=c and completion(c)+I-j<=R; or
 M: c<1 and one rational L with both unchanged E9 certificates <=0.
There is no tree-validity, interval-enclosure, graph-cost, selected-slope,
regularity or external BCL premise. The upper c bound is not needed by the
implementation's high-exterior algebra, but remains in the public statement.
The theorem is over rationals; finite graph cardinality/edge ratios are
rational. No claim of a separately formalized real-parameter theorem is made.

Generic interval semantics
Mem x (lo,hi) is the closed pair of inequalities lo<=x<=hi. Membership
itself gives endpoint order. Constants, addition and negation preserve
membership directly. Multiplication takes extrema of all four products;
its proof splits signs for multiplication by a scalar, twice. It does not
assume input signs. Square uses lower bound zero exactly when the interval
contains zero; otherwise the lower bound is the smaller endpoint square.
Its upper bound is the larger endpoint square. The sign splits in sq_sound
justify both statements, including intervals wholly below zero.

The reciprocal interval is (1/hi,1/lo). inv_sound explicitly requires
lo>0; it is not an inverse rule across zero or for arbitrary signed boxes.
Expr.safe recursively checks that precondition at every reciprocal node.
Expr.sound is structural induction over every expression constructor and
threads both safety and membership through each child. Literal denominators
are checked too. There is no unchecked real evaluator or floating arithmetic.
The added critic control demonstrates why safe cannot be dropped: c=1/2
lies in [-1,1], but its reciprocal 2 does not lie in the naive inverted
pair (1,-1), and safe correctly returns false. The false unguarded version
must be rejected for its mathematical goal, not for setup errors.

Exact expressions and shared slope
The expressions set y=1/(2c), m=4cj, b=y-1/2,
L0=8R(y-m)-2m, L1=(j-R)/b-2m, D=L1-L0 and L=L0+theta*D.
mean_eq proves E9's meanDegree y j=4cj for c!=0. A_eq proves
 eA=C+theta^2*D^2/[4(4R+1)] = lowCertificate y j R L.
H_eq proves
 eH=Hmin+(1-theta)^2*D^2/4 = highCertificate y j R L.
These are ring identities, not endpoint approximations or swapped bounds.
The SAME slope occurs in both identities and moment_sound's existential.
It may depend on c,j, and adjacent cells may choose different witnesses.
It cannot depend on a graph vertex. No continuity of the selector is needed.

momentOK checks positive lower c, upper c<1, expression safety and both
computed upper endpoints<=0. moment_sound derives c!=0, applies Expr.sound,
then substitutes A_eq/H_eq and returns that single L. The actual reciprocal
checks certify b>0 on each moment box. The main M branch excludes c=1.
SquareCertificate and its low/high definitions match accepted E9 byte for
byte; no independently reconstructed substitute is used in the endpoint.

Other leaves and completion boundary
uniform_sound requires nonnegative lower c and lower j. It bounds c^2 by
the squared upper c and j by upper j, with all sign conditions discharged.
completion_sound requires lower c>=3/4 and upper c<=1. It uses monotonicity
of x(c)=1-1/(2c), followed by antitonicity of Q(x) on [1/3,1/2]. Q has its
join at x=3/8, with both values 3/128. The mixed-branch proof compares via
that join. Since c>0, x<=3/8 is equivalent to c<=4/5. Thus the expression
matches the previously reviewed completion envelope. The lower j endpoint
is the correct worst case for completion(c)+I-j.

Closed coverage and generated source
split_c and split_j perform a <= midpoint case split. Equality belongs to
the left child; the other child starts at the same midpoint. There is no
open boundary gap or missing endpoint. These are general logical coverage
lemmas, not area arguments. Each generated node invokes a proved leaf rule
or the two actual child theorems. Lean checks the child box types against
the parent parameters. All arithmetic obligations use decide +kernel.

Analytic exteriors
At/below c=767/1000, U follows from the nonnegative parameter bounds and
I*(767/1000)^2<R. Above c=22/25 the correct high completion branch is
 -1/4+3/(8c)-1/(8c^2).
exterior_identity proves
 (1+c^2)R-c^2(completion(c)+I)
 =54123/293750000+(201409/5875000)(c-22/25)
  +(109297/470000)(c-22/25)^2>0.
If U fails and Q also failed, multiplying the Q failure by c^2 and adding
the U failure would contradict this identity. The coefficients/signs are
correct. The outer cases are closed; the remaining strict interior is
contained in the closed middle theorem. c=1/2 and c=1 are covered without
using the moment denominator. At c=1 the eventual graph use is U or Q.

Controls and what they mean
The .025 control proves a real all-slope obstruction for the selector:
at c=133/160,j=71/1880, U and Q fail and
 H(L)=1255919/50895360000+(L-L1)^2/4>0 for every rational L.
This is not a triangle-free graph counterexample. At R=.0251 the matched
c=5347/6400,j=895/24064 point fails U/Q and theta=0, while theta=1 makes
both certificates strictly negative. The target and slope tests therefore
address actual missing scalar conclusions, not arbitrary broken source.

The other builder controls reject omitted-child membership, a strict shared
boundary, false square lower bound, wrong reciprocal order and cancellation
with different slopes. They are deliberately false claims; they are not
seven full mutations of the complete selector implementation. The last is
a two-point mean arithmetic control, not a graph realization. Each rejected
goal must be read in the new replay log. A compiler failure alone is not a
mathematical falsification.
