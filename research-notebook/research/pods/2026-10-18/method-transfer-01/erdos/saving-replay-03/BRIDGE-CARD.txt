Target
For a nonempty finite triangle-free G and an oriented BCL cut C, construct
an actual rational fractional half with normalized cost at most
  C.mu/4 - savingLQ(C.c)/C.c^2 * C.mu^3.
Equivalently, for c=n/(2|Y|) and j=e(Y)/n^2, the bound is
  c^2*j -64*savingLQ(c)*c^4*j^3.
This is the missing graph-level bridge for comparison02. The graph's order
need not be even because this conclusion is a fractional half of mass n/2.
No global edge-density condition should appear in the target statement.
