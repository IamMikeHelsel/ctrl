Candidate written theorem
Under exactly the existing eventual BCL input I=2/47, every finite simple
triangle-free graph on n vertices has floor(n/2) vertices spanning at most
(63/2500)n^2 edges. The written proof constructs a rational fractional half
first. It then invokes the unchanged balanced-blowup, parity and rounding
interfaces. Edges are counted once. No regularity, optimal-cut, maximum-degree
or new global density hypothesis is added.

Checked by builder tools
A complete exact-rational cover certifies the fixed coefficient .0252.
The middle rectangle c in [769/1000,22/25], j in [0,2/47] has 1200 closed
leaves: 98 uniform, 287 completion and 815 refined moment cells. Exact
inequalities cover both outer c intervals. An independent implementation
checks all interval bounds, the partition tree and a separate 281-strip
tiling. Fresh copied-source runs reproduce the certificate and all results.

The refined moment sufficient condition is C<=0 and T=C+S<=0. Two exact
quadratic identities plus the actual graph identities E[d]=mu and
E[s]=E[d^2] give the fractional-half conclusion. Polynomial coefficient
checks, exact failed-shortcut points, an irregular triangle-free example,
a triangle counter-control and five certificate mutants are retained.

Scope limits
This is a written proof candidate with exact arithmetic evidence, pending
independent acceptance. It is not a new Lean endpoint or kernel proof.
The cubic-saving lemma is not used. The coefficient .0252 is not optimized.
No best-known, priority or publication claim is made. The neighborhood
profile and completion construction are existing work. The unaccepted
external .02491 comparison uses a different cut premise and is not imported.
