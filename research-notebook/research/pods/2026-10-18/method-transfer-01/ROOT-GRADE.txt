C5 closes a real logical gap. It proves the forward travel limit through
command delay and subsequent sampled control. The proof derives the error
budget from the sensor and state history. It does not assume that budget.
Its first measured state can have substantial braking acceleration.

The comparison remains limited. C5 moves the nominal holding target back
by about 44.27 mm. The older target misses the same enclosure test, but its
actual trajectory has not been shown unsafe. The final position, speed and
acceleration bands are too loose for precision standstill. The 64 micrometre

ready for internal proof review, not a new-controls-method submission.

reading on the actual sample clock? C6 is assigned to answer it. Then the
actual switch and post-switch existence must be connected. A matched
