Problem
Determine whether L(F_2) and L(F_3) are isomorphic as von Neumann algebras, and prove the answer.
Here F_n is the discrete free group on n generators. Its left regular representation on the complex
Hilbert space l^2(F_n) is defined by (lambda_n(g)xi)(h) = xi(g^(-1)h). Define L(F_n) =
{lambda_n(g): g in F_n}'', the double commutant, equivalently the weak operator closure of the
unital complex algebra generated by these unitaries.
The precise question is whether there exists a unital normal *-isomorphism Phi: L(F_2) -> L(F_3).
Normal means preservation of suprema of bounded increasing nets of self-adjoint operators,
equivalently ultraweak continuity. The isomorphism may send the canonical group generators to
arbitrary elements: no requirement to respect a chosen group presentation is imposed. The canonical
normalized traces are tau_n(T) = <T delta_e, delta_e>; a normal *-isomorphism necessarily preserves
them.

2 Small projections and the recurring boundary obstruction
The assistant adjoins nonseparable auxiliary factors, then finds that its extensions reduce to a separable
support algebra. It cannot transport the invariant distinguishing nonseparable abelian free powers [ 5]
through an arbitrary separable isomorphism. Popa–V aes’s vanishing theorem [ 43] prompts it to con-
sider derivations on restricted domains.
Factoring polynomial approximations through fewer variables bounds free-Jacobian rank, but the as-
sistant cannot control derivatives in the limit. Its commutative checkerboard example suggests that
strong convergence of bounded kernels need not preserve module rank; gradient relations supply no
uniform estimate.

constructs a shift on normalized vectors supported on shrinking orthogonal projections, concludes that
the shift removes the index defect while preserving regularity , and abandons the invariant.

dimension counts, while iterative encodings force increasingly rough decoders.
