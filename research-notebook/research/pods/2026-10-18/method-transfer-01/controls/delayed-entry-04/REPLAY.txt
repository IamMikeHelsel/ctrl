C4 REPLAY AND FALSIFIER SCOPE

The complete local closure is six modules, in this order:
  DigitalHold, AdaptiveAdmission, WeightedEntry, DelayedEntry,
  AdaptiveWitness, DelayedWitness.
All sources are copied into fresh-replay before compilation. LEAN_PATH uses
that directory plus the recorded pinned external package products. It
excludes the project's cached local proof products. Every declared theorem
closure is checked against only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.
Every module receives a separate leanchecker replay. Exact commands, source
hashes, compiler/checker hashes, pins, exit codes and log hashes are recorded.

For independent replay, copy the six frozen sources into a new reviewer-owned
scratch directory, use the recorded toolchain and package paths, build in
order, recreate the audit and replay the negative files. Do not rerun this
verifier over the frozen packet. It requires an empty fresh-replay directory
and refuses to overwrite the recorded fresh build.

Eight negative controls
1. WiderResidual changes .111 to .12 in the main proof. The unchanged face
   certificate fails. A true exact tuple gives outward third-face motion at
   .12. That tuple is not claimed to arise on a sampled history.
2. FiveMillisecondAge changes total age .001 to .005 in copied C3 source.
   Its retained-history/expiry route fails. This does not prove every other
   controller or proof at .005 is impossible.
3. FalseEarlyDeadline asserts the envelope reaches .045 at E instead of D+E.
   An exact positive-D envelope counterexample rejects it. This is not a
   physical minimum-settling-time theorem.
4. FalseDigitalLeftAtDispatch claims the witness left command at D is zero.
   Its actual left command is -1.21; zero is the right command.
5. FalsePendingResidual claims actual pending mismatch is <=.111 at time zero.
   The genuine witness has larger actual mismatch. The proved .111 budget
   concerns digital feedback and is used as actual mismatch only after D.
6. FalseResetCausality subtracts D from the retained zero stamp and claims
   the result is nonnegative. The exact witness rejects this reset.
7. FalseWholeHorizonClearance15 claims the nominal center+R<=.15.
   The exact value is .1564625. This is a false tube claim, not a physical
   trajectory that necessarily crosses .15.
8. FalseRenewedAge claims a zero-stamped read at original time .002 has
   age<=.001. Exact arithmetic rejects the reset. C3's actual-schedule
   no_first_hold_after_full_age is retained and audited.

Missing modules, unknown identifiers and missing files do not count as
negative evidence. Development failures and harmless warnings are preserved.
No remote job, paid service, new dependency, source download or Git command
was used. Independent proof acceptance is separate from builder verification.
