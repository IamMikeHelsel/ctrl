C7 NEGATIVE CONTROLS
The verifier changes one contract/formula at a time and expects a semantic
Lean rejection. These failed proofs are tests, not physical counterexamples.

1. ReadCurrentInsteadOfPriorNode: replace the prior sample by the sample at
   the cell start. This breaks the stated delay recurrence.
2. ResetCellToInitialState: replace the actual dispatch state by f(0). The
   required phase initial-value equality then fails.
3. WrongCellRateIndex: use R(index) instead of R(index+1). The rate field
   no longer matches the actual digital cell.
4. DropIncomingPendingEndpoint: remove h from the incoming pending side.
   This changes the first endpoint contract.
5. FalseIncomingStamp: claim that incoming .001 s uses stamp .0005 s.
6. FalseFinalOutgoingStamp: claim that incoming H=.002 s uses stamp .0015 s.
7. FalseRateContinuity: equate the two rate sides at .001 s.
8. FalseCommandContinuity: equate the two command sides at .0005 s.
9. FalseStartupStateReset: equate startup acceleration with initial acceleration.
10. FalseOneMillisecondPeriod: retain the 1 ms age budget with a 1 ms sample
    period plus .5 ms latency.

Controls 5-9 are contrasted with exact positive witness facts in the ordinary
source. The state and command changes are actual facts of a generated physical
execution, not an aggregate-enclosure impossibility argument. Control 10 is
an exact clock arithmetic obstruction. None proves a broader hardware claim.
