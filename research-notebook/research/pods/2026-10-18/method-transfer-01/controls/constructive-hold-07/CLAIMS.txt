C7 CLAIMS — FINITE CONSTRUCTED HOLD CONTINUATION
18 October 2026. Builder evidence; independent acceptance is separate.

Main endpoint
ConstructiveHold.continuation constructs a specific physical state, readings,
clock and side-dependent coefficients through H=N/2000, for every natural
N>=2. It proves C5 Execution, C5 IsSchedule, full equality with the supplied
pending prefix on [0,h], and the unchanged C5 clearance on every real time
in [0,H]. Here h=D=1/2000 s. The ceiling is
  x(t) <= 3/20 - 1277551/20100000000 < 0.15 m.
No invariant, endpoint cube, residual bound, or future Execution is a premise.
The old startup and sampled-history bounds enter only through the unchanged
C5 theorem, after the new Execution has been constructed.

Exact inputs
- An arbitrary state function g and an actual initial reading m. The pending
  prefix is ClearanceAdmission.Execution h h m g rp dp (constant stamp 0)
  (constant reading m). Thus g is continuous on [0,h], has the required right
  ODE on [0,h) and left ODE on (0,h], and satisfies the original rate and
  target-error boxes. The pending command is -1.21. Its rate/error need not
  be constant. No classical derivative across a dispatch is required.
- For clearance, the unchanged C5 Guard: m_x<=.143, |m_v|<=.01122,
  and -1.27<=m_a<=-.2. The execution constructor itself needs no Guard.
- Each digital cell n>=1 has constant R_n in [8,12] and constant physical
  target error delta_n in [-.01,.01]. Values can change between cells.
- A sequence epsilon with component bounds (.001,.01,.05). The implementation
  accepts bounds also at index zero, but epsilon_0 is unused by measure.
  The actual first sensor error is m-g(0), fixed and bounded by pre.error.
  Later readings are generated as state(n*h)+epsilon_n. Arbitrary old future
  braking readings are not retained.

Causality and joins
Cell n>=1, from n*h to (n+1)*h, uses reading n-1. The command is clipped
first. Then delta_n is added to obtain the physical target. The heldSolution
formula initializes from the actual state at n*h. node_recurrence exposes
this second-order recurrence: the next endpoint uses the two prior nodes.
state_prefix proves full physical prefix equality, not just initial equality.
No state is reset at h or any later dispatch.

At h, the incoming derivative uses the pending command and pending fields;
the outgoing derivative uses the first digital command and digital fields.
At each later dispatch the incoming side uses the prior cell and the outgoing
side the next cell. The final time H has the incoming derivative required by
Execution.left_ode. No outgoing physical ODE at H is claimed.

Samples occur at n*h and dispatches at (n+1)*h. Incoming age is at most
2*h=.001 s. used_index proves that each auxiliary index through grid(n+1)
is at most n. The schedule is algebraically defined for all natural indices.
The physical state family is proved to execute the controller only through
its own finite horizon. Its last formula outside that horizon is not an
infinite-horizon controller execution.

The pending interval's stamp-zero/read-m fields are unused by its independent
-1.21 command. They do not rename the timestamp of an old queued command.
The constructed first digital command uses the actual supplied reading m.

Nonvacuity and fair baseline
ConstructiveWitness proves both contracts at N=4, H=.002 s, from the same
nonconstant pending prefix and reading (.14,-.01,-1.17). Its changing case
uses rates 8,12,8 on the three digital cells, physical target error .005 on
the middle cell, and nonzero sensor errors (.0005,.005,.025) after index zero.
It proves startup acceleration changes, incoming/outgoing rates differ at
.001 s, and the command jumps from -1.21 to .29084264 at h. The matched
baseline uses the same constructor and prefix with rate 8 and zero digital
target/sensor errors. Both receive exactly the same clearance theorem.
This tests admissible continuation, not improved control performance.

The Lean witness proves the contracts and exact boundary facts. The separate
witness-trace.json gives binary64 endpoint illustrations only. It is not an
interval certificate and is not used by any Lean theorem.

Limits and remaining glue
This closes finite continuation for this piecewise-constant digital subclass,
given an admitted actual pending prefix. It does not establish existence for
arbitrary time-varying digital rate signals or a global infinite execution.
It does not construct the detector's earlier braking trajectory or prove a
full braking/detection/switch/hold operation. C8 still needs actual prefix and
time-coordinate composition; C6 is not a proof dependency of this packet.
It does not prove precision rest, hardware behavior, contact physics, or
novel control theory. All sources use the existing scalar response model.
