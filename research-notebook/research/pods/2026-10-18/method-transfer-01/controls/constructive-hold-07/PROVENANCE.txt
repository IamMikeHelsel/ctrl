C7 SOURCE AND VERIFICATION PROVENANCE
All writes for this packet are confined to controls/constructive-hold-07.
No previous frozen source, Git state, toolchain pin, or notebook copy is edited.
Root owns repository storage and independent review.

INPUTS.json binds ten research source copies to their exact prior files.
They are the accepted DigitalHold, C2, C3, C4 and C5 chain, plus their witness
helpers. ConstructiveClock reuses the already proved half-millisecond clock
from DelayedWitness. ConstructiveFlow reuses UncertainLag.heldSolution and
its exact initial-value and ODE theorems. It proves new one-sided pasting and
the causal node recurrence. ConstructiveHold builds the actual C5 Execution
by induction, then invokes the unchanged C5 clearance theorem. The existing
C6 proof is context for a later composition; it is not imported here.

PREFLIGHT-SOURCES.json binds the actual C7 preflight, independent statement
gate and startup-field addendum. It also binds the accepted C5 and C6 root
review manifests/reports. C6's manifest hash is read from its actual file;
the rejected typo in an earlier dispatch is not used.

verify.py copies all fourteen research modules and the full 31-module local
LeanControls dependency closure into fresh-replay, then rebuilds them there.
Its LEAN_PATH excludes the local project's cached build directory. It reuses
only the existing pinned package artifacts. verification.json records exact
source hashes, import graph, build order, compiler/checker hashes, toolchain,
package revisions, commands, return codes, durations, and per-log hashes.
It separately kernel-checks every rebuilt local/research module. It audits
all research theorem declarations plus UncertainLag.exact_initial/exact_ode;
only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound are allowed in their closures.
It rejects new sorry/admit/axiom/native_decide/unsafe tokens in research sources.

The verifier checks the complete C1-C6 frozen manifests and their recorded
files before and after replay, and the preserved transfer shortlist manifest.
It also checks each new copied source and each local dependency against its
pre-build hash. Local LeanControls source hashes must match the earlier C1
full-closure record. No external source was downloaded for this milestone.

attempts/ preserves seven development failures with exact source and log.
They concern clock abbreviation rewriting, one-sided pasting, normalization
of pending endpoint cases, and witness normalization. The successful files
are not substituted for these records. build.py and development logs are
convenience evidence only; verify.py and its fresh replay are the final check.

witness_trace.py uses standard-library binary64 exponential evaluation for
an illustrative N=4 trace. It records its own and the Lean source hashes.
The numeric endpoint is not used by any Lean proof or clearance decision.
