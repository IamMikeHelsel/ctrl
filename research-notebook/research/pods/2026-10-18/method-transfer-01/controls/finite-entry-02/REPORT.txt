C2 — FINITE ENTRY UNDER THE ACCEPTED 1 ms SAMPLED CONTRACT
18 October 2026. Builder result; independent acceptance is pending.

Result
WeightedEntry.sampled_finite_entry proves entry into weighted radius <= .045
by 7157/1200 s (about 5.9642 s), then containment at every real time through
the supplied horizon H. The theorem explicitly requires H >= 7157/1200.
It retains the old cube throughout. It uses the same initial cube, physical
state, feedback, sensors, dispatch schedule and original clock. The mismatch
bound is derived from the old invariant theorem; it is not a new premise.

Let z=(x-c, x-c+v/4, x-c+v/2+a/16). The weighted radius is
  rho(z)=max(|z0|/1.02, |z1|/1.01, |z2|).
For every t in [0,H], the new theorem proves
  rho(z(t)) <= max(.045, .055525-(3/1700)*t).
It also proves the original |zi(t)| <= .055525 throughout [0,H].

Proof mechanism
1. Apply the accepted DigitalHold.digital_invariant on the entire horizon.
2. Apply its prefix_mismatch with the proved cube to derive W=.1090800376.
3. Prove robust inward drift on all six weighted faces for every radius
   K >= .045, rate r in [8,12], and |w| <= W. The common normalized drift
   is 3/1700. The first face attains that drift at the plateau.
4. Apply the existing finite_prefix_barrier to six signed faces of the
   shrinking box, with a positive epsilon inflation. Remove epsilon.
The original left ODE handles dispatch boundaries and the incoming slope
at the envelope kink. Continuity covers endpoints. There is no clock reset,
new measurement, or new command at entry. Original initialization at time 0
still requires the accepted fresh-first-command schedule contract.

Witnesses and rejection controls
An exact periodic 1 ms schedule supplies an equilibrium execution on every
finite horizon, including six seconds. It starts inside the target box; it
is not a nonconstant arrival experiment. An algebraic initial-state witness
shows that the original initial cube includes states outside the target box.
No general execution-existence theorem from those states is claimed.

The equal-weight box has two active faces with zero drift at z=(rho,rho,rho),
r=8,w=0. It cannot supply the selected strict uniform drift. At radius .04,
an extreme allowed aggregate residual makes a weighted face point outward.
That is an obstruction to this enclosure proof, not a sampled-history
impossibility result. Failed earlier-deadline and larger-age proof mutations
likewise do not establish that every alternative physical proof must fail.
