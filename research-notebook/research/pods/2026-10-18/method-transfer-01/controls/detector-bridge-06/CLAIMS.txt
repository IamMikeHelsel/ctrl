Physical contract
BrakingExecution supplies one physical state f=(x,v,a) through .530 s.
It is continuous on that interval. Inside each phase, x'=v, v'=a and
 a'=r(q-a). Each phase rate is continuous. The effective target q is
physical acceleration authority, not merely an actuator command.

Initial state: x<=.001, v in [.39,.41], a in [-1.22,.1].
Loss phase, t in [0,.05]: r>=0, q in [-1.22,.1].
Recovered phase, local s=t-.05 in [0,.48]: r in [8,12],
q in [-1.22,-1.2]. These bounds persist through the stated horizon.
The same state f supplies both phases, including their common boundary.

Compared with C1, positive initial speed, lower initial acceleration,
lower targets, the recovered rate ceiling, and extension through .530
are new premises. The rate ceiling is retained for later C5 compatibility;
the scalar comparison itself does not need that ceiling. This is not a
consequence for every trajectory allowed by C1.
