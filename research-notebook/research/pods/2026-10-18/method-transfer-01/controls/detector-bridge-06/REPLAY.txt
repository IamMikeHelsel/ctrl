REPLAY AND PRESERVATION

The final builder run is verification.json. verify.py binds its own hash,
Lean/toolchain/package hashes, source hashes, local module order, commands,
exit codes, log hashes and elapsed times. It excludes the project's cached
local oleans from LEAN_PATH. Only pinned external package artifacts are reused.

The complete local closure is 31 LeanControls source modules and 12 research
modules. fresh-replay/ contains the exact copied sources and generated audit.
Build caches are excluded from the frozen manifest. All 43 modules are built
and separately checked with leanchecker. The theorem audit is transitive.

verify.py deliberately refuses an existing replay directory. Do not rerun it
in the frozen packet or delete its evidence. For independent review, copy the
sources into a separate review directory, retain the toolchain/package pins,
use that directory before the pinned package paths, and replay the recorded
build order, audit and kernel commands. Root/critic owns that separate review.

Five mutation controls remove lower initial acceleration, lower loss target,
positive initial speed, the recovered rate floor, or the original velocity
sensor error. Each must fail the relevant derivation, not module resolution.
Five false-claim controls test the 1.5ms age, tied dispatches, Zeno coverage,
large-negative-acceleration reading, and positive-acceleration initial zero.
The corresponding true facts are separate proved declarations.

INPUTS.json binds the nine copied research inputs. SOURCE-DERIVATION.json
binds the adapted witness and helper templates. PREFLIGHT-SOURCES.json binds
the original failed sampling card, its finite-grid repair, the independent
