  does not improve a triangle-free BCL input. No special spectral hypothesis
  for our graphs has been established. Use its all-cut transfer lemma only.

  incidence-pattern correlation. No map from that rank geometry to our
  triangle-free support constraint has been found. Do not transfer its theorem
  or its claimed counterexample to Erdos128 by analogy.
