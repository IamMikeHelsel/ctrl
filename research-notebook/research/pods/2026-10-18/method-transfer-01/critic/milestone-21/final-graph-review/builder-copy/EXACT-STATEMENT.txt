E11 ALL-FINITE-GRAPH ENDPOINT

Erdos128.FinalGraph.sparse_half_251_10000:
For a finite type V in universe u with decidable equality, a simple graph G
with decidable adjacency, and G.CliqueFree 3, given BCLEventual.{u}, there
exists a finite vertex set S such that
  S.card = Fintype.card V / 2
and
  (edgeCountOn G S : rational) <= (251/10000)*(Fintype.card V)^2.
Natural-number division by 2 is floor division. Each unordered edge is
counted once. Empty, odd and even graph orders are included.

The only external mathematical hypothesis is the transparent definition
Erdos128.CompletionBridge.BCLEventual:
  There exists N : Nat such that, for every finite type W in universe u
  with decidable equality and every decidable-adjacency simple graph H,
  N <= Fintype.card W and H.CliqueFree 3 imply BCLCutCertificate H.
BCLCutCertificate H means that there exists a vertex cut whose two internal
edge counts sum to at most (2/47)*(Fintype.card W)^2.
The exact definition and full endpoint type are printed in the fresh audit.
BCLEventual is a hypothesis, not an axiom and not a theorem proved here.

Intermediate endpoints:
- fractional_of_oriented_cut_even takes an actual oriented BCL cut and a
  positive even-order representation, then constructs a normalized rational
  fractional half of cost <=251/10000. It derives the scalar domain from
  that cut. It has no separate scalar-domain or desired-cost premise.
- fractional_251_10000_all_orders gives the corresponding fractional half
  for all finite orders from BCLEventual, including the empty graph.

No regularity, maximum-cut optimality, stronger density bound or assumed
cost profile is added. This formalizes the existing written E5 route. It
is not the conjectural 1/50 bound and carries no novelty or best-known claim.
