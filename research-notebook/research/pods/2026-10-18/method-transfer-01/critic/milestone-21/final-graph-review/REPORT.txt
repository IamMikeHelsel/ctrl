Accepted result

Erdos128.FinalGraph.sparse_half_251_10000 proves: given the transparent
CompletionBridge.BCLEventual premise, every finite triangle-free simple
graph G on n vertices has a set S with |S|=floor(n/2) and at most
(251/10000)n^2 induced unordered edges. Empty, odd and even orders are
included. The final elaborated type has no extra regularity, maximum-cut,
profile-cost or stronger-density premise.

BCLEventual means that above some finite order N, every triangle-free
graph has a cut with total internal edges at most (2/47)n^2. It is an
explicit hypothesis, not a custom axiom and not a theorem proved here.
Finite examples do not establish this universal premise. This acceptance
does not prove the Erdos 128 coefficient 1/50, novelty, best-known status,
sharpness, or an efficient executable witness algorithm. The stronger
external cut claim remains unaccepted and unused.
