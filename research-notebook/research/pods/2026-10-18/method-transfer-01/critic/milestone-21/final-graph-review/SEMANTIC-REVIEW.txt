E11 INDEPENDENT STATEMENT AND COMPOSITION REVIEW
18 October 2026

The actual endpoint matches the stated conditional graph claim. This file
records the source review. REPORT.txt records the final replay verdict.

Endpoint and external premise

Erdos128.FinalGraph.sparse_half_251_10000 gives a set S of floor(n/2)
vertices with at most (251/10000)n^2 unordered induced edges in every finite
triangle-free simple graph. The only external mathematical premise is
CompletionBridge.BCLEventual in the same universe. This transparent Prop
says that, above some finite order N, each triangle-free graph has a cut
whose two internal edge counts sum to at most (2/47)n^2. It is neither a
custom axiom nor a theorem established by this packet. Finite examples do
not supply this universal premise. The printed elaborated endpoint has no
extra density, profile-cost, regularity or maximum-cut premise.

Actual witnesses and scaling

An OrientedBCLCut contains an actual small side X, its complement Y, the
orientation |X|<=|Y|, and the BCL internal-edge bound. Set n=|V|, t=n/2,
c=t/|Y|, y=|Y|/n, j=e(Y)/n^2, q=e(X)/n^2. For nonempty even order, the
proof derives c in [1/2,1], j>=0, q>=0 and q+j<=2/47 from this cut. Thus the
E10 selector's premises are derived rather than supplied as extra inputs.

U uses the actual profile c on Y and zero on X. It has mass t, weights in
[0,1], and normalized cost c^2 j. Q deletes edges inside X, constructs a
fractional half on the resulting triangle-free graph, and restores at most
e(X) cost. The selector gives c>=3/4. Together with n=2t and the partition,
this implies 2t<=3|X|, the exact completion domain. It also has |X|<=t.
The homogeneous completion envelope scales by n^2, not n. Its raw bound
becomes completion(c)+q, then at most completion(c)+2/47-j.

The piecewise completion conditions agree exactly. At c=4/5, x=3/8,
completion(c)=3/128. For |X|=3,t=4,n=8, the raw value is 3/2. Both sides
of the piecewise formula agree at the join. There is no strict-domain gap.

M supplies c<1 and one rational L satisfying both E9 square certificates.
The same L is passed to the low and high branches. The proof derives
y=1/(2c)>1/2, uses the graph-derived degree and edge moments, and obtains
an actual bounded profile of local mass alpha=1/(2y). Extension to V has
mass n/2. Local edge cost is multiplied by y^2; the local slope is L/y.
The balanced case c=1,y=1/2 cannot enter M. U or Q covers it. No zero
moment denominator is canceled. The nonempty condition supplies n>0.

All finite orders

The BCL transport first applies the eventual premise to the positive
balanced blowup of multiplicity N+1. Its order is at least N. Normalized
cut descent averages clone indicators, then rounds the internal-cut
objective without increase. This is an actual cut descent, not an
assumption that the chosen large-graph cut is constant on clone classes.

The final fractional endpoint then uses a second positive blowup of
multiplicity 2 to make the order even. Clone averaging preserves the
fractional-half mass and normalized cost on descent. This does not attempt
to descend an integral half by arbitrary clone selection. Pair rounding
leaves at most one fractional coordinate. Discarding that coordinate gives
exactly floor(n/2) vertices, with no increase in nonnegative edge cost.

Empty types are handled directly by the empty set/profile. Their branch
does not divide by a positive graph order. Natural division in the final
cardinality is floor division, including odd n.

Concrete controls

The six-vertex one-edge graph supplies a nonzero scaling test: mass 3,
raw unordered cost 9/25, normalized cost 1/100, ordered adjacency sum
18/25. Its zero-cost independent half is a separate possible witness;
the theorem is existential and does not assert that all halves satisfy it.
K2 checks balanced admission. C5 checks an odd nonempty graph: its U
bound fails, while Q passes; the doubled cut has raw envelope 2. The
fractional endpoint descends and rounds to two vertices. Every three-vertex
set has an edge, which rejects ceil rounding at the claimed constant.
None of these examples instantiates BCLEventual.

Limits

This is formal closure of the written E5 route, conditional on the stated
BCL input. It does not prove BCL itself, the Erdos 128 coefficient 1/50,
a sharp bound, novelty, best-known status, or an efficient executable
witness algorithm. The unaccepted stronger external cut premise is unused.
