Verdict
Accepted as a written algebraic optimum for the EXACT fixed comparison
class. It is not a new Lean theorem or a physical trajectory optimum.
The actual C5 definitions match the proposal. All weights are positive
and no weight exceeds the forward multiplier W=51/50.

Proof and quantifiers
Fix any reading m, its three offsets A_i, errors e_i, weights w_i and
radius floor K0. Take ANY feasible real center c and radius K, where
K>=K0 and |A_i-c|+e_i<=w_i*K for every i. Absolute value gives
 A_i+e_i-w_i*K <= c <= A_j-e_j+w_j*K.
Therefore (w_i+w_j)*K>=A_i-A_j+e_i+e_j. Since the denominator is positive,
K is at least every ordered pair ratio, including i=j, and hence K>=K*.
This uses precisely C5's max over all nine pairs, not a sampled subset.

For each i,
 c+W*K >= A_i+e_i+(W-w_i)*K
        >= A_i+e_i+(W-w_i)*K*.
The second inequality is valid because W-w_i>=0. Taking the finite maximum
gives c+W*K>=max_i(A_i+e_i-w_i*K*)+W*K*=c*+W*K*.
C5's accepted feasible theorem proves that (c*,K*) belongs to the class.
Thus the lower bound is attained and is its global minimum. The proof does
not require a guard, compactness, differentiation, or bounded center domain.
It also does not claim a unique minimizer; flat portions can occur.

The interval center is the left endpoint, not the midpoint. A larger radius
can reduce that left endpoint, but it cannot reduce this objective with the
actual weights. The midpoint is strictly worse for the nominal reading, as
the independent exact check confirms. A fixed different center cannot beat
the candidate after making the same interval constraints feasible.

Limits and practical meaning
This compares c+1.02*K using the same offsets, interval errors, weights and
radius floor. It does not prove that an arbitrary alternative controller is
stable or that its actual maximum position equals this bound. A competitor
using different reachable-state information, errors, weights, certificate
shape, radius floor or control contract is outside this comparison class.
The argument rules out a better value from center-only optimization within
the stated class, even when a larger feasible radius is allowed.

At the retained worst guard corner (.143,.01122,-1.27), the exact objective
is 3013722449/20100000000. The prior independently checked corner packet is
copied here. It is still a certificate extremum; physical reachability of
that joint corner is not proved. Tightening only measured acceleration's
upper bound to -27/28 keeps this corner and hence keeps that optimum over
the rectangular guard. This does not rule out a useful joint physical
constraint that removes the corner, and does not establish such a constraint.

The sign condition is essential
With one coordinate A=e=0, w=2, W=1, K0=1, the minimum-radius left endpoint
is (c,K)=(-2,1), giving U=-1. The feasible pair (-4,2) gives U=-2. Thus
the same conclusion is false when a weight exceeds the forward multiplier.
Also retain diagonal pairs: A=0,e=1,w=1,K0=0 requires K>=1. Omitting its
only diagonal pair would incorrectly permit K=0 and an empty feasible set.
