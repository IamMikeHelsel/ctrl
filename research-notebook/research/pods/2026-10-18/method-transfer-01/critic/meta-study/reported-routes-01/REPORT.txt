Method and inference limits
The notes distinguish reported proposals from our 'Our use' reconstructions.
That distinction is essential. Similar failures can suggest tests, but do not
show that a cited discipline caused a result, that a rare combination was
necessary, or that our later work independently rediscovered the mechanism.
There is selection, abridgment and hindsight. Related source parts and E1-E6
are dependent lineages. No cross-field frequency, success probability, verified
solution count or strategy superiority follows from this sample.

The proposed filter is useful as a test protocol: name the missing statement,
map objects and hypotheses, list what the map must preserve, give a falsifier,
and compare full costs with the baseline before spending more proof time.
The planned fair baseline includes task information, model, time and review
budgets; it counts failures, reading and review costs and separates useful
rejections from useful theorems. This is an exploratory design, not an executed
controlled comparison. Downstream reuse must retain upstream hypotheses.
