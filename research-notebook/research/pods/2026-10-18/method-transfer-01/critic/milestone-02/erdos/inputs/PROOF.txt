1. Exact proposed theorem and external input

Let hBCL be the following proposition: there exists N such that every finite
simple triangle-free graph H with at least N vertices has a bipartition with
at most (2/47)|V(H)|^2 internal edges. This is the same eventual BCL input
used by PublishedBCL.lean, definition BCLEventual. It is a hypothesis here.

Conditional on hBCL, every finite simple triangle-free graph G on n vertices
has a set S of floor(n/2) vertices with
  e_G(S) <= (1293/50000)n^2 = .02586 n^2.
Each edge is counted once. There are no loops, multiple edges, edge weights,
regularity assumption, maximum-degree assumption, or optimal-cut premise.
The coefficient 1/50 is not claimed. The new argument first constructs a
fractional half, then uses the unchanged rounding interface in section 7.

It suffices first to prove this statement for n>0 even, given any oriented
cut X,Y with |X|<=|Y| and internal cost at most I n^2, where I=2/47. Section 7
removes evenness and obtains this cut from hBCL. The n=0 case is the empty set.

2. Existing graph constructions and their exact scalar domain

Put x=|X|/n, y=|Y|/n, c=1/(2y), q=e(X)/n^2, j=e(Y)/n^2. Thus
  x+y=1, 0<=x<=1/2, 1/2<=y<=1, 1/2<=c<=1,
  x=1-1/(2c), q>=0, j>=0, q+j<=I, and hence j<=I.
Let beta be the minimum normalized fractional-half cost on G. A profile z
is a fractional half if 0<=z_v<=1 and sum_v z_v=n/2. Its normalized cost is
n^(-2) sum_{uv in E(G)} z_u z_v. A minimum exists by compactness.

The uniform profile c on Y and zero on X gives beta<=c^2 j. For c>=3/4,
x>=1/3. Delete the edges inside X, apply the existing independent-set
completion theorem, and restore those edges at cost at most q. This gives
  beta <= Q(x)+q,
where, on the domain used here,
  Q(x)=3x^2/2-5x/4+9/32 for 1/3<=x<=3/8;
  Q(x)=x(1/2-x)/2       for 3/8<=x<=1/2.
The two definitions agree at x=3/8. These are existing graph constructions,
not assumptions about beta. They are described in PMA sections 2--3 and
proved in the local completion modules listed in INPUTS.json. The local
CutScalar wrapper asks for its second witness only above c0=7797/10000.
Every use of that wrapper's domain below has c>=781/1000>c0>3/4.

Adding the first bound to c^2 times the second gives
  beta <= c^2 (I+Q(x))/(1+c^2),  c>=3/4.                 (OLD)
The addition bounds the same minimum beta by two feasible costs; it does not
assert that one profile has both costs. Equivalently choose the cheaper of
the two profiles. All denominators here are positive.

3. Reused neighborhood profile and actual graph moments

In this section Y can have arbitrary positive vertex masses w_v summing to
y>1/2. Its adjacency is still that of a finite simple triangle-free graph.
For the unweighted graph in section 2, w_v=1/n. Let
  b=y-1/2>0,
  j=sum_{uv in E(Y)} w_u w_v,
  d_v=sum_{u in N_Y(v)} w_u,
  s_v=sum_{u in N_Y(v)} w_u d_u.
The neighborhood N_Y(v) is independent. Therefore s_v is exactly the edge
mass incident to N_Y(v); it has no internal edges. The mass of edges in its
complement is j-s_v. The following two feasible profiles are the negative
endpoint from Sarid's existing one-step lemma; they are not a new construction.

If d_v<=b, put z=1/[2(y-d_v)] on Y minus N_Y(v), and z=0 elsewhere. Since
y-d_v>=1/2, its values lie in[0,1], its selected mass is1/2, and its cost is
  F_v=(j-s_v)/[4(y-d_v)^2].                            (LOW)
If d_v>b, put z=1 outside N_Y(v) within Y and z=1-b/d_v on N_Y(v), zero on X.
Now 0<b/d_v<1. The selected mass is y-b=1/2 and its cost is
  F_v=j-(b/d_v)s_v.                                   (HIGH)
The cost follows because all edges meet N_Y(v) at most once. At d_v=b>0
the profiles and formulas agree. At d_v=0 use LOW, with no division by d_v.
Zero-mass vertices can be removed. Positive masses give d_v<y. The case
y=1/2 is excluded from this lemma and is covered by OLD, with c=1, below.

Take expectation with probability w_v/y over vertices of Y. Finite double
counting, with no regularity assumption, gives
  E[d_v]=mu=2j/y,
  E[s_v]=(1/y) sum_v w_v sum_{u~v} w_u d_u
        =(1/y) sum_u w_u d_u sum_{v~u} w_v
        =(1/y) sum_u w_u d_u^2=E[d_v^2].                (MOMENTS)

4. Affine moment majorant

Fix R=1293/50000. Define the continuous piecewise function
  psi(d)=j-4R(y-d)^2       when d<=b;
  psi(d)=(j-R)d/b         when d>=b;
and phi(d)=psi(d)-d^2. If every F_v>R, LOW and HIGH imply s_v<psi(d_v),
because all denominators used in the rearrangement are positive. MOMENTS
then implies E[phi(d_v)]>0.

Define
  mu=2j/y,
  L=8R(y-mu)-2mu,
  C=j-4R(y-mu)^2-mu^2,
  B=(j-R)/(y-1/2)-L,
  S=B^2/4+Lmu-C.
Expansion gives the following identities, for every real d:
  j-4R(y-d)^2-d^2
    =C+L(d-mu)-(4R+1)(d-mu)^2;                       (ID-LOW)
  (j-R)d/(y-1/2)-d^2-[C+L(d-mu)]
    =-(d-B/2)^2+S.                                  (ID-HIGH)
Thus C<0 and S<=0 imply phi(d)<=C+L(d-mu) on both branches. By MOMENTS,
E[phi(d_v)]<=C<0, a contradiction. At least one feasible fractional half F_v
has cost<=R. In fact the strict C bound excludes all F_v>=R as well, but the
stated theorem only uses the non-strict conclusion.

This is not an application of Jensen to phi. Its derivative can jump up at b.
The negative control below records that failure. The affine majorant works
across the jump and uses the actual graph second-moment identity.

7. Integral halves, all orders, and the unchanged BCL input

For n>0 even, sections 2--5 give a rational fractional profile at cost<=Rn^2.
Every neighborhood profile is rational for the unweighted finite graph.
The reused completion witnesses are rational as well. There is no limiting
step and no divisibility condition on d_v: selected fractions are permitted.

For arbitrary n>0, first use the positive balanced two-fold blowup G[2].
It is triangle-free and has even order. The old BCL transport argument
obtains a BCL cut for it from hBCL: take a sufficiently large positive
balanced blowup to meet N, then descend the normalized internal-cut bound.
The descent works because the cut cost is affine in the number of twins
on one side when the other classes are fixed. Successively choose class
endpoints without increasing cost. This gives a cut of the original graph
with the same normalized bound. No optimality of the chosen cut is needed.

Apply sections 2--5 to G[2]. Average each independent twin class to descend
the fractional profile to G. This preserves its selected mass n/2 and its
normalized quadratic edge cost exactly. The retained formal interface is
  Erdos128.hasNormalizedFractionalHalfBoundQ_balancedBlowup_iff
in Erdos128/BalancedBlowup.lean:417, for any positive k and rational bound.

Finally use the existing rounding theorem
  Erdos128.hasSparseHalf_of_hasFractionalHalfBound
in Erdos128/RoundingFinal.lean:84. Its argument transfers mass between two
fractional coordinates. The objective is concave along the transfer, so a
feasible endpoint does not increase cost. Iteration leaves at most one
fractional coordinate. For even n none remains; for odd n it is1/2, and
deleting it does not increase cost. The resulting set has floor(n/2) vertices.
This proves the stated mathematical implication from hBCL.

The existing PublishedBCL.lean uses these exact interfaces for .02587.
E1 has not changed that source, imported a new axiom, or built a new Lean
endpoint for .02586. The graph argument and interval certificate here still
need independent review before root decides whether to formalize them.

8. Attribution and novelty boundary

Sarid's lemma onestep already contains the profile in section 3. Under the
normalization w'_v=w_v/y, alpha=1/(2y), delta=d_v/y, its direction is
1_N-delta and its negative endpoint radius is
min(alpha/(1-delta),(1-alpha)/delta). The two cases reduce exactly to LOW
and HIGH. Razborov's Lemma4.1, pages10--11, also uses a neighborhood-complement
fractional half. The old cut/completion envelope is PMA's combination of BCL
and Razborov's completion argument. These ingredients are reused.

The candidate delta is the specific affine second-moment majorant C,L,S and
the exact uniform cover which combines it with OLD at R=.02586. It is our
reconstruction, prompted by the source study; it is not described as a
discovery mechanism reported by a released reasoning summary.

The focused primary-source check found no explicit .02586 statement or this
same affine separator in the sections read. Sarid states.0262; Razborov
states27/1024; PMA's latest pinned main still states.02587. This does not
establish priority. The new numerical implication could be a corollary of
known ingredients, and related arguments may exist elsewhere. See
PRIOR-WORK.txt for exact locations, source hashes, and reading limits.
