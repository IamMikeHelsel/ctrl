Independent signed-deficit control
For one abstract type, let m=-1/25, probability p=1, total weight l=2 and
weight floor a=1. Then l>=a, but l*m<a*m. This respects the natural deficit
range m>=-(2/25)p, unlike an unrestricted negative toy. It still is not an
actual graphon or a refutation of an unseen certificate.

For nonnegative weights, the valid general comparison is
  sum_c lambda_c*g_c >= l*m >= a*m-(2/25)(l-a)p,
where m=min_c g_c and m>=-(2/25)p. Exact l=a removes the correction. A source
may use another valid correction, but it must be shown in the actual proof.
Also, any correctly normalized mixture of honest global cuts is an upper
bound on the optimum. A claim that fixed mixtures are inherently invalid
needs qualification; they can instead be too weak or improperly normalized.
