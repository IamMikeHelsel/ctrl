theorem exists_oneStepSaving
    (H : SimpleGraph V) (W : RatProbabilityWeight V)
    (htri : H.CliqueFree 3) (ψ : V → ℚ) (α : ℚ)
    (hα0 : 0 ≤ α) (hα1 : α ≤ 1) :
    ∃ π : V → ℚ,
      IsUnitProfile π ∧ weightedMean W π = α ∧
        weightedObjective H W ψ π ≤
          weightedObjective H W ψ (fun _ ↦ α) -
            4 * savingLQ α * edgeMass H W ^ 3

RatProbabilityWeight requires strictly positive rational weights with sum1.
weightedObjective is one half the ordered adjacency sum plus a linear term.
IsUnitProfile bounds each rational coordinate in[0,1].
savingLQ(alpha)=min(4*min(alpha,1-alpha)^2, min(alpha,1-alpha)*(1-min(alpha,1-alpha))).
