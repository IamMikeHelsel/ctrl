C1 MILESTONE CLAIM MAP
18 October 2026. Builder evidence; independent acceptance is separate.

1. Reusable physical certificate transfer.
RecoveryPeak.first_zero_of_envelope_certificate uses the same physical model
x'=v, v'=a, a'=r(q-a) and the same loss/recovery assumptions as the frozen
Recovery theorem. It permits an arbitrary certified recovered horizon S>=0.
Parameters satisfy v0,A,T>=0 and b,k>0. One state f is continuous on [0,T+S],
including recovery. Initially x<=X, 0<=v<=v0, and a<=A. During loss, q<=A and
r>=0. During recovery, q<=-b and r>=k. Rates are continuous in each phase.
The phase ODEs hold on their open intervals, as in the supplied trajectory
predicate. Target and rate guarantees persist through the chosen horizon.
No upper rate bound, endpoint box, physical path bound, or acceleration
envelope is added as a premise. General trajectory existence is not proved.

The two analytical certificate premises concern only the explicit heldSolution
with upper initial state [X+v0*T+A*T^2/2, v0+A*T, A]: its terminal velocity
is nonpositive and its position is <=B on [0,S]. They are exposed certificate
conditions, not hidden physical safety assumptions. The theorem derives the
physical loss-phase and engagement bounds, applies recovered_upper_flow, and
extracts a first physical velocity zero tau in [0,T+S]. Velocity is positive
before tau and physical position is <=B through tau.

2. Concrete 15 cm test passes, with a stronger certificate.
RecoveryPeak.old_contract_first_zero discharges the analytical certificate
for X=.001 m,v0=.41 m/s,A=.1 m/s^2,T=.05 s,b=1.2 m/s^2,k=8/s.
It proves tau<=.529 s and x(t)<=.142 m for every t in [0,tau].
The recovered guarantees are required for S=.479 s. The position result is
a reference-position upper bound. It is not an absolute distance bound from
an unknown initial position. It does not bound negative travel.

old_upper_position_le proves the explicit upper flow <=.142 m for every
nonnegative time, using three time intervals and exact exponential bounds.
old_upper_velocity_negative proves its velocity is negative at s=.479 s.
These are full-time analytical statements, not a sampled plot or an assumed
terminal position. The physical result remains limited to its supplied
execution horizon. old_upper_exceeds_14cm proves that the same explicit upper
flow exceeds .14 m at s=.48 s; the new .142 certificate is not exact optimality.

3. Actual phase-change witness and zero-speed scope.
RecoveryPeak.Witness.restored_trajectory supplies an exact trajectory with
rate 8, effective target +.1 for .05 s, then effective target -1.2.
It starts at [.001,.41,.1]. All physical coordinates agree at recovery.
restored_first_zero instantiates the .142 m/.529 s theorem for this state.
This is an actual target change in the scalar model, not a constant-target
trajectory relabeled as loss and restoration. It is not contact mechanics.

is zero, even when A>0. zero_speed_not_hold gives a lagged negative-target

Thus first zero does not mean maintained rest. No unique nonnegative root is
asserted. When v0=A=T=0, the initial-zero result applies directly.

The frozen simple formula remains .1605859375 m and fails .15 m. The older
fixed-contract exponential proof already gives .15 m with rate in [8,12].
The new result gives .142 m under the current recovery contract with only

Thus the numerical inputs match, but these structural premises must remain
visible when comparing theorem domains.

This milestone uses existing scalar order comparison and exact exponential
inequalities. The older auxiliary-function reading helped select a full-time
