The verifier requires an empty milestone-owned fresh-replay/ directory.

reviewer-owned location and adjust only the replay harness's workspace path,
or use an independently written runner. Do not overwrite frozen evidence.
build.py is the development runner; verify.py is the fresh closure check.

The verifier copies all local LeanControls dependencies of UncertainLag and
the seven research modules. It builds these from source without any cached
local LeanControls oleans in LEAN_PATH. Existing pinned package products
(including Mathlib) are reused. Every local module is separately checked by
leanchecker. The public research theorem closures permit only propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound. It also checks source/provenance hashes,
preservation of the preflight, and six meaningful rejection controls.

The controls weaken the loss target, remove restored authority, remove the
rate floor, assert a false 14 cm upper-flow bound, assert the old simple
formula passes 15 cm, and infer maintained rest from zero initial speed.
Expected rejection is distinguished from a missing import or tool failure.

The scope excludes a source rebuild of Mathlib or a proof of compiler
correctness. Tool binaries, package revisions, local sources, source copies,
commands, exit codes and log hashes are recorded in verification.json.
