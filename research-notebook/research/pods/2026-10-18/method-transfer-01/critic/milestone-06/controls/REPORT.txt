Verdict

build, axiom audit and separate kernel replay. It derives delayed admission
from the actual first reading and its sensor box. No endpoint cube, ongoing
invariance or aggregate mismatch bound is a main premise. It removes the old

c=m_x+m_v/4+m_a/32. The guard is |m_v|<=.02 and |m_a|<=1.25. This is not a

center is .1009375 m and the position tube is [.0454125,.1564625] m. The old
fixed case had center .088125 and upper tube .14365. C3 does not retain the

Actual contract and ordering
The global clock starts at the actual first sample, time zero. Require
0<=D<=H and D<=.0005 s. One continuous physical state f=(x,v,a) obeys
x'=v, v'=a, a'=r*(u+d-a), with the stated left and right derivative channels.
Rates on both channels lie in [8,12]/s; target error has magnitude <=.01.
The pending command is exactly -1.21, so its effective target lies in
[-1.22,-1.20]. It applies before D and on the left at D. Digital feedback
applies on the right at D, clips to [-2,2], and adds target error afterward.
The center is fixed for the whole execution, including all later readings.

The schedule has sample 0 at time zero, dispatch 0 at D, strict locally
finite dispatches, causal samples and shared held measurements on the stated
half-open/half-closed cells. Startup indices are zero for both channels.
Thus both readings at D still equal m and both stamps remain zero. There is
no fresh-at-dispatch substitution. Every stamp is nonnegative, no later than
the current time, and at most .001 s old. Startup consumes that total age.
The theorem does not assume a sampling period alone or reset the clock at D.
The first-cell age theorem forbids continued use of stamp zero past age 1 ms.

The feedback slope during the pending interval is derived from its actual
ODE. Independent exact corner arithmetic gives the enclosing interval
[1.9548,7.7252], so the proof's absolute bound 8 is valid. Under a temporary
cube prefix with R<=K<=1/16, clipping is proved inactive. The first history
pass uses the coarse digital residual and the separately derived pending
slope. The second yields slope <=8 over the entire original sample history.
Sensor and target errors then give residual <=.103+.008=.111. The remaining
inward-face margin is 2R-.111=.00005.

The global first-contact proof uses epsilon-inflated cube faces. Startup
contact is impossible by the derived startup cube. Later contact uses the
prefix residual, the incoming left ODE and the old cube-face inequality.
The prefix premise is discharged by the barrier argument and epsilon tends
to zero. No future-state bound or already-proved smaller tube is assumed.
The final result holds at all admitted real times, including dispatches and
the finite horizon endpoint. It retains |v|<=.4442, |a|<=3.5536 and effective
target magnitude <=1.4356. Those bounds do not express precision rest.

It also proves a positive-width sufficient true-state sub-band: every allowed

modules. Wider speed .03 and acceleration 1.4 guards have explicit initial
