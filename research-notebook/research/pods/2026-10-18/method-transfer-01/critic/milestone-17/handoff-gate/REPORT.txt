Clock, measurements and causality

The first qualifying m_k is sampled at sigma and acts at sigma+h. Local
hold sample j is at sigma+j*h=(k+j)*h and arrives at (k+j+1)*h. It is the
same clock, not a newly sampled value at dispatch. Incoming digital age
is at most 2h=.001; outgoing age at a dispatch is h=.0005.

Keep the original readings m_j for j<=k. At the simultaneous next sample
and switch, time sigma+h, choose ONE explicit convention. Either generate
the local m_1 from the shared state plus its chosen future error, or require
it to equal the old m_(k+1) and fix that error accordingly. One cannot both
preserve an arbitrary old m_(k+1) and independently choose its new error.
