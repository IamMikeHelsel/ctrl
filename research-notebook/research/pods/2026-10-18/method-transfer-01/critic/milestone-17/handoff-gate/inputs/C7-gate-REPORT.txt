The phase formula is correct
For r>0 and fixed q=u+delta, E'= -r*E. The proposed formulas give
 a'= -r*(a0-q)*E=r*(q-a),
 v'= q+(a0-q)*E=a,
 x'= v0+q*s+(a0-q)*(1-E)/r=v.
At s=0, E=1 and the state is exactly (x0,v0,a0). The target error is added
AFTER clipping: u=clip(command(c,m_old)), q=u+delta. Replacing this with
clip(command+delta) changes the model.

Let z0=g(0), z1=g(h). For n>=1, define
 m_(n-1)=z_(n-1)+e_(n-1),
 z_(n+1)=Flow(r_n, clip(command(c,m_(n-1)))+delta_n, z_n, h).
A recurrence on the pair (z_(n-1),z_n) is explicit. No unknown future state
occurs on its right side. The reference c is chosen once from m_0. Clipping
and the exponential phase are defined for every such input, so no invariant,
smallness assumption or C5 safety conclusion is needed to define the next
state. C5 is applied only after the execution has been constructed.

through each candidate time sigma. If that comparison covers the whole
braking prefix, it protects both early and late first hits. C5 then protects
[sigma,sigma+h] and the subsequent hold; an extra first-zero case split is

For a supplied hybrid execution, a no-hit contradiction can prove a hit

  until the first qualifying reading ARRIVES, with incoming/outgoing sides
