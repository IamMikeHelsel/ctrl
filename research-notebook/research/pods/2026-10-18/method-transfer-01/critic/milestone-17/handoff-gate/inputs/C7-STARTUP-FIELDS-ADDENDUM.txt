C7 STARTUP AUXILIARY FIELDS — ADDENDUM
18 October 2026

C5 Execution.causal requires nonnegative stamps, including during pending
startup. Fill that interval's unused measurement fields with the actual initial
reading m0 and stamp zero. The pending command is independently specified as
-1.21 and does not use these fields. These fields do not identify the physical
timestamp of an earlier queued command. No old queued reading is renewed.

This clarifies the frozen report's startup convention. It does not change its
finite-continuation verdict or accept the separate hybrid-liveness question.
The original FINAL-manifest.json and its 16 inputs remain byte-for-byte intact.
