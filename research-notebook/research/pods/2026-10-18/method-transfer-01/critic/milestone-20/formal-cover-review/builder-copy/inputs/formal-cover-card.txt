Required semantic statement
Work over rational numbers. Set I=2/47 and R=251/10000. For every
1/2 <= c <= 1 and 0 <= j <= I, prove at least one of:
  U: c^2*j <= R;
  Q: c >= 3/4 and completion(c)+I-j <= R;
  M: c < 1 and there is one rational L with A(c,j,L)<=0 and H(c,j,L)<=0.
Here x=1-1/(2c), y=1/(2c), m=4cj, b=y-1/2, K=4R+1,
completion(c) = 3x^2/2-5x/4+9/32 for c<=4/5,
                x(1/2-x)/2 for c>=4/5.
The two formulas agree at 4/5. Let
L0=8R(y-m)-2m, C0=j-4R(y-m)^2-m^2,
A=C0+(L-L0)^2/(4K), H=(((j-R)/b-L)^2)/4+L*m.
M uses one L for all vertices of the selected graph. Do not let L depend
on the vertex degree. Denominators must be proved positive on this branch.

Middle rectangle
Use the exact existing 1743-node tree on c in[767/1000,22/25], j in[0,I].
It has 872 leaves: 53 uniform, 250 completion, 569 moment. The moment menu
has theta=0 or 1 and L=L0+theta*(L1-L0), L1=(j-R)/b-2m.
The evaluator encloses A=C0+theta^2*(L1-L0)^2/(4K) and
H=Hmin+(1-theta)^2*(L1-L0)^2/4, Hmin=((j-R)/b)*m-m^2.
Prove these equal the semantic A and H above.

Every accepted leaf must imply its semantic disjunct for every point in
that closed cell. Prove add, subtract, multiply, square and positive
reciprocal enclosure. A closed Boolean result alone is insufficient.
The completion leaves use completion(c_left)+I-j_left. They require the
decrease of completion(c) on [3/4,1], with its branch join handled.
Use a structurally recursive tree or a well-founded indexed tree with
explicit child boxes. Prove both children cover every point in the parent,
including the split boundary. Total area and leaf count are not coverage.
The generator may be untrusted if the kernel checks all generated data.
