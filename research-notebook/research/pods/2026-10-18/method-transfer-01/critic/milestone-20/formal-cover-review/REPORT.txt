Accepted statement

the theorem gives at least one of:
- c^2*j<=R;
- c>=3/4 and completion(c)+2/47-j<=R;
- c<1 and ONE rational L with both unchanged E9 low/high certificates
  at y=1/(2c), j, R, L nonpositive.
completion(c)=Q(1-1/(2c)); Q uses the two prior completion polynomials
joined at x=3/8 with common value 3/128. The endpoint has exactly the four
parameter-bound premises. It has no supplied certificate, cover-validity,
graph-cost, regularity or external BCL premise. The selected L can depend
on c,j but is common to both certificates and all later vertices.
