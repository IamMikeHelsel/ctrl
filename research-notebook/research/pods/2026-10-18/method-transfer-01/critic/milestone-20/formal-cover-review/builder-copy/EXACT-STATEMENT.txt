E10 UNIVERSAL RATIONAL SELECTOR

All variables and constants are rational. Set R=251/10000 and I=2/47.
For every c,j with 1/2<=c<=1 and 0<=j<=I, the theorem
  E10.universal_selector
proves at least one of the following three statements:

U: c^2*j<=R.
Q: 3/4<=c and completion(c)+I-j<=R.
M: c<1 and there exists ONE rational L such that
  Erdos128.FiniteProfileBridge.lowCertificate (1/(2*c)) j R L <= 0,
  Erdos128.FiniteProfileBridge.highCertificate (1/(2*c)) j R L <= 0.

The M definitions are the unchanged accepted E9 definitions:
  y=1/(2c), mu=2j/y,
  C=j-4R(y-mu)^2-mu^2,
  L0=8R(y-mu)-2mu,
  A=C+(L-L0)^2/[4(4R+1)],
  H=[(j-R)/(y-1/2)-L]^2/4+L*mu.
The selected L can depend on c,j but is a single value for the point.
There is no vertex-dependent slope and no hidden graph regularity premise.

Put x(c)=1-1/(2c). The completion function is
  3*x^2/2-5*x/4+9/32 when x<=3/8,
  x*(1/2-x)/2 otherwise.
The Q disjunct explicitly includes c>=3/4. Both pieces equal 3/128 at x=3/8.

The endpoint has only the four parameter-bound hypotheses. It has no
certificate-validity, tree-validity, interval-bound, selected-slope or graph
cost premise. R and I are fixed definitions, not variables in this endpoint.
