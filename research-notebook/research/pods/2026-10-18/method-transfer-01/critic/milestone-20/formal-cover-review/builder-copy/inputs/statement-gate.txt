Semantic endpoint

[1/2,1] x [0,2/47], with fixed R=251/10000, and return U or Q or M exactly as
in the card. Its only variable hypotheses are those parameter bounds. A

so a rational selector is sufficient for the intended later graph call.

M has one rational slope L for the parameter pair (c,j). Each leaf may choose
theta 0 or 1, while L0,L1 and thus L vary with the actual point within that
leaf. There is no need for one numerical L across an entire cell. There must
be one common L across the vertices when that parameter pair is later used
in a graph. A degree-dependent slope does not cancel in expectation: equally
weighted d=1/4,3/4 have mean 1/2, but slopes -1,+1 give mean
L(d)*(d-1/2)=1/4>0. This is a cancellation counter-control, not a graph example.

when L=L0+theta*(L1-L0). These are the card's semantic A and H. The later

Completion monotonicity and join
Let x(c)=1-1/(2c). It increases for c>0 because
 x(c2)-x(c1)=(c2-c1)/(2*c1*c2).
For c in [3/4,1], x is in [1/3,1/2]. On the lower branch, for x1<=x2<=3/8,
 Qlow(x1)-Qlow(x2)=(x2-x1)*(5/4-3*(x1+x2)/2)>=0.
On the upper branch, for 3/8<=x1<=x2,
 Qhigh(x1)-Qhigh(x2)=(x2-x1)*(x1+x2-1/2)/2>=0.
Both values at x=3/8 are 3/128. Splitting a crossing interval at that point
therefore proves the full decreasing property, with equality at the join.
The root card's c=4/5 join is correct. Existing attributed
completionEnvelope_antitone in PiecewiseCompletion.lean expresses the same
fact, with t=1/2. Reuse it with its actual premises or prove the small scalar
lemma directly. A derivative calculation over floating-point samples is not
needed and would not replace this rational argument.

Required interval and coverage proof
Use an interval-membership invariant with ordered endpoints. Prove addition,
negation/subtraction, endpoint-product multiplication, square enclosure and
positive reciprocal enclosure. In particular, the square of an interval
that straddles zero has lower endpoint zero: using only min(lo^2,hi^2)
would give the false lower bound 1 for [-1,2]. Reciprocal reverses the positive
endpoint order. Prove composition of these operations into the exact A/H
expressions. Closed Boolean evaluation must then imply the semantic U/Q/M
property for EVERY rational point in that leaf.
