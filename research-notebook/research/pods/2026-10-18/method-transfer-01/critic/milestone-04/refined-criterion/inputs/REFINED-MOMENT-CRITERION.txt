REFINED MOMENT CRITERION — PROPOSAL FOR INDEPENDENT REVIEW
18 October 2026

The E1 graph construction and double-counting identities stay unchanged.
For its piecewise function phi, mean degree mu, and constants L,C,B,S:

  low branch:  phi(d) = C + L(d-mu) - (4R+1)(d-mu)^2;
  high branch: phi(d) = C+S + L(d-mu) - (d-B/2)^2.

If R >= -1/4, C <= 0 and C+S <= 0, both branches satisfy
phi(d) <= L(d-mu). Their weighted expectation is therefore at most zero.
If every feasible neighborhood profile had cost greater than R, E1's exact
graph moments would imply E[phi(d)] > 0. This is a contradiction. Thus one
profile has cost at most R. Strict negativity of either bound is unnecessary.

E1 used the stronger sufficient condition C<0 and S<=0. Its accepted proof
remains valid. The proposed change can admit S>0 when C offsets it. It does
not repair the failed claim that the higher branch lies below C+L(d-mu).
It uses the higher affine line L(d-mu) instead.

This is an elementary refinement of the existing certificate. Its graph
consequence still depends on E1's assumptions and full parameter coverage.
A sampled scalar screen is neither coverage nor a graph counterexample.
No universal constant, Lean endpoint, or novelty claim is made here.
