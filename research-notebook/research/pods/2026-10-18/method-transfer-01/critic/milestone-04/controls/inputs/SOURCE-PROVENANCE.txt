Our reconstruction
Negative weighted radius is a finite affine minimum. We implement its
finite-entry consequence with six moving signed faces and the existing
DigitalHold.finite_prefix_barrier. That uses left derivatives at first contact,
rather than importing the source's right-minimum theorem. Our own exact robust
face inequalities discharge the drift requirements, including dispatch times.
The family source guided the proof target; generic weighted polyhedral
Lyapunov/comparison mathematics is older standard work. Neither a new method
nor novelty of this controls corollary is claimed.
