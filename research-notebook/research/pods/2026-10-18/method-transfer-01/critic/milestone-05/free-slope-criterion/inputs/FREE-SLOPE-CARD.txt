Objects and sufficient certificate
Use the accepted E4 graph/moment setup without alteration. Let R>0,
y=1/(2c)>.5, b=y-.5, mu=2j/y, K=4R+1, a=(j-R)/b.
Under the opposing assumption that every neighborhood profile costs >R,
the two branch functions phi satisfy E[phi(d)]>0, and E[d]=mu.

For ANY real L, complete squares:
 phi_low(d) = A(L)+L(d-mu)-K*(d-(8R*y-L)/(2K))^2,
 phi_high(d)= H(L)+L(d-mu)-(d-(a-L)/2)^2,
 A(L)=C+(L-L0)^2/(4K),
 H(L)=(a-L)^2/4+L*mu,
 L0=8R(y-mu)-2mu,
 C=j-4R(y-mu)^2-mu^2.
Thus A(L)<=0 and H(L)<=0 imply E[phi(d)]<=0, a contradiction.
These are global quadratic bounds; applying them on smaller branch domains
is conservative. Do not claim that failure proves the graph conclusion false.
All masses, triangle-free hypotheses, profile endpoints, rounding/parity,
BCL transport and exterior cases remain the E4 obligations.

Proposal search

For L=L0+theta*(L1-L0), theta in[0,1],
