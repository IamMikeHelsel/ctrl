Argument checked
Use R>0, y>1/2, b=y-1/2, mu=2j/y, K=4R+1>0 and a=(j-R)/b.
Let L0=8R(y-mu)-2mu and C=j-4R(y-mu)^2-mu^2.
For the low branch phi=j-4R(y-d)^2-d^2, and for the high branch
phi=a*d-d^2. Exact completion of squares gives the two identities on the
root card. check_identities.py expands their denominator-cleared forms in
Q[R,y,j,mu,d,L,a,theta]. It also checks both interpolation identities.
These are generic polynomial identities, not evaluations on a grid.

For any L with A(L)<=0 and H(L)<=0, nonnegative squares imply
phi(d)<=L(d-mu) on both branches. The E4 graph setup has E[d]=mu and
E[s]=E[d^2], with positive finite vertex weights. If every neighborhood
profile cost exceeds R, then s is strictly below its branch threshold at
every vertex. Therefore E[phi]>0. Taking expectation of the affine bound
gives E[phi]<=L(E[d]-mu)=0, a contradiction. The low branch includes d=0;
the two formulas agree at d=b. No distribution optimization, strong duality,
interior moment, indicator objective or SDP assumption enters this argument.
Triangle-free neighborhood independence remains required for the costs.

Set L1=a-2mu and D2=(L1-L0)^2. For theta in [0,1],
A=C+theta^2*D2/(4K), H=a*mu-mu^2+(1-theta)^2*D2/4.
Thus A is nondecreasing and H is nonincreasing on this interval, including
the constant case D2=0. A proposed rational theta must pass both exact tests.
These monotonicities alone do not prove a parameter cover.
