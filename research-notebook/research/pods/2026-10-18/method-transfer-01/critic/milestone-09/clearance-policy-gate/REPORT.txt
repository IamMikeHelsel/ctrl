Interval formula
For fixed radius k, each coordinate imposes
  ai+Ei-wi*k <= c <= ai-Ei+wi*k.
The intersection is nonempty exactly when every lower endpoint is at most
every upper endpoint. Since wi+wj>0, these conditions are precisely
  k >= (ai-aj+Ei+Ej)/(wi+wj), for all ordered pairs i,j.
Including the target floor gives the stated K. This proves feasibility and
the least radius for this specified coordinate-interval enclosure. Choosing
c as the maximum lower endpoint gives the least allowed center at that K.
All pairs include i=j. The conclusion is |ai-c|+Ei<=wi*K, not an assumption.
This box can overapproximate correlated physical errors; optimality for this
box does not mean a physically optimal controller or least trajectory tube.
