review directory into a new location inside the same workspace. Its ancestors
must include the pinned lean-controls directory. All paths below are relative
to the new review copy. No installation, network access or pin edit is needed.

   earlier freezes, copied inputs and pins; writes only preservation.json in

5. Run python3 check_preservation.py again. The final manifest binds main and
