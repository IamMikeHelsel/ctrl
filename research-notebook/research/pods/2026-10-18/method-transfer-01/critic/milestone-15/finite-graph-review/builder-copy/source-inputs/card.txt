Concrete statement
Take nonempty finite triangle-free G and an oriented cut C. Put
 n=|V|, k=|Y|, y=k/n, j=e(Y)/n^2, m=2j/y, b=y-1/2.
Require y>1/2 and R>=0. For an ambient affine slope L define
 K=4R+1, C0=j-4R(y-m)^2-m^2, L0=8R(y-m)-2m,
 A(L)=C0+(L-L0)^2/(4K),
 H(L)=((j-R)/b-L)^2/4+L*m.
If A(L)<=0 and H(L)<=0, conclude
 HasNormalizedFractionalHalfBoundQ G R.
These are explicit arithmetic certificate inputs. No profile feasibility,
moment identity or graph objective bound may be a premise.
The cut's BCL budget may remain in its structure; no maximum-cut or global
edge-density hypothesis is needed for this bridge.

E8's positive alpha<1 and b_normalized=1-alpha follow from y>1/2.
The completed-square identities give both required pointwise affine bounds:

Here ambient_phi_low=j-4R(y-d)^2-d^2 and
ambient_phi_high=d(j-R)/b-d^2. Each ambient difference is y^2 times
its normalized E8 difference. Nonnegative squares and positive denominators
