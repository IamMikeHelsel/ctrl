E9 SOURCE ATTRIBUTION AND EXTRACTION

Upstream repository: https://github.com/aimir/erdos-128-sparse-halves
Revision: ba85f319ebb33ec2194ef4c2929a12916329e1e7, as pinned by root and E3.
Seven upstream modules are copied without change: GraphBasics, GraphRational,
ExternalInputs, LowDensityGraph, WeightedGraph, CubicNeighborhood and
OneStepSaving. Their imports and hashes are recorded in INPUTS.json and the
fresh verification record. E8's accepted WeightedProfiles.lean is copied
without change and bound to its frozen manifest.

The original LowDensityConstruction.lean is also preserved in full. Its
needed zero-extension helpers are extracted into CutHelpers.lean. Exact
inclusive source ranges, exported private helpers and the compatibility
repair are listed in extraction.json. CutHelpers.patch is the complete
diff from the original file to the extracted module. Namespaces remain
Erdos128 and Erdos128.OrientedBCLCut. The new module imports only
LowDensityGraph and WeightedGraph. It omits low-density calculus, the
small-side-one construction, cross-potential optimization and the unrelated
global-density theorem. No hypothesis was weakened in a selected helper.
Because it retains those names, later integration must select this helper
module or the original full LowDensityConstruction module, not import both.

One selected cardinality proof needed an explicit congruence step for finite
subtype instances under Lean 4.35. Its statement is unchanged. This helper
module is an attributed extraction and compatibility port, not an unchanged
replay of the full LowDensityConstruction module. All failed attempts remain.

The upstream pin declares Lean 4.30.0 and mathlib
c5ea00351c28e24afc9f0f84379aa41082b1188f. The executed existing pin is
Lean 4.35.0 and mathlib 56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679.
Both pin sets are preserved. No package or toolchain is installed or changed.
The final replay rebuilds every local module in its closure from a fresh
source copy. Existing cached mathlib dependency products are the recorded
library boundary; their complete source tree is not rebuilt here.

ExternalInputs defines explicit propositions for BCL and C4. It introduces
no axiom. The C4 and global-density inputs are not premises of E9. The
existing cut structure carries a BCL budget, but the E9 construction uses
its finite side and orientation, not that numerical bound. It does not
prove that every graph has such a cut.

The neighborhood construction and graph-to-weighted API are attributed
existing work. E9 adds the stated exact square transport and finite-graph
wrapper for the accepted E8 criterion. This packet makes no claim of a new
construction, new numerical constant or best-known graph bound.
