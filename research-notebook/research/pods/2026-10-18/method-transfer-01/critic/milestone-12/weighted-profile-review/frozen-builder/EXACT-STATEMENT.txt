E8: RATIONAL WEIGHTED GRAPH TO ACTUAL PROFILE

The Lean endpoint is Erdos128.ProfileBridge.exists_affine_profile in
WeightedProfiles.lean. Audit.lean prints its full elaborated statement and
the definitions below. All numbers and profile values are rational.

Let V be a finite type and H a simple graph on V. Let W assign strictly
positive weights w_v with sum_v w_v=1. Assume H is triangle-free.
Define from the graph, rather than from input moment data,
  d_v = sum_{u adjacent v} w_u,
  s_v = sum_{u adjacent v} w_u*d_u,
  mu  = (1/2)*sum_v w_v*d_v,
  cost(pi) = (1/2)*sum_v sum_{u adjacent v} w_v*w_u*pi_v*pi_u.
Thus each unordered edge is counted once. The linear potential is zero.

Let alpha,T,L be rational and assume 0<alpha<1. Set b=1-alpha.
The only scalar certificate assumptions are
  mu-(T/alpha^2)*(1-d)^2-d^2 <= L*(d-2*mu), for every 0<=d<=b;
  d*(mu-T)/b-d^2 <= L*(d-2*mu), for every b<d<1.
Then there is a rational profile pi with
  0<=pi_v<=1 for every v,
  sum_v w_v*pi_v=alpha,
  cost(pi)<=T.
There is no hidden regularity, cut-optimality, profile-cost, moment-identity,
or graph-realizability assumption. T and L have no additional sign premise.

Derived graph facts
  E[d]=2*mu and E[s]=E[d^2].
The second identity follows by swapping symmetric adjacent sums and needs
no triangle-free premise. Looplessness and positive weights give 0<=d_v<1.
The normalization implies that V is nonempty.

Actual profiles and exact costs
For each root v, if d_v<=b, use pi=0 on N(v) and alpha/(1-d_v) elsewhere.
Its mean is alpha and its cost is alpha^2*(mu-s_v)/(1-d_v)^2.
If d_v>b, use pi=1-b/d_v on N(v) and 1 elsewhere. Its mean is alpha and
its cost is mu-b*s_v/d_v. The proof derives both costs by expanding the
actual edge sum. Triangle-freeness makes N(v) independent.

The low branch includes d_v=0 and d_v=b. Its denominator is positive.
The high branch has d_v>b>0. At d_v=b, both profile definitions agree.
The proof uses the low branch at equality; it does not divide by zero.

Contradiction argument
If every candidate cost exceeds T, each root satisfies
  s_v-d_v^2 < L*(d_v-2*mu).
Positive weighted summation gives a strict inequality. Both sums equal
zero by the two graph moment identities. This proves the endpoint.
The source proves capacities, exact means, exact costs and the averaging
step; none of these are replaced by premises.

Scope
This is a graph-derived rational profile of prescribed mass alpha. It is
not an integral half theorem. Alpha=0 and alpha=1 are outside this statement.
No BCL input or cubic-saving inequality is needed by this lemma. The full
.0251 graph endpoint, cut-side normalization and rounding remain separate.
