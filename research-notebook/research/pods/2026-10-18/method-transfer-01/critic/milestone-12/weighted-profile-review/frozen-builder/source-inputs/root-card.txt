E8 CANDIDATE: ACTUAL GRAPH PROFILES FOR THE ACCEPTED AFFINE CRITERION
Root proposal. Not assigned; reconsider after E7 review.

Purpose
E5 has a written conditional graph proof and exact scalar cover, but no new
Lean graph endpoint. The most useful formal step is a graph-to-profile lemma.
It must construct an actual feasible profile and derive its graph moments.
Another scalar inequality wrapper would not close this gap.

Source objects
Use the pinned attributed RatProbabilityWeight, weightedDegree,
weightedSecondDegree, edgeMass and weightedObjective API rebuilt in E3.
Keep upstream sources byte-for-byte; put new lemmas in a separate module.
For a finite triangle-free H with positive probability weights W, write
delta(v)=weightedDegree, s(v)=weightedSecondDegree, mu=edgeMass.
The required identities are E[delta]=2mu and E[s]=E[delta^2]. They must follow
from the actual symmetric adjacency sums, not be supplied as assumptions.

Proposed theorem, normalized on the larger cut side
Given rational 0<alpha<1, target T and an affine slope L, put b=1-alpha.
Define
 phi_low(d)=mu-(T/alpha^2)*(1-d)^2-d^2;
 phi_high(d)=d*(mu-T)/b-d^2.
If phi_low(d)<=L*(d-2mu) on0<=d<=b and phi_high(d)<=L*(d-2mu)
onb<d<1, prove existence of a unit profile pi with weightedMean W pi=alpha
and weightedObjective H W 0 pi<=T. The explicit pointwise inequalities are
arithmetic certificate inputs. They are not assumed graph feasibility,
assumed profile cost, or assumed moment identities. State this boundary.

Actual candidates and graph obligations
For v with delta<=b, set pi=0 on N(v) and alpha/(1-delta) elsewhere.
For delta>b, set pi=1-b/delta on N(v) and1 elsewhere.
Prove capacities, exact mean and exact objective for these profiles. Their
costs are alpha^2*(mu-s)/(1-delta)^2 and mu-b*s/delta, respectively.
Triangle-freeness makes N(v) independent. Positivity of denominators follows
from alpha>0, b>0, and weightedDegree<1. Include zero degree and d=b.
If every candidate cost exceeds T, each phi(delta)>s-delta^2. Averaging gives
E[phi]>0, which contradicts the affine upper bound and the two exact moment
identities. Positive weights and a nonempty probability space are essential
for the strict average step.

Connection to E5, still a separate obligation
For large-side mass y, alpha=1/(2y), j=y^2*mu, and T=R/y^2.
Extending pi by zero on the smaller cut side gives a fractional half.
The explicit two-quadratic complete-square criterion must instantiate the
pointwise inequalities. Then connect exact cover, BCL, rounding and blowup.
Do not call the weighted lemma the final .0251 graph endpoint.

Nonvacuity preflight

Then b=1/5. The high-branch difference phi-L*(d-2mu) is -(d-1/2)^2.
The low difference is(-15+62d-79d^2)/64. It increases on[0,1/5] and

Draft correction before assignment
The first draft accidentally reversed the strict inequality after assuming
that every profile cost exceeds T. It was corrected to phi>s-delta^2 before
dispatch or acceptance. The derivation must be audited from the cost formula;
the corrected contradiction is E[phi]>0 versus the affine bound E[phi]<=0.

Independent statement-preflight clarifications
Use rational T and L in the existing rational API, or state every cast.
For the E5 object map, L_normalized=L_ambient/y, since ambient phi is
 y^2*normalized_phi and ambient(d-mean)=y*(normalized_d-2mu).
The balanced y=1/2 case gives alpha=1 and is outside this lemma. Keep the
separate completion/uniform endpoint from E5; do not force it through a
positive-b denominator. Symmetric adjacency supplies the moment identity;
triangle-freeness supplies the candidate objective formulas.
