E8 STATEMENT PREFLIGHT — NOT A FORMAL BRIDGE ACCEPTANCE
18 October 2026. Independent critic.

Verdict: the proposed weighted-profile theorem is sound as a builder target.
The graph moment identities and candidate costs must be derived in Lean.
Pointwise scalar inequalities may be inputs. Profile feasibility, cost and
moment identities may not be substituted as input assumptions.

For positive probability weights, looplessness gives 0<=d(v)<1 and the
probability identity implies a nonempty vertex type. E[d]=2mu is the API
normalization. Swapping symmetric adjacent sums proves E[s]=E[d^2]; this
identity does not need triangle-freeness. Triangle-freeness instead makes
N(v) independent. Its incident edge mass is then exactly s(v).

For d<=b=1-alpha, the profile zero on N(v), alpha/(1-d) elsewhere has
mean alpha, capacities in [0,1], and cost alpha^2(mu-s)/(1-d)^2.
For d>b, the profile 1-b/d on N(v), one elsewhere has mean alpha and
cost mu-b*s/d. The denominators are positive in their respective branches.
At d=0 only the first formula is needed. At d=b both profiles and costs
agree; the stated first-branch convention includes this boundary.

If a low cost exceeds T, mu-(T/alpha^2)(1-d)^2 > s. If a high cost
exceeds T, d(mu-T)/b > s. Thus the corrected direction is phi(d)>s-d^2
in either branch. Positive weighted averaging gives E[phi]>0. The COMMON
slope bound gives E[phi]<=L(E[d]-2mu)=0. This is weak averaging only;
no strong duality or optimal slope claim is needed. Strict positivity of
all weights is sufficient; it need not be advertised as a logically minimal
hypothesis for every alternative formulation.

The rational API requires alpha,T,L in Q, unless explicit coercions are
introduced. Under the E5 change of scale, ambient D=y*d, S=y^2*s,
j=y^2*mu, alpha=1/(2y), T=R/y^2 and B=y-1/2=y*b. Each ambient phi
is y^2 times normalized phi. The affine slope must also change:
L_normalized=L_ambient/y. Its centered mean is D-2j/y. The endpoint
y=1/2 gives alpha=1 and is outside the proposed strict-alpha lemma; keep
its separate endpoint argument. Extending the actual profile by zero yields
mass 1/2 and ambient cost y^2 times the normalized objective. The graph
endpoint still needs the cover, BCL, all-order transfer and floor rounding.

The K2 nonzero sanity case checks exactly. With weights 1/2, alpha=4/5,
mu=1/4, T=3/20 and L=-1/2, the high difference is -(d-1/2)^2.
The low difference is (-15+62d-79d^2)/64; its derivative is positive
on [0,1/5] and its maximum is -9/100. Profile (1,3/5) has mean 4/5
and cost 3/20. This is a useful future formal witness, not proof of the
universal Lean statement. The draft sign error is explicitly disclosed
