Root exact arithmetic preflight only. Not assigned or independently accepted.

Why test this
C3 chooses c=measured_x+measured_v/4+measured_a/32. This balances two transformed
coordinates, but does not directly minimize forward travel. Its nominal full
cube reaches156.4625mm. A reference chosen for a symmetric norm need not fit
an asymmetric travel limit. Changing c changes the commanded physical target.

Fixed algebraic test
Use alpha=1/28 instead of1/32 in c=m_x+m_v/4+alpha*m_a. Test acceleration
guard1.27 instead of1.25. Keep speed guard.02, errors(.001,.01,.05), delay.5ms,
total age1ms, pending-1.21, rates[8,12], target error.01 and old radius.055525.
check.py derives a pending-state enclosure from the widened guard:
A=1.32,V=.03066,J=30.48. Exact corners still bound pending feedback slope
by8. The sensor/startup coordinate budgets with alpha1/28 fit the old cube.
These are algebraic checks, not a changed Lean execution theorem.

More useful comparison to ask for
Keep the old cube to derive history error. Then allow the moving weighted
barrier to start from the actual measured startup enclosure, rather than the
single worst-case radius R. Its proposed later radius is at least.045, with
the same weights(1.02,1.01,1). This needs a new variable-initial-radius proof;
the current C4 fixed-R conclusion does not supply it automatically.

At the old nominal reading(.14,-.01,-1.17), this proposed comparison gives
upper position .141630488854314m for alpha1/28. The same proposed comparison
with alpha1/32 gives .1514125366m. Both use the same widened sensor/guard
enclosures. No new integration was run. Neither number is yet a trajectory
certificate or a comparison of simulated controller performance.

Important negative
At measured_x=.143, measured_v=.02, measured_a=-1.1, the alpha1/28 proposed
upper envelope is .1546142857142857m. Thus this center change alone still
does not guarantee150mm across the declared larger measurement rectangle.
Do not select the favorable nominal row and call the recovery task solved.
Translation also prevents any absolute ceiling without a position premise.

Possible next gate, not current authorization to build
After C4 review, decide between a proved measured clearance guard, a task-aware
center selected from the feasible interval, or a less conservative invariant.
A clearance guard must be derived and its reachability checked separately.
The widened acceleration guard could remove the1.20versus1.22 mismatch in
the proposed robust detector band, but it does not prove sampled detector
liveness or hybrid continuation. Preserve old C3/C4 sources and commands.

Value and novelty
This is a concrete task-design issue: choose a safe target that can actually
be admitted, then prove the same trajectory remains within its travel budget.
It resembles established reference-governor and invariant-set work. No new
control principle, novelty claim, hardware result or completed composition is
established by this arithmetic screen. Do not spend on a new optimizer yet.
