CAN ANOTHER CENTER IMPROVE THE SAME C5 CERTIFICATE?
18 October2026. Root mathematical deduction, awaiting independent review.
Not a new controls principle, hardware result or trajectory optimum.

Fixed comparison class
For one reading m, retain C5's offsets A_i, interval errors e_i,
weights w=(1.02,1.01,1), radius floor K0=.045 and forward multiplier W=1.02.
Allow ANY center c and radius K with K>=K0 and
 |A_i-c|+e_i <= w_i*K for every i.
Compare the identical enclosure formula U=c+W*K. This is a comparison
of certified bounds, not a comparison of actual physical trajectories.

Candidate and source
ClearancePolicy defines
 K*=max(K0, max_(i,j) (A_i-A_j+e_i+e_j)/(w_i+w_j)),
 c*=max_i(A_i+e_i-w_i*K*).
Its accepted feasible theorem proves all three inequalities at(c*,K*).
The actual source has the ordered pairs, including i=j; all w_i>0.
It chooses the left endpoint of the common interval, not its midpoint.

Direct proof of restricted optimality
Any feasible(c,K) satisfies
 c>=A_i+e_i-w_i*K and c<=A_j-e_j+w_j*K.
Subtract to get (w_i+w_j)K>=A_i-A_j+e_i+e_j for every ordered pair.
Together with K>=K0, this implies K>=K*.
For each i, the first inequality gives
 c+W*K >= A_i+e_i+(W-w_i)K.
Because W>=w_i and K>=K*, this is at least
 A_i+e_i+(W-w_i)K*.
Take the maximum over i. The result is c+W*K>=c*+W*K*.
Thus the accepted candidate attains the least possible forward enclosure
within this fixed comparison class, for every reading m. No guard is needed
for this algebra. The guard is needed for C5's stated uniform clearance.

Implication if independently accepted
Changing only the chosen center or allowing a larger feasible radius cannot
improve this certificate. At the worst accepted rectangular guard corner,
the exact existing C5 corner value gives the matching lower bound. This
complements the earlier finding that tightening only acceleration's upper
bound retains that worst corner.
To improve the certificate, at least one part of this class must change:
the retained state/error constraints, weights, radius floor, enclosure form
or control contract. Joint braking information might exclude the corner,
but its physical reachability is still unproved. This calculation does not
show that such changes work or that no different controller can improve.

Falsifiers
The sign W>=w_i is essential. With one coordinate A=e=0,w=2,W=1,K0=1,
the minimum-radius left endpoint gives K=1,c=-2,U=-1. The also-feasible
K=2,c=-4 gives U=-2, which is smaller. Thus the argument cannot be applied
with an arbitrary enclosure multiplier. A midpoint choice also need not
minimize the upper bound. Keep the actual C5 definitions as source inputs.

Value and stopping decision
This is a small analytic limit on a fixed certificate. It may stop wasted
center-only optimization and clarify a fair comparison. It does not raise
the current work to a novel control method. No new Lean build is requested
just to count another theorem. Independent reading and exact numerical
controls are sufficient for the stated written, conditional deduction.
