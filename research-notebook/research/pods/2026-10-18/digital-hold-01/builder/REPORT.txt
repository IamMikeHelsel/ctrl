Dispatch witness
DispatchWitness constructs an exact two-cell trajectory on [0,.001] s.
The first command and state are zero. At.0005 s the new reading is
(-.000125 m,0 m/s,0 m/s^2), sampled from the still-zero physical state.
Its legal position error makes the command jump from0 to.001 m/s^2.
Rate is8/s and physical target error is zero. The later state is the exact
exponential lag flow. The source proves the sampled predicate, initial cube,
continuous state, positive terminal acceleration, and the command jump.
At dispatch, acceleration derivative is0 from the left and.008 m/s^3 from the
right. It also proves that acceleration is NOT classically differentiable at
that dispatch. Thus the witness exercises the boundary the old global
classical trajectory predicate would exclude. It is not a simulation.
