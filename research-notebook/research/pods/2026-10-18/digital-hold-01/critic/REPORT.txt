Exact accepted statement and assumptions
sampled_hold takes H>=0, one physical state f(t), an IsSampledExecution, and
initial membership of the transformed state in [-R,R]^3, R=2221/40000 m.
Its result includes the first fresh reading, cube containment at every real
time in [0,H], and the physical and command bounds below. The predicate has:
- A continuous state f=(x,v,a), with x'=v, v'=a and
  a'=r*(clip(k(read))+d-a). Clipping to [-2,2] precedes d.
- Right derivatives on [0,H) and left derivatives on (0,H]. The rate and
  physical error have left/right channels. All rate values lie in [8,12]/s;
  every physical error has magnitude at most .01 m/s^2.
- Causal samples: 0<=sample<=current time and total age<=.001 s. Reading errors
  from the physical state at that timestamp are at most .001 m, .01 m/s and
  .05 m/s^2. These are past-state errors, not errors from the current state.
- Strictly increasing, locally finite dispatches. First sample and dispatch
  are zero. Both initial indices are zero. Each sample precedes its dispatch.
  The right reading is held on [dispatch_n,dispatch_(n+1)); the left reading
  is held on (dispatch_n,dispatch_(n+1)]. Unique cell selection is proved.
- A single fixed reference c and the unchanged k=-8(x-c)-6v-a/2.

The proof derives |x-c|<=.055525 m, |v|<=.4442 m/s and |a|<=3.5536 m/s^2.
It derives raw measured-command magnitude <=1.4256 m/s^2, proves clipping
inactive, and derives effective target magnitude <=1.4356 m/s^2. The velocity
and acceleration bands are coarse containment bounds, not precision standstill.

Noncircularity and boundary review
There is no ongoing cube, aggregate mismatch or feedback-slope bound in the
exported Execution/IsSampledExecution premises. Only the initial cube is assumed.
On a strict first-contact prefix, causal samples lie in the already bounded
past. The proof first obtains finite mismatch <=48K+.103. It then applies the
right-derivative age estimate twice. The final prefix mismatch is
  .103621708+.098304K.
At K=R this is exactly .1090800376 m/s^2. Its slack below2R is exactly
.0019699624 m/s^2. Independent Lean arithmetic checked both numbers.
The prefix premise is discharged by finite_prefix_barrier. That lemma uses the
left derivative at first contact. Expanding cubes are restricted to K<=1/16,
which makes clipping inactive before invariance has been proved. Their expansion
then tends to zero. No continuity of mismatch is used.

The proof uses right derivatives only on sample-to-current intervals contained
in the causal prefix. It uses the left channel at a possible contact, including
contacts at dispatches. No future state or reset is hidden in the argument.
Age equality and sample=dispatch are permitted. H=0 reduces to the initial state.
The right channel at H and left channel at0 are endpoint algebraic data; no ODE
is required there. This is explicit in the model note. It does not prove an
extension beyond H or consistency of separately supplied finite-horizon runs.

Nonvacuity review
The checked two-cell witness starts from zero state, uses r=8 and d=0, and runs
for1 ms. At.5 ms a legal position-reading error changes the command from0 to
.001 m/s^2. The exact exponential trajectory is continuous and has positive
terminal acceleration. At the update, acceleration derivative is0 from the
left and.008 m/s^3 from the right. The source proves that no classical derivative
exists there. Thus this execution class includes a real dispatch jump that the
old global classical trajectory model excludes.
I independently applied sampled_hold to this witness in CriticChecks.lean.
This witness does not prove existence for arbitrary initial states/signals.

Required scope qualifications
1. General existence remains outside Lean. The analytic cell construction in
   MODEL-AND-EXISTENCE.txt is sound as an informal argument for fixed causal
   clock sequences, a fixed bounded additive error sequence, and bounded
   piecewise-continuous rate/physical-error signals with the stated one-sided
   limits. Measurements must be generated from the constructed past state.
   Since formal Schedule also contains measurement values, 'fix a schedule'
   must not mean fixing arbitrary measurements independently of that relation.
   I do not count this text as checked existence or uniqueness evidence.
2. The first sample and first dispatch are both exactly at hold time zero.
   The prior delayed capture reading cannot initialize this theorem directly.
   Composition needs a fresh initial command at the same physical state or a
   new certified-history bridge. No whole-operation claim is accepted here.
3. Real arithmetic, sensor limits, physical error, response range and total age
   are assumptions. No finite-precision or scheduling implementation was bound
   to this predicate. No calibrated hardware data was supplied.
4. No matched baseline, conservatism study, novel controller, independent
   reuse result or publication merit follows from this verification. The
   progress reviewer should decide the next usefulness gate separately.
