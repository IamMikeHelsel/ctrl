AGE-DEPENDENT CERTIFICATE CONDITIONS
These are sufficient conditions for this proof method, not a necessary
condition for physical safety. AgeBudget proves the exact split and formula;
the fixed T1 values and all inflated radii are checked in Lean.

Let a>=d>=0 be age and startup-duration caps. Let R be the intermediate
cube radius, K in [R,1/16], N=.103, P=8, and target B=.045.
  W0=N+a*(384K+.618)
  S=96K+6W0
  W1=N+8d+S*(a-d)=c+bK
  c=N+8d+.618*(a-d)*(1+6a)
  b=(a-d)*(96+2304a).
The split requires 0<=S<=8. Since S increases with K, its upper check is
at K=1/16. Pending overlap is ell=min(t,D)-min(s,D), not an independently
allocated interval of length d. The replacement by d uses S<=8.

The unweighted first-contact proof needs W1<=2K for all K in [R,1/16].
When c>=0 and b<2, this is equivalent within this affine estimate to
R>=c/(2-b). The initial measured weighted box must lie inside R, and
24K+.093<=2 must hold for unclipping. Neither condition is omitted.

After that cube is derived, use
  Wfinal=N+8d+108R*(a-d).
For nonnegative rounded W>=Wfinal, the normalized worst weighted faces at
K=B give the entry constraints
  gamma <= .04B/1.02,
  gamma <= .04B/1.01,
  gamma <= 1.9B-.75W.
The all-K face proof is in AgeBudget.weighted_face. These equations do not
assert that every abstract face residual is produced by an actual sensor
history. A positive gamma is a progress bound; gamma=0 only gives enclosure.

At a=.0015,d=.0005 and W=.1132, the exact support-radius interval from these
two scalar budget checks is
  53811781/950272000 <= R <= 31/540,
before intersecting startup and clipping conditions. R=.0567 lies inside.
The lower endpoint is about .0566278 and the upper about .0574074.
The old .055525 radius lies outside this chosen proof envelope. The new
R is a simple rational interior choice, not a physically optimal radius.

The weights, policy K(m), center c(m) and startup D bound are unchanged.
Thus a larger support cube need not increase the final forward tube: that
tube follows from the smaller policy-weighted barrier after history closure.
No center retuning, wider sensor guard, command increase or plant-rate change
is used to obtain the 1.5 ms certificate. screen-results.json retains old
