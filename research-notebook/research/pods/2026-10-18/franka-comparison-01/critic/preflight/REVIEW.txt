Verdict

Timing findings
A fresh 1 ms stream with fixed 0.5 ms arrival delay has maximum age 1.5 ms.
At an incoming dispatch, the left command uses the old reading with age exactly
1.5 ms. The right command uses the next reading with age 0.5 ms. A dispatch does
not renew a reading timestamp. T and D must be separate parameters: the old
constructive clock tied both to 0.5 ms.

For K in [R,1/16], the proposed first refinement is

At old R=.055525, W1=.1131458564 exceeds 2R=.11105. This rejects the old
certificate, not physical safety. At R=.0567, W1=.1132627172 < .1134.
The margin is .0001372828. S<=6.839562<8 on the full inflated K range.
The chosen R is a simple sufficient repair, not an optimized plant limit.
result.json records the exact threshold for this particular affine budget.

The split-age argument needs actual pending overlap ell, actual age h, and
0<=S<=8, ell<=h<=a, ell<=d<=a. Its decisive identity is
 [8d+S(a-d)] - [8ell+S(h-ell)] = S(a-h)+(8-S)(d-ell).
Without S<=8 the replacement of actual overlap by its upper bound can fail.
The exact control S=9, ell=0, h=.0015, d=.0005 gives .0135 > .013.
At the pending/digital join, continuity of k(f) can join the two estimates;
the proof must use the correct one-sided derivative. No classical derivative
at every command dispatch may be assumed.

After a noncircular outer-cube proof, the digital slope is at most 108R=6.1236.

inactive on the relevant prefix: 24K+.093<=1.593<2. The initial pending command
and startup bounds must remain explicit. Before the first dispatch, exclude

Do not assume the final cube or residual in that prefix proof.

With rate .0006, the policy's worst entry-duration certificate grows from

The original policy still has a weighted initial box strictly inside the new

Two integration gaps remain beyond this gate. First, the old finite state

A holding-age theorem cannot silently repair either gap.

Baseline findings
The primary network RG paper is TR2015-154. I read its model, Assumptions 2/3,
Lemma 10, (15), (19), Theorem 16, Remark 9 and output-feedback assumptions.
It uses an asymptotically stable LTI reference-to-output loop. Its delayed
signal is the reference, and the full-state result has exact current state.
Our delayed held acceleration from noisy state is a different topology.
A fixed r=8 adaptation is a fair restricted comparison if both arms use it
and the difference from the paper is stated.

The augmented map must retain the pending command and compose the D and T-D
flows. Verify sampled closed-loop stability; continuous gain stability is not
sufficient. Clipping must either enter the model or be proved inactive.
Both arms must receive the same noisy measurement, known command memory,
requested reference, uncertainty set and state set. Record internal reference
retreat separately from tracking the requested target.

The paper's recursive-feasibility argument uses D(Delta v)=0 at fixed reference.
Our independent sensor/target errors persist at fixed reference. A robust
invariant tail is required. A finite prediction alone is not that tail.
Keeping an earlier accepted constant-reference certificate after a fresh
measurement-box rejection can be sound; do not claim that every reset-box
optimization must remain feasible. Check all four augmented terminal states.

Two constant scalar disturbance values per sample represent arbitrary bounded
time-varying target error only if a sound vector integral enclosure is proved.
Otherwise restrict both arms to a declared piecewise-constant disturbance
subclass. State components have different convolution kernels. A single scalar
average generally cannot represent all three integrals.

The paper's displayed Theorem 16 gives constraints at sample indices. Remark 9
adds finite intersampling instants. Neither alone establishes every real-time
travel bound. An all-time comparison needs an analytic between-sample bound or
validated enclosure. Binary64 certificate margins are numerical evidence until
the arithmetic is bounded. Nonempty admissible cases and deliberate exclusions
are required to avoid an empty-set performance claim.

Measurement and practical limits
The copied official RobotState 0.15.0 documentation calls ddq_d desired joint
acceleration. It is not an actual acceleration measurement with our .05 m/s^2
error bound. The copied latest API exposes link accelerometers, which still
need a frame, gravity, kinematic, timestamp and error mapping to this scalar.
The scalar acceleration-target lag is also not established by a Franka command
API name. The robot/software/mode and physical bounds have not been validated.

I independently replayed and checked root's exact sensor conversion control:
with independent velocity errors +/- .01 m/s, a 1 ms first difference has
attainable 20 m/s^2 noise, 400 times the assumed acceleration allowance. Constant
true velocity suffices. This concerns the stated error box, not measured robot
noise. A jerk-bound endpoint bias JT/2 is additional to that noise bound.

I also checked root's equilibrium control. The fixed holding law permits
x-c=-ex-.75ev-ea/16+d/8=.012875 m with negative sensor extrema and d=.01.
The raw command is -.01 and its sum with d is zero, so clipping is inactive
and the physical state is stationary for every positive response rate. The
acceleration reading -.05 fails the admission guard. Thus this is a law-level
precision obstruction, not a guard-reachable execution or a lower bound for
all controllers. A smaller enclosure alone cannot remove it from the law's
full uncertainty class.

Publication gate
Repair, then reassess. Sampled-data safety with state uncertainty, known delay,
and ZOH already appears in Singletary/Chen/Ames 2020. I inspected its opening
model, sampled invariant-set definition, Proposition 1 and discussion of
nonsmooth update points; I did not re-prove or deeply review the whole paper.
The network RG source also supplies an established constraint-governor method.
The basic combination is not evidence of novelty. A useful next result would
combine an explicit implementation contract, independently checked robust
certificates, and a fair demonstrated advantage or a clear measured tradeoff.
Current evidence does not yet establish that contribution. This is not a claim
that no publishable contribution exists.
