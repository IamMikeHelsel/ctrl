Decision: ACCEPT the real Clifford contraction gate. Grade: A for this theorem
milestone. This grade does not measure GU validity, novelty, or publication odds.

The literal increasing-pair operator is proved equal to minus scalar curvature
in Mathlib's actual Clifford algebra for the diagonal (7,7) form. The target has
the intended pair antisymmetries, pair exchange, and first Bianchi assumptions.
The proof includes the separate all-index normalization C = 2K and the weighted
Ricci/scalar bridge. It does not define K by the desired answer or leave the

separate kernel replay. The mixed tensor R_0707 = 1 has scal = -2 and K = +2.

The submitted positive sectional control has scal = 2 and K = -2. The
non-Bianchi four-form has Ric = 0, Bianchi residual 3, and
K = 12 gamma_0 gamma_1 gamma_2 gamma_3 != 0. The blade squares to 1 in the
same actual, nontrivial Clifford algebra. Thus dropping Bianchi is disproved
by a concrete theorem, not merely by an unsuccessful proof search.

counterexample. None of these failures was caused by an unknown import or name.
