ONE OPTIONAL NEXT GATE

If the coordinator continues this declared candidate, the useful next gate is
the missing conjugate-linear adjoint in an actual complexification of Cl(7,7).
Keep it separate from global GU claims and from author intent.

Feasibility judgement: one bounded attempt is warranted. The local pinned
Mathlib already supplies the tensor-product additive star and the algebra
tensor-product multiplication. Use the actual algebra C tensor_R Cl(Q), not a
new algebra defined only by desired adjoint laws. The additive tensor star
sends z tensor a to conjugate(z) tensor star_Cl(a). The real Clifford star is
compatible with real scalar multiplication. Tensor induction should then prove
the product reversal and conjugate-linearity. The main remaining work is
connecting these existing constructions and checking the exact instances.
This review has inspected those APIs, but has not compiled this next bridge.

Relevant pinned files are Algebra/Star/TensorProduct.lean,
RingTheory/TensorProduct/Basic.lean, and
LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Star.lean. The real embedding is a -> 1 tensor a.
If no injection lemma fits, a real-part map on the complex factor gives an
explicit linear left inverse and avoids assuming faithfulness. Retaining the
real contraction under this embedding is a useful acceptance check.

Construct bar on an actual complex algebra that contains the accepted real
Clifford algebra. Prove bar(i A) = -i bar(A), bar(AB) = bar(B)bar(A),
bar(bar(A)) = A, and bar(gamma_a) = -gamma_a. Use these actual definitions to
show that the bar-skew elements are closed under AB-BA and i(AB+BA).
Require an explicit example showing why the scalar-fixed Mathlib star cannot
replace this bar in the second closure proof. An abstract theorem that simply
assumes all adjoint laws would not close the actual construction gate.

Use gamma_0, whose square is 1, for the wrong-adjoint control. Its J product
with itself is 2i times 1. A scalar-fixed adjoint fixes this scalar, so the
result cannot be skew under that adjoint in a nontrivial algebra. This keeps
the negative control small and exposes the exact missing conjugation.

record the exact obstruction and pause. The full curvature-to-Einstein
