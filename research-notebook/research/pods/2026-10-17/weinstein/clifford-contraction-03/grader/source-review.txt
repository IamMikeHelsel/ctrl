Algebraic route
The general proof uses an associative real algebra and the Clifford generator
relation. It first contracts symmetric coefficients. Pair exchange makes the
intermediate r_abcb symmetric in its free indices. Two adjacent Clifford swaps
give crossed = -total - 2 diagonalContract and
cycled = -crossed + 2 diagonalContract. Bianchi is used in bianchi_sum to prove
total - crossed + cycled = 0. Their combination gives total = -2 traceR.
The proof does not assume Ricci symmetry as an extra input.

The normalization is separate. Pair antisymmetry and off-diagonal Clifford
anticommutation make each pair ordering contribute equally. Diagonal pair
terms vanish. This gives total = 4 sortedWord. Pair exchange gives
sortedK = 2 sortedWord, hence total = 2 sortedK. The main scalar theorem then
cancels the factor two over the reals. This bridge is explicit and does not
redefine the operator.

Weighted trace bridge
rho_curvature proves all required symmetries after the four metric weights.
rho_diag proves rho_abab = R_abab by eps_a squared = eps_b squared = 1.
trace_rho unfolds the Ricci and scalar definitions and reorders the finite
sums. Thus its trace is precisely sum_b eps_b sum_a eps_a R_abab.
actual_clifford_contraction discharges the general generator relation using
the actual Q and iota. No free gamma or relation hypothesis remains.
