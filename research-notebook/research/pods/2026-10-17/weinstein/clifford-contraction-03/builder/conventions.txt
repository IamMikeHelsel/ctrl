Proof map
1. The general associative-algebra lemma symmetric_contraction contracts a
   symmetric coefficient matrix using the Clifford anticommutation relation.
2. diagonalContract_eq applies this to the Ricci-type intermediate sum.
3. word_swap and word_last_swap derive two adjacent Clifford reorderings.
4. bianchi_sum reindexes finite sums to expose the Bianchi equation.
5. total_contraction combines these results. For the all-index sum C, it proves
   C=-2 times the weighted scalar trace times 1.
6. sum_sorted, term_swap1 and term_swap2 prove C=4 times the increasing-pair
   ordered-word sum. Pair exchange proves K=2 times that same sum.
   normalization_bridge therefore proves C=2K; K is never replaced by C/2
   as a definition.
7. rho_curvature and trace_rho discharge the weighted coefficient identities.
   gamma_relation instantiates the general algebra theorem in actual Cl(Q).
