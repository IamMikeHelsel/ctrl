Convention ledger required before code
Use V = Fin 14 -> R, where R is the real field, with the ordered coordinate basis.
Use B = diag(+1 seven times, -1 seven times). Let vol send the ordered top wedge
to +1. This is a declared local mathematical model; it does not select missing
GU invariant tensors. Write mu(I) = product of the diagonal entries indexed by
I. Write eps(I) for the sign of the permutation (I, complement I), with each
block increasing. Let e_I be the ordered exterior-basis vector.

Two Hodge conventions must be kept distinct:
  S_k: alpha wedge S_k(beta) = B_k(alpha,beta) times the positive top wedge.
  H_k: B_(14-k)(H_k(alpha), beta) = vol(alpha wedge beta).
Mathlib's hodgeStar has the second definition. The expected formulas are
  S_k(e_I) = eps(I) mu(I) e_(complement I),
  H_k(e_I) = eps(I) mu(complement I) e_(complement I).
Because product_i B_ii = -1, the expected relation is H_k = -S_k.
For k=2 and its complementary degree 12, either convention has square -Id.
Check these claims in Lean. Do not silently call the two conventions identical.
