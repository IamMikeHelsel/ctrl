What the source actually defines
HodgeBridge.lean:9-23 defines V=R^14 and B through the ordered coordinate basis
and its dual. The unit weights are +1 at 0..6 and -1 at 7..13. B_basis_basis
proves the diagonal entries. B_bijective follows from an actual linear
equivalence. It does not assume nondegeneracy or the Hodge conclusion.

Lines 36-48 define the volume map from the actual top exterior basis. vol_top
proves that the ordered top wedge maps to +1. Lines 50-54 name the subset/Fin
index equivalence and use it consistently to reindex the actual exterior basis.
The Fin enumeration is not asserted to be lexicographic or computationally
canonical; this is acceptable for a coordinate bridge. Reports must not imply
that numeric array indices already have an external standard ordering.

Lines 62-71 define H directly with pinned Mathlib exteriorPower.hodgeStar and
establish its defining pairing. Lines 81-97 derive the induced metric diagonal
on the actual exterior basis. Lines 108-127 derive the signed-complement formula
from Mathlib's actual exterior wedge pairing and induced metric injectivity.
This is not an arbitrary signed array relabeled as Hodge star.

Lines 130-200 derive the negative total metric product, the complementary signs,
and H12(H2(x))=-x for every real 2-form. The proof uses actual graded exterior
multiplication to establish the 2/12 swap sign. It has no star-square assumption.
The last extension uses basis extensionality, so it covers all real 2-forms,
not only the two later examples.

Convention review
Lines 203-240 define S=-H, then prove S's independent characterization
  vol(x wedge S(y)) = B_k(x,y).
H_eq_neg_S alone would be definitional. S_pairing supplies the substantive
bridge to the other Hodge convention. The signed-complement coefficients are
correct for the stated metric and orientation. The general S_basis statement
also covers degree 12. Neither definition silently changes the earlier gauge
or torsion signs.

Coordinate interface review
Lines 242-274 define star2 from H's actual basis images, prove its finite-sum
representation, and derive its signed-complement entries. HodgeInterface.lean
then identifies that same sum with frozen GUOperatorAudit.linearCoeff over R.
It supplies both H and S conventions with opposite arrays. This removes the
arbitrary-coefficient gap for this one map. It does not instantiate or validate
all fields of SwerveCoefficients, and makes no such claim.
