Independent algebra check
independent_contraction.py imports none of the analyst model. It reduces words
by adjacent swaps and repeated-generator cancellation. It checks all 4186
independent coordinates in Sym^2(Lambda^2 R14), including the non-Bianchi ones.
It verifies the full scalar-plus-four-vector remainder, the factor C=2K, Ricci
symmetry, and the two diagonal contractions in the recommended proof.
It also checks all 3185 stated algebraic-curvature basis elements.

The basis argument is sound. There are 91 two-index pairs and 4186 symmetric
pair coordinates. Of these, 1183 have intersecting pairs. The remaining 3003
split into 1001 disjoint four-index blocks. Each block has one independent
Bianchi equation x-y+z=0. Its two vectors (1,1,0) and (0,1,1) span the kernel:
their x,z coordinates are the standard basis. Different blocks have disjoint
support. Repeated-index Bianchi equations already follow from the pair
symmetries. Thus the basis has dimension 1183+2002=3185. The finite test is
exhaustive for this coefficient model, but its Python interpreter and the
basis-to-mathematics argument are outside Lean's trusted kernel.

Normalization and domains
The metric signs, increasing-pair convention, and scalar definition are
consistent. For R_0101=1, scal=2 and K=-2. The Hodge convention uses S(vol)=-1
in signature (7,7), so K=-scal gives Q=i scal under the declared J product.
The full C/J/J algebra then has the claimed Einstein trace ratio. This ratio
depends on the specified scale of Phi2 and the product choices. Weyl
annihilation alone cannot determine it. Scaling Phi2 by beta preserves zero
on Ricci-free curvature for every beta. The declared additional invariant
tensors involving the volume element also show that a selection is needed.

The four-form negative control is essential. It obeys both pair
antisymmetries and pair exchange symmetry but violates Bianchi. Independently,
K=12 gamma_0123, scal=0. The four-generator product squares to 1, so it is
nonzero in a nontrivial real Clifford algebra. Ricci zero alone is insufficient.

The gauge counterexample is also correct. Conjugation by gamma0 gamma1 flips
exactly gamma0 and gamma1. For the stated four sectional components, the
rotated Phi2 contraction becomes 8i. The first term has no gamma4 component;
the scalar term gives +8 S(theta4) gamma4. Rotating the input curvature too
restores zero by covariance. The valid statement is at the identity, or on the
matching transported Weyl space. This does not refute the author's claim until
his intended domain and gauge identification are fixed. The reported kernel
is exactly the Weyl kernel only on the declared ordinary-curvature domain;
it is not a kernel statement on all adjoint-valued two-forms.

Adjoint and spinor checks
The candidate's bar must conjugate complex scalars. Pinned Mathlib
CliffordAlgebra.Star has star_smul and star_algebraMap that fix those scalars.
It cannot supply the required complex adjoint directly. This is a real API
boundary, not a notation difference.

independent_fock.py constructs separate integer matrices from exterior
creation and contraction. It verifies Clifford relations, symmetric K,
K^2=I, trace K=0, and every generator's K-skew adjoint relation. This implies
signature (64,64). It also constructs the exact vacuum projector and all 128
creation columns and contraction rows. Their outer products give all 16384
matrix units, which supplies the missing elementary spanning argument for
faithfulness once the standard Clifford dimension is used. These are exact
integer computations and a mathematical argument, not a Lean representation
or a formally proved isomorphism. No floating-point rank or spectrum is used.

Gromov, Four Lectures on Scalar Curvature:
https://cims.nyu.edu/~gromov/lectures%20on%20Curvature%202025/Scalar-Jul-8-2021.pdf
The coordinator also supplied Debray's notes, theorem 5.10:
https://adebray.github.io/lecture_notes/m392c_IND_notes.pdf
These are versions of the algebraic core of the Schroedinger-Lichnerowicz
formula. Their metric, Clifford, and curvature conventions must be translated
before comparing factors. This gate is a formalization and audit contribution. No
new mathematical theorem or publication value is established here.
