APPROVED NEXT LEAN GATE: A REAL CLIFFORD CURVATURE CONTRACTION

Scope
Prove a standard pointwise algebraic identity for the declared candidate.
This does not select the author's preferred GU operator or prove a field theory.
Use the pinned Mathlib actual CliffordAlgebra. Do not define K by its desired
scalar value. No complex star or Hodge theorem is required at this first gate.

Precise data
I=Fin 14; epsilon(a)=1 for a<7 and -1 otherwise.
V=I -> Real. Q(v)=sum_a epsilon(a)*v(a)^2 as a QuadraticForm.
e_a is the coordinate unit vector; gamma_a=CliffordAlgebra.iota Q e_a.
R : I -> I -> I -> I -> Real satisfies, for all a,b,c,d:
  R_abcd = -R_bacd;
  R_abcd = -R_abdc;
  R_abcd = R_cdab;
  R_abcd - R_acbd + R_adbc = 0.
Ric_bd=sum_a epsilon_a R_ab a d.
scal=sum_b epsilon_b Ric_bb.

Define the displayed object, with its actual finite sums and products:
  K(R)=sum_(a<b,c<d) epsilon_a epsilon_b epsilon_c epsilon_d R_abcd
       * (gamma_a gamma_b gamma_c gamma_d
          + gamma_c gamma_d gamma_a gamma_b).
Real multiplication in that display means scalar multiplication in the algebra.
Main target: K(R)=algebraMap Real (CliffordAlgebra Q) (-scal).

Recommended proof route
Use a general finite index set for the main algebra, if it simplifies code.
Assume real weights epsilon with epsilon_a^2=1, and elements gamma in a real
associative algebra satisfying
  gamma_a gamma_b + gamma_b gamma_a
      = (if a=b then 2 epsilon_a else 0) * 1.
Prove the contraction there, then discharge this relation for the actual Q and
actual iota. The final actual-Clifford instance is mandatory. A theorem with
uninstantiated gamma assumptions alone is partial progress.

Let rho_abcd=epsilon_a epsilon_b epsilon_c epsilon_d R_abcd, and define
  C=sum_all rho_abcd gamma_a gamma_b gamma_c gamma_d,
  C'=sum_all rho_abcd gamma_a gamma_c gamma_b gamma_d,
  C''=sum_all rho_abcd gamma_a gamma_c gamma_d gamma_b,
  Tbc=sum_all rho_abcd delta_bc epsilon_b gamma_a gamma_d,
  Tbd=sum_all rho_abcd delta_bd epsilon_b gamma_a gamma_c.
All sums run over a,b,c,d in I. These are proof auxiliaries, not assumptions.

1. Pair symmetry implies Ric_bd=Ric_db. Hence Clifford anticommutation gives
   sum_(a,d) epsilon_a epsilon_d Ric_ad gamma_a gamma_d = scal * 1.
2. The two antisymmetries, epsilon^2=1, and the Ricci definition give
   Tbc=-scal*1 and Tbd=scal*1.
3. One adjacent swap gives C'=-C+2Tbc. Two adjacent swaps give
   C''=C-2Tbc+2Tbd.
4. Sum the Bianchi identity with the Clifford word, then reindex the last three
   indices. This gives C-C'+C''=0. Thus 3C-4Tbc+2Tbd=0, so C=-2 scal*1.
5. Establish the normalization bridge C=2K. First, pair antisymmetry and the
   off-diagonal Clifford relation turn the ordered sum into four times the
   increasing-pair sum. Pair exchange symmetry makes the two terms in K equal.
   The bridge is part of the gate. Do not silently replace the declared K by C/2.

This route is preferable to 3185 generated proofs: it exposes the Bianchi use,
is independent of dimension, and avoids a large trusted translation script.
A Fin 14 implementation of these same lemmas is acceptable if general finite
index bookkeeping slows the proof. A basis-vector proof is a fallback only if
its spanning theorem and its actual Clifford interpretation are also proved.

Mandatory negative and normalization controls
Define the alternating four-form A supported on indices {0,1,2,3}, with
A_0123=1. It has both pair antisymmetries and pair exchange symmetry. It has
Ric=0, scal=0, and Bianchi residual A_0123-A_0213+A_0312=3.
Compute in the same actual Clifford algebra:
  K(A)=12 gamma_0 gamma_1 gamma_2 gamma_3 != 0.
For nonzero, the four-generator product squares to 1. Use the actual
nontriviality of CliffordAlgebra Q (available through the exterior equivalence);
there is no need to formalize the full Fock representation at this gate.

Also use the sectional tensor with independent component R_0101=1. Its scalar
is 2 and K=-2*1. This catches a sign flip and a missing factor of two.
A mutated target K=+scal*1 must be rejected. Dropping Bianchi must also be
rejected by the explicit four-form control, not merely by a tactic timeout.

Verification gate
Fresh source build under the pinned toolchain; all public target axiom audits;
independent leanchecker run; meaningful failed mutation; source hashes and logs.
No sorry, admit, native_decide, extra axiom, unsafe theorem, or changed pin.
Only then call the real Clifford identity proved. The Q=i scal and full
C/J/J candidate bridges remain separate future targets.

Relevant pinned APIs
CliffordAlgebra.Basic: iota_sq_scalar and iota_mul_iota_add_swap (Lean identifiers
use the actual Greek iota character). CliffordAlgebra.Contraction provides the
exterior equivalence and the characteristic-not-two Nontrivial instance.
Check exact local signatures before using them. Mathlib's CliffordAlgebra star
fixes scalars; it is not the complex-conjugate bar in the candidate ledger.
