The candidate K_R=-scal*1 is a convention-dependent algebraic ingredient of
standard spin geometry. Treat a Lean proof as a reusable formalization. Do not
present it as a new Einstein equation or independent support for GU physics.

Misha Gromov, Four Lectures on Scalar Curvature, July8,2021, PDF page86,
section3.3.3, gives the Lichnerowicz identity. Bianchi and curvature symmetries
reduce the Clifford curvature action to one-quarter scalar curvature in that
source's conventions. This is a Riemannian spin-geometric statement. The signs,
curvature indexing, representation, and factor conventions must be compared
before importing any coefficient into our explicit Cl(7,7) model.
https://cims.nyu.edu/~gromov/lectures%20on%20Curvature%202025/Scalar-Jul-8-2021.pdf

Arun Debray's M392C Index Theory notes, theorem5.10, PDF page21, display the
four-index curvature sum and its reduction using Bianchi to scalar curvature
in the Weitzenbock formula. These lecture notes give a direct algebraic proof
pattern. They are not an audit of our chosen tensors or the GU draft.
https://adebray.github.io/lecture_notes/m392c_IND_notes.pdf
