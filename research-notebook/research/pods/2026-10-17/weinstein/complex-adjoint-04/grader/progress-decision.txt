the scalar-fixed Clifford star does not support the proposed J product, and

exterior forms, followed by Hodge action and explicit Phi_1/Phi_2 expansions.

The accepted scalar and adjoint lemmas make this plausible. However, that work
would certify the operator and normalization chosen in our analyst ledger.
It would not select the author's preferred tensors, settle the source's domain,
or establish a new physical claim. The definition-choice boundary persists.

Do not open another theorem gate merely to increase the checked statement
count. Resume this line when there is a fixed, source-supported operator to
compare, or when the coordinator explicitly chooses a standalone mathematical
formalization contribution with a precise target and value beyond GU. This is
a scope and value recommendation, not a claim that the remaining mathematics
is impossible. No further proof work was started by this reviewer.
