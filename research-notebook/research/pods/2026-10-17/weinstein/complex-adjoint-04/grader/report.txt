INDEPENDENT REVIEW: ACTUAL COMPLEX ADJOINT GATE 04
Decision: ACCEPT this algebra gate. Grade: A for the specified construction
and closure milestone. Pause automatic GU proof expansion after this gate.

The construction uses the actual complex tensor algebra C tensor_R Cl(Q),
with a faithful inclusion of the accepted real Cl(7,7). Its bar conjugates
complex scalars, reverses products, is an involution, and negates the embedded
generators. The two operations AB-BA and I(AB+BA) preserve bar-skew elements.
The proof uses this actual adjoint; it does not assume its laws as new inputs.

The scalar-fixed comparison is also an actual constructed anti-involution.
It fails the second closure rule on J(gamma_0,gamma_0)=2I times 1. Direct
counterexample theorems also reject omission of I and preservation of product
order. The order witness uses gamma_0*gamma_7, whose square is 1. Nontriviality
and scalar injectivity make these controls non-vacuous.

complexK is the literal accepted sorted-pair sum with embedded generators.
The proof first shows complexK=embed(K), then applies the accepted real
contraction under the same symmetries and Bianchi identity. The independent
client exposes the full sum, checks the sectional value -2, and preserves the
nonzero four-form defect. See source-review.txt for the statement audit.

Independent verification
independent_verify.py copied and built the three new modules and all four
accepted local dependency modules in a fresh directory. It did not execute
the builder's verification script. All seven modules passed separate
leanchecker replays. An independently written ten-theorem client also built,
passed its own replay, and was included in the axiom audit.

The new modules have 37 public theorems and 49 named public declarations.
The inherited modules have 52 public theorems and 79 named declarations,
including their structures. All 128 submitted or inherited declarations and
the ten client theorems were audited. Their axioms are subsets of propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound. These are coverage counts, not new-result
counts. No target source uses sorry, admit, an added axiom, native_decide,
or unsafe declarations.

Three changed sources and one wrong-adjoint client were rejected. The errors
expose the missing complex conjugation, wrong multiplication order, missing I,
and substitution of fixedBar for bar. The direct counterexample theorems show
that the proposed variants are false; acceptance does not rely only on a
rewrite tactic failing. No failure came from a missing import or identifier.
