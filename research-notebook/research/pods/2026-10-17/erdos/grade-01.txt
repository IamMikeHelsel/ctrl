EPSILON DESCENT: VALID
The assumed order of quantifiers is sufficient:
  forall rational eps>0, exists N, forall large triangle-free H, exists cut
  with normalized internal cost <=2/47+eps.
Fix a nonempty G of order n. Set eps=1/(94*n^2), obtain N, and take the
positive balanced blow-up factor k=N+1. Then k*n>=N. Triangle-freeness is
preserved. The retained blow-up equivalence accepts any rational threshold,
so its use at 2/47+eps is valid and gives a cut in G of integer cost d with
  d <=2*n^2/47+1/94.
Hence the integer 47*d-2*n^2 is <=1/2 and must be <=0. The empty case is
immediate. This proves exact all-order BCL from the stated uniform premise.

The positive unnormalized gap above 2*n^2/47 is at least 1/47. The normalized
gap is at least 1/(47*n^2). The selected epsilon is half the latter. No
quantifier reversal or missing factor of n^2 was found. The uniform premise
itself is still missing; a fixed numerical residual cannot replace it.

HIGH-DENSITY REDUCTION: VALID
For triangle-free G, N(v) is independent. Thus the crossing count for the
cut (N(v), complement) is C_v=sum_{u in N(v)} deg(u). Double counting gives
sum_v C_v=sum_u deg(u)^2. Cauchy-Schwarz gives an average crossing count at
least 4*m^2/n^2. Some neighbor cut therefore has internal cost at most
m-4*m^2/n^2. Only n>0 and finite simple triangle-freeness are needed. No
regularity, optimal-cut, minimum-degree or stability hypothesis is hidden.

Write r=2m/n^2. Its normalized cost is at most g(r)=r/2-r^2. For
r>=a=49/125, the identity
  g(a)-g(r)=(r-a)*(r+a-1/2)
proves g(r)<=g(a), since 2*a-1/2=71/250>0. Exactly,
  g(a)=1323/31250=2/47-319/1468750.
The paper's near-2/5 region lies inside this range. Its omitted stability
argument is dispensable for target 2/47 if the elementary branch is proved.
This does not dispense with the stronger 1/25 problem or the middle-density
certificate. The fair-cut branch covers r<=8/47. The remaining gap is
8/47<r<49/125, with uniform finite-error and normalization control.
