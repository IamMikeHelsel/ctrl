Semantic review
1. FrozenReference contains exactly the eight rational position polynomials,
   start times, and durations in causal-stop-05/builder/reference.json. An
   independent parser reads the Lean source and compares each coefficient and
   time as a rational number. It does not call the builder's generator.
2. DirectReference defines reference velocity and acceleration as polynomial
   derivatives, and jerk as the third derivative. It proves their analytic
   derivative identities. These facts are not assumptions about opaque data.
3. The seven state joins and seven jerk joins match. All durations are positive.
   The first reference state is zero, its initial jerk is 10, and the final
   reference state is (3/5,0,0). An independent rational derivative check
   confirms those values.
4. The reference jerk proof covers every closed phase interval. The brake proof
   uses its exact polynomial square and a nonnegative square identity. Other
   phases use constant or affine jerk inequalities. A separate rational
   Bernstein check covers the same eight phases with 11 closed cells. This
   Python check is additional evidence; the Lean theorem is the formal proof.
5. DirectPlant contains the actual classical ODE, pointwise rate and disturbance
   bounds, matched physical state at every join, and the initial physical state.
   It does not contain the desired tracking or corridor conclusion. IsTrajectory
   means continuity on the closed local interval and x'=v, v'=a,
   a'=r*(u+w-a) on its interior. No trace is reset at a join.
6. The actual input is q'' - 800*(x-q) - 60*(v-q') - (a-q'')/2 + j/96.
   The j/96 coefficient is checked by an extra Lean witness. No input filter
   occurs in the model. The rate bounds are [80,120] per second and the
   disturbance magnitude is at most 1/100 m/s^2.
7. The reference facts discharge finite_replans_scaled. That theorem transports
   the physical plant and reference by chain rules, derives the error dynamics,
   and uses the robust invariant cube. The finite induction passes the actual
   final state to the next phase through matched_error. The exact budget is
   37/1200 <= 1/25. No step assumes the eventual error bound.
8. phaseIndex_valid and phaseIndex_local cover every time in [0,3/2]. atTime
   selects the left phase at a join. The proved state matching makes this choice
   harmless for the position, velocity, and acceleration values.
9. Nonnegative reference velocity and endpoint inequalities prove reference
   corridor [0,3/5]. The resulting actual corridor is only
   [-1/5000,3001/5000]. The report does not claim the smaller actual corridor.

Scope limits
The theorem is conditional on supplied classical trajectories. It does not prove
existence for arbitrary discontinuous rate or disturbance functions. This limit
is stated in the source and report. It does not invalidate the conditional bound.

The endpoint bounds on actual velocity and acceleration are 2/125 m/s and
32/25 m/s^2. These are nonzero and coarse. The proof does not establish a physical
stop, an actuator limit, a physical jerk limit, sampled servo execution, floating
point behavior, or hardware performance. It does not establish the analyst's
other reference acceleration or snap limits. Those limits are not premises of
this tracking theorem.

The two slow filters still fail the fixed sampled position target. This review
changes none of their evidence. The direct theorem covers more rate and
disturbance variation than that fixed numerical comparison, but only for the
stated plant and direct controller.

Next useful test
The direct half of the fixed comparison is now ready for an all-time claim under
the stated plant assumptions. To make the adverse filter results equally
rigorous, certify one position-target violation for each fixed filter with an
explicit enclosure of numerical error. Agreement between high-precision replays
is strong evidence but is not, by itself, such an enclosure. No new tuning is
needed for that test.

If physical stopping is the next goal, first state the required endpoint speed,
acceleration, position, and actuator limits. Then prove a shrinking error bound
through the final hold phase or give a counterexample to those limits. Reusing
this constant invariant cube alone cannot establish smaller endpoint residuals.
For the robot-motion work, this direct tracking bridge can be reused only after
its new reference has discharged the same derivative, join, and jerk premises.
