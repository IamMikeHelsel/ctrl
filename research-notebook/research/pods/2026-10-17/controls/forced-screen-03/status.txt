Model and execution
The exact gate used 2 controllers x 4 rate cases x 3 disturbances x 2 jerk cases.
Physical sigma=10/s and lambda=20/s; normalized lambda=2. The normalized state
is xi=(ex,ev/10,ea/100,(u-q2)/100), starting at zero. Absolute reference values
start at q0=q1=q2=0, with matched plant and u=0. This explicit convention fixes
absolute u and u_star, which xi(0)=0 alone does not fix. See equations.txt for
all equations, units, schedules, and the analytical segment solution.

Every constant segment uses the exact matrix-exponential solution formula,
evaluated in floating point. The primary method uses SciPy expm independently
from each segment start. A separate 80-digit mpmath Taylor exponential advances
an augmented forced system and checks every saved row. It independently builds
the schedules and reference, and does not use SciPy segment-start states.
The grid is 0.5 ms with both one-sided values at events: 72,120 rows in 48 traces.
Maximum discrepancy was 3.067e-18 m in position, 5.060e-15 m/s^2 in u_star,
9.696e-14 m/s^3 in u_prime, and 2.536e-13 m/s^3 in actual jerk.
No numerical disagreement remains at the stated tolerance.
