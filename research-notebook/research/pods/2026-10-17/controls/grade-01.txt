Exact result and assumptions
The physical theorem derives, for each t in [0,H], both |zi(t)|<=R and
|u(t)-u_star(t)|<=D from the initial joint box and the actual plant, reference,
and un-clipped first-order filter equations. Here zi are the three existing
scaled tracking coordinates. The reference jerk is constant within each segment.
The plant is x'=v, v'=a, a'=r*(u+w-a). The filter is u'=lambda*(u_star-u).
Assume sigma>0, R>0, D>0, W>=0, J>=0, H>=0, 8*sigma<=r<=12*sigma,
|w|<=W, |j|<=J, and the two budgets
  W+D+J/(48*sigma) <= 2*sigma^2*R,
  96*sigma^3*R+(13/12)*J+6*sigma*W <= (lambda-6*sigma)*D.
Physical, reference, and filter trajectories are supplied and continuous on the
closed segment. Their equations hold on its open interior. No explicit derivative
or continuity condition on r or w is added. The supplied trajectory hypotheses
still restrict which signals admit such an execution. Existence is not proved.
Uniqueness is neither required nor proved.

Practical limitation
The budgets themselves imply lambda>=54*sigma. The first gives D<=2*sigma^2*R.
The second gives (lambda-6*sigma)*D>=96*sigma^3*R. With D>0, their combination
gives the bound. The new Lean theorem checks this implication. It limits this
sufficient certificate; it is not a necessary condition for system stability.

At sigma=10, lambda=4000/s means a 0.25 ms filter. The assumed actuator rates are
80 to 120/s, with time constants about 8.33 to 12.5 ms. Thus this witness's filter
rate is 33.3 to 50 times the actuator rate. Feasibility alone does not demonstrate
useful smoothing, a viable sampled implementation, or better tracking. Even the
certificate's universal minimum lambda=54*sigma exceeds r_max by a factor of 4.5.
The report must keep this limitation beside the positive result.

suggest conservatism. They do not prove stability under arbitrary rate variation,
a tracking tube, command smoothness quality, or hardware benefit. The diagnostic
