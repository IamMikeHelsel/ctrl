Result
A rational common quadratic certificate exists at normalized sigma=1, lambda=2
for every actuator rate r in [8,12]. A is the exact matrix in grade-01.txt.
With xi=(ex,ev,ea,u-q2), let V=xi^T P xi, where

P = (1/100) * [[22700,16487,1640,143],
               [16487,14809,1421,1567],
               [1640,1421,311,36],
               [143,1567,36,1027]].

The exact margin is mu=1/2. P is symmetric positive definite. At both endpoints,
A(r)^T P+P A(r) <= -(1/2) I. Affine dependence on r gives the same inequality
throughout [8,12]. Exact rational LDL factorizations certify positive definiteness
of P-I/2, -A(8)^T P-P A(8)-I/2, -A(12)^T P-P A(12)-I/2, and 500I-P.
All pivots are strictly positive. Every factorization identity was checked with
Python Fraction arithmetic and independently encoded as exact Lean sum-of-squares
identities. The final Lean theorem common_quadratic_certificate states the matrix
quadratic inequality for every r in [8,12] and every real xi.

Actual ODE conclusion
For supplied continuous actual trajectories on [0,H] whose equations hold on
(0,H), H>=0, the homogeneous plant/reference/filter equations imply
  V(t) <= exp(-t/1000)*V(0).
The proof uses (1/2)||xi||^2 <= V <= 500||xi||^2 and derives the augmented ODE
from x'=v, v'=a, a'=r*(u-a), q0'=q1, q1'=q2, q2'=0, and
  u'=2*(q2-8*(x-q0)-6*(v-q1)-(a-q2)/2-u).
Only 8<=r(t)<=12 is assumed about r. No derivative or continuity condition on r
is added. The supplied trajectory hypotheses restrict admissible executions.
Existence is not proved; uniqueness is neither assumed nor proved. No all-time
tracking, filter-error, or command-rate estimate is assumed.

Search and exact arithmetic
The existing Python had NumPy and SciPy, but no CVXPY. No package was installed.
One fixed-parameter HiGHS search used violated-eigenvector linear cuts, with a cap
of 160 solves or 120 seconds. It found a candidate after 69 LP solves in about
0.08 seconds on this machine. Solver eigenvalues are recorded as numerical search
evidence only. Rounding to tenths failed an exact pivot test. Rounding to hundredths
passed with mu=1/2. The earlier midpoint Lyapunov candidate's endpoint failures
and every solver iteration are preserved in search-result.json.

Practical meaning and open scope
This certifies a substantially slower filter for homogeneous stability under
pointwise rate variation. It shows the earlier box rate restriction is conservative
for that homogeneous property. The decay bound is conservative; it is not a
settling-time or performance measurement. This result does not prove a forced
tracking tube, finite replan safety, stopping, sampled or clipped execution,
actuator feasibility, or measured smoothing. Those claims need separate evidence.
