Exact model and hypotheses
Fix sigma>0, lambda>0, H>=0, R>0, D>0, W>=0, J>=0. All are real.
On [0,H], f=(x,v,a), q=(q0,q1,q2), and u are continuous.
On (0,H), f satisfies x'=v, v'=a, a'=r(t)*(u+w(t)-a).
The reference satisfies q0'=q1, q1'=q2, q2'=j0, with constant j0 and |j0|<=J.
The un-clipped filter satisfies u'=lambda*(u_star-u), where
  u_star=q2-8*sigma^2*(x-q0)-6*sigma*(v-q1)-(a-q2)/2
         +5*j0/(48*sigma).
Assume 8*sigma<=r(t)<=12*sigma and |w(t)|<=W on [0,H].
No derivative bound on r or w is needed for this conditional trajectory theorem.
Existence of the stated continuous, interior-classical trajectories is a premise.
This batch need not prove existence or uniqueness for arbitrary r and w.

Define ex=x-q0, ev=v-q1, ea=a-q2 and d=u-u_star. Define
  z0=ex,
  z1=ex+ev/(4*sigma),
  z2=ex+ev/(2*sigma)+ea/(16*sigma^2).
Assume only the initial joint box: |zi(0)|<=R and |d(0)|<=D.
Require these two parameter inequalities:
  W+D+J/(48*sigma) <= 2*sigma^2*R,
  (lambda-6*sigma)*D >= 96*sigma^3*R+(13/12)*J+6*sigma*W.
The second inequality with R,D>0 implies lambda>6*sigma.
Conclusion: for every t in [0,H], all |zi(t)|<=R and |d(t)|<=D.
In particular, actual position error is at most R. No all-time tracking or filter
bound, and no all-time command-rate bound, may appear as an independent premise.
