NUMERICAL METHOD AND REFINEMENT

Physical state uses a nine-state augmented matrix exponential on every polynomial
piece. All switches, replans, readiness/observation times, truth changes, deadlines,
tails and horizons are events. No actual state resets occur. Per-case compressed
CSV files contain all reference and physical states, command, physical jerk, all
three errors, and both sides of events. Event CSV files give the jump identities.
Largest actual state jump is exactly zero in the implementation. Command-jump
identity residual is below 1.78e-15 m/s^2; the physical-jerk identity residual is
below 2.28e-13 m/s^3.

The .5 ms refinement changes no pass/fail decision. Maximum sampled jerk-peak
change is 4.04e-5 m/s^3. Jerk energy changes by at most .747% and command TV by
.0404%, relative to the fine result. Settling changes by at most .5 ms. Event
state differences are below 5.11e-16 m, 6.99e-15 m/s and 5.33e-14 m/s^2. Energy
uses trapezoidal quadrature. TV integrates |u'| on smooth pieces and adds every
command jump. This is refinement evidence, not a rigorous quadrature enclosure.

Raw reference derivatives at position jumps are not bounded impulses. Ruckig
jerk jumps produce command and physical-jerk jumps. For matched q,v,a,
Delta u=Delta j/96 and Delta physical_jerk=r*Delta u. The largest Ruckig physical
jerk jump is 18.75 m/s^3. FIR's theoretical corresponding jumps are zero; the
largest recorded residual is 6.40e-13 m/s^3. No finite snap peak is inferred from
grid differences across a jump.
