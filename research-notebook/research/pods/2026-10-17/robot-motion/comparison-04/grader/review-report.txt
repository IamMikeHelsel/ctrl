Decision: ACCEPT the fixed numerical gate. Grade B for its research scope.

Required qualification: this noise is especially favorable to the FIR
The target alternates every 20 ms. Its full period is 40 ms. The fixed box width
is 200 ms, exactly five periods. One box average therefore cancels the sustained
alternating target after its start transient. An exact rational check of this
fixture confirms that one-box output is zero throughout [0.42,1.02] seconds.
The four-box filter inherits this frequency zero. Its good hold result cannot
establish general rejection of irregular or off-grid jitter.

The FIR also has an 800 ms tail after a persistent accepted step and a 400 ms
mean delay. Its 20 mm step uses less reference speed, acceleration, and jerk than
the comparator's available limits. The reported lower energy buys a slower
response. Equal limit ceilings do not make response times equal. Raw target
steps provide a useful diagnostic baseline, but their impulsive reference
derivatives and large command peaks exclude them from the smooth-reference
contract. All primary packets that reach the jerk guard pass; removing that
guard would leave these primary FIR trajectories unchanged. The primary runs
therefore do not measure the guard's benefit. The rejected negative case remains
useful evidence of the admission rule's cost and limited scope.

The first six executed 20 ms intervals of the hold case alternate reference
jerk +7.5 and -7.5. The first positive interval reaches acceleration +.15 m/s^2.

Velocity therefore accumulates during this prefix. At .34 s, exact integration

that it ignores time, or that every replanning policy has this behavior.

Clock and phase limits
The native cumulative t_sum clock is preserved. The reviewer did not replace
it by a sum of exact binary durations. Two executed positive hold phases last
3/144115188075855872 and 3/576460752303423488 seconds. Both collapse under a
rounded double absolute clock; both remain distinct in the rational schedule
and the physical replay. The third sub-picosecond exported phase belongs to
the active-braking smoke fixture and is covered by its native reconstruction.

Native absolute-double execution and the canonical rational-commit replay are
not bit-identical. Across all four streams, 22 pointwise jerk-branch differences
occur within 1.288e-16 s of a canonical switch. State agreement does not erase
these branch differences. The review retains each discrepancy. The benchmark
therefore specifies a polynomial interpretation of native profiles, not exact
floating-runtime equivalence. Both sides of every reference event are retained;
small exported state mismatches enter the full command-jump formula. The actual
physical state is carried continuously and is never reset to the reference.

Retained failures and next test
The 50 mm proposal still has future reference jerk peak 12.5 > 7.5 and is
rejected, leaving its external goal unmet. At the short deadline, 400 ms after
acceptance, the 20 mm FIR change still reaches only 10 mm. These are reference
negative cases, not extra plant successes.

A duration-matched irregular-noise gate has higher priority than an 81-cell
exact hold proof for this periodic fixture. The latter would certify a case
whose cancellation is already special. Freeze irregular/off-grid packet times
and noise values before running them. Match step-response duration or report a
predeclared response-time/effort tradeoff. Include a simple target-smoothing
baseline and a same-FIR guard ablation. Keep the unchanged controller, external
goal error, actual jerk, command effort, and endpoint requirements visible.
No new parameter case, controller setting, width, or tolerance was introduced
in this review.
