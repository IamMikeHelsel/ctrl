PUBLICATION CLAIM TO TEST
Coordinator assessment, 18 October 2026 UTC. This is a research plan, not a
novelty finding or an accepted proof report.

The useful result would be a checked contract between changing target requests
and physical motion. The contract must say which requests can execute, which
future actions are already committed, how long the response can take, and which
physical errors remain bounded. A smooth reference alone is not the full result.

What the current candidate can support
The reference uses an established cascade of four moving-average filters. Its
jerk is piecewise affine. This structure permits an exact finite check: include
the current time and every later break point of every retained event. The Lean
package aims to prove that its rational Boolean test is equivalent to the
continuous-time jerk condition. Causal admission and the existing uncertain-lag
tracking theorem then connect accepted history to an explicit physical bound.

The possible contribution is that checked connection, its executable guard and
its failure cases. It is not the discovery of FIR smoothing, jerk-limited motion,
stateful replanning, asynchronous action execution, or the need for timing.
The certificate-03 package is still under builder verification at this writing.

What the fixed comparison can and cannot answer
Comparison-04 freezes 72 cases and separates internal tracking error from error
against the declared task goal. Independent numerical review is in progress.
The FIR reference has a tail of 800 ms. Ruckig seeks a fast bounded-jerk response.
A lower motion peak can therefore be purchased with delay. We must compare that
cost and preserve short-deadline failures. The primary examples do not establish
a benefit of admission itself if every changing target in those examples passes
the guard. In that case, ungated FIR produces the same reference. Admission earns
its value in explicit rejection and retained-commitment tests, not a new curve.

The packets are synthetic scalar targets. They are not measured outputs from a
learned policy, joint torques, coupled arm dynamics, contact, or a hardware test.
A claim about VLA task success requires an actual policy integration and task
benchmark. We should first finish the scalar guarantee and its fair comparison.

Useful next gates, in order
1. Independently accept or reject the current Lean certificate and 72 cases.
2. Bind the fixed hold-jitter reference to an all-time true-position bound in
   Lean. The existing controller is unchanged. Exact candidate arithmetic gives
   |q| <= 401/12000000 m and a tracking radius 41/320000 m, for a combined bound
   3877/24000000 m, below the 1/5000 m hold target. This requires proof of the
   actual quartic reference on every cell; a sampled graph is insufficient.
3. Freeze a duration-matched baseline comparison before execution. Use a stated
   endpoint deadline or response-time budget and no post-result tuning. Include
   ungated FIR as an identity check where admission accepts every update.
4. Choose one real action-stream integration only if the advantage survives the
   timing comparison. Measure task error, response delay, actual motion and
   failure rate together. Do not replace a failed task by a convenient reference.

A simple limit on any interpretation
The executor cannot infer hidden intent from identical input histories alone.
If two worlds present the same observations and target packets through time t,
a deterministic causal executor has the same output at t. If the worlds require
positions g1 and g2 then |g1-g2| <= |x-g1|+|x-g2|. At least one error is at least
half the separation. Thus a packet labeled as jitter by a benchmark can be a real
small target change in another task. The design needs a declared intent or delay
contract. This elementary argument is not a new research theorem and is not yet
a separate Lean result. It explains why smoothing cannot solve every mismatch.

- Biagiotti and Melchiorri, 2012, FIR Filters for Online Trajectory Planning with

  derivative constraints are established. The search engine exposed the author
  PDF abstract; direct PDF retrieval timed out in this refresh.

  generation. The publisher abstract and introduction describe arbitrary initial
  velocity/acceleration, fixed-time and time-optimal variants, and a Kuka test.
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S096706611730151X
- Biagiotti and Melchiorri, 2019, Trajectory generation via FIR filters: A procedure
  for time-optimization under kinematic and frequency constraints. Institutional
  metadata and abstract describe parameter selection and experimental tests.
  https://iris.unimore.it/handle/11380/1175126
- Black, Galliker and Levine, Real-Time Execution of Action Chunking Flow Policies,
  arXiv:2506.07339v2, 5 December 2025. The abstract describes freezing actions
  committed to execution and generating the rest asynchronously. Preserving
  committed actions is therefore not itself a new principle.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07339v2
- Zhao et al., VLA-RAIL, arXiv:2512.24673v1, 31 December 2025. The abstract describes
  polynomial smoothing and chunk fusion with position, velocity and acceleration
  continuity. A general claim to introduce a VLA smoothing layer is too broad.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24673v1
- Yu et al., When to Switch: Reliable Action-Chunk Extension for Vision-Language-
  Action Models, arXiv:2610.05719v1, 5 October 2026. The abstract treats subskill
  transition timing in longer action chunks. This is recent adjacent work on
  intent changes, not a proof that the present scalar guard is duplicated.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05719v1

These are scoped source readings, not an exhaustive novelty audit. Novelty must
be assessed against full methods and assumptions before a submission claim.
