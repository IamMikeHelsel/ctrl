Why command total variation changes

Command total variation changes by as much as 1.5625 m/s^2. This is a real change
in the declared piecewise polynomial metric, despite the tiny position change.
At a reference event, continuous actual state gives the exact identity
  delta_u = 800 delta_q+60 delta_qv+(3/2) delta_qa+delta_j/96.
An opposite-jerk microphase has two jerk jumps of magnitude 15 m/s^3. Its jerk
feedforward contributes 2*(15/96)=5/16=.3125 m/s^2 of command jump variation,
regardless of its positive duration.

The fast biased stream has ten such sub-picosecond phases in 05 and five in 06.
Its jerk-only jump variation therefore decreases by 25/16=1.5625 m/s^2. Exact
reference-join corrections change this delta by only -1.65e-16 m/s^2. Hold gains
two phases, sign_60ms loses two and irregular gains one. Their jump-variation
deltas are +.625,-.625 and+.3125 m/s^2. No phase was discarded to improve a metric.

The exact jump algebra matches all 144 independently replayed native-method
metric rows: largest jump-delta residual 9.99e-16 m/s^2; largest full decomposition
residual 3.39e-15 m/s^2. Maximum continuous-variation change is 6.88e-8 m/s^2.
Thus the large total-variation changes come from the jump part.

Finite q/v/a agreement does not imply exact jerk branching or invariant command
variation. These numbers are not sampled firmware effort or physical robot effort.
Show jump variation separately from continuous variation, position error and
energy. Do not claim that every effort measure is unchanged by the repair.

Exact-proof boundary

The exported histories are still not globally C2. Maximum q/v/a join gaps are
1.87e-18 m,5.30e-18 m/s and 3.86e-16 m/s^2. The exact binary irregular/fast cubic
exceeds acceleration .5 by 1/144115188075855872, about 6.94e-18 m/s^2. Configured
input limits are therefore distinct from exact bounds on exported polynomials.
A rounding-aware contract is needed before applying an exact smooth-reference
Lean theorem to these pieces.
