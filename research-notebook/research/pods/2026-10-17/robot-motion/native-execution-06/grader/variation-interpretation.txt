Command variation qualification

For the declared controller, actual x,v,a are continuous at a reference event.
Thus the exact command jump is
  delta_u = 800 delta_q + 60 delta_qv + (3/2) delta_qa + delta_j/96.
Each term has units m/s^2. Command jump total variation adds abs(delta_u).

An opposite-jerk microphase has jerk +7.5 between two -7.5 phases, or the reverse.
Each edge changes reference jerk by magnitude 15 m/s^3. The jerk-feedforward
command variation of the pair is 2*(15/96)=5/16 m/s^2. Its positive duration does
not appear in that jump contribution. The exact q/v/a join corrections in these
frozen schedules are tiny and are preserved in jump-variation.json.

The time-optimal biased stream has 10 such executed phases in 05 and 5 in 06.
Its jerk-only command jump variation therefore decreases by 25/16=1.5625 m/s^2.
The full exact reference-join formula changes this delta by only -1.65e-16 m/s^2.
Hold gains two phases, sign_60ms loses two, and irregular gains one. Their exact
jerk-only jump-variation changes are +.625, -.625, and +.3125 m/s^2 respectively.

These phases are shorter than a picosecond. They are not new target packets and
not a new control design. Their appearance depends on the floating-point retained
history and its exported near-zero native phases. The review preserves them;
it does not suppress them with an arbitrary duration threshold.

Finite q/v/a agreement does not bound jerk-branch disagreement, nor does it make
command total variation insensitive to tiny phases. The physical forcing here
is an explicitly declared piecewise polynomial interpretation. This audit is not
an estimate of command variation from a sampled firmware controller or a robot.
Do not claim every effort metric is unchanged when the clock model is repaired.
For comparisons, show the jump part separately from continuous variation and
retain position/energy measures alongside it. No new parameter test is implied.
