Exact change in execution contract

Before: 05 sampled each separately exported plan at a rational scheduler interval
and supplied that result as the next native plan's input. Its physical plan
origin was the rational scheduler commit. An extra native absolute-clock audit
failed an acceleration tolerance on sign_60ms/time-optimal Ruckig.

Now: native_execution.cpp retains the previous actual C++ trajectory. At each
commit it calls at_time using native subtraction of two binary absolute commit
times. That native q/v/a becomes the next native input. The same process exports
the resulting trajectory. No physical or polynomial replay state enters replans.

The physical reference is piecewise polynomial. Each plan's origin is the exact
rational value of its binary execution commit. Each phase boundary adds the
exact binary value of the exported native cumulative boundary. The original
rational scheduler record remains visible. The largest execution/scheduler
rounding offset is 3/28147497671065600 s, about 1.066e-16 s. This is a representation
offset, not an added transport delay or a timing guarantee at that resolution.

Raw and FIR schedules remain exactly equal to 05. Every native positive phase
and zero-duration record remains exported. A positive local duration can have
coincident native cumulative endpoints. One such phase occurs in a plan that
is superseded before that phase executes: biased_alternation, fast Ruckig,
commit 31/50, main phase 6, local duration 2^-58 s. It remains metadata; no artificial
physical interval is made by summing durations on another clock.

Small exported reference joins and all jerk/command jumps remain explicit.
Actual x/v/a are retained across every event. No actual-state reset occurs.
The piecewise reference is not claimed globally C2 at its small joins. Native
q/v/a agreement is separate from exact jerk-side selection or firmware fidelity.
