All positive native phase durations remain in the native export. If a positive
duration has equal native cumulative start and end, it has zero elapsed measure
under the declared cumulative-clock interpretation. Retain that metadata and
omit a zero-measure physical interval. Do not create an interval by summing phase
durations on a different clock. A decreasing cumulative boundary is a failed
source assumption and stops reference generation. Boundary jumps between the
remaining positive physical intervals remain explicit.

This clarifies "keep every positive phase" in contract.txt without changing its
input, state-carry, clock or fixed-tolerance requirements. The clarification is
recorded before native generation and before any physical run.

"Continuous polynomial model" means a model in continuous time with piecewise
polynomial forcing. It does not assert global C2 continuity. Tiny exported joins
are retained as explicit reference jumps, as the main contract requires.
