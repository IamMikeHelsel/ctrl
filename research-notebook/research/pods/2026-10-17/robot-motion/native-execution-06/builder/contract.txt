Changed native execution contract

Keep rational packet, ready and commit records unchanged. Convert each accepted
commit once to its nearest binary double, as the C++ execution time. Record both
the rational scheduler value and the exact binary execution value. For each
Ruckig method, one native C++ history owns its previous trajectory and calls
previous_trajectory.at_time(current_binary_commit-previous_binary_commit).
The subtraction and retained q/v/a are native double operations. Pass that actual
native state into the next native calculation. Export each resulting trajectory
inside that same process. No polynomial replay state enters a native replan.

For the physical polynomial interpretation, the absolute origin of a native
plan is the exact rational value of its binary execution commit. A phase begins
at that origin plus the exact binary value of the exported native cumulative
boundary. Do not sum exact binary durations to invent alternative boundaries.
Cut execution at the next exact binary commit, or the fixed rational horizon.
Keep original native t/t_sum, durations, q/v/a/j and their binary ratios. Keep
zero-duration metadata and every positive phase. Omit inactive brake arrays;
assert accel.duration=0. After a completed plan, retain native final acceleration
and its polynomial extrapolation; do not snap to the requested target.

This interpretation is a continuous polynomial model of the exported plans.
It is not bit-identical at_time execution for every real time. Native query
subtraction and rounding can select different jerk branches close to a boundary.
Record those differences separately from q/v/a agreement. Small reference-state
mismatches at profile boundaries and replans remain explicit jumps. Preserve
both sides and their command/actual-jerk effects. Never reset actual x/v/a.

Raw and FIR retain their prior exact-rational commit convention. The common
accepted timestamp stream and packet identities do not change. The native
execution clock maps those tick labels to binary values, which can lie on either
side by floating-point rounding. Record the maximum offset and do not interpret
it as a new transport delay or claim a timing guarantee below that resolution.
