MATHEMATICAL REVIEW

The primitive proves d/dx (max(x,0))^n = n(max(x,0))^(n-1) for every n>=2 and
every real x. At zero it uses the derivative-extension theorem with continuity
of the function and proposed derivative; away from zero it uses local equality
to the zero or polynomial branch. Thus the joining point is actually covered.

Kernel.lean applies that primitive to the exact five signed truncated powers.
It proves F0'=F1, F1'=F2 and F2'=F3 at every point, and all four functions are
continuous. This explicit derivative chain establishes the stated C3 property;
the package does not need to assume a regular unspecified spline. It derives
the six F3 branches and the left/right tails from the same F functions.

The unit conversion is also proved. Knots.eventJerk uses physical-time hinges
with delta/h^4. Reference.eventJerk_eq proves equality to delta F3((t-tau)/h)/h^3
when h>0. Reference then proves the actual position/velocity/acceleration chain
and continuous jerk for the finite summed history. No normalization factor is
missing and no unrelated jerk function is substituted into the controller.

future_knot_iff constructs its own finite set: c plus all five roots of every
event at or after c. For a non-knot t, it constructs the maximum eligible root
below t and minimum eligible root above t. Roots before c are handled directly;
every later root must belong to the constructed set. The hinge identity gives
affine interpolation on the resulting interval, followed by the absolute-value
bound. If no later root exists, all event tails have passed and jerk is zero.
The proof has no supplied partition or desired global-bound hypothesis. It
covers c between roots, repeated roots, empty histories, old active events,
future events, unsorted lists and the infinite tail.

check is executable over Rat. It validates h>0 and J>=0, then checks c and its
filtered rational roots. cast_qJerk proves the arithmetic bridge; cast_guardPoints
proves membership and order correspondence. check_real_iff establishes both
directions of the real all-future bound, for arbitrary real time. It does not
certify Python, floating-point input parsing, or an unrelated runtime checker.
The implementation retains signed sums and therefore preserves cancellation.

The induction is not circular. Invariant contains Safe, but initial_invariant
derives it from the empty list and J>=0. accepted_safe obtains the old-prefix
bound from zero new contribution before execution and the future bound from
check_real_iff. admit_invariant and run_invariant then propagate it. The final
finite_admission_safe has no assumed global jerk bound.

Admission time c and execution time tau are distinct. The executable causal
test rejects decreasing admission times, negative c, tau<c, or tau before the
last committed execution. Accepted events append to the complete committed
list, and their increment is relative to its final target. run_extends prevents
deleting queued events. Rejection, invalid timing and no-change retain history.
The analytic prefix lemma covers position and derivatives through jerk up to c;
run_prefix_jet preserves the prior executed prefix through later finite runs.

The target identity is proved by telescoping deltas. The invariant bounds every
accepted execution by lastExecution and proves that a nonempty history attains
that time. run_final_rest includes all queued commitments and gives the final
target with zero reference velocity, acceleration and jerk after lastExecution
plus 4h. empty_rest handles the empty history separately. These do not assert
physical rest or completion of a rejected request.

checked_tracking and admitted_tracking use the exact referenceState and jerk
in ScaledTracking.physicalInput. They discharge reference continuity, derivatives
and jerk from the new package. The physical ODE, uncertainty bounds, initial
transformed-cube condition, positive scale and budget remain explicit. Neither
composition theorem inserts a command filter or assumes a filter mismatch bound.
The actual initial state is not reset at reference events.

BOUNDARIES THAT REMAIN

submit verifies readiness, stale retention, watermark advancement on a new ready
sequence, and preservation of the motion invariant. It receives one winner from
an upstream ready-packet batch. It does not prove maximum-sequence selection or
conflicting-duplicate rejection. The producer must satisfy that interface.
The code cannot establish that caller-supplied current timestamps equal a real
clock. No sampled actuator implementation or measured deadline is certified.

The history is a finite list. There is no theorem about bounded online memory,
expired-event pruning, infinite event streams, or infinite switching in finite
time. Exactness of the history guard does not imply that every useful target
stream is admissible or that sequential admission can realize every bounded
final history. Rejection can prevent task progress.

The tracking conclusion concerns x-q under the classical plant assumptions. It
does not provide a true-goal tube for arbitrary inputs, a physical stopping time,
or the fixed endpoint speed/acceleration tolerances. The 144.975 m/s^3 item in
Composition is an arithmetic evaluation of a conservative expression, not a new
theorem that physical jerk equals or stays below the reference limit 7.5.
Continuous physical jerk still needs the extra regularity assumptions described
in preflight02. No plant identification or solution existence is proved here.

The four-box filter and retained-state architecture have prior art. Mike Helsel
receives credit for the problem framing and architecture direction. Acceptance
of this proof artifact does not establish general research novelty.
