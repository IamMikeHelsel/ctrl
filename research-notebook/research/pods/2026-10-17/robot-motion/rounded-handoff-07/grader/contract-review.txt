Independent contract review — rounded handoff 07

The generic theorem addresses a real gap in the previous application. Frozen06
exports have small, nonzero joins in reference position, velocity and acceleration.
The old finite_replans_scaled theorem required those joins to be exactly zero.
The new theorem allows them and accounts for their effect on the tracking error.

The mathematical mechanism is a finite hybrid-system bound. The transformed
error is linear. With an exactly matched actual state, a reference jump d changes
that error by -T_sigma(d). The triangle inequality gives the new radius R+B.
The old continuous-time theorem proves that each larger cube remains invariant.
The finite induction adds each join reserve once. This is not a novel general
principle by itself. The useful contribution here is the checked composition
with the actual feedback equations and the exported polynomial data.

The final generic theorem has IsTrajectory premises and invokes
tracking_cube_scaled internally. It does not assume the desired segment bound.
IsTrajectory means continuity of actual position, velocity and acceleration on
each closed piece, and the stated plant ODE on the open interior. The reference
has its own local derivative chain. No global C2 reference or derivative at a
switch is required. Actual state equality across each switch is explicit. It is
not supplied by the reference exporter and it must not be silently removed.

The correct transformed jump at sigma=10 is
  (dp, dp+dv/40, dp+dv/20+da/1600).
Each component has units of metres. Position matching alone is insufficient.
The error bound does not require a global reference velocity or acceleration
limit. Absolute state bounds require separate reference bounds. Thus the tiny
exact acceleration-limit excess found in06 does not invalidate this tracking
contract. It still invalidates an exact nominal acceleration-limit claim.

The constant radius choice starts at41/320000 metres. Its disturbance/jerk budget
is exact for W=1/100 m/s^2, J=15/2 m/s^3 and sigma=10/s. One 1e-15 metre allowance
per join gives at most0.128125000188 mm on the longest frozen schedule. The
1e-15 choice is a conservative bound derived from already frozen data, not a new
empirical-performance hypothesis. Every tiny positive executed phase must remain
in the source-bound list. Empty metadata phases are not invented as intervals.

The data checker must establish positive durations, jerk bounds, all transformed
joins, initial rest and chain endpoints from the exact same fields used to form
the real cubic references. A generic theorem that merely assumes those joins
would not finish the concrete binding. A list-index and rational-to-real bridge
is required. All-time results on each local piece are distinct from a formal
assembly of one global time-indexed plant. Exact start/end coverage is separately
checked by the rational audit unless a global coverage lemma is supplied.

This review cannot upgrade Gate05, prove native at_time evaluation for all real
times, establish firmware behavior, or prove that a real robot fits the plant.
It does not change the fixed hold-task failures. Tracking a reference closely
does not mean that the reference meets the user's task goal. It also does not
bound jump-sensitive command total variation by the small reference-state gaps.

Stop condition: accept the bounded mathematical bridge only after fresh local
closure compilation, axiom and kernel checks, meaningful negative controls, and
exact data binding pass. If the rational-to-real link is not completed, accept
the generic theorem and exact fixtures separately, and leave their composition
open. No new plant run or tuning is needed for this gate.
