ROUNDED HANDOFF 07 — WHAT THE THEOREM SAYS

For each piece, let f=(x,v,a) be the actual physical state and q=(q,q',q'')
the reference state. A piece has duration H_k >= 0. The actual state is continuous
on that piece and solves the stated third-order lag model on its open interior.
The reference is continuous on that piece and has the stated derivative chain
on its open interior. The physical state matches exactly at adjacent endpoints.
The reference can jump there.

The transformed jump is
  T_sigma(dp,dv,da) =
    (dp, dp+dv/(4 sigma), dp+dv/(2 sigma)+da/(16 sigma^2)).
Choose a nonnegative B_k that bounds each absolute transformed component.
Then the new transformed error is the old transformed error minus this jump.
The triangle inequality gives a new radius R+B_k.

The main theorem starts with R_0 >= 0 and uses
  R_k = R_0 + sum_{l<k} B_l.
It proves the transformed tracking cube for every time in every closed piece.
Both endpoint copies are covered. No derivative at a switch is required. There
is no premise or conclusion of global C2 reference continuity.

Plant conditions are explicit: sigma>0, 8 sigma <= r <= 12 sigma, |w|<=W,
|j|<=J, and W+J/(48 sigma)<=2 sigma^2 R_0. The physical command is the existing
ScaledTracking.physicalInput. Each larger radius still satisfies that budget.
The proof obtains each segment bound from tracking_cube_scaled and IsTrajectory;
it does not accept a segment invariance claim as a substitute for the plant ODE.

The physical tracking errors satisfy
  |x-q| <= R_k,
  |v-q'| <= 8 sigma R_k,
  |a-q''| <= 64 sigma^2 R_k.
Bounds on actual |x|, |v|, and |a| require separate reference bounds P,V,A.
The theorem then gives P+R_k, V+8 sigma R_k, and A+64 sigma^2 R_k.

The cubic specialization uses TrackingReplan.reference, its proved derivative
chain and continuity, with arbitrary real initial q/v/a and constant jerk.
It retains the physical trajectory, disturbance, rate, initial-error and exact
physical-state matching premises. A rational fixture bridge must discharge the
reference duration, jerk and join bounds from its actual data.

With sigma=10, W=1/100 and J=15/2, R_0=41/320000 m satisfies the budget exactly.
A uniform B=10^-15 m and 188 joins give R=0.128125000188 mm. This is a bound
relative to the reference. It is not a true-target or hold-band guarantee.

The reserve sum ignores decay and cancellation. The result is finite-horizon and
conditional on the stated physical model. It proves neither physical trajectory
existence, machine execution, native double arithmetic nor full robot-arm safety.
No new planner, experimental result, or superiority claim is made in this gate.
