What is already established

SmoothReplan.lean certifies scalar command splices that match position, velocity,
and acceleration. It gives a reference jerk bound over finite histories. It does
not certify physical jerk, task completion, or an arbitrary learned chunk stream.

CommandSmoothing.lean transfers the old tracking cube only when an all-time filter
mismatch bound is supplied. It does not prove that a filter maintains that bound.
The old forced screen rejects the 0.2 mm claim in several filtered-controller
cases. Its references do not stop. Admission-04 proves a separate acceleration
deadline limitation; it does not establish a new robot motion smoother. This pod
does not duplicate that stopping/reference task.

The new candidate leaves the direct controller unchanged. Its reference is C3,
meaning position has three continuous derivatives. Each new target enters a
finite-memory history. The resulting reference jerk is continuous and affine
between a finite set of known times. Checking those times is therefore the
proposed exact all-time admission test. The proof obligation is still open.

Exact evidence produced here

The Python scripts use rational arithmetic. They verify the kernel's piecewise
polynomials, derivative joins and constants. They check every proposed update
against future jerk knots. They also generate sufficient Bernstein enclosures
for the fixed references' speed, acceleration and jerk. These are reference
calculations, not physical measurements or a completed Lean theorem.

Synthetic case             Reference jerk peak       Reference tail time
Hold jitter               3/80 = .0375 m/s^3          1.82 s
Persistent 20 mm goal      5 m/s^3                    1.02 s
Revised estimates         403/80 = 5.0375 m/s^3       1.28 s
Late goal, stale packet    5 m/s^3                    1.12 s

All primary proposals pass the fixed 7.5 m/s^3 jerk gate. The hold-jitter stream
has local total variation of 80 mm. A simple 30 mm variation gate would fail it.
The exact history check preserves the cancellation and passes it. This is a
useful reason to prove the exact guard, rather than formalize only a loose bound.

Two failures are retained. A 50 mm proposal requires a 12.5 m/s^3 reference jerk
peak with the fixed filter width, so the gate rejects it and leaves the external
goal unmet. A 20 mm goal with only 400 ms after acceptance reaches 10 mm at that
deadline. Smoothing delay is part of the task result.

The inherited tracking budget passes at sigma=10/s, disturbance .01 m/s^2,
reference jerk 7.5 m/s^3, and position radius .2 mm. Conditional composition must
still be proved. The corresponding simple physical-jerk bound is 144.975 m/s^3,
far above the reference limit. Thus the certificate does not by itself show a
useful actual-jerk magnitude. The fixed simulation must measure it. Physical
jerk continuity additionally requires continuous actuator rate and disturbance.

Closest prior work and novelty boundary

LiPo already post-processes learned chunks using overlap blending and jerk
minimization. VLA-RAIL already models asynchronous timing and claims matching
position, velocity, and acceleration at fusion. RTC and Legato already preserve
or learn continuation. Recent spline policies and persistent-noise methods add
other forms of retained state. sources.json gives versioned primary sources and
the inspected sections. A stateful VLA smoother is not an unoccupied category.

FIR trajectory generation already derives smooth polynomial motion from moving
average cascades. Ruckig already provides online jerk-constrained trajectories
from complete states. Our possible artifact adds an exact finite guard on the
accepted history, a proof of prefix preservation and tail behavior, and checked
composition with the specific uncertain-lag tracker. Those would be new artifacts
in this workspace. Their research novelty is not established.

The quantitative gate compares raw targets, the real local Ruckig implementation,
and this fixed FIR reference using identical targets and the identical controller.
It reports internal tracking, true endpoint error, actual jerk, command variation,
packet age, and delays. Ruckig may perform better. No gain or deadline changes
are permitted within the fixed gate. The input streams are synthetic throughout.

The current filtered-controller plots use aligned timestamps. A lightly damped
closed-loop response after adding a filter is not evidence of a stale-frame bug.
Timing mismatch and filter-induced dynamics are distinct failure mechanisms.
The broader cruise-control hunting analogy motivates tests; it is not a diagnosis
of every plotted trajectory or proof of a shared mechanism.

Open items for the ultra independent assessor

1. Prove or reject exact future-knot sufficiency, including c inside an interval,
   duplicate knots, empty history, the infinite tail, and finite update induction.
   Derive C3 regularity from the actual F. Prove a rational executable guard and
   its cast to the real reference; a finite predicate over reals is not enough.
2. Check that the C3 reference and the inherited direct-controller hypotheses
   connect without a hidden filter-error or state-reset premise.
3. Check fixed-stream semantics, endpoint tolerances and Ruckig fairness. Builder
   must pin and hash its local source and expose all jerk-switch events.
   If public segment events are unavailable, label stepwise evaluation separately
   and leave exact-event validation open. Do not invent switch times.
4. Judge whether the artifact is worth completing as a verification benchmark.
   A publication claim needs stronger evidence and a closer novelty audit.

The downstream gate needs an identified full-arm model and a recorded learned
stream, with joint/Cartesian mapping, limits, torque, singularity and contact
conditions. This first batch does not install MuJoCo or make a dexterous-arm,
industrial-controller, human-motion, or general safety claim.
