Proof, numerical method and limits

The FIR ablation is a finite exact input/history identity. All six histories
and all 246 prefixes agree with unguarded FIR, from the same initial history
and h. Their deterministic reference sums are therefore identical. This does
not count as another physical method and shows no benefit from the guard on
these accepted streams. Exact rational guard and enclosure transcripts are
retained. This batch supplies no new Lean theorem or general implementation proof.

Actual state is propagated with the event-polynomial augmented matrix
exponential. All reference switches, commits, truth events, deadlines and
horizons are included. Energy and continuous command variation use composite
trapezoidal quadrature. Analytic one-sided command jumps are added to command
variation. Both sides of reference/command/actual-jerk jumps are saved. Raw
position steps have impulsive reference derivatives; their between-step zero
derivative fields are not bounded impulse claims. No finite snap peak is assigned
to a jerk jump.

At .5 ms versus 1 ms, all pass/fail decisions stay the same. The maximum hold
peak change is 7.7404e-8 m. The maximum event-state changes in x/v/a are
5.10e-16 m, 6.95e-15 m/s and 5.62e-14 m/s^2. The largest jerk-energy relative
change is .7467%, largely relevant to the raw discontinuous forcing. These
checks are within one builder. Continuous-time peak certification and independent
high-precision physical validation remain open. The 72 rows for the original
two streams and three original methods match comparison-04 exactly.

The plant is one scalar axis with a constant first-order actuator rate, constant
disturbance, retained actual state and the stated unsaturated controller. It has
