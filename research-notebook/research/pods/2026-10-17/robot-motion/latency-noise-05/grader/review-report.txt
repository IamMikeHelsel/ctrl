LATENCY-NOISE-05 — INDEPENDENT REVIEW

Decision: ACCEPT the frozen canonical polynomial numerical results only.
REJECT acceptance of the full native-clock comparison gate. Its fixed acceleration
limit fails on two stream/method pairs. Do not describe this as a repaired gate.

Grade: B for this research milestone. The review gives reproducible negative
results and a useful comparison. This grade is not publication readiness or a
probability. The batch adds no Lean theorem and no learned-arm or hardware result.

What was independently checked

The reviewer reconstructed all six inputs, packet decisions, exact jerk guards,
accepted histories, truth signals and required event times. All 246 packets and
all 246 prefix guards accept. Guarded and unguarded FIR histories are identical.
The review rebuilt all 24 schedules. It used the pinned native executable to
reproduce 492 plans from their recorded input states, plus six smoke fixtures.
All 246 minimum-duration plans return exactly .8 s in these frozen histories.
This does not make .8 s a universal returned duration or equal physical latency.

The physical replay covers all 144 parameter cases at both resolutions: 288
traces, 1,502,784 saved rows and 21,288 one-sided event rows. It uses an independent
solution method: an exact rational polynomial particular solution plus an
80-digit 3-by-3 homogeneous matrix exponential. The primary solver uses a
larger augmented matrix exponential. No primary simulated state was imported.
Actual x,v,a start at zero and carry through all events without a reset.

The reviewer treats each frozen binary reference coefficient as an exact rational
number. It treats disturbance .01 as exactly 1/100; this differs negligibly from
the primary binary representation. The polynomial differential-equation residual
is checked exactly before numerical propagation. This is high-precision numerical
evidence, not an interval enclosure or a kernel-checked plant proof.

Every task decision agrees. Largest row discrepancies are 4.01e-16 m in x,
5.32e-15 m/s in v, 5.20e-14 m/s^2 in a, 6.41e-14 m/s^2 in command, and
3.60e-12 m/s^3 in actual jerk. Largest jerk-energy metric difference is 4.92e-11
m^2/s^5. Settling times agree exactly on both sampled grids. Both implementations
use trapezoidal quadrature, so agreement does not remove quadrature error.
Command and actual-jerk jump formulas agree; physical state jumps remain zero.

The original periodic input favors FIR: h=.2 s is exactly five .04 s periods.
The irregular input has zero arithmetic mean but time-weighted mean -1/2600 m
(-.384615 mm). Positive targets last .32 s and negative targets .72 s. It is a

has mean -.01095 mm; it is one realization, not a distribution-wide result.
The explicit biased input has mean +.4 mm. No input was changed during review.

For the isolated 20 mm step, settling after commit is .2835-.2840 s for raw,
.4695-.4700 s for time-optimal Ruckig, .7975-.7980 s for minimum-duration Ruckig,
and .7435-.7440 s for FIR. Settling lasts only through the fixed 2 s horizon.
FIR uses far less jerk energy here and on the noise cases, but fails some hold
requirements that the duration-limited baseline passes. Delay, physical effort,
and task error need separate comparison. Raw steps have impulsive reference
derivatives. The all-accepted guard shows no measured benefit in this batch.

Native-clock failures

The independent native review covers all 12 stream/method pairs, including the
seven pairs not run by the stopped builder: 84,120 queries and 492 retained-state
commits. Ten pairs pass the fixed q/v/a limits. Two fail acceleration 1e-10 m/s^2.
No limit changed. All observed q and v differences remain below their 1e-8 limits.

1. sign_60ms / time-optimal Ruckig reproduces the builder failure. At
   t=.38219205429988534 s, acceleration differs by 3.5708696111e-9 m/s^2.
   A -1.6653e-16 m/s^2 retained acceleration difference at commit .34 s changes
   a very short terminal phase. The canonical phase lasts 1.0846 ns; the native
   phase lasts .8465 ns. Both jerks are -7.5 at the witness. This is not just
   selecting opposite sides of one common jerk jump. More than 1 microsecond
   from canonical boundaries, maximum sampled acceleration difference is
   2.53e-15 m/s^2 in this stream.

2. biased_alternation / minimum-duration Ruckig is a new failed witness. At the
   ordinary grid time 1.824 s, native acceleration is -.09838079044986006 and
   canonical acceleration is -.09838086461314689 m/s^2. Their difference is
   7.4163286831e-8 m/s^2, 136 microseconds from the nearest canonical boundary.
   Both jerks are +7.5. At the final 1.82 s commit, the retained acceleration
   already differs by -7.28236e-8 after 80 replans. The saved per-commit series
   records growth and contraction of these small execution differences. This
   finite trace does not prove general numerical instability or unsafe motion.

The canonical clock uses rational commit intervals and exported native cumulative
phase boundaries. The native replay subtracts absolute double commit times and
carries its own at_time state. Both paths use the same targets and source pin.
The fresh per-plan exports reproduce both witnesses. The physical significance
of the alternative native clock remains untested; it is not declared harmless.

The review retains 14 executed positive phases shorter than 1 ps; 12 collapse
when converted to absolute double time. It also records 324 jerk-side differences
at queries within 6.04e-16 s of canonical boundaries. These remain visible and
are separate from the two failed acceleration checks.

Formal proof boundary

Every frozen Ruckig history has nonzero q/v/a join gaps from binary rounding.
Global maximum gaps are 1.86e-18 m, 3.89e-18 m/s and 3.85e-16 m/s^2. They are
numerically small but do not satisfy exact C2 smoothness. The exact binary cubic
for irregular/time-optimal also exceeds acceleration .5 by 1/72057594037927936,
about 1.39e-17 m/s^2. This is a formal-bridge qualification, not evidence of a
material physical limit violation. Do not apply a smooth-reference Lean theorem
to these exported pieces without accounting for joins and rounding.

those pieces. Keep both failures in this gate. Compare task classifications and

After that check, a new noise experiment can separately freeze a time-balanced
irregular stream. The current irregular data must remain labeled and preserved.
A proof for the favorable periodic example is lower priority than resolving the
execution contract and the performance tradeoff already visible here.
