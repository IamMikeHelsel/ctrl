CLOSE WORK FOUND DURING THE FRANKA PREPARATION

Scope: bounded primary-source check on 18 October 2026 UTC. This is not an exhaustive
novelty review. No author was contacted, no method was reproduced, and no
comparison result is inferred from an abstract. Earlier FIR/governor/ModelPlex,
Ruckig, RTC and VLA-RAIL sources remain relevant.

Langella et al., Keeping the Franka Emika Panda alive, 20 August 2026.
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19740v1
Read sections 2–4 and 6. The authors attribute position-interface artifacts to
external-loop timing and sampling jitter. They test asynchronous communication,
rate matching, and position-domain reference generation on Panda hardware.
They explicitly leave formal guarantees open. This is a close practical
baseline. Our possible addition is a checked execution/task contract under
explicit assumptions; we have not proved their algorithm or its hardware behavior.

Black, Galliker and Levine, Real-Time Execution of Action Chunking Flow Policies.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07339v2
Abstract and metadata rechecked. RTC keeps actions that must execute and
generates the remaining chunk asynchronously. It already addresses the central
committed-prefix idea. The existing manuscript cites it. Rebranding that idea
as new would be incorrect.

Yun et al., GROOVE, 12 September 2026.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13695v1
Abstract checked. The regulator uses delivered commands as boundary data,
optimizes jerk, and constrains cumulative path deviation. It reports LIBERO and
UR5e experiments. This is a high-priority comparison for any broad execution-layer
claim. Our scalar FIR study does not establish superiority over it. Full method,
code, path constraints and evaluation protocol still need a reproduction review.

Wang et al., Real-Time Robot Execution with Masked Action Chunking, 27 January 2026.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20130v1
Abstract checked. REMAC addresses mismatch between perception and action within
a chunk, alongside continuity between chunks. The implication for us: correct
curve joins do not resolve stale observations or incorrect task intent. This is
important for the learned-policy stage, which tonight's synthetic goals omit.

Guo et al., SplineWAM, 30 September 2026.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39873v1
Abstract checked. It adapts action horizons and preserves committed decoded
actions through a spline representation. It is another close adjacent work;
continuity of executed actions is already an active research topic. Treat its
reported gains as the authors' results, not evidence for this project.

Research decision
Do not pitch a newly discovered general cause or universal cure for VLA jitter.
Continue toward a narrow, reproducible claim: checked reference admission and
physical task bounds that expose infeasible timing and retained commitments.
The present mathematics is an artifact candidate; publication novelty needs
expert comparison. A Franka result must add useful behavior to capable existing
execution stacks. The missing formal guarantees in one paper do not establish
that a formalization is itself novel or sufficient for publication.

Mike's contribution remains the task framing, the timing/motion-history concern,
the requirement to count useful progress, and the Franka demonstration target.
This note records sources that constrain our claims; it does not erase those
contributions or assert scientific priority over the cited authors.
