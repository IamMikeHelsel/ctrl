Stop treating numerically rank-four equality points as prospective strict

2. Same-square comparison: independent hand derivation, NOT Lean-checked

Write L_A(X)=AX+XA^T and E_A(X)=||L_A(X)||_F^2. If S is symmetric, K is skew, and
S^2=-K^2, expansion gives

  E_A(S)-E_A(K) = 2 tr((AS)^2 + (AK)^2).                    (1)

The reason is that SS^T=KK^T, so the two terms containing A^T A cancel; the
remaining cross terms have opposite signs for symmetric and skew inputs.

Put K in real orthogonal skew canonical form diag(lambda_i J,0), where
J=[[0,1],[-1,0]]. Choose symmetric reflections

  R(theta)=[[cos(theta),sin(theta)],[sin(theta),-cos(theta)]],
  S=diag(lambda_i R(theta_i),0).

Then S^2=-K^2 and ||S||_F=||K||_F. Let B_ij be the 2x2 blocks of A on the support
of K. Formula (1) becomes exactly

  2 sum_i lambda_i^2 [tr(B_ii R_i)^2 + tr(B_ii J)^2]
  +4 sum_{i<j} lambda_i lambda_j
       [tr(B_ij R_j B_ji R_i)+tr(B_ij J B_ji J)].           (2)

For the diagonal terms, use the 2x2 identity
tr(M^2)=(tr M)^2-2 det M, together with det R=-1 and det J=1.
Every diagonal contribution in (2) is nonnegative.

Rank at most four: there are at most two active blocks, hence only one cross
term. Apply a signed 2x2 SVD to B_12 using rotations U,V in SO(2). Such rotations
preserve J. In the new coordinates B_12 is diagonal. Choose R_1=R_2=X, where
X=[[0,1],[1,0]]. For any diagonal D and any N,

  tr(D X N X) + tr(D J N J) = 0.

Thus the cross term vanishes and (2) is nonnegative. For a rank-two witness there
is no cross term. Coupling to the orthogonal complement is harmless: if
K=diag(K_support,0) and A=[[B,C],[D,E]], then

  E_A(K) = E_B(K_support)+2||D K_support||_F^2.

The same-square symmetric choice has exactly the same D contribution. C and E
do not enter either expression.

Provisional conclusion, requiring peer or formal verification before use as an
established project theorem: every real skew witness of rank at most four has a
symmetric same-norm witness with at least its Lyapunov amplification, in ANY
ambient dimension. This is a clear candidate for a useful Lean formalization,
but it is not a new mathematical discovery claim.

Independent numerical checks: 400 random examples in ambient dimensions 4,6,7,10
verified the signed-SVD construction and the exact formula underlying it; the
maximum floating-point formula residual was 1.94e-13. This checks implementation
consistency only; it is not a proof of the universal statement.

Important failed extension: the three-block same-square ansatz cannot be a
universal proof strategy. The published 7x7 skew witness has rank six. Its support
is six-dimensional; any same-square symmetric comparator in that support has the
same outside-support contribution. A universal three-block same-square lemma
would therefore contradict the published exact dimension-seven counterexample.
The six-dimensional conjecture can still hold because a successful symmetric
comparator is allowed to have a DIFFERENT square/covariance. Do not silently impose
S^2=-K^2 as a lossless restriction in the rank-six problem.

3. Coupled-pair reduction: algebra now checked in Lean

For a positive skew singular value sigma, scale A by 1/sigma. Let K,Y be real skew
right/left singular witnesses with equal nonzero Frobenius norm. Their equations
are

  A K + K A^T = Y,     A^T Y + Y A = K.                   (3)

If the provisional rank-four exclusion is confirmed, a strict dimension-six
counterexample must have both K and Y invertible. For Y this applies the same
argument to the adjoint operator L_(A^T), whose symmetric and skew restricted
norms equal those for A. This explains why rank deficiency is a useful diagnostic.

Assume K invertible. Define H=A K-Y/2. From the first equation in (3), H^T=H, and

  A=(H+Y/2) K^{-1}.

Substitution of this expression into the second equation and multiplication on
the left and right by K gives the LINEAR equation in H

  (K Y) H - H (Y K) = K^3 -(Y^2 K+K Y^2)/2.              (4)

This derivation is reversible for invertible K. However, our current Lean file
formalizes the forward polynomial identity, not the full inverse/iff statement.
For division-free statements it uses B=2H=A K-K A^T:

  (K Y) B - B (Y K) = 2 K^3 -Y^2 K-K Y^2.              (5)

New file: lean-controls/LeanControls/CoupledLyapunov.lean
Six theorem declarations compile without warnings. Each was independently
axiom-audited; only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound occur.

Checked results: B is symmetric when K is skew; B+Y=2AK from the first equation;
the elimination identity (5); the commutator identity below; and its trace moments
for every natural j.

  [Y K,A^T]=Y^2-K^2,
  tr((Y K)^j (Y^2-K^2))=0 for every j>=0.                 (6)

No claim that three moments suffice is made. No generic-density argument, generic
rank theorem, or spectral multiplicity assertion has been imported from the lead.

4. A fixed-pair convex subproblem and exact calibration

For fixed K,Y, solve (4) exactly as a linear system in the 21 independent entries
of H. If consistent, write H=H0+sum z_i H_i. The symmetric restricted operator
matrix B_S(A(H)) is affine in z. Its spectral norm is at most t exactly when

  [[t I_21, B_S], [B_S^T, t I_21]] is positive semidefinite.

This yields a convex problem for EACH fixed pair. A validated feasible t<1 gives
a strict counterexample, because (3) already provides skew gain 1. An exact dual
lower bound >=1 excludes just that pair. A numerical infeasibility or lower bound
is insufficient without a certificate. The continuum of compatible K,Y, including
degeneracies, is the unresolved global problem.

Use K=Y=diag(J,2J,3J) as a nonempty exact calibration. In this case the compatible
A are exactly block diagonal, with each 2x2 block having trace one. To see why,
write the off-diagonal equations for M=A_ij and N=A_ji^T, with distinct nonzero
weights a,b:

  b M J+a J N=0,    b N J+a J M=0.

Eliminating N yields (a^2-b^2) J M=0, hence M=N=0. On a diagonal block either
coupled equation is simply tr(A_ii)=1. Therefore the affine dimension is 3*3=9.

Parametrize A_ii=[[p_i,q_i],[r_i,1-p_i]]. The symmetric input I provides

  ||I||_F^2=6,
  ||A+A^T||_F^2=6+2 sum_i [(2p_i-1)^2+(q_i+r_i)^2] >=6.  (7)

Thus the normalized symmetric gain is at least one throughout this family. The
choice A=I/2 attains one, so the fixed-pair optimum is exactly one. This is a
calibration family, not an unresolved result or a universal dimension-six proof.

5. Suggested next bounded experiment

First formalize the rank-four same-square exclusion, preferably with an
independent review of the signed-SVD step. Next formalize the invertible-K converse
of (4). Then implement a small exact linear-system generator for arbitrary rational
K,Y, retaining all 21 symmetric variables until the rank has actually been checked.
Use the calibration family above to test primal and dual certificate verification.

Only after those checks should we sample compatible full-rank pairs or perturb
singular pairs from a search. Normalize their common Frobenius norm, record the
smallest singular values of both witnesses, and avoid confusing near-rank-four
equalities with progress toward a strict gap. A barrier against small singular
values is a search heuristic; it does not certify coverage or exclude tiny gaps
near the boundary. Do not announce novelty or a resolution from these reductions.
