Checked 2026-10-17. Bounded research only; no submissions, accounts, outreach or spending.

Decision
Keep controls first and a bounded Erdős contribution second. None of the giant
fixed-problem prizes currently supplies an evidence-based 24–48-hour solve plan
for this project. An independently replayed uncertain-actuator safety theorem
would be a legitimate nearer result; it would not qualify for these famous
problem prizes. Prize size does not measure research tractability.

CURRENT CLAY ROSTER — THE USUAL “SIX OPEN” SUMMARY NEEDS QUALIFICATION

The original seven each have US$1 million allocated. Clay's current official
roster separates five Unsolved, one Active, and one Solved:

Problem                              Official current category / question
Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer             Unsolved; elliptic-curve rank and L-function.
Hodge                                Unsolved; algebraic cycles and Hodge classes.
P versus NP                          Unsolved; efficient verification vs solution.
Riemann hypothesis                   Unsolved; nontrivial zeta zeros on Re(s)=1/2.
Yang–Mills existence and mass gap     Unsolved; rigorous quantum theory and gap.
Navier–Stokes existence/smoothness    Active; announced resolution under evaluation.
Poincaré                             Solved; Perelman's three-manifold result.

This status is taken from Clay itself, not a social-media claim. On September11,
2026, Clay stated Navier–Stokes had “apparently been settled”, while emphasizing
its evaluation and attribution process would be deliberately unhurried. This
screen did not inspect that proof and does not assert a prize has been awarded.
Do not market Navier–Stokes as a vacant first-solution target, or call the
announced resolution independently validated by this project.

Clay requires publication in a Qualifying Outlet, at least two years after
publication, and general acceptance in the global mathematics community before
consideration. It does not accept direct proposed-solution submissions. A Lean
file or preprint alone does not satisfy these conditions. The prize program has
no overall solution deadline. Rules determine attribution and payment; an
accepted-looking proof does not itself establish a payment entitlement.

Fit judgment, not a solve probability:
- Navier–Stokes is closest in physical subject to controls, but our finite-
  dimensional, closed-form ODE certificates are not a route to the full PDE
  problem. The announced resolution also changes priority status materially.
- P vs NP connects to algorithms and proof complexity, but a useful certified
  algorithm or faster finite search does not resolve its universal question.
- Yang–Mills is not made accessible by studying classical controller stability;
  the quantum-field existence and mass-gap obligations are different.
- Riemann, BSD and Hodge require deep new work outside our demonstrated pipeline.
  Bounded library formalizations could be useful without being prize solutions.

SELECTIVE OTHER LARGE OFFERS

1. Beal conjecture — US$1,000,000 fixed-problem prize; sponsor still advertises it.
   For positive integers A,B,C,x,y,z with x,y,z>2, A^x+B^y=C^z should imply
   that A,B,C have a common prime factor. A complete proof or counterexample
   is the objective. The sponsor's page identifies the money as held by AMS.
   AMS's published procedures require a respected refereed mathematics
   publication and reject direct unpublished submissions.
   Evidence limitation: live AMS prize/rules pages returned403 in this pass.
   The sponsor page was fetched; AMS's official2014Council record and2023
   treasurer report corroborate the restricted prize fund and rules. No current
   payment/resolution notice was verified. Do not use UNT's obsolete $50,000
   page as current terms, or aggregator descriptions of a nomination award.
   Fit: explicit finite counterexamples are easy to verify, but no promising
   counterexample family or proof mechanism has been established here.
   https://www.bealconjecture.com/
   https://www.ams.org/profession/prizes-awards/ams-supported/beal-prize-rules
   https://www.ams.org/about-us/governance/council-meetings/council-minutes0114.pdf
   https://www.ams.org/journals/notices/202308/rnoti-p1296.pdf

2. Collatz — ¥120,000,000 advertised by Bakuage, not a fixed US$1 million.
   The sponsor's live page still links the July7,2021 terms as Latest. They
   specify validity to July6,2031, subject to earlier expiry/withdrawal. A full
   proof or disproof, qualifying publication, at least two years and community
   acceptance are required. Direct submissions are not accepted. The sponsor
   retains broad discretion to decline consideration, reduce awards or withdraw
   terms/decisions; no segregated fund was verified. Japanese controls if the
   translation differs. No withdrawal was posted on the inspected English page.
   Fit: enormous finite verification remains insufficient for universal
   convergence; we have no demonstrated new mathematical mechanism. Keep
   currency in yen rather than repeating its2021US-dollar conversion.
   https://mathprize.net/posts/collatz-conjecture/
   https://mathprize.net/files/collatz-conjecture-rule-en-20210707.pdf

3. Proximity Prize — advertised US$1 million PROGRAM for Reed–Solomon proximity.
   This is not $1million for any arbitrary Lean theorem or leaderboard increment.
   Current terms close eligibility December31,2027 at23:59UTC, require reputable
   scientific peer-review acceptance and public academic-repository availability,
   and require truthful human authorship/AI disclosure. Significant partial
   progress can be recognized; allocations and awards remain discretionary and
   are payable only after an authorized written Award Notice. Its terms expressly
   exclude separate platform challenges unless stated otherwise; better.codes
   has its own rules. Current primary landing-page title advertises $1M; the

   Fit: most aligned giant-prize program with our search/certificate/Lean workflow,
   but the existing local audit ruled out the obvious rounding and parameter
   tweaks. No new winning inequality or record has been found. Realistic first
   output is a pinned replay and a reusable construction; record improvement
   still needs a mathematical idea. No next-day expiry exists here.
   https://proximityprize.org/
   https://proximityprize.org/terms/

ATTAINABILITY AND ORDER

A defensible near-term result: finish the actual uncertain-rate clipped actuator
execution theorem with the original travel region, sampling and error budget;
independently replay it; document novelty and limitations; have Mike supply
substantive engineering specification, evaluation and interpretation. Then a
bounded Erdős #128 graph-bridge contribution, with the BCL hypothesis explicit,
is more grounded than starting any giant conjecture merely because of its prize.
The reward for these steps may be a useful artifact/publication rather than cash.
No solve probability or expected income is justified by the current evidence.

This is a selective source-backed screen, not an exhaustive worldwide prize
