1. Lean Kernel Challenge, Stage 1 — strongest bounded project fit

Published window: registration/team formation from August 26; launch September
15; submission cutoff November 20, 2026, 23:59 AoE. Stage 2 is planned for December
with exact date pending. Cash amount: absent from the reviewed official overview
and organizer announcement. Individuals or teams may register with a SAIR account,
required profile, and agreement to competition terms. Each individual or
organization may join only one team; members and sponsors must be registered.
No academic-only restriction appears in these public rules. Entrants pay their
own computation costs. These are the published rules, not a completed account
eligibility check. Source:
https://github.com/SAIRcompetition/lean-kernel-challenge/blob/main/rules/overview.md

Eight independently ranked problems include Fibonacci, partitions, prime counting,
matrix permanent, and polynomial discriminant. Submit a total implementation and
a Lean proof of agreement with the fixed specification for every input. Only one
human-readable Submission.lean is submitted, at most 1 MiB. Kernel instruction
counts determine ranking; compilation alone is not official acceptance or scoring.
Source: https://github.com/SAIRcompetition/lean-kernel-challenge

Lean 4.33.1 and locked dependencies are required; our controls project's 4.35 setup
cannot simply be substituted. Allowed axioms are the standard three used in our
audit. No sorry/native_decide/unsafe/partial or precomputed input-specific answers.
Packed-input specifications cover their fixed generators, not arbitrary matrices.
Source: https://github.com/SAIRcompetition/lean-kernel-challenge/blob/main/rules/problems/README.md

Assessment (inference): a credible small contribution is one algorithm or data
representation improvement with a universal correctness proof. Start by replaying
the supplied partition/permanent examples, then measure a single optimization.
Polynomial discriminants offer longer-term exact-algebra reuse but more proof
overhead. Stop the spike if no measurable gain survives the official-style kernel
replay. This is a practical verification/performance opportunity, not evidence
of an available cash prize or a likely competition win.

2. Andrews–Curtis Conjecture Challenge — bounded constructions, harder research

Both tracks opened September 11; deadline November 30, 2026 AoE. Registration,
one-team-per-individual-or-organization, declared members/sponsors, and own-compute
rules resemble Lean Kernel. No academic-only restriction or cash schedule appears
in the reviewed official overview. Source:
https://github.com/SAIRcompetition/Andrews-Curtis/blob/main/competition/rules/overview.md

Discovery asks for explicit short move sequences on 10,115 presentations, scored
separately for AC and Stable AC. The stable contest permits at most eight generators;
this is a contest bound, not the unrestricted stable conjecture. Only shortest
verified paths earn points; 424 training presentations are unscored. Limits include
100,000 moves/path, 500 lines/file, and 40 submissions/team/day. Source:
https://github.com/SAIRcompetition/Andrews-Curtis/blob/main/competition/rules/discovery.md

Proof Track accepts partial results and ideas; Lean is optional. It requires clear
established/open boundaries and citations. A linked Lean repository needs a full
commit hash. Submissions are automatically published with contributor information
and history after sharing consent; listing does not certify correctness. Source:
https://github.com/SAIRcompetition/Andrews-Curtis/blob/main/competition/rules/proof.md

Assessment (inference): selecting a small presentation family, finding shorter
paths, and producing replayable certificates is tractable as a bounded experiment.
Formalizing a useful move invariant or checker could be reusable, but neither
would resolve the general conjecture. Compare with existing examples/leaderboards
before claiming a new contribution. No solve probability is supportable here.

3. Inverse Galois — Stage 2 announced, concrete new entry terms unverified

SAIR's September 21 statement announces XTX support for a fall Stage 2 with LMFDB.
It supplies no cash amount, exact Stage 2 dates, or technical entry specification:
https://blog.sair.foundation/an-open-commitment-to-mathematics-and-science/

The publicly available IGP24 rules are the earlier June 16–August 15, 2026 round;
the official evaluation page labels that competition Finished. It accepts explicit
monic degree-24 integer polynomials; Magma verifies groups/signatures and PARI/GP
computes scoring discriminants. This is computational algebra verification, not a
Lean proof requirement. Teams may include organizations, with one team per person
or organization and no team-size cap. AI/agents and public mathematical sources
are explicitly allowed. No cash schedule was found in those rules. Sources:
https://github.com/SAIRcompetition/IGP24-public/blob/main/competition/rules/overview.md
https://competition.sair.foundation/competitions/igp24/evaluation-setup

Assessment: potentially useful explicit construction or independent-certificate
work, but wait for a verified Stage 2 target and baseline. Do not present the old
round's dates, eligibility, or scoring as the new round's rules. Public catalog
and repository checks found no concrete Stage 2 entry window in this bounded pass.

Separate award with a verified amount: the SAIR Prize

The official page advertises $100,000 annually, shared by up to three recipients,
for completed AI advances in physics or astronomy. It covers US-based scientists
in academia, industry, or government; under 40 is a guideline. Self-nomination is
allowed. Nominations are marked Opening Soon; recipients are scheduled for April
2027, with a May ceremony. This is an achievement award, not a solve-a-problem bounty.
Our controls proof alone does not establish the required physics/astronomy impact.
Source: https://sair.foundation/prize/

Evidence grade: A for the cited published dates/rules and the separate $100,000
award; B for cash/Stage 2 completeness because some public portal routes expose
only a short rendered summary and account-only terms were not accessed. Cash-prize
claims remain deliberately unverified where the official materials give no amount.
