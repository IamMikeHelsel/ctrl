Controls: the ODE correspondence gap is closed

The guarantee now applies to every continuous trajectory satisfying its own sampled-feedback ODE, not only a selected analytic construction. Existence, uniqueness, continuity at switches and the physical normalization are proved. Derivatives are required only inside hold intervals.

x′ = v; v′ = a; a′ = r [clip(−xₙ − 3vₙ − 2aₙ + eₙ) + dₙ − a]

One fixed controller covers an unknown fixed rate r ∈ [0.9,1.1], uniformly positive dwell times h ≤ 0.2, held errors with |eₙ| + |dₙ| ≤ 0.01, and initial quadratic energy ≤ 12.
