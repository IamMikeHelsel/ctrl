FINITE-CERTIFICATE FEASIBILITY PROBE — 17 October 2026

Question: do exact computation and certificate checking provide a useful
bounded entry point to a non-Erdos prize problem?

Chosen diagnostic: Shallit's 2014 Problem9, slide35. For the fixed point of
1->12, 2->23, 3->14, 4->32, is every shifted Hankel determinant nonzero?
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/Talks/bc4.pdf
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/talks.html

Result: exact integer computation found no zero among 508,032 determinants,
using the first4,096 symbols and all matrix orders1..128 whose windows fit.
The script uses the Desnanot-Jacobi determinant recurrence with nonzero
denominators and exact division checks.51 selected determinants were recomputed
by independent fraction-free Bareiss elimination. Saved JSON binds source and
sequence hashes to the exact search domain. No Lean proof was produced here.

Files: finite/hankel_probe.py and finite/hankel_probe.json.
Replay: python3 research/opportunity-assessment/finite/hankel_probe.py

Interpretation: computational entry is inexpensive, but this experiment does
not settle the infinite conjecture or establish a new literature result.
The original slides already report finite evidence through length800. More
finite evidence is not automatically a paper, a mathematical advance, or prize
eligibility. We did not reconfirm current payment of the historical GBP50.

The author's current update says the related three-real-value problem was
solved in July2026, while the three-/four-integer variant remains unresolved.
This is not proof that every exact subquestion in old slides is untouched.

A potentially useful next step is a structural determinant recurrence or a
finite-state invariant covering all orders/shifts, with proof of completeness.
A complete certified counterexample would also settle the specific universal
claim. Stop a bounded investigation if it yields only a larger search rectangle
and no structural reduction, new witness, or independently useful checker.

Prior-work caution: the 2010 Oberwolfach report already proposes modular Hankel
methods and notes obstacles. Do not advertise that idea as our discovery.
https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46296

Assessment: good technique-development example; low priority as a full prize
solve today. A three-by-three magic square of distinct positive squares is
another easy-to-check witness, but ease of checking provides no evidence that
finding a witness is tractable. Prefer engineering problems with a controllable
model family, or a genuinely new bounded result with a reason it matters.
