#708: what is actually established in the inspected source
--------------------------------------------------------
The reference proposition g_le c is:
  for every finite A of natural numbers >=2, a maximum M belonging to A,
  and every natural x, some B subset [x+1,x+M] satisfies
  |B| <= c |A| and product(A) divides product(B).
Thus A is nonempty and the interval is positive, with exactly M integers.
The bounded-cardinality convention avoids the known exact-cardinality wording
problem in the catalog. It is not a statement about arbitrary integer starts;
reflection would be needed for negative intervals, and intervals containing
zero have different trivial divisibility behavior. No such extension was
checked here.

#128: two different formal proof scopes
-------------------------------------
Sarid's graph-level endpoint proves the 131/5000 = 0.0262 sparse-half bound
CONDITIONAL on BCLEventual. Its exact extra hypothesis is:
  there exists N such that every finite triangle-free graph H with >=N vertices
  has a cut X whose two internal edge counts sum to <= (2/47)|V(H)|^2.
The proof does remove the largeness requirement by balanced blow-ups. Its
target half has exactly floor(n/2) vertices. The four-cycle certificate is
proved inside the package; BCL is not. Axiom reporting only the three standard
axioms is entirely compatible with this explicit mathematical hypothesis.
Inspected source, not freshly rebuilt in this pass.
https://github.com/aimir/erdos-128-sparse-halves/blob/ba85f319ebb33ec2194ef4c2929a12916329e1e7/lean/Erdos128/ModuloBCL.lean
https://github.com/aimir/erdos-128-sparse-halves/blob/ba85f319ebb33ec2194ef4c2929a12916329e1e7/lean/Erdos128/ExternalInputs.lean

The BCL input is a published mathematical theorem, not a conjecture invented
to plug the gap. Its original extended abstract states n sufficiently large
and uses flag-algebra/local-cut machinery. Closing this formal gap means
formalizing a substantive theorem and its certificate semantics. The existing
Sarid repository already has rooted-cut expectation and blow-up bridges, so
proposing those bridges alone would duplicate work. I did not locate a complete
formal proof of BCL in this bounded search; absence from search is not proof
that nobody has one.

Specific bounded contribution and next experiment
------------------------------------------------
Best candidate within these two leads: formalize the cut/deletion/completion
composition behind 0.02587, reusing existing rounding and blow-up APIs. This
is not claimed implemented in the inspected repositories. It is a formalization
gap, not our mathematical discovery and not globally certified unclaimed work.

First bounded milestone: for an arbitrary supplied cut X,Y and completion
envelope f, prove the two actual graph-profile bounds
  beta_*(G) <= c^2 j,
  beta_*(G) <= f(1-1/(2c)) + q   when c>=3/4,
where c=n/(2|Y|), q=e(X)/n^2, j=e(Y)/n^2, |X|<=|Y|.
The second bound must construct G with X-internal edges removed, preserve
triangle-freeness, lift a fractional half back, and bound restoration by q.
Then derive (1+c^2)beta_* <= c^2(I+f(...)) from q+j<=I.
No graph result may be hidden as an axiom. Initially make the completion
theorem explicit; subsequently prove its graph construction (the longer
Razborov Case 2), not just its polynomial identity. This gives a concrete
interface and one meaningful checkpoint before committing to the full chain.

Success condition for the first block: this graph-level composition compiles
with stated hypotheses, and a precise list of remaining completion lemmas is
small enough to plan. Final useful outcome would be a theorem about EVERY
finite triangle-free graph conditional only on the independently stated BCL
theorem, with the 0.02587 conclusion and exact floor convention. Removing BCL
is a separate, larger project. A reusable graph deletion/fractional-profile
library plus the completed chain could support a formalization contribution;
one glue lemma alone is unlikely to support a strong standalone paper.

Kill criteria: stop if the graph extension already exists, its claimed analytic
completion step fails review, or a short inspection reveals a large missing
combinatorics library exceeding the intended block. Do not continue by putting
the hard graph claim into an assumption and calling the result complete. For
new mathematics, stop constant optimization once it reproduces the known
route ceiling; a relation between optimal-cut imbalance and edge density is
a speculative new ingredient, not an established attainable target.

Economics and the niche
----------------------
Yes, reliable translation from actual mathematical/engineering semantics to
kernel-checked certificates is specialized work. The scarce part is selecting
the right theorem, supplying the missing bridge and validating scope/novelty;
the repositories above demonstrate substantial competition in automated proof
production. A niche does not by itself establish buyer demand or income.

Neither partial #708 nor #128 milestone qualifies for the original full-problem
prize on the evidence found. The historical displays are only $100 and $250;
current payability remains unverified. Even at certainty of payment, $250 for
100 hours is $2.50/hour before compute; this arithmetic is an illustration,
not an estimate of completion time or success probability. Expected prize
income cannot responsibly be projected without those unknowns. Current
formalization platforms also impose priority, scope and payment conditions;
some reject first Lean formalizations of already-proved mathematics.
See ../prize-problems/formalization-bounty-screen.txt for current primary rules.

Treat prizes as an occasional upside. Publications, reusable formal libraries,
and engineering credibility are the realistic outputs to optimize. Paid
verification work may be a possible business route, but no customer or price
has been validated here. For this user, controls semantics and certificate
infrastructure use existing robotics expertise more directly than a race on
small historical Erdős rewards. No numerical probability of a research success
or repeatable income is justified by this audit.

Assessment grades (judgment, not probabilities): #708 independent near-term
contribution C; #128 graph-bridge experiment B-; either original prize as an
income plan D. Confidence high on the inspected proof-scope distinctions,
moderate on novelty screening, low on any claim that the full problems are
within reach.
