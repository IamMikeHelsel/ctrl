EXACT PROPOSED NEXT THEOREM — NOT YET FULLY ASSEMBLED

Use the SAME fixed feedback request q(z)=-x-3v-2a, clamp magnitude 1, quadratic
P, region z^T P z <=12, maximum sample duration 1/5, and position envelope 2.
Let a single unknown constant rate r satisfy 9/10 <= r <= 11/10. On each
sampling interval [T_n,T_(n+1)), use the actual held command
  c_n = clamp_1(q(z_n)+e_n)+d_n
and the plant
  x'=v, v'=a, a'=r*(c_n-a).
Assume hmin>0, hmin<=h_n<=1/5, |e_n|+|d_n|<=1/100, and z_0^T P z_0<=12.
Define the execution by the exact held solution and the existing sampling clock.
Target conclusion: every t>=0 satisfies z(t)^T P z(t)<=12 and |x(t)|<=2,
with continuity, sample agreement, and the stated ODE between samples.

No gain depends on the unknown r. Constant unknown rate is the first target.
The same local certificates may later support r_n chosen independently and held
on each interval, but neither that trajectory theorem nor continuously varying
r(t) is proved by this spike. A time-varying rate inside an interval changes the
solution and cannot be substituted into exp(-r*t).

Physical interpretation uses a FIXED nominal tau0 and command scale U. Normalize
t=T/tau0 and z=(X/(U*tau0^2), V/(U*tau0), A/U). Then r=tau0/tau_actual, so
  r in [9/10,11/10] means tau_actual/tau0 in [10/11,10/9].
This is +/-10% RATE, approximately -9.09%/+11.11% time constant, NOT +/-10% tau.
The fixed physical request is -X_n/tau0^2-3V_n/tau0-2A_n. Proposed assumptions:
physical h_n<=tau0/5, error sum<=U/100, normalized initial ellipsoid. Proposed
position conclusion: |X(T)|<=2*U*tau0^2. The existing PhysicalTravel theorem
instead uses the true known tau in the gains; it does not imply this robustness.
No measured actuator, calibration, arbitrary load force, or delay is assumed.

WHAT THE SPIKE ESTABLISHED

1. A useful failed attempt and its cause.
The old certificate covers all frozen gains alpha in [1/4,1]. Reusing that
range with r in [9/10,11/10] fails even after lowering the decay margin to1/2:
2 of12 exact producer checks reject negative LDL pivots. This is not merely
floating-point noise. At r=9/10, alpha=1/4, the continuous infinitesimal matrix
D0=-(M^T P+P M) has leading 2x2 determinant -510841/4000000, and
z=(-1529/2000,-9/4,0) gives z^T D0 z=-4597569/16000000.
The rational arithmetic is retained in broad-sector-obstruction.json. This is
an obstruction to that common-P sector condition, NOT a counterexample to the
actual clipped controller or its travel safety. The artificial low sector gain
need not occur at that state. Reducing H or refining the h partition cannot
repair a negative infinitesimal residual for the same P and broad sector.

2. Tightening a true assumption, without shrinking the region or changing gains.
The existing geometry proves q(z)^2 <= V(z)/2, hence |q(z)|<=sqrt(6) on V<=12.
Consequently (2/5)*|q(z)|<=1. The scratch Lean theorem tighter_sector proves
this exact statement. The reusable clamp-sector theorem can therefore use
alpha in [2/5,1]. Active clipping still occurs at z=(0,0,1), whose request is
-2 and energy is278/25<12. The valid tighter sector removes spurious models
introduced by the previous 1/4 lower endpoint.

3. All12 new polynomial vertex inequalities were checked by Lean.
Use exp(-r*h)=1-r*h+theta*r^2*h^2 with theta in[0,1]. The exact held state is
  x_h=x+h*v+u*h^2/2+(a-u)*theta*h^2,
  v_h=v+u*h+(a-u)*(h-theta*r*h^2),
  a_h=u+(a-u)*(1-r*h+theta*r^2*h^2).
For u=-alpha*[1,3,2]z this is affine separately in alpha and theta, and quadratic
in r. Endpoint interpolation for alpha and theta plus degree-two Bernstein
interpolation over r gives 2*2*3=12 polynomial-in-h matrices. The middle rate
Bernstein coefficient is generally NOT the actual matrix at the midpoint.

Every one of those12 matrices passes the existing rational producer on [0,1/5]
with the unchanged P and decay c=3/2. Each emitted source was separately compiled
against the frozen cached imports, and its certified_decrease closure audited:
all12 pass, only propext/Classical.choice/Quot.sound. Each certifies
  V(A_vertex(h)z) <= V(z) - (3/2)*h*||z||^2.
Evidence: vertex-replay.json, tight-sector/*.lean, *.input.json,
*.certificate.json, and *.verification.log. The manifest records source hashes.
This is kernel-checked vertex mathematics; the full12-way plant-to-certificate
composition is a remaining obligation, not silently assumed complete.

4. A checked analytic bridge and exact budget arithmetic.
RateEnclosure.lean checks the initial state, exact exponential-to-polynomial
identity, one common theta for all state components/held inputs, the whole rate
box side condition, the quadratic Bernstein rate identity, nonnegative weights
summing to1, the tighter sector and proposed disturbance-budget arithmetic.
It additionally checks the exact held ODE x'=v, v'=a, a'=r*(u-a).
No unsafe elaboration shortcut, custom axiom, or admitted proof is used in the
accepted scratch results. This is still a local bridge, not a global execution.

5. Exact rational response candidates and positive error budget.
The response to a unit held command divided by elapsed time is
  [(1/2-theta)*h, theta*r*h, r-theta*r^2*h].
Its h/theta/r tensor Bernstein coefficients give12 rational vectors. Exact
SymPy arithmetic verifies each has P-energy <=16; the largest is18289/1250.
That vector check and reconstruction are untrusted preparation until their
convex-energy bridge is added to Lean. They are not a sampled approximation.
With trace(P)=451/10 and c=3/2, use eta=15/902. The invariant budget holds:
  (1/5 + 1/eta)*16*(1/100)^2 =181/1875 <=90/451 =eta*12.
This last rational inequality IS separately checked by Lean. Thus there is no
apparent need to reduce the original .01 held error budget for this interval.

Measurement-error semantics: use the sector for the NOMINAL q(z), then define
the held effective disturbance
  w_eff=clamp_1(q(z)+e)-clamp_1(q(z))+d.
Existing clamp nonexpansiveness gives |w_eff|<=|e|+|d|. Do not instead apply
the nominal sector claim directly to q(z)+e without checking its new bound.
The effective-error recurrence must be linked to actual uncertain samples,
as MeasuredTravel already does for the known-rate model.

Disturbance placement is essential: d above is a command-equivalent error
INSIDE r*(clamp(q+e)+d-a), exactly the kind of error handled by the original
known-rate theorem. It is not an additive jerk outside the rate multiplier.
For the DISTINCT model a'=r*(clamp(q+e)-a)+j, rewrite j as command error j/r.
If |e|+|j|<=1/100 and r>=9/10, the effective budget is at most1/90.
Scratch Lean separately checks that scalar bound and that W=1/90 still satisfies
  (1/5+1/eta)*16*W^2 =724/6075 <=90/451.
This is conditional budget evidence for an external-jerk variant, not a completed
proof of that model. Neither interpretation covers a force added directly to v'.

6. Numerical diagnostics are explicitly not proofs.
Each of five rate intervals used a25-rate,41-time,2-sector-endpoint grid.
With the valid tighter sector the smallest sampled exact-exponential divided
energy margins were approximately1.761 on[.9,1.1], .639 on[.8,1.2], and -3.591
on[.7,1.3]. These are diagnostics only; they neither certify the larger interval
nor locate the maximal robustness boundary. The broad-sector grid already
became negative on[.95,1.05], explaining the certificate problem. Grids use h
down to .0001 and are subject to floating-point cancellation at small h.

REPEATABLE NICHE AND NOVELTY BOUNDARY

A plausible niche is auditable controller contracts: numerical/exact search
produces finite witnesses; the prover checks uncertainty enclosure, sampling,
clipping, sensing assumptions and continuous safety for the actual execution.
Repeated useful work could target calibration intervals, delayed held updates,
or coupled axes, with one explicit plant and failure case per contract. Merely
changing numerical constants or porting textbook inequalities is not a durable
publication program. The present spike shows useful reuse: parameter uncertainty
reveals a real conservative abstraction, and tightening that abstraction restores
the original region/timing/error specification without retuning the controller.
