Proximity / Better Codes feasibility assessment
2026-10-17. Bounded independent spike; no submission, account, purchase, global installation, or change to our controls project.

Judgment
This is a real fit for Lean proof engineering, but the current frontier is not a cheap constants exercise. We can deliver a reproducible audit and a parameterized version of an existing upper-bound construction in 1-2 weeks, subject to obtaining the pinned build environment. A new leaderboard record is not currently supported by evidence. The smaller upper-bound route is a more sensible entry point than the 14 MB lower-bound proof. A publishable result would need a new construction/counting theorem, a significant reusable formalization, or a clearly useful verified algorithm; a replay of the current winner is not a new mathematical result.

Exact target and mathematical scope
IRSProfile fixes a 2^18-point multiplicative NTT domain over the base KoalaBear field, embedded in its degree-six extension, 8 interleaved rows, total message dimension 2^20, base polynomial dimension 2^17, rate 1/2, 128 repetitions. Relative minimum distance is 131073/262144.
At a safe radius delta, lower requires the executable IRS extractor's certified combination-round error <=2^-128 and (1-delta)^128 <= 2^(-B/100). Upper requires the worst-case winning-set density >2^-128 for EVERY admissible radius at least its selected grid point, plus the opposite score inequality. Upper cannot merely exhibit one bad radius. No monotonicity of winning-set density is assumed.
The reported bits are a spot-check term induced by the radius, not -log2 of winning-set density and not a complete SNARK or zkVM security guarantee. The upper source explicitly does not construct an end-to-end attacking prover; the formal library supplies one direction of the game coupling rather than the converse equality to minimal game error.
Primary context: https://proximityprize.org/ and https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/680 (Arnon, Boneh, Fenzi; July2026 revision). Full PDF retrieval returned403 in this spike, so no claim of complete paper review. The grand challenges and their peer-review prize route are distinct from this companion scoreboard.

Actual bounded probe: score rounding and construction-count saturation
Reproducible code: proximity_probe.py; output proximity-probe.json. Decisions use exact Python integers; decimal logs are explanatory only. The complete run took about14 seconds.
1. Existing lower radius has68.1505647953 induced bits. Even its current Hamming-cell supremum80900/262144 gives only68.1505650441. Replacing6815 by6816 is impossible at those radii. The first grid numerator capable of68.16 from score alone is80910. This does not certify the reduction error there; the coding-theory argument would have to extend into new Hamming cells.
2. Current upper radius has116.1284071163 induced bits:11613 is the correct centibit ceiling;11612 fails. Merely changing metadata cannot improve it.
3. The current lower proof's MCA budget274980718452113030 plus list budget9659282057 exactly equals floor(p^6/2^128), p=2130706433. There is zero remaining INTEGER allocation slack in that final split. Better component estimates or a new radius argument are necessary; this does not prove the mathematical bound itself is optimal.
4. The ordinary full-domain prescribed-top pigeonhole count C(262144,131072+r)>p^r*2^59 holds at r8431 and fails at8432. The existing PrescribedTop uses exactly8431, so simply increasing that parameter is unavailable with the same inequality.
5. OrbitPencil uses512 fibres,272 selected,14 prescribed top coefficients plus a product key. Its exact count passes. Adding one selected fibre requires15 coefficients to retain the current degree argument and FAILS the same count by a factor about349,941,548 (28.38bits). This is not a rounding-sized gap.
6. A bounded exact feasibility sweep of analogous powers-of-two fibre counts16 through262144 gives no score improvement over the existing512/272 case. The sweep checks counting, degree-budget arithmetic, and a sufficient collision-avoidance inequality. It DOES NOT prove a generalized construction or exhaustive optimality: we did not search arbitrary fibre partitions, higher-order pencils, correlated signatures, different combinatorial estimates, or every possible family size. These are exclusions for a specified template only.

A defensible 1-2 week deliverable
Days1-2: provision a version-pinned environment; independently replay upper candidate and protected-target/candidate axiom audits, record timing/memory, and map every hypothesis to the fixed protocol. Stop if pinned reproduction is unreliable until corrected.
Days3-6: parameterize the existing OrbitPencil construction over compatible fibre size, selected-fibre count and coefficient budget; prove a small exact certificate interface and reproduce116.13. This yields understandable reusable mathematics and a reliable search/check separation, even if no improvement is found.
Days7-10: investigate ONE mathematically motivated refinement: tighter counting of attainable coefficient/product signatures, or a justified richer pencil construction. First derive an independently checkable integer inequality and show strict gain over the current frontier; only then spend formalization effort. Literature/competitor review must precede novelty claims.
Days11-14: either kernel-check the improvement and document its relation to prior work, or package the reusable formalization with an honest negative parameter-search report. Whether that package merits a paper depends on a specialist's assessment of generality and novelty, not passing compilation alone.

Kill criteria / decision gates
- Stop score-only tuning now: the exact probes already exclude it.
- Do not attempt a blind lower-proof rewrite:155 files of specialized multivariate polynomial/factor geometry exceeds a sensible first spike.
- If after2-3 focused days the proposed upper refinement supplies no inequality better than the frozen frontier, stop that refinement; adding compute cannot repair the missing mathematical premise.
- If a proposed improvement depends on changing the protected code, radius quantifiers, axiom policy, or score interpretation, it is not an admissible improvement.
- If the best result duplicates a live submission or only rechecks an existing result, label it reproduction/tooling and reconsider publication scope.

Niche assessment
The fit is real: search outside Lean, translate a compact exact certificate, prove the certificate's mathematical implication, and independently replay it. This competition demonstrates active use of that workflow, but its22-solvers leaderboard means it is not an untouched niche. Our controls project is currently much closer to a coherent original application; Proximity would be a separate coding-theory apprenticeship with a bounded useful formalization deliverable. No numerical probability of winning or solving is justified by this spike.
