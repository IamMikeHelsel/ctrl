Erdos 128: assembled conditional finite-graph theorem
2026-10-17

RESULT AND SCOPE
The complete local graph construction, exact scalar optimization, finite-order
transport and integer rounding now compile under Lean 4.35.0. The endpoint is
Erdos128.CompletionBridge.sparse_half_2587_100000 in PublishedBCL.lean.
Its exact mathematical meaning is:

  Assume there exists N such that every finite triangle-free simple graph H
  with |V(H)| >= N has a cut X satisfying
    e_H(X) + e_H(V(H)\X) <= (2/47)|V(H)|^2.
  Then every finite triangle-free simple graph G on n vertices has a set S
  with |S| = floor(n/2) and e_G(S) <= (2587/100000)n^2.

The size threshold in the hypothesis is explicit, as in the published BCL
statement. The result covers zero, positive even, and odd orders. Edges are
unordered simple-graph edges counted once. The conclusion is an actual
integral vertex set, not just a fractional profile or a scalar inequality.
It is a non-strict bound; the argument's stronger strict positive-order form
was not added to this final wrapper.

This formalizes the existing proposed 0.02587 argument. It is NOT a new
numerical bound, NOT a proof of the conjectural 1/50 coefficient, and NOT a
formalization of the external BCL theorem. The graph completion is proved
locally without an assumed completion lemma. Only BCL remains an external
mathematical hypothesis at the final endpoint.

EXTERNAL INPUT AND PRIMARY SOURCE
Jozsef Balogh, Felix Christian Clemen and Bernard Lidicky, "Max Cuts in
Triangle-free Graphs", arXiv:2103.14179v1, 25 March 2021, PDF Theorem 2(a):
for sufficiently large n, D_2(G) <= n^2/23.5 for every triangle-free G.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14179v1
https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14179
The explicit cut formulation above is the corresponding bipartition form of
D_2, the least number of edges deleted to make G bipartite. Part (a) has no
extra density or parity hypothesis. The HTML conversion renumbers it 1.2(a).
The source was inspected for applicability; its proof and computational
certificates were not independently formalized here. See BCL-INPUT.txt.

WHAT WAS CONNECTED HERE
1. Deleting internal edges of X makes X independent and costs at most e_G(X)
   when the same fractional half is restored to the original graph.
2. A maximum independent set, greedy extension and low-degree candidate set
   are selected with finite graph semantics and explicit cardinality proofs.
3. The large-neighborhood branch selects an actual independent extension.
   The small-neighborhood branch constructs bounded rational profiles with
   exactly n/2 total weight and proves their edge-cost expressions.
4. Incidence double counting, variance and finite averaging select a profile.
   Mantel's contribution follows from mathlib's proved Turan theorem.
5. The piecewise completion envelope is proved and decreases with the size
   of the independent seed. The deletion/completion theorem follows.
6. A second profile uniformly weights the complement of a cut. The minimum
   of the two witnesses is combined with the attributed exact scalar proof.
7. The smaller cut side is chosen internally. The even-order fractional
   0.02587 conclusion follows from an explicit fixed-graph BCL cut witness.
8. Attributed balanced-blow-up results remove BCL's size threshold, then a
   two-fold blow-up handles arbitrary parity. Attributed rounding produces
   a set of exactly floor(n/2) vertices without increasing edge cost.

The technically new artifact here is this graph-level composition and its
checked interface to the existing scalar proof. This is a formalization
contribution, not a claim of new extremal graph theory or publication priority.
