independent vertex set X with n<=3|X| and |X|<=t, there is a rational profile
z:V->Q with 0<=z(v)<=1, sum z=t, and unordered weighted edge cost at most

  F(|X|,t) = 3|X|^2/2 - 5|X|t/2 + 9t^2/8, if 4|X|<=3t;
             |X|(t-|X|)/2, otherwise.
