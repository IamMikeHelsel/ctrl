Jozsef Balogh, Felix Christian Clemen and Bernard Lidicky,

25 March 2021, an extended abstract.
Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.14179v1
Abstract record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14179
Primary HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2103.14179v1

Theorem 2(a) on PDF page 2 states that, for sufficiently large n, every
triangle-free graph G on n vertices has D_2(G)<=n^2/23.5=(2/47)n^2.
D_2 is defined as the minimum number of edges removed to make G bipartite.
The HTML conversion renumbers this as Theorem 1.2(a); use PDF Theorem 2(a)
when citing the pinned v1. Part (a) has no edge-density, regularity or parity
restriction. The separate density hypotheses in parts (b) and (c) are not
needed here. The abstract's abbreviated universal wording does not erase the
formal theorem's sufficiently-large-n qualifier.

Our fixed-graph Lean input is deliberately a concrete cut witness:
  exists X, e_G(X)+e_G(V\\X) <= (2/47)*n^2.
This is the required bipartition formulation of D_2(G)'s bound. Its use is
explicit; it is not introduced as an axiom or inferred just from a name.
Orienting the smaller side is elementary, but must be proved in the wrapper.

To claim the bound for an arbitrary finite graph from the published theorem,
one must additionally remove the size threshold. The pinned aimir baseline
has a balanced-blow-up bridge for exactly this purpose, and a separate
fractional-half blow-up equivalence. These source declarations have been
inspected, not yet rebuilt or integrated into this completion spike:
