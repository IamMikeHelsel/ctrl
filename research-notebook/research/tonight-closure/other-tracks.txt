Erdős #128

Proved locally:
For every finite triangle-free simple graph G with n vertices, an integral set
S of exactly floor(n/2) vertices has at most 0.02587*n^2 internal edges,
CONDITIONAL on the explicitly stated eventual BCL max-cut theorem.

The input says that there exists N such that each finite triangle-free graph H
of order at least N has a cut X with
  e_H(X) + e_H(V(H)\X) <= (2/47)*|V(H)|^2.
This is a theorem parameter, not an added Lean axiom. A standard-axiom audit
does not discharge it. The endpoint is
  Erdos128.CompletionBridge.sparse_half_2587_100000
in ../opportunity-assessment/erdos-completion-spike/PublishedBCL.lean.

The graph construction, fractional profiles, scalar composition, removal of
the size threshold, odd orders, empty graph and integer rounding are connected.
There is no remaining local parity or rounding gap in this endpoint. Earlier
README and BCL-INPUT checkpoint text is superseded by FINAL-STATUS.txt.

Geometric Unity

Proved locally:
The first module checks an explicit quadratic-form calculation with six positive
and four negative directions. The later audit proves a factor-of-two defect when
the printed negative tilted action is combined with the printed minus torsion.
It gives a real 3 by 3 matrix witness and checks two local sign repairs.
It also proves conjugation covariance of a fixed-coefficient ordered-product
construction with the degrees used by the draft's equation (9.3).

Exact blockers to calling this the GU operator:
1. Choose one consistent action/connection/torsion sign convention. Each local
   repair changes other printed properties; neither preserves the whole draft.
2. Specify Phi_1, Phi_2, the Clifford/Lie algebra and product or bracket convention,
   including factors of i. These are mathematical inputs, not just missing code.
3. Identify the finite coefficient arrays with an actual exterior basis, wedge
   product, metric, orientation and (7,7) Hodge star, with their required laws.
4. Prove output closure, then the intended curvature/Weyl and Bianchi properties.
   Generic covariance does not imply any of these claims.

The draft itself leaves its preferred operator selection unresolved in section
8.2. A selected explicit candidate could be formalized and tested. It must be
labelled as that candidate; implementing a choice cannot establish that it is
the author's intended construction. No result here validates or refutes the
whole theory or gives a physical prediction.
