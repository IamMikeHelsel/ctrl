BEST CONCRETE FINITE-CONSTRUCTION LEAD: PARKER SQUARE
Primary announcement: Numberphile, 2025-02-09, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=stpiBy6gWOA
Primary producer's contemporaneous confirmation of $10,000: https://bsky.app/profile/bradyharan.bsky.social/post/3lhqwmnnmbc2e
Official episode page: https://www.numberphile.com/videos/magic-square-breakthrough
The primary video description still exposes a prize submission address. Primary text confirms that an offer was announced, but accessible text did not expose its complete payout terms. No current escrow, availability, or prize adjudication was independently confirmed.
Target: a 3-by-3 grid of nine distinct positive integer squares with equal row, column and both diagonal sums. A candidate is a small exact arithmetic certificate, readily checkable in Lean using explicit integers and equality/distinctness proofs. This is excellent verification fit, not evidence of good solving odds. Finding such a square is a longstanding difficult problem, and bounded search failure proves no global impossibility.
Secondary corroboration only (not accepted as a primary rulebook): https://labs.ofertiber.com/magic-square/ reports US$10,000 for the first working grid, excludes a nonexistence proof, and describes committee adjudication. These details need direct verification before relying on payout. Auto-generated secondary summaries also confuse squares of squares with bimagic squares; do not inherit their mathematical statement. An attempted primary caption retrieval yielded no caption content. No reliable resolution or closure was established in this bounded check.
Suggested final-answer wording: "Magic-square constructions: a $10,000 Parker prize was announced; a candidate would be easy to verify exactly, but discovery is difficult and current detailed terms remain unconfirmed."

MOST CLEARLY CURRENT POSTED ALGORITHMIC OFFERS: NANONGKAI
Sponsor's live page: https://sites.google.com/site/dannanongkai/open
Explicitly posted: EUR500 to prove/disprove the OMv conjecture; EUR300 for v-hinted Mv, Conjecture 5.2 in the linked vdBNS paper. First solver only; offer expires in 2028. Both first offered at ADFOCS2018.
These concern asymptotic algorithmic complexity and lower bounds, so a finite input/output witness alone will not settle them. Lean could certify an algorithmic reduction or recurrence after the main idea is found. Prioritize below more concrete certificate tasks for our current project.
Do not recommend older cut-query rewards: the same live page now marks reachability solved (reported roughly |V|^1.5 query complexity). Its EUR5 undergraduate questions are explicitly discontinued. Posted terms are current evidence, not independent evidence of funds or guaranteed eligibility.

SHALLIT: USEFUL FORMALIZATION LEAD, HISTORICAL REWARD
Primary slides (2014): https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/Talks/bc4.pdf
Primary actively updated status: https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/talks.html
Slide35 / Problem9 offers GBP50 for deciding whether the fixed point of 1->12, 2->23, 3->14, 4->32 has all shifted Hankel determinants nonzero. A counterexample would consist of a finite sequence window and an integer matrix with zero determinant; proving every determinant nonzero needs a structural all-size proof.
The update page still describes the related 3- or 4-integer-valued nonvanishing-sequence variant as unresolved, while marking the 3-real-number version solved July2026. This does not independently reconfirm payment or conclusively establish exact Problem9's present status. Treat as a follow-up literature target, not a verified current cash opportunity.
The update page also records numerous other old slide problems solved/partially solved; do not copy the old open list. In particular Problems1,5,6,8,10,15,16-18 no longer support naive historical-open labels. Problem4 has August2026 partial progress.

KIMBERLING: DONATION REWARDS, NOT PERSONAL CASH
Author's primary page: https://faculty.evansville.edu/ck6/integer/unsolved.html
January15,2025 note says solutions after January1,2025 receive donations to OEIS in the solver's name. Ordinary rules require first publication in a refereed journal, with an alternative for short complete solutions accepted onto the author's page.
Potential tractable kind: Problem13's locally quantified sign-pattern counterexamples in a greedily defined integer sequence could have finite replay certificates. Its global coverage conjectures cannot be settled by checking a long prefix. Page lists $25 for proofs / $20 for counterexamples, now subject to the donation policy. Problem2 lists $300; Problem4 $100, likewise donations. Useful research exercises if publication is the aim, not a route to personal prize earnings.
