1. #708: interval divisibility selections — first bounded proof-reuse review.
   https://www.erdosproblems.com/708
   https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/discuss/708
   https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/708/proof-claims
   The target concerns selecting few integers from an interval so that their
   product is divisible by a given product; the desired scale is about 2n.
   It is a good METHOD fit: a September claim reports an LP-duality reduction
   and exact counting certificates, with a Lean-checked 12n bound and an
   informal 11n bound. We must inspect that work before redoing it.
   Proposed bounded step: reproduce its intended statement and final axiom
   closure, then inspect whether one certificate bottleneck admits a meaningful
   parameter improvement or reusable abstract lemma. Stop if no distinct gap.
   Critical issue: discussion notes that universal exact cardinality |B|=g(n)
   appears inconsistent with reported asymptotics; the intended minimax bound
   likely requires |B|<=g(n). Read Erdos-Suranyi 1959 before formalizing.
   Historical display $100; improving 12n does not solve the 2+o(1) target and
   no prize eligibility for a partial improvement is established.

2. #128: sparse halves in triangle-free graphs — certificate-method fit.
   https://www.erdosproblems.com/128
   https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/discuss/128
   https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/128/proof-claims
   A triangle-free graph should have a large induced vertex subset with at
   most n^2/50 edges. Exact floor(n/2) and induced-subgraph conventions matter.
   Existing work uses flag-algebra and spectral certificates. A recent claim
   improves the coefficient to 0.0262, still above 0.02, and explicitly assumes
   an external max-cut theorem rather than formalizing it. Another discussion
   supplies finite graph/bisection certificates and a census.
   Proposed bounded step: inspect this claim and identify one independently
   useful certificate interface or restricted family not already covered.
   Do not rerun the finite census as supposed novelty or call the conditional
   formalization an unconditional proof. Historical display $250; full gap
   remains substantial and no likelihood of closing it is estimated.

3. #634: congruent triangle tilings — geometry bridge, review-first only.
   https://www.erdosproblems.com/634
   https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/634/proof-claims
   https://www.michaelbeeson.com/research/talks/TriangleTilingSlides.pdf
   The question classifies tile counts. The curated narrative still calls 19
   unknown, but two July proof claims specifically rule out 19 and all primes
   3 mod 4 above 3, invoking substantial recent classification results.
   This immediately blocks treating the 19 case as an untouched opportunity.
   Proposed bounded step: review those classifications/claims and their formal
   hypotheses, then decide whether an exact geometry certificate is missing.
   Such proof checking could be valuable; novelty and publication merit are
   not yet established. The $25 display applies to the larger classification
   question, not a confirmed separate bounty for the 19 case.
