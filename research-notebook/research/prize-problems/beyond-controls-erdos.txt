OTHER PROOF TYPES AND PRIZE ROUTES
Primary-source screening, 17 October 2026. Fit judgments are not winning odds.
No entries, purchases, accounts, submissions or sponsor contacts were made.

RECOMMENDATION
Keep the controls paper as the main publication project. The strongest new
bounded investigation is the Proximity Prize's Better Codes companion: a
specific Lean target, incremental progress and an existing comparison baseline.
Numerical simulation remains the closest adjacent applied research direction.
Neither route has an established probability of success or award entitlement.

1. IMPROVE A RIGOROUS BOUND: CODING THEORY
The Proximity Prize advertises a $1,000,000 total awards program for Reed-Solomon
list decoding and correlated agreement. Significant partial results may count.
Grand-challenge entries require peer-reviewed acceptance plus public posting;
the current closing date is 31 December 2027, 23:59 UTC. AI assistance is allowed
with human verification and responsibility. The site labels statements preliminary.
https://proximityprize.org/

Better Codes is a separately administered companion with Lean-checked lower and
upper bounds. Its current repository expressly limits the score to a spot-check
quantity induced by a certified threshold radius, not full-protocol security.
Do not infer a whole deployed cryptographic system's security from its score.
https://better.codes/
https://github.com/proximity-prize/proximity-prize
https://blog.ethereum.org/2026/08/20/better-codes-challenge

Both programs use discretionary awards: accepted proofs, publication, rankings
or improved scores do not alone create a payment entitlement. Better Codes has
separate terms; the grand challenge's deadline must not be assumed to govern it.
https://proximityprize.org/terms/
https://better.codes/program-terms

First experiment: pin the exact current target and dependencies, replay the
baseline locally, read leading submissions, then identify one distinct bound
improvement. No automatic entry or substantial compute spend is proposed.

2. PROVE NUMERICAL METHODS CORRECT: SIMULATION AND OPTIMIZATION
The Langtangen Prize offers a $3,000 annual pool for the best paper(s) on the
foundations of numerical simulation. Self-nominations and arXiv papers are
allowed. Nominations are due 1 April for the preceding year's publications.
This is a competitive paper award, not a bounty for a named theorem.
https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~ridg/prizes/langtangenprize.pdf

Useful mathematical directions include verified discretization error,
convergence, preservation of constraints and rigorous bounds for numerical
optimizers. Our current artifact needs a stronger numerical-method contribution
to fit this award. A new second application should serve the paper, not merely
add a benchmark count.

3. PROVE A GLOBAL DYNAMICAL OR COMPUTABILITY PROPERTY
Wolfram's current Rule 30 page lists three $10,000 prizes, concerning the center
column's nonperiodicity, limiting balance and computational effort. Complete
solutions need committee acceptance and a publishable technical paper. A long
simulation is not a proof of the infinite-time statement. Lower-priority,
speculative research for us.
https://rule30prize.org/

The S Combinator Challenge lists $20,000 for resolving computation universality.
Formal semantics, simulation relations and encodings could suit Lean, but the
precise permitted encoding/decoding model needs careful interpretation. A 2022
paper about inability to express K directly does not by itself establish that
the sponsor's broader computational-universality challenge has been adjudicated.
No accepted award resolution was verified in this screen.
https://www.combinatorprize.org/
https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/06/1920-2020-and-a-20000-prize-announcing-the-s-combinator-challenge/
https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.12893

4. FIND AN EXACT FINITE WITNESS
A finite construction or counterexample can be fully verified even when its
discovery is difficult. Examples include special integer grids, graph
constructions and specified hash collisions. A finite search with no witness
does not prove an unbounded nonexistence theorem.

Matt Parker's magic-square-of-squares $10,000 offer was announced in 2025 and
confirmed by Numberphile producer Brady Haran. Target: nine distinct positive
integer squares in a 3x3 grid with equal row, column and both diagonal sums.
Current payout terms and continued availability were not independently
reconfirmed. Treat as an exploratory lead, not confirmed receivable cash.
https://bsky.app/profile/bradyharan.bsky.social/post/3lhqwmnnmbc2e
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=stpiBy6gWOA

The Ethereum Foundation/Protocol Labs MiMC page currently lists $20,000 for
specified full-round collisions, with payment in USD, ETH or DAI. It also lists
implementation-bug rewards as possible. No deadline, detailed adjudication
terms or fresh unclaimed-status confirmation was obtained. Specialist
cryptanalysis is required; cheap verification does not imply cheap discovery.
https://crypto.ethereum.org/bounties/mimc-hash-challenge

5. VERIFY SOFTWARE OR FORMALIZE EXISTING MATHEMATICS
See formalization-bounty-screen.txt for independently reviewed current terms.
Conjectures float kernels are an active verified-optimization competition, but
rewards are token allocations and entry requires subnet registration.
Ordinary Conjectures submissions cost tokens, require AI-log rights, and do not
reward first formalizations of already-established conventional results under
their current novelty rule. Justin Sun legacy awards permit separate
formalization contributions, but no fixed payable amount was verified; the
new MathDB pilot is awaiting activation. None is recommended as predictable cash.

6. PROVE AN ALGORITHM OR COMPLEXITY LOWER BOUND
Nanongkai's current Open EUR page offers EUR500 for OMv and EUR300 for v-hinted
Mv, first solver only, expiring in 2028. These concern asymptotic complexity;
one finite instance cannot settle them. A new algorithm or a structural lower
bound is needed. His old cut-query problem is marked solved and the EUR5
undergraduate challenges are discontinued. This is a substantive theory route,
below incremental certificate work in our present priorities.
https://sites.google.com/site/dannanongkai/open
