DATA CONTRACT AND REPRODUCTION
- erdos-problems.yaml is the complete unmodified metadata source. License,
  schema, README, CONTRIBUTING and statistics at the exact revision accompany it.
- erdos-provenance.json records source URLs, SHA-256, bytes and retrieval time.
  Official HTML snapshots have separate source/time/hash provenance files.
- erdos-manifest.json is lossless: it retains every raw community entry as well
  as explicit screening fields. erdos-manifest.csv is a flattened view.
- erdos-counts.json contains the recomputed counts. erdos-official-prizes.json
  reconciles all official prize-page IDs without silently editing community data.
- erdos_build_manifest.py validates the upstream Draft2020-12 JSON schema,
  formats, duplicate keys and IDs, and source hashes before creating outputs.
  Replay: .tools/research-python/bin/python3 research/prize-problems/erdos_build_manifest.py
  Dependencies used: PyYAML 6.0.3 and jsonschema 4.26.0 in workspace-local venv.
- Null means unknown/absent; CSV empty cells retain that meaning. Literal 'no'
  is a source assertion, not unknown. No foreign exchange rate was invented.
