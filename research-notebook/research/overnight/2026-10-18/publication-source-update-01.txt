Common quadratic certificates
Stephen Boyd, Laurent El Ghaoui, Eric Feron and Venkataramanan Balakrishnan,
Linear Matrix Inequalities in System and Control Theory, SIAM,1994.
Author-hosted record: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/lmibook/index.html
Book: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/lmibook/lmibook.pdf
Read the extracted Chapter5.1 passage, printed pp62–65, including equations
(5.8)–(5.9). It gives common quadratic certificates for polytopic linear
differential inclusions, including equivalent conditions in P and Q=P^-1.
This supports crediting our certificate method as established. It does not
validate our specific rational matrix, which has separate exact/Lean checks.
No full-book review was performed. Do not copy the book into a public artifact.

Solver-status meaning
Official CVXPY tutorial: https://www.cvxpy.org/tutorial/intro/index.html
Official solver guide: https://www.cvxpy.org/tutorial/solvers/index.html
The tutorial distinguishes optimal from optimal_inaccurate and explains that
the latter reports lower achieved accuracy. Such outcomes may contain candidate
variables. Our frozen procedure rejected them by policy, not by a mathematical
proof that no feasible candidate exists. The failure to save three candidate
vectors and residuals is explicitly recorded in the experiment's errata.

Use in the next paper
Cite the1994 book when introducing the quadratic hold certificate. Cite the
actual solver versions and official status semantics in the stronger comparison.
Keep global optimality, exact polynomial feasibility, numerical plant replay,
compute latency and hardware acceptance as separate claims.
