CONTROLS CRITIC04 — ACCEPT THE BOUNDED SCALAR CLAIM
Evidence gate: A. This grades the stated proof and review, not novelty or hardware.

Accepted result
For every supplied classical trajectory of the original scalar hold model,
a fixed goal, response 80<=r(t)<=120/s, disturbance |w(t)|<=.01 m/s^2, and the
original transformed initial cube of radius .0002 m, every hold time t in [2,H]
satisfies |v(t)|<=.001 m/s and |a(t)|<=.02 m/s^2. Use H>=2 for a nonempty claim.
Both response and disturbance can change. There is no added derivative,
constancy, dwell-time, hidden forced-trajectory, reset or retargeting premise.

The accepted primary theorem is
  LeanControls.QuadraticHold.two_seconds_actual_changing_input.
The accepted inherited-endpoint theorem is
  LeanControls.QuadraticWitness.fixed_reference_then_changing_hold.
It carries the actual existing eight-phase DirectPlant endpoint into this hold.
The hold clock starts after 1.5 seconds of preceding motion. Its two-second
entry is therefore at global time 3.5 seconds. It is not a composition with the
separate FIR robot-motion reference.

Limits and decision
Accept and integrate this bounded mathematical result. Stop certificate
optimization for the fixed target. The identifier energy denotes a quadratic
measure, not mechanical energy. The theorem gives residual bands, not exact
finite-time rest, physical stopping safety or validated robot performance.
It assumes a classical trajectory and does not prove existence for every
bounded input pair. It does not cover a sampled controller, actuator clipping,
sensor error, joint coupling, contact, response below 80/s, full loss of grip,
Franka hardware, or PLd/PLe. The separate position theorem and this speed/
acceleration theorem must retain their individual assumptions. Publication
novelty and the hardware relevance of the chosen bands still need review.
