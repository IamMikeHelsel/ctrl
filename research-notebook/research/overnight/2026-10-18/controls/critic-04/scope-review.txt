CONTROLS CRITIC04 — statement and assumption review
Working review; final decision is in report.txt after replay.

Target
The fixed controller is u=-800*(x-c)-60*v-a/2. The plant is
x'=v, v'=a, a'=r*(u+w-a). Thus the acceleration equation contains
-3*a/2 after the controller is substituted. The source proves this exact
scaling in qState_ode. The reviewer raw_physical_equations_suffice client
starts from these scalar equations, the explicit initial inequalities,
and a nonempty H>=2 horizon.

The theorem two_seconds_actual_changing_input requires H>=0 and returns a
universal conclusion on [2,H]. That set is empty when H<2. This is standard
conditional interval scope, not a nonempty-duration claim. The inherited
composition theorem explicitly requires H>=2. Reviewer clients use H=4
and check the endpoint H=2 is nonempty.

Trajectory assumptions
A supplied state f is continuous on [0,H]. Each component has the actual ODE
derivative at every interior time. The equations are classical, not merely
almost-everywhere equations. r and w have pointwise closed-interval bounds.
They have no separate continuity, derivative, dwell-time, or constancy premise.
The scalar comparison uses a constant coefficient 20, so it does not smuggle in
continuity of physical r. Arbitrary bounded signals need not admit a classical
trajectory. Existence for every bounded signal pair is not claimed.

State and input
c is fixed during the hold. No estimated disturbance is supplied to the
controller. The initial assumptions bound the three coordinates
x-c, x-c+v/40, x-c+v/20+a/1600 by 1/5000 m. This cube is not replaced by the
unit ellipsoid. The proof retains it and derives the generous V(0)<=1000000.
V=q^T Pq is a dimensionless quadratic measure. The identifier energy is not
mechanical energy, power, work or actuator effort.

Exact reasoning
q=(80000*(x-c),2000*v,50*a) and d=100*w.
A(r)=[[0,40,0],[0,0,40],[-r/2,-3r/2,-3r/2]], B=(0,0,r/2).
The endpoint SOS inequalities give V'<=20*(d^2-V). Affine interpolation with
weights (120-r)/40 and (r-80)/40 is valid throughout 80<=r<=120. |d|<=1 gives
V'<=20*(1-V). Independent exact checks reconstruct these matrices and both
SOS decompositions. Q and -M80,-M120 have 37 positive principal minors.
The coordinate matrices Q11*P-e1*e1^T and Q22*P-e2*e2^T are positive
semidefinite; their exact SOS formulas agree with the source.

The comparison gives V(t)<=1+999999*exp(-20*t). At t>=2, exp(-20*t)<=16^-10
follows from the checked exp(-4)<=1/16 and the exponential product law.
1+999999/16^10<=101/100. Consequently q1^2<=.518*1.01<4 and
q2^2<=.81*1.01<1. Dividing by 2000 and 50 gives |v|<=.001 m/s and
|a|<=.02 m/s^2. All-time extension follows from t>=2, not from sampling.

Inherited endpoint
DirectPlant retains all eight actual physical phases, their state matches,
rate/disturbance bounds, and zero initial physical state. direct_cube proves
the transformed tracking-error cube through the last closed phase. The exact
reference endpoint is [3/5,0,0]. hmatch identifies hold f(0) with the actual
previous phase 7 endpoint. matched_hold_cube is an algebraic change of error
coordinates. It does not reset the state or assert zero endpoint error.
The two-second clock starts after the 1.5-second previous motion. It corresponds
to global time 3.5 seconds. This is not a composition with the separate FIR
retained-history reference and not a coupled Franka theorem.

Non-vacuity and controls
The supplied exponential moving witness changes both rate and disturbance,
starts at speed 8 mm/s and acceleration .32 m/s^2, and obeys the classical ODE even
at its switch. Its compensated switch keeps the actual derivative consistent.
The reviewer also rebuilds an analytic nonzero-disturbance handoff client of
the full DirectPlant composition, adapted from critic02's witness and declared
as such in ReviewClients.lean. Exact rest is false for these exponential tails.
Negative certificate cases distinguish necessity of the proof assumptions from
necessity for all possible controllers or trajectories. They do not prove that
the original target fails when a bound is removed.

Publication boundary
This is a conditional all-time mathematical guarantee for a fixed scalar
continuous-feedback model. It adds useful speed and acceleration residuals
under changing response and changing disturbance. It proves neither exact
finite-time rest nor safety of a sampled, clipped, sensor-limited, coupled or
contact robot. It does not justify rate/disturbance bands on hardware, cover
r<80 or full loss of response, establish PLd/PLe, or settle novelty. The
quadratic method is established mathematics. No optimizer rerun is needed for
this review; a rational certificate is sufficient once checked.
