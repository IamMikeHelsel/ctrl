STOP NEW CONTROLS THEOREM BRANCHES

The declared FIR-to-terminal-hold gap is closed and independently accepted.
Integrate the exact assumptions, global times 1.42 and 3.42 seconds, and residual
bands into the manuscript and expanded replay bundle. Keep the 1.7-second
numerical task separate from the proved physical terminal time.

The missing items before a broader claim are concrete:
1. Which shorter physical deadline, if any, is a requirement rather than an
   illustrative task? A new certificate or reachability argument would need
   to be evaluated against that fixed requirement.
2. What sample period, delay, sensing errors, saturation, actuator bounds,
   and coupled robot model will the implementation actually use?
3. What evidence justifies the response and disturbance bands on the selected
   Franka setup, including payload and contact conditions?
4. What is the contribution beyond the known FIR and quadratic methods, and
   which venue values this precise formalization and artifact?

These questions do not block acceptance of the present conditional theorem.
They do block a claim that it is a physical robot safety or performance result.
