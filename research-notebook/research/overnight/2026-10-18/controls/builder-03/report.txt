Changing response and disturbance: no violation found in a bounded scout
Builder result. Independent review and acceptance remain open.

Outcome
The strongest saved case reaches acceleration 0.01315390944069874 m/s^2 at
t=3.998177 s. This is a ratio 0.657695472034937 of the frozen 0.02 m/s^2
limit. The strongest observed velocity across the search traces was about
0.0002730313 m/s, below the 0.001 m/s limit. The search did not find a failure.
It does not prove the limits for all continuous inputs, all initial states,
or every time. No mathematical counterexample was claimed or certified.

Model and scope
The controller and commanded goal remain unchanged:
  x'=v, v'=a, a'=r[-800(x-c)-60v-1.5a+w].
Both r and w vary continuously and piecewise linearly. Exact saved knots obey
80<=r<=120 per second and |w|<=0.01 m/s^2. Initial transformed coordinates
z=(x-c, x-c+v/40, x-c+v/20+a/1600) range over the eight vertices of the cube
[-0.0002,0.0002]^3; each input is also replayed from zero.
A failure from this cube would not prove reachability from the particular
eight-phase DirectPlant. No Franka, sampled controller, saturation, sensor error,
joint coupling, contact or hardware result is included.

Search performed
The protocol was frozen before execution; its SHA-256 is
20e19c6a1b1d59ef1201752ca2cf6adb6be2bd6d348bd90d44e4c4fd588c6ae2.
The search used the specified three seeds, two target times (2 and 4 seconds),
both outputs, and 0.01-second input knots. A linear adjoint selected adverse
disturbance-knot signs and initial-cube signs for each rate schedule.
L-BFGS-B varied the rate parameters. Twenty fixed constant-rate controls and
twelve optimized candidates were replayed. One local input refinement inserted
0.002-second knots around the strongest peak, for 33 candidates in total.
Every candidate was replayed from all eight cube vertices and zero, with output
probes every 0.001 second on [2,4]. The final peak time was locally refined and
frozen as the rational 3998177/1000000 seconds. This is sampled optimization,
not an all-time supremum calculation. The outer search is nonconvex.

There were 11,698 rate-objective evaluations. One original search and the local
refinement reached their evaluation budgets; their failed convergence statuses
and complete objective histories are preserved. No extra search was added after
those budgets. The final candidate has 528 exact rational input knots. Its
initial physical state is x-c=-0.0002 m, v=0 and a=0. Disturbance and rate both
change. The result is not a constant-bias case in disguise.

Declared search deviation
The protocol said the rate prefix before the last second would be constant.
The decoder instead linearly interpolated between its time-zero rate and the
first knot of the last second. The actual prefix is therefore a linear ramp.
This was found in the final audit. All actual inputs still belong to the declared
bounded continuous piecewise-linear class. The frozen candidate and replay remain
valid numerical evidence, but the intended constant-prefix parameterization was
not executed as written. The source, protocol and run are preserved unchanged.
No correction run or additional search was made. Review must assess this stated
deviation rather than assume perfect protocol compliance.

Replay and controls
The exploratory compiled RK4 integration used step at most 0.001 second. Its
final ratio was 0.657695072839217; the higher-accuracy replay differs by about
0.0000003992. SciPy DOP853 was written separately, split at every input knot,
and replayed all nine initial states. A separate 60-digit power-series method
used the exact rational knots and integrated 526 intervals to the frozen time.
Its orders 100 and 120 agreed at the working precision. DOP853 and this replay
differed by about 1.69e-14 m/s^2 in acceleration. Neither solver is a rigorous
interval enclosure. Agreement is numerical evidence, not a proof certificate.

Twelve constant-rate adjoint controls were checked against independent matrix
exponential sensitivities. Halving RK4's step reduced every support-value error.
Exact rational checks passed for knot ordering, input bounds, initial cube and
target time. Zero input and zero state stayed zero. Constant-bias controls
approached the expected position offset. A deliberately tighter diagnostic
acceleration limit of 0.013 was detected as violated by the saved candidate;
this control did not replace or relax the actual 0.02 target. The candidate
also refutes exact rest at its target time while satisfying the finite limits.
