CONTROLS BUILDER04 — a tighter bound under changing response and disturbance
Status: BUILDER CANDIDATE. Independent review is required.

Main result
The original speed and acceleration targets now have a direct actual-state
proof candidate. After two seconds of hold, every supplied classical trajectory
of the original scalar controller satisfies
  |v(t)| <= 0.001 m/s and |a(t)| <= 0.02 m/s^2,
for every hold time t in [2,H], under the fixed assumptions below. This is an
all-time conditional theorem, not a sampled numerical pass. Lean verification
status and exact replay steps are in verification.json.

Fixed model and assumptions
  x'=v; v'=a; a'=r(t)*(u(t)+w(t)-a),
  u=-800*(x-c)-60*v-a/2,
  80 <= r(t) <= 120 per second; |w(t)| <= 0.01 m/s^2.
The goal c remains fixed throughout this hold. At hold time zero, all three
coordinates (x-c, x-c+v/40, x-c+v/20+a/1600) have magnitude at most 0.0002 m.
The physical state is continuous on [0,H] and obeys the ODE at every interior
time. No derivative, continuity, constancy, or minimum dwell-time assumption
is added for r or w. The theorem is conditional on such a classical trajectory;
it does not prove its existence for every arbitrary bounded input pair.

No actual state is reset. fixed_reference_then_changing_hold also carries the
existing eight-phase DirectPlant endpoint into this same hold with exact state
matching. Its horizon premise H>=2 makes the terminal interval nonempty. The
two-second clock starts at the hold, not at the start of the preceding motion.
This is the earlier DirectPlant trajectory, not a new composition with the
separate FIR robot-motion reference.

Certificate construction
The predeclared grid was alpha={4,8,12,16,20,24,32,40}/s. For each fixed alpha,
one Clarabel semidefinite solve maximized a shared strict matrix margin, with
Q11<=9/4 and Q22<=81/100. No random search or target revision was used.
The first exact rational pass was alpha=20/s, at rounding denominator 1000:
  Q=(1/1000)*[[2162,-549,-234],[-549,518,-151],[-234,-151,810]].
The numerical proposal is not trusted as proof. Candidate bytes were frozen
before the separate exact checker. All eight solver outcomes and every declared
rounding attempt are preserved. Alpha 40 returned optimal_inaccurate with a
negative margin and its warning remains. Negative margins at other grid points
are not claims of mathematical infeasibility.

Set q=(80000*(x-c),2000*v,50*a) and d=100*w. Then
  q'=A(r)q+B(r)d,
  A=[[0,40,0],[0,0,40],[-r/2,-3r/2,-3r/2]], B=(0,0,r/2)^T.
The exact checker verifies Q>0 and strict negativity at r=80 and r=120 of
  [[A Q+Q A^T+20Q, B],[B^T,-20]].
It checks all 37 nonempty principal minors across these three matrices with
Python Fraction arithmetic. It independently computes the cofactor inverse and
checks Q*P=I. The search uses a separate rational LDL check.

Direct trajectory proof
P=Q^-1 and V=q^T P q. The exact P is
 (1/68317631)*[[49597375,60003000,25513875],
 [60003000,212058000,56866000],[25513875,56866000,102314375]].
The Lean identifier energy denotes this quadratic measure. It is not mechanical
energy, power, or effort. No mass model is used.

The Lean proof contains explicit rational sums of squares for both endpoint
inequalities. Their nonnegative affine combination proves the inequality at
every interior rate. It also proves the coordinate inequalities
  q1^2 <= (259/500)*V; q2^2 <= (81/100)*V.
Differentiation of the actual physical trajectory gives V'<=20*(1-V).
The inherited cube gives |q(0)|<=(16,32,64). Exact arithmetic gives
  V(0)<=995542624000/68317631, about 14572.265.
For a shorter Lean entry proof, a looser bound V(0)<=1000000 is proved directly.
The existing scalar comparison theorem then yields
  V(t)<=1+999999*exp(-20t).
The existing checked exp(-4)<=1/16 gives exp(-40)<=1/16^10. Thus V(t)<=101/100
for all t>=2. The two coordinate inequalities yield the fixed physical targets.
There is no introduced forced trajectory, superposition premise, existence
shortcut, input derivative, state reset, or shifted command goal in this route.

Non-vacuity and false controls
The supplied movingState has x=exp(-40t)/5000, v=-exp(-40t)/125,
a=8*exp(-40t)/25. It starts at 8 mm/s and 0.32 m/s^2, outside both final bands.
For t<=1, r=80 and w=0. For t>1, r=120 and w=(4/75)*exp(-40t).
Lean proves the actual ODE, initial cube, both changing inputs, the pointwise
bounds, and the new conclusion at t=2. The state remains classical across the
switch because the changed disturbance compensates the rate change.

Exact false statements test the disturbance bound, rate upper bound, initial
ellipsoid reset, and false immediate settling. The expected Lean failures must
have resolved imports and an unsolved false goal. A separate standard-library
checker also rejects a corrupted Q22. These are failures of altered certificate
statements, not counterexamples to the original target or to physical control.

Limits and publication boundary
This proves a bounded scalar continuous-feedback model. It does not prove a
sampled implementation, actuator saturation, sensor-error model, joint coupling,
Franka hardware, PLd/PLe compliance, contact recovery, or a full robot stopping
certificate. It also does not cover complete loss of grip or a response rate
below 80/s. The previous position theorem remains separate; this new terminal
statement adds speed and acceleration under changing disturbance. It gives
small residual bands, not exact finite-time rest. The quadratic method itself
is established mathematics. Novelty and the value of this checked composition
still need a literature and human-review judgment.
