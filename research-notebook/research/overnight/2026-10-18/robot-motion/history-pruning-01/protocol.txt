HISTORY PRUNING — coordinator proof gate, 18 October 2026

Claim to test
At current time c, an event whose support ends at or before c contributes its
full position increment and zero velocity, acceleration and jerk at every
future time. Remove only those completed events and add their increments to
the constant position offset. Keep all active and queued future events.

Prove equality of the entire future reference jet, including t=c. Then prove
that the exact rational jerk and acceleration guards return the same answer
for the retained event list. The position offset is not part of either guard.

Checks
Use the existing pinned Lean and source closure. Challenge premature removal,
loss of the position offset, and removal of queued future actions. Keep signed,
repeated, empty and exact-endpoint cases. Freeze sources before final replay.

Boundary
This does not preserve queries before c. It does not bound storage unless the
arrival rate and future queue are also bounded. It does not solve the all-pairs
checker cost for many still-active events. No timing claim is planned here.
Independent review is required before acceptance. The coordinator owns this
folder only; existing frozen artifacts remain unchanged.
