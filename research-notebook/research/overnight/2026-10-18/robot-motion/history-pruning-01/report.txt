COMPLETED ACTIONS CAN BE REMOVED FROM FUTURE CHECKS
Coordinator proof gate, 18 October 2026

Result
The candidate theorem removes an event only when its complete four-box support
has ended. It adds that event's signed position increment to the constant
offset. At every time t>=c, including t=c, the resulting position, velocity,
acceleration and jerk are exactly equal to those of the original history.

HistoryPruning.lean proves future_jet_eq over real histories. The executable
rational qRetained and qCompletedOffset operations are connected to that result
by rational_future_jet_eq. jerk_guard_pruned and acceleration_guard_pruned prove
that the existing exact guards accept precisely the same inputs after pruning.
This is equivalence to the accepted all-future properties, not sampled equality.

The retained predicate is c < tau+4*h. It keeps events still active at c and all
queued future events. The completed offset uses tau+4*h <= c. Repeated and signed
increments are retained as such; their absolute values must not be summed.

Limits
The theorem concerns future times only. A constant offset does not reconstruct
past motion. It gives no bound on the number of active or future queued actions.
It does not speed up the current all-pairs checker when many events remain
active. A bounded arrival rate and future queue would be additional assumptions.
No execution-time, physical-state, infinite-stream or hardware claim is made.
