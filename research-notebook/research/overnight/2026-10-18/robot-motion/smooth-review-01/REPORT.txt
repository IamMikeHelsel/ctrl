Accepted readout
For the six constant-rate/disturbance scalar cases, the N90/10mm return gives
0.6086–0.6153 of FIR's sampled squared-jerk integral and0.6980–0.6983 of its
sampled forward excursion. Its peak actual jerk is1.4113–1.6182 times FIR's.
The integral and travel therefore improve while the peak worsens. The native
comparators have less travel than this smooth candidate. No method dominates
all declared criteria. Squared jerk is not mechanical energy.

All six accepted generous cases pass the sampled task. All six tight cases
fail. The planner is deliberately blind to the task deadline. “Accepted” means
the fixed solver/status/exact-reference procedure accepted the return plan; it
does not mean the deadline contract was accepted. Native fast alone passes the
tight task in this comparison. Every rejected solver attempt remains visible.

The strict10mm reference cap is NOT an actual10mm cap. At rate80/s, both
disturbance cases exceed10mm sampled actual travel; the largest is
0.010003275995800445m. The figure and paper must preserve this distinction.
Agreement of1ms and0.5ms output grids is a resolution check. It proves neither
convergence nor error bounds. N180 has no accepted candidate and establishes
no reference-grid convergence.

Comparison fairness and timing limitation
The prefix, moving reference state, actual state, return goal, arrival time,
plant, controller, output grids and scoring are matched. The new comparator
has extra jerk-continuity and reference-travel constraints. Native fast is a
different arrival policy; native matched is not a squared-jerk optimizer.
It is fair to report the resulting tradeoff. It is not fair to claim planner
dominance, equal objective optimality, equal feasible sets or general robustness.

The accepted solve records0.11887738s solver time and0.13767125s whole-attempt
time. Neither is charged before the scheduled0.52s execution start. The root
protocol's wording about receiving the request at0.52s must not be read as
evidence that this computation can finish and execute at that same instant.
All comparisons here idealize planning latency. The timing measures are one
host observation, not a worst-case execution bound. Append an explicit erratum
and label the comparator offline before manuscript integration. Do not alter
the old data or shift this plan retrospectively to hide the issue.
