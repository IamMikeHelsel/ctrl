VELOCITY ADMISSION — bounded builder milestone, 18 October 2026

Task
Add a sufficient all-future velocity check to the existing scalar retained
four-box FIR reference. Use the existing strict-successor knot cells. Preserve
all old sources and interfaces. Stop expansion when the guard, full candidate
contract, nonvacuous example and falsification controls are checked.

Mathematical statement
Assume rational h > 0, a rational current time c, rational signed events, and
V >= 0. The finite guard contains c and every future event knot. On each
adjacent cell [L,R], check the four cubic Bernstein coefficients:
  v(L), v(L)+(R-L)a(L)/3, v(R)-(R-L)a(R)/3, v(R).
Endpoints are shared. The last tail has zero reference velocity. The Lean
proof derives this cubic from the actual truncated-power FIR expressions.
It then proves the convex-hull bound and the rational-to-real bridge.

No exactness claim
The Boolean checker is exactly its declared finite coefficient test. Passing
implies |v(t)| <= V at every real t >= c. Rejection does not prove a speed
violation. It also does not prove infeasibility over all candidate times.
An explicit safe but rejected history is part of this milestone.

Composition
The new candidate checker calls the existing timed A/J/deadline checker and
adds this velocity check. The old acceleration checker is unchanged. The
successor acceleration optimization is imported for cell selection; its
measured runtime is not transferred to this new combined checker.
The selected reference keeps its issued prefix and reaches reference rest
by D. No physical-state, sample-time, torque, contact, saturation or Franka
claim follows automatically.

Examples
The retained delayed return uses the existing h=1/5 s and V=1/15 m/s bound.
Its first event is (11/50 s,1/50 m). Its return is (31/50 s,-1/50 m).
The candidate list stays [13/25,31/50] s. A=1/2 m/s^2, J=15/2 m/s^3 and
D=17/10 s are unchanged. The shorter D=13/10 s rejects the supplied list.
The other exact examples are mathematical controls, not new physical tasks.

Verification and independence
Development uses disposable local caches. The final test starts from empty
local sources and objects, excludes the project build cache, and reuses only
pinned dependency caches. It rebuilds every local module, audits all local
declarations for standard axioms, and replays every module in leanchecker.
An exact Fraction generator constructs cubic powers independently, converts
them to Bernstein coefficients, and creates 180 decisions on 60 histories.
These are builder controls. Only an independent critic can accept the work.
No wall-clock or worst-case execution claim is made.

Preservation
Three failed development versions and their logs are retained. They concern
Bool rewriting, rational casts and negation normalization. Earlier successful
development runs were not treated as final fresh verification. The final
source freeze precedes verification. Root owns repository sync and acceptance.
