A finite speed check for a changing reference

The old motion stays in the calculation when a new goal arrives. Each event
adds a shifted four-box step response. Velocity is a sum of cubic positive
parts. Acceleration is its derivative and is a sum of quadratic positive parts.

Put every event knot after the current time in a finite set. Include the
current time itself. Consecutive points L<R define a cell with no interior
knot. On this cell, each positive-part term is either zero or an ordinary
cubic. Their sum is therefore a cubic.

Let d=R-L and u=(t-L)/d. The exact velocity on the cell is
  v(t)=(1-u)^3 b0 +3u(1-u)^2 b1 +3u^2(1-u)b2 +u^3 b3,
where
  b0=v(L), b1=v(L)+d*a(L)/3,
  b2=v(R)-d*a(R)/3, b3=v(R).
For 0<=u<=1, the four weights are nonnegative and add to one.
If all four |bi|<=V, then |v(t)|<=V throughout that cell.
After the final knot the reference velocity is zero.

Lean checks the cubic identity against the existing reference definition.
It does not assume an externally fitted cubic or sampled trajectory. The
rational checker computes all coefficients exactly. The proof transfers
these rational inequalities to every real future time.

Why endpoints alone are insufficient
Take h=1 and events (0,1),(1,-1). Every event knot has |v|<=1/2. But at
t=9/5, v=409/750, which is greater than 1/2. The coefficient test rejects it.
These numbers describe a mathematical example, not the robot demonstration.

Why rejection is not a proof of failure
Take h=1 and events (0,1),(1/4,-1). The checker rejects V=1/6 because a
Bernstein coefficient is too large. Add the zero-size events (1/2,0),(3/4,0).
They add knots but no motion. The refined checker passes at V=1/6. A Lean
proof then transfers this all-future bound to the unchanged original motion.
Thus the original rejection was conservative. The history representation
and cell partition can change the sufficient test without changing velocity.
No optimal subdivision algorithm or complete speed test is supplied here.

The useful guarantee
The finite candidate selector now checks velocity, acceleration, jerk and
reference-rest deadline conditions. A selected delayed return satisfies all
four and preserves the previously issued reference up to its execution time.
It is still a reference guarantee. A separate physical tracking result must
supply the link to an actual actuator or arm.
