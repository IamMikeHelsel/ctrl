SMOOTH RETURN UNDER A FIXED TRAVEL CAP — ROOT BUILDER REPORT
18 October 2026. Independent review is required before acceptance.

Result
One of six predeclared solver attempts produced an accepted reference. The
N90 plan with a 10 mm forward-reference cap returns to exact reference rest at
1.42 s. It starts from the same moving reference at .52 s, with continuous
position, speed, acceleration and jerk. The actual plant starts at zero and
keeps its state through the shared prefix and revision.

In six declared scalar plant cases, this plan lowers the integral of actual
squared jerk and forward travel relative to the checked FIR return. It raises
peak actual jerk. It passes the generous task and fails the tight task. Thus it
is a stronger smooth comparator, not a universal performance winner.

There are90 intervals of.01 s. Jerk is linear on each interval. Its integral

Interpretation and next decision
The comparison supports a clearer research question: which properties does
the task require, and which are checked before a new plan is executed?
An execution contract can still be useful when a different planner gives a
better tradeoff. Do not present the FIR smoother as the contribution or claim
that minimizing a single derivative measure solves VLA motion in general.

Stop this fixed solver batch. Independently reconstruct the rational reference,
replay saved actual states with a separate engine, recompute all metrics and
task decisions, check the borrowed common-prefix logic, and inspect comparison
fairness. Accept or repair the bounded claims before paper integration.

Limits
No physical robot, learned-policy evaluation, seven-axis dynamics, contact,
sampled controller, actuator clipping, all-time physical bound or global
continuous-time optimum is established. The current protocol has one return
request and one moving initial state. It is not a general planner benchmark.
The candidate was selected by the declared procedure, not chosen after ranking
plant outcomes. New source and figure files are separate from prior frozen
artifacts. The first plot environment probe found no system matplotlib; the
saved data were rendered with existing .tools/franka-sim-python instead.
