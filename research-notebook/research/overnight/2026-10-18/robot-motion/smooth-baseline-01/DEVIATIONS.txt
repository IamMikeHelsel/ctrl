IMPLEMENTATION LIMITS FOUND AFTER THE FIXED SOLVER BATCH

The solver script preserves status, warnings, objective and iteration metadata
for every case. It saves floating variables and constraint residuals only when
the status is optimal. Four cases returned optimal_inaccurate. Their floating
candidate vectors and constraint residuals were not saved. This falls short of
the protocol's intended preservation of all solver residuals. The failed
attempts remain recorded. No solve was repeated to recover or replace them.

The rejected cases therefore support only the operational statement that this
fixed procedure did not deliver an accepted candidate. They do not support an
infeasibility result, reference-grid convergence, or a comparison among optima.
The single accepted N90/10mm candidate retains its raw arrays, exact repair,
exact polynomial checks, residual, timing and both reference objectives.

The baseline field reference_exact_extrema uses floating polynomial roots.
It remains unchanged for provenance. The new candidate's separate rational
Bernstein checks give sufficient exact reference bounds. No floating field is
used as a Lean proof or an all-time bound for physical motion.
