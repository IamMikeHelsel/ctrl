MINIMUM-SUCCESSOR ACCELERATION CHECK — frozen builder report
18 October 2026. Independent review pending; root cannot accept its own proof.

Result
SuccessorGuard.lean proves that the new rational check accepts exactly when h
is positive, A is nonnegative, and reference acceleration stays within A at
all real times at or after current. It also proves the same true/false
acceptance as the accepted all-pairs checker. No knots are sorted by external
code. A minimum strictly later point defines each consecutive interval.

The new algorithm checks all guard-point accelerations. For each point it also
checks an interior zero of affine jerk, when one exists in its next interval.
A flat jerk interval needs no division or interior candidate. Current time,
queued events, repeated knots and signed increments stay in the contract.

Verification
Seven local modules rebuilt from saved source. Standard axiom audit covered
231 local declarations. Five separate kernel replays passed. Four deliberately
false acceptance/successor statements were rejected. Fixtures include a
nonzero acceleration plateau with zero jerk, a strict interior maximum,
current time inside a cell, expired/queued events, cancellation, invalid h/A,
and the original failed and repaired return. Earlier development failures
remain in dev-run-01.log. No proof source changed after the saved freeze.

Limits
This is the declared scalar reference acceleration bound. It is not physical
arm acceleration, a velocity contract, dispatch latency, or a hardware test.
The proof does not establish bit complexity or runtime bounds. Completed-tail
pruning is an independently accepted separate equivalence. Many active events
can still be expensive. The benchmark did not combine pruning with this check.
