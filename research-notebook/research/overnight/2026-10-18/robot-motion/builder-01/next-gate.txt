RANKED NEXT GATES

1. Review the frozen exact acceleration proof before integration.
   Ask a fresh critic to reconstruct the point list, test off-knot extrema,
   constant-jerk intervals, current time, old active events and future events.
   The all-pairs implementation is exact but is not a measured real-time kernel.
   A sorted-adjacent optimization needs its own equivalence proof.

2. Compose the new reference witness with the existing actual-plant tracking
   theorem. Use the old initial state at time 0. Keep the physical state and
   retained history continuous at 0.52 s and 0.62 s. Do not reset a tracking
   error cube at the deferred event. This can turn the reference position
   arrival into a conditional physical position result. It cannot turn the
   reference acceleration/velocity bounds into actual acceleration/velocity
   bounds without separate estimates.

3. Test the new policy against the frozen strong baselines on a NEW protocol.
   The policy is: try candidate execution times in order, require all retained
   reference events to finish by the supplied deadline, and accept only when
   both exact acceleration and jerk checks pass. The concrete return list
   [0.52, 0.62] chooses 0.62 s. It is causally available at 0.52 s and still
   finishes the reference at 1.42 s before the unchanged 1.70 s deadline.
   The old negative result remains intact. Compare actual task completion,
   delay, peak error and actual jerk. Include outcomes where no candidate fits.
   Our finite-list rejection is not a proof that no controller can succeed.

4. Add a general velocity check or a conservative fallback service theorem.
   The isolated-tail fallback is tractable: wait until the full retained tail
   ends, then propose an isolated increment. Candidate isolated constants are
   max |F1| = 2/3, max |F2| = 2/3, max |F3| = 2. F1's full range is proved in
   this batch; the exact F2 value 2/3 at 4/3 is proved but its general upper
   bound still needs a theorem in the fallback's own source closure. F3's
   isolated bound is inherited from task-contract-08. Require a supplied
   deadline and all three scaled bounds. This service rule is conservative.
   It cannot substitute for competitive mid-motion planning.

   A full velocity checker is harder because velocity is cubic and its
   stationary points are roots of quadratic acceleration. Those roots need
   not be rational. Options are certified algebraic roots or rational interval
   and Bernstein certificates. Neither is assumed solved here. A sufficient
   interval certificate would be useful but must not be called exact.

5. Tie the model to Franka before any hardware guarantee.
   A scalar reference result does not cover coupled joint dynamics, task-space
   mapping, torque, position-dependent joint limits, firmware filters or
   emergency-stop behavior. Use the simulation harness to identify and state
   the needed model error and controller assumptions first.

PUBLICATION QUESTION

Can this become a useful formal contract for an existing replan interface,
with a clear benefit over strong delivery-time and spline-motion baselines?
The exact polynomial check is established mathematics; formalization and a
small feasible witness do not establish a novel robot controller. The most
useful next evidence is physical-task composition plus a fixed, challenging
comparison that allows the strong baselines to succeed.
