Results and fairness
At the fine grid, all three methods pass all six generous plant cases.
Native fast passes all six tight cases. Retained FIR rejects all six tight
revisions and continues to the old 20 mm target. Native matched accepts the
revision but fails all three tight terminal bands in every plant case.
Twenty-four native trace pairs are identical across the two deadline tasks.

All 198 reported summary range/count fields agree with the independent
replay. Suffix metrics use the same intervals, [0.52,1.42] and [0.52,2.5] s.
Command variation includes the jump at revision. The generous FIR/native
matched squared-jerk ratio is 0.39780–0.40154. The forward-excursion ratio
is 2.08536–2.08963; the path-length ratio is 1.83847–1.84224.
Thus the lower jerk result survives the common-state comparison, but has a
clear travel cost. Matching arrival removes a timing confound; it does not
match path geometry or prove an equal-objective optimum. Ruckig's duration
constraint is not a squared-jerk minimization objective.

Squared-jerk integral has units m^2/s^5. It is a smoothness proxy, not physical
energy. Command variation has units m/s^2. Extra forward excursion is travel
beyond actual position at revision; it is not overshoot past the final goal.
Actual native acceleration can exceed the reference acceleration limit.
No reference limit was transferred to the physical plant by this review.
