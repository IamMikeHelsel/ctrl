Proof sketch for the candidate sign formula
The rightmost factor acts first, so indices are toggled in the order 6,...,0.
At index i, changes to larger indices do not alter the number of occupied indices
below i. The exterior reordering contributes that count. A contraction adds one
minus sign because Ni=Ci-Ai. The total parity is therefore
  sum_i #{j in I : j<i} + |I| = sum_{j in I}(7-j).
This derivation uses the already accepted actual creation/contraction basis
formulas. It remains to be expressed as a Lean theorem about the finite product.
If U^T U=2I is proved for the paired columns, the explicit inverse is U^T/2.
No eigenvalue numerical tolerance or choice of an unrelated form is required.
