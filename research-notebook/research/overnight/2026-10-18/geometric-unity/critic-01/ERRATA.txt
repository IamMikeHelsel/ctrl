GU analyst-01: independent scope corrections
18 October 2026 UTC

The frozen analyst-01 files remain unchanged. Use these corrections when preparing
an external question list or a paper. The corrections do not invalidate the
bounded Lean action or the finite adjoint calculation.

1. An action is already written.
The draft gives the first-order bosonic action in equation (9.4), page 44.
Analyst question 6 says that a concrete action is needed. That wording can imply
that no action is supplied. Replace it with a request to complete the domains,
independent fields, variation conventions and boundary assumptions of (9.4).
An explicit expression and a fully specified variational problem are different
requirements. The independent review has not derived its field equations.

2. The compatible-form uniqueness uses all vector generators.
The exact calculation solves G_a^T H + H G_a = 0 for all 14 Clifford vector
generators. Under this premise it correctly gives H=cK. Do not shorten this to
"Spin invariance selects a unique Hermitian form." That weaker premise is false
for this claim. With D the even/odd exterior parity matrix, iKD is Hermitian and
invariant under every real bivector. It is independent of K and fails the vector
skew-adjoint conditions. review_adjoint.py checks this exact witness.

3. The complex adjoint must conjugate scalars.
The accepted complex bar sends i to -i, reverses products and negates real vector
generators. This convention is required for the proposed K^{-1} A^dagger K bridge.
It agrees with the real/imaginary grade pattern in equation (3.27). The nearby
source prose names the grade automorphism and reversal but does not state scalar
conjugation explicitly. Ask the author to confirm it. Do not silently treat
complex-linear Clifford conjugation as a Hermitian adjoint. The iI control fails
if conjugate transpose is replaced by transpose.

4. The bounded source result remains bounded.
This gate constructs an actual representation and proves two dimensions. It does
not prove faithfulness, identify the real form in Lean, select Phi, or validate
the wider claims in equations (3.19)-(3.20) or the global theory. The subsequent
projector and matrix-unit gate may prove faithfulness without resolving Phi.
