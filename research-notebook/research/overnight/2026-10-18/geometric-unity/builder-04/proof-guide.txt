What this gate proves

The preserved algebra is GUComplex.CCl = C tensor_real Cl(7,7). The real Clifford
algebra and its ordered exterior representation are the accepted ones. The new
complexMatrixEquiv is a complex algebra equivalence to 128 by 128 complex matrices.
For every complex z and real Clifford element a, its exact coordinate formula is:
  complexMatrixEquiv(z tensor a)[I,T] = z * ofReal(splitMatrixEquiv(a)[I,T]).
It has an inverse for every complex matrix. Its multiplication is matrix
multiplication, which sums over the common index.

The map construction
ComplexEquivalence.lean defines realToComplexMatrix by the coefficient algebra
map R -> C, applied entrywise. complexLinearEquiv first applies the existing
real splitMatrixEquiv under tensor extension. Two finite-product linear
equivalences then distribute the complex tensor over the matrix rows and columns.
Their inverses are defined in the pinned Mathlib source. This proves bijectivity
without a fresh faithfulness assumption or a dimension-only assertion.

The library helper algEquivOfLinearEquivTensorProduct upgrades that actual linear
equivalence only after the source proves multiplication and the identity on pure
tensors. The pure multiplication proof uses realToComplexMatrix.map_mul and the
commuting scalar/matrix multiplication law. A function-space algebra with
pointwise multiplication is never substituted for the matrix algebra.

The adjoint bridge
ComplexBridge.lean defines Kc by entrywise extension of the accepted real K.
K is the ordered product N0*N1*N2*N3*N4*N5*N6 of the actual negative generators.
The source proves Kc is Hermitian and Kc*Kc=I from the accepted real identities.
The accepted GUComplex.bar is reused byte-for-byte. It conjugates complex
scalars, negates real Clifford vectors and reverses products.

The pure-tensor bridge combines bar_tmul with the accepted real adjoint bridge.
Tensor induction then gives, for every x in CCl:
  complexMatrixEquiv(bar x) = Kc * conjugateTranspose(complexMatrixEquiv x) * Kc.
The inverse-image theorem covers every complex matrix. skew_iff_matrix transports
the actual bar-skew condition, without assuming it of the representation.

Non-vacuity and false controls
ComplexControls.lean proves the imaginary scalar identity is not fixed by bar,
and that replacing conjugate transpose with ordinary transpose fails on that
actual input. Two off-diagonal matrix units do not commute. Their actual
Clifford preimages therefore do not commute either. Client.lean.txt checks the
forward and inverse maps, scalar mixtures, product reversal, vector behavior and
the form-adjoint involution. The deliberately false clients are separate files
and must fail for mathematical reasons, not missing imports or names.

Limits
This is standard algebra formalization. No signature theorem, global Spin-group
claim, Phi choice, action variation, field equation or physical prediction is
proved here. In particular, this result does not repair missing GU operator
choices. It does not establish publication novelty. Fresh local verification
still trusts pinned Lean, prebuilt third-party dependencies and hardware. An
independent critic must accept this gate before it enters accepted claims.
