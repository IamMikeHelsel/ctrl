The local Mathlib source supplies:
- RingTheory/TensorProduct/Maps.lean:
  Algebra.TensorProduct.lift and lift_tmul; congr; map.
- LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Pi.lean:
  TensorProduct.piRight and TensorProduct.piScalarRight are linear equivalences.
- LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Free.lean:
  Algebra.TensorProduct.equivPiOfFiniteBasis.

A concrete route to bijectivity may avoid a fresh dimension argument. First use
Algebra.TensorProduct.congr with the accepted splitMatrixEquiv. Then use the
linear piRight equivalence for matrix rows, and piScalarRight for columns.
The resulting pure-tensor coordinate formula should be
  (z tensor A)_(I,J) -> z * ofReal(A_(I,J)).
Check the exact signatures and scalar towers before implementing this route.

Do not directly use RingTheory/TensorProduct/Pi's algebra equivalence as the
matrix-algebra result: its function algebra multiplies pointwise, while matrix
multiplication sums over the middle index. Use the linear equivalence for
bijectivity and separately prove compatibility with matrix multiplication, or
identify it with the algebra lift through the exact pure-tensor formula.

CliffordModel. Its sibling AdjointControls.lean has the same module filename as
this new gate's controls; avoid that module-name collision rather than editing
frozen old sources. The main accepted ComplexAdjoint module has no such clash.
