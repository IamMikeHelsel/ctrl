Proof route

1. TransposeBridge.creation_transpose compares each matrix entry. If a basis
   subset contains i, reuse the ordered split A ++ i :: B in contraction and
   creation from the erased subset. Both coefficients use (-1)^length(A).
   All other entries are zero. Thus this is the actual exterior-basis transpose,
   not an adjoint axiom for unrelated abstract generators.

2. The accepted Fock formulas identify each positive generator as contraction
   plus creation, and each negative generator as creation minus contraction.
   Their transpose laws follow from step 1.

3. VolumeForm obtains squares and pairwise anticommutation from the accepted
   Clifford relations through splitMatrix. K is the ordered matrix product
   N0 N1 N2 N3 N4 N5 N6. Rewriting these finite products proves K^T=K and K^2=I.
   The product commutes with all negative generators and anticommutes with all
   positive generators. The finite inequality checks use ordinary decide.

4. RealAdjoint defines the real-linear map A -> K A^T K. K^2=I makes it reverse
   multiplication. The generator transpose and commutation results make it
   negate all fourteen vector generators. A basis expansion covers every real
   vector. CliffordAlgebra.induction then covers scalar maps, vectors, products
   and sums. The final statement uses the same splitMatrixEquiv as builder-02.

5. AdjointControls proves the positive generator is not Euclidean-skew, omitting
   the right K fails on that vector, and replacing a negative generator by the
   creation-plus-contraction combination gives the wrong square. It also proves
   that reversing the K factors gives -K, and -K gives the same adjoint.
   A separate complex scalar matrix control shows why plain transpose cannot
   replace conjugate transpose on iI. That control is not a complex map theorem.

Logical boundary
The only construction inputs are the real split Clifford algebra, its accepted
exterior representation and ordered basis, and Mathlib's real Clifford star.
No faithfulness, transpose or matrix-unit law is added as a premise. Existing
faithfulness is reused from the separately accepted concrete equivalence.
The source uses real scalars. In this setting Clifford star fixes scalars.
The accepted complex tensor algebra bar conjugates complex scalars, so extending
this result to it is a separate proof. Signature and operator Phi remain open.
