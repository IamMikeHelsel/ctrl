Development notes, not verification evidence

The early FockOperators logs are retained in this folder. The final successful
source is in the parent directory. No failed source is imported by the final
closure. Development logs can be superseded by later compilations; fresh-*.log
and verification.json are the final self-check evidence.

Resolved API issues in this batch included:
- ExteriorAlgebra.ι_add_mul_swap takes the scalar ring as a named implicit argument.
- List membership in Fin 7 enumeration was proved through List.mem_ofFn, avoiding
  an unhelpful expansion into seven equality cases.
- Fin.natAdd normalizes to Fin.addNat in this pinned library. The generator image
  lemmas use a consistent constructor and explicit Pi.single_apply facts.
- Products of annihilator operators reverse their input list. This is proved by
  the list product API; it is not a definitional reduction.
- Algebra.lmul product laws require linear-map extensionality and associativity.
- The final finite sum needed the sum-of-zero simplification before the delta sum.
- FiniteDimensional R Cl is supplied from the already proved positive finrank.
- A direct change tactic on the explicit seven-factor projector reached the
  elaborator recursion limit. Rewriting Module.End.mul_apply instead avoids that
  expansion. The failed source and log are retained as VacuumProjector-idempotent-01.

The deliberate negative clients are preserved as complete .lean.txt files in the
parent directory, with their rejection logs. OrderControls.lean also proves the
actual counter-results, so the controls do more than test elaboration failures.
