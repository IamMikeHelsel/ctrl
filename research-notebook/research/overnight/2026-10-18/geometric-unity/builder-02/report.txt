Meaning and value
This is a formal proof of a standard split Clifford construction. It supports
one precise algebraic statement associated with the GU source. It is useful as a
formal-library component and as the base for the missing adjoint bridge. It is
not new mathematical physics. There is no publication-readiness claim here.
The fixed dimension seven proof can be generalized later if a library review
finds that generalization useful. No such generalization is needed for this gate.

Still open
The complex scalar extension, conjugate-linear adjoint bridge, and Hermitian
signature (64,64) are not established by these new Lean files. The earlier exact
matrix calculation remains separate computational evidence. This gate does not
identify a unitary Lie algebra in Lean. It does not choose Phi, derive the global
field equations, or test the physical claims of Geometric Unity.
