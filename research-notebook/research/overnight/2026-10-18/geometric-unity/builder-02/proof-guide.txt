Proof guide

1. Fix the actual operators.
FockOperators.lean defines c_i as left exterior multiplication by e_i. It defines
a_i as contraction by the dual coordinate. They act on ExteriorAlgebra R R^7.
The proofs use Mathlib's contraction laws, not assumed anticommutation laws.

FockImage.lean proves, for the actual splitAction:
  G_i^+ = a_i + c_i
  G_i^- = c_i - a_i
  splitAction ((gamma_i^+ + gamma_i^-)/2) = c_i
  splitAction ((gamma_i^+ - gamma_i^-)/2) = a_i
The factors of one half and the negative-generator sign are explicit.
The four low/high coordinate lemmas check the split map on every coordinate.
No surjectivity of splitLinear is assumed.

2. Link to the actual basis.
VacuumProjector.basis_word identifies spinBasis T with its increasing exterior
word. WordActions proves the signs obtained when a generator passes a prefix.
BasisActions proves the insertion and erasure formulas for every subset:
  c_i b_T = 0 if i is in T;
  a_i b_T = 0 if i is not in T;
  c_i b_T = (-1)^k b_(T union {i}) when i is absent;
  a_i b_T = (-1)^k b_(T minus {i}) when i is present.
Here k is the length of the increasing prefix before i. The exact Lean statements
quantify that prefix and suffix, so there is no unstated ordering convention.

3. Project to the vacuum.
Let P be the increasing product of a_i c_i. Multiplication of endomorphisms means
composition: a_i c_i first creates, then annihilates. Each factor preserves a
word with i absent and kills a word with i present. Therefore
  P b_T = 1 if T is empty, and 0 otherwise;
  P P = P.
FullImage.vacuum_image exhibits the actual Clifford element that maps to P.

4. Build every matrix unit.
Let C_I create the increasing word I. Let A_J be the reversed product of the
annihilators for J. The rightmost operator acts first. MatrixUnits proves
  C_I P A_J b_T = b_I if J=T, and 0 otherwise.
The reversed annihilator order makes the nonzero coefficient +1.
The proof covers arbitrary subset indices I,J,T, not a finite list of examples.

5. Lift an arbitrary endomorphism.
FullImage.preimage f is the double finite sum of the actual Clifford matrix-unit
elements, weighted by the coordinates of f(b_J). The theorem preimage_image
shows that its image is f. This proves surjectivity of splitAction and splitMatrix.

6. Deduce faithfulness.
RealEquivalence uses this proved surjectivity and the earlier dimension equality.
Both the real Clifford algebra and the target matrix algebra have dimension
16384. The finite-dimensional linear-map theorem then proves injectivity.
AlgEquiv.ofBijective packages this specific map as splitMatrixEquiv.
Dimension equality alone is not used as a faithfulness argument.

7. Check the dangerous conventions.
OrderControls proves three counterchecks on the same exterior space:
  c_i a_i sends the vacuum to 0, so it is not the vacuum projector;
  (gamma_i^- - gamma_i^+)/2 sends c_i(1) to -1;
  a_0 a_1 applied to b_{0,1} gives -1 after vacuum projection.
The correct reversed annihilator product gives +1. The negative clients try the
wrong +1 claims and must fail. The positive client checks the intended results.
