ONE TARGET FOR THE NEXT GU MATHEMATICS POD
18 October 2026 UTC

Decision
Build the faithful split Clifford representation and its compatible adjoint.
This is a standalone formalization of standard mathematics. It also checks a
specific source claim. It does not select a Shiab operator or validate physics.

Exact target
Let Cl be the accepted real CliffordAlgebra Q, where Q has seven positive and
seven negative squares. Let S = ExteriorAlgebra R (Fin 7 -> R), with its ordered
subset basis. The new splitMatrix is the actual Clifford action in this basis.
Prove that splitMatrix is an algebra equivalence onto all 128 by 128 real
matrices. Define K as the product of its seven negative generator matrices.
Prove K^T=K, K^2=I, and a concrete congruence to diag(I64,-I64). After complex
base change, prove that the accepted bar is transported to K^-1 A^dagger K.
Conclude that the accepted skew subspace maps exactly to the K-skew-Hermitian
matrices. This is the precise u(64,64) identification that remains open.

Why this target ranks first
The draft names this algebra and real form, and asks for its decomposition to
be checked. This target does not depend on the missing Phi choice. It fills an
actual API gap in the pinned Mathlib. The construction is useful for other split
Clifford representations, not only this audit. A formalization contribution is
plausible; a new physics result or mathematical discovery is not claimed.

What this analyst already built
HyperbolicSpinor.lean defines, for any commutative ring R and R-module M,
  c(f,v)(x) = contraction_f(x) + v wedge x.
It proves c(f,v)^2 = f(v) Id and uses the universal Clifford lift to construct
an actual action of Cl(dualProd) on the actual exterior algebra. The mixed
anticommutator normalization is one, not two.
SpinorDimensions.lean gives the actual matrix coordinates and proves dim S=128.
SplitSpinor.lean gives an explicit form-preserving map from the accepted Q to
dualProd, composes the action, and proves dim Cl=16384. It checks positive and
negative generator squares and supplies a nonzero witness. There is no theorem
that this representation is faithful or onto. No adjoint claim is smuggled into
its definition. The new modules are a draft until an independent review passes.

Gate ladder and stop rules
1. First, review the draft and its exact normalization. Expose its action on
   basis subsets and compare with creation/contraction signed permutations.
2. Build a vacuum projector P and matrix units a_I P b_J. Prove their action on
   every subset basis vector. This is the main missing proof. It proves
   surjectivity without assuming irreducibility or the structure theorem.
3. Use the already proved dimensions to prove bijectivity. State the actual
   algebra equivalence. A dimension equality by itself is insufficient.
4. Prove the generator K-adjoint identities. Extend the adjoint bridge by
   Clifford induction and complex tensor induction. Do not assume the bridge.
5. Give explicit positive/negative basis vectors for K, or an exact invertible
   congruence matrix. A floating-point eigensolver is not acceptable evidence.
6. Require an independent client, all-public declaration axiom audit, separate
   kernel replay, and semantic negative controls.

Do not claim the complete target after only steps 1 or 2. A first 60-minute
builder gate should deliver basis action formulas and a working projector.
If it cannot, preserve the exact API obstacle and reconsider scope. Do not spend
hours expanding representation theory beyond this finite split case. A later
generic n theorem is a separate library-quality improvement.

Exact preflight
exact_adjoint_gate.py solves all 229376 entry equations G_a^T H+H G_a=0 using
signed union-find over 16384 variables. There are 128 components: 127 are forced
to zero and one is free. Thus the computed solution space is cK. Hermitian H
requires c real. K pairs each subset with its complement. The supplied 64 pairs
provide an integer congruence with diagonal entries +2 and -2. This proves the
signature by elementary algebra if the construction is reviewed. It is exact
Python evidence, not yet a kernel proof or a bridge to splitMatrix's coordinates.

Falsification tests
- Ordinary transpose with Euclidean I must fail for positive generators.
- Replacing negative a-b by a+b must fail the negative square test.
- Contraction/creation mixed factor 2 must fail (the form is f(v)).
- Replacing conjugate transpose with transpose must fail on iI.
- Omitting any matrix-unit family must not leave a claim of surjectivity.
- The K congruence must be invertible and cover all 128 basis coordinates.
- Match the matrix generator signs with the accepted Q and bar conventions.

Other candidates, ranked

4. Global field equations, physical predictions, or a full GU consistency verdict.
   Not ready. The operator and domain are not yet fixed by the inspected sources.

Publication gate
The accepted package plus a faithful matrix/adjoint bridge could support a
reproducible formalization note or library contribution. It is not enough to
claim GU is complete, correct, or refuted. Before submission, identify the venue,
check current library/prior-art overlap, and obtain a subject-matter review of
both the source reading and the actual theorem statements. Do not count theorem
names as mathematical novelty. No submission or external contact was made.
