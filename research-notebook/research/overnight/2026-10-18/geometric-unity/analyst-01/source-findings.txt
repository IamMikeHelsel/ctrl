Questions that would unblock the source theory
1. Which normalized invariant tensors are Phi_1 and Phi_2? Give either basis
   coefficients or a uniquely defining representation-theoretic construction.
2. For each bracket in (9.3), is the product AB-BA or i(AB+BA)? State all form
   signs, factors, curvature normalization, orientation and Hodge convention.
3. What is the exact Weyl domain? Is it ordinary algebraic Riemann curvature,
   a larger ad-valued two-form space, or a domain transported with epsilon?
4. Which Bianchi identity selects the operator? State the full identity and
   which connection and torsion assumptions it uses. Give the selection proof.
5. Which consistent gauge/torsion sign convention is intended throughout the
   2021 draft and 2025 update? The local negative-tilt defect is already proved.
6. Which variables are fixed in the action variation and pullback? A concrete
   action, domain and boundary assumptions are needed before deriving equations.

These are prepared questions, not messages sent to an author. The matrix target
can proceed without answers. Global GU claims cannot be completed by silently
choosing answers on the author's behalf.
