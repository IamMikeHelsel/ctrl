Independent statement review: builder-05

Actual objects
The matrix K is the existing ordered product N0*N1*...*N6 in VolumeForm.lean.
TransposeBridge.lean defines each Ni as the image of the corresponding negative
Clifford generator. Its negativeMatrix_fock theorem identifies that same matrix
with the actual creation matrix minus the actual contraction matrix.
FockOperators.lean defines these operators by exterior left multiplication and
CliffordAlgebra.contractLeft. They are not arbitrary matrices with desired laws.

Actual basis action
ComplementAction.negative_basis uses BasisActions' insertion and contraction
formulas. prefix_length identifies their list-position sign with the count of
occupied lower indices. negative_mulVec transports that result through the actual
spinBasis coordinate map. product_mulVec then proves the action of the ordered
matrix product. Only after this bridge does signedToggles_seven use ordinary
kernel evaluation to enumerate all 128 finite subsets. K_mulVec_basis therefore
proves the action of the actual K; it has no assumed complement-action premise.

The rightmost factor acts first. In order 6, ..., 0 an initially occupied index j
contributes 6-j lower-index swaps and one contraction minus sign. The total sign
is (-1)^(sum over j in T of (7-j)). The empty set has sign +1. The singletons {0}
and {6} have sign -1; {0,6} has sign +1. These are independently probed.

Change of basis and normalization
D is diagonal with +1 on subsets without 0 and -1 on subsets with 0. Complementation
flips this sign. Thus the actual K anticommutes with D. The already proved K^T=K
and K^2=I, together with D^2=I, give U=I+KD and U^T U=UU^T=2I. The same U satisfies
KU=UD and U^T K U=2D. Both inverse equations use U^T/2. The factor cannot be omitted.

The source has two exact cardinality theorems, each 64. It also states every
congruence entry, including off-diagonal zeros. It does not construct a Fin64
reindexing or use an abstract inertia API. The explicit invertible congruence is
a sufficient mathematical certificate of 64 positive and 64 negative directions.
Any prose should say that grouping these two indexed sets yields diag(2I64,-2I64).
The choice of coordinate 0 is an explicit basis choice, not a physical field choice.

Complex result
ComplexSignature maps the same real U,D and inverse entrywise to complex matrices.
Kc remains the accepted entrywise image of the actual K. realToComplexMatrix is
an algebra homomorphism for matrix multiplication, not pointwise multiplication.
The mapped transpose is conjugate transpose because the source matrix is real.
The result proves Uc^H Kc Uc=2Dc and both inverse equations with Uc^H/2.
The complex form is Hermitian by the inherited Kc_conjTranspose theorem.
It is a split Hermitian form with the same 64+64 certificate.

Non-vacuity and limitations
There are no additional premises in the fixed matrix congruences. Explicit
positive and negative entries and a nonzero killed vector for I+K prevent a
vacuous or singular-basis reading. New reviewer clients also check eigen-columns
for the actual K and the sign of two concrete subset columns.
The (7,7) generator form and (64,64) spinor form concern different vector spaces.
No physical dimension, observable, field equation or stability statement follows.
This gate does not prove uniqueness of Spin-invariant forms. Earlier review found
that full vector-skew conditions are stronger than Spin invariance.
The definition of the complex bar remains fixed in the formalization; matching
that convention to the source is a separate unanswered question.
