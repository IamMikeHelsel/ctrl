Next GU mathematics gate: 60 minutes on the actual matrix adjoint

The real matrix-equivalence gate is the input. Work in a new builder folder and
keep all accepted files unchanged. Do not choose Phi or alter the accepted bar.

Primary target
For the actual splitMatrix, define G_i^+ and G_i^- from the fourteen real
generators. Define K as the ordered product of its seven negative generators.
Prove in Lean:
  K^T=K, K*K=I,
  splitMatrix(star x)=K*(splitMatrix x)^T*K for every real Clifford element x.
The source convention is Clifford conjugation: reverse products and negate each
real vector generator. This is Mathlib's real Clifford star in the accepted gate.

Acceptance path
1. Prove from the actual exterior-basis formulas that the creation matrix's
   transpose equals the annihilation matrix. Do not postulate a new adjoint law.
   Hence each G_i^+ is symmetric and each G_i^- is skew-symmetric.
   Reuse one decomposition ordered(T)=A++i::B for both entries. The proved
   ordered_erase_of_split gives ordered(T.erase i)=A++B, so creation and
   contraction have the same prefix sign without a new sign-count convention.
2. Use the actual Clifford relations to prove K^2=I, K^T=K, anticommutation with
   each positive generator and commutation with each negative generator.
3. Prove the real star bridge on scalars and generators, then extend it to every
   Clifford element. State the theorem for the existing splitMatrixEquiv map.

If this closes before the deadline, begin the complex bridge, but report its
status separately. Use the accepted CCl = C tensor_R Cl and GUComplex.bar.
Construct rho with
  rho(z tensor a)=z * ofReal(splitMatrix(a)),
and prove
  rho(bar z)=K*conjugateTranspose(rho z)*K.
Complex scalar conjugation is required: bar(i)=-i. The plain-transpose variant
must fail on iI. Do not identify the complex skew space with a full matrix real
form until the complex map is also proved bijective.

Signature is the following gate, unless it fits without weakening this target.
The earlier exact congruence is only a guide. Link K to complement pairs in the
actual ordered exterior basis. Prove an explicit invertible congruence to a
diagonal matrix with 64 positive and 64 negative entries. No floating-point
eigenvalues or unrelated matrix model can replace that theorem.

Required controls
- Ordinary Euclidean transpose skewness fails for a positive generator.
- Substituting creation+annihilation for a negative generator must fail its square.
- Omitting a K factor must fail a required vector-adjoint identity. Reversing the
  seven-factor order changes K to -K, which leaves the adjoint bridge unchanged;
  do not label that harmless overall scale a counterexample to the bridge.
- Complex transpose without scalar conjugation fails on iI.
- Keep the exact iKD witness showing that Spin/bivector invariance alone does
  not select a unique compatible Hermitian form. Full vector skewness is the
  stronger premise used by the prior uniqueness calculation.

At 60 minutes, freeze the proved statements, attempts and exact missing lemma.
Return for independent review. A partial transpose or real bridge is useful, but
must not be called a completed complex adjoint or signature proof.
