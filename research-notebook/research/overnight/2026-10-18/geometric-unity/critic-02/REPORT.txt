The actual map splitMatrix is proved to be an algebra equivalence from Cl(7,7)

Logical audit
The proof constructs creation and contraction on the actual exterior algebra.
It does not assume their operator laws. Their Clifford preimages use the correct
halves: creator=(gamma_positive+gamma_negative)/2 and
annihilator=(gamma_positive-gamma_negative)/2. Every one of the seven coordinates
is covered. No surjectivity of splitLinear is assumed or needed.

The ordered basis is Mathlib's exterior basis, linked to the increasing word by
basis_word. Insertion and erasure use the length of the ordered prefix for the
sign. Present creation and absent annihilation give zero. The proofs of the
exact basis formulas include the ordering and membership conditions explicitly.

The vacuum product uses annihilation after creation. Its proof covers all basis
vectors: the empty basis vector is preserved and every nonempty one is killed.
It is idempotent. Reversing each factor gives a different operator; the finite
control shows that opposite product selects the fully occupied word instead.

For all I,J,T, matrixUnit I J sends b_T to b_I when J=T and to zero otherwise.
The annihilator product is reversed so its application follows increasing J.
The actual Clifford matrixUnitElement has that exact action. The double finite
sum preimage reconstructs every endomorphism from its basis coordinates. This
proves surjectivity of splitAction and splitMatrix without a dimension argument.

Only after surjectivity is proved does RealEquivalence use the equal dimension
16384 to prove injectivity. AlgEquiv.ofBijective packages this same splitMatrix.
There is no hidden faithfulness assumption or unrelated representation swap.
The statements have no unproved projector, basis-action or matrix-unit premises.
