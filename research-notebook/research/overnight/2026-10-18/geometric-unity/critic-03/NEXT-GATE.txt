Recommended next gate: actual complex scalar extension

A bounded builder batch can now construct rho on the preserved algebra
GUComplex.CCl = complex tensor_real Cl. Require the pure-tensor equation:
  rho(z tensor a) = z * ofReal(splitMatrix(a)).
Use the actual accepted real representation. A complex matrix model unrelated
to that formula would not close this gap.

Prove rho is a complex algebra map. Derive bijectivity from the real algebra
equivalence plus a linear tensor-to-matrix equivalence, or from an equally
explicit inverse. TensorProduct.piRight and piScalarRight in the pinned library
supply the relevant linear maps. They do not directly prove matrix multiplication:
pointwise multiplication of a function space is a different algebra structure.
The matrix product must be handled by the algebra lift or by a separate proof.

Then extend K entrywise and prove for every complex tensor element a:
  rho(GUComplex.bar a) = Kc * conjugateTranspose(rho a) * Kc.
Begin with pure tensors, then tensor induction. Test i times the identity,
products with noncommuting factors, scalar mixtures and the inverse image of
arbitrary matrices. Retain a false plain-transpose control. If bijectivity does
not close, report the all-tensor bridge as partial and name the exact missing
lemma. Do not call a homomorphism an equivalence.

Reuse ComplexAdjoint.lean from complex-adjoint-04 byte-for-byte. Its sibling
AdjointControls module has the same filename as this gate's control module;
avoid that collision without changing either frozen source.

After that, the actual K signature theorem is a separate useful gate. The exact
complement-pair calculation provides a test, not its Lean proof. No source input
for Phi should be invented to extend this algebra work into dynamics or physics.
