Other controls: possible companion result
The new target is a shrinking physical error bound after the reference stops.
It must retain the previous physical state and state the disturbance floor.
A conservative floor is not proof that a tighter target is impossible. A
constant disturbance can permit zero velocity at an offset position. Keep this
sanity check separate from arbitrary time-varying disturbance guarantees.
Integrate this result with the motion paper only if it closes the same task
contract. Do not append an unrelated lemma merely to increase paper size.

Geometric Unity: different deliverable
The accepted Clifford contraction and adjoint results are mathematical pieces.
They do not validate the proposed physical theory. The next gate may provide
the split Clifford representation and its adjoint bridge. Faithfulness and the
matrix signature are separate obligations. An unspecified operator Phi cannot
be supplied silently. A useful deliverable may be a source-specific question
list plus a reusable formalization of known mathematics, rather than a GU paper.
