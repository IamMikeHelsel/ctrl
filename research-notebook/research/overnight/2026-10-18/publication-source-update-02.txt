FOCUSED PRIMARY-SOURCE CHECK — 18 October 2026

Source: Di Cairano, Kalabic and Kolmanovsky, Reference Governor for Network
Control Systems Subject to Variable Time-delay. MERL TR2015-154, author preprint.
https://merl.com/publications/docs/TR2015-154.pdf

Read Section 5, Algorithm 1, equations 32a/b and Remark 23 (printed pp8–9,
PDF pages10–11 in one-based indexing). The plant checks received commands against
constraints and tracking improvement. The authors explicitly discuss packet
overtaking and a command paired with an older desired reference. They distinguish
continued constraint enforcement from degraded tracking of the changed request.

This is close prior work for the timing and task-binding motivation. We cannot
claim that rejecting stale commands, keeping old commands during delay, or
separating task tracking from constraint enforcement is a new design idea.
Our candidate is a specialized checked FIR contract and its exact witnesses.
Our ideal common-clock, atomic-commit assumptions differ from their network
model. No theorem equivalence or broad superiority is asserted.

This was a focused method/source check, not an audit of every proof in the paper.
No source file was copied or published. The paper cites the author's public link.
