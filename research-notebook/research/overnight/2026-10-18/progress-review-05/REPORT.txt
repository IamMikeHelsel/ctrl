What value survives the stronger comparison
The reusable result is a precise interface between finite signed motion history,
all-future reference bounds, and a conditional scalar physical-state guarantee.
The jerk and acceleration checks are exact. The speed check is sufficient and
can reject a safe reference. The proof exposes this difference. Completed tails
can be removed while retaining their position offset; the adjacent-cell checker
preserves acceleration acceptance. These are concrete operations that a future
Lean client can reuse. They do not yet certify the parsing, clock, queue,
floating-point execution, sampled servo, or machine code of a robot controller.

The stronger offline comparator improves both the sampled squared-jerk integral
and forward travel over retained FIR in the six declared plant cases. Its peak
jerk is worse. Native fast alone passes the tight task. Thus retaining every
commitment is a design choice with costs, not a general performance improvement.
The exact reference is valuable as a checked comparison input. It is not a Lean
proof of the numerical plant traces. Its 10 mm reference cap is exceeded by the
sampled physical travel in two cases. Its computation starts and finishes outside
the simulated time budget. Those boundaries must be prominent in the next draft.

The comparison is useful negative evidence: a smoothness score, a derivative
limit, and task success rank the methods differently. The paper can teach this
through a reproducible case. It cannot claim that this distinction or the need
to account for delay is new. SOURCE-MATRIX.txt checks five close primary works.
In particular, a network-governor method already separates delayed-command
constraint checks from stale-goal tracking, and RTC already retains actions
while inference runs. A future-start contract should build on those precedents.
