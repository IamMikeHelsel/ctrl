Version 3 fixed the external input-path race but could still mislabel a proof if its newly saved input.json was edited during verification. The independent reviewer reproduced that case. Version 4 records the hash of the original parsed bytes and requires the saved snapshot to match those bytes before success. All Lean sources, replay code, proof plans, parser and examples are unchanged. This is a narrow provenance repair. It does not authenticate a hostile machine or arbitrary later edits to output files. V3 evidence and its counterexample remain preserved.
