What this result gives us

A robot has three different targets to check. The task states where it must go
and when. The reference states the motion that the controller should follow.
The physical system determines how closely the robot follows that reference.
A guarantee at one stage does not automatically pass to the next stage.

Our example starts moving toward 20 mm, then gets a request to return to zero.
An immediate change can keep jerk below its limit while breaking acceleration.
Waiting 0.10 s repairs the reference limits, but takes longer and adds travel.
A late computation or a result for an old task must not be inserted as though
it was ready earlier. The checked commit rule makes those conditions explicit.

Lean checks the finite reference for all future real times, including between
samples. For the declared scalar motor model, another proof carries the same
physical state into the final hold. It gives a small position band from 1.42 s
and small speed/acceleration bands from 3.42 s. That is a precise conditional
promise. The input bounds and model still need evidence from a real machine.

The comparison keeps the same moving start. It also keeps several definitions
of smoothness separate. A plan can reduce total squared jerk and still increase
its largest jerk. A smoother plan can take more room or miss a deadline.
The strongest smooth comparator was calculated offline; its computation time
was not charged to the motion. The numerical task passes are sampled evidence.

This is a small verified case study and a reusable proof example. The formal
contract is complete within its stated scope. The next publication question is
whether that specialization offers enough value beyond existing work. The next
robotics question is how to connect it to a measured, coupled arm and live clocks.
No robot was tested and no paper was submitted.

Start with examples/on-time.json and examples/late.json. README.txt gives the
commands. Acceptance proves a reference contract for the supplied values.
Rejection proves only that the existing plan stays in place.
