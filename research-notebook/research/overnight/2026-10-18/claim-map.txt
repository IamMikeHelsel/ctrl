Practical implementation gaps
The old all-pairs acceleration checker takes about 22.5 seconds at 32
events in the recorded shared-host Lean --run harness. This is a measured
implementation limitation, not a bound on an optimized native implementation.
history-pruning-01/ is an independently accepted future-reference equivalence.
It removes completed tails, but does not reduce the cost of many active events.
successor-check-01/ proves the same acceleration acceptance using consecutive
intervals. Its builder checks passed; independent review is pending. The new
32-event median is 0.220 seconds. The runs were not simultaneous and do not
establish real-time suitability or a worst-case execution bound.
Actual dispatch time, timestamp error, sensors, saturation, coupled dynamics,
contact and hardware acceptance still need separate assumptions and evidence.
