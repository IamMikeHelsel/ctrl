INDEPENDENT SOURCE AND ATTRIBUTION REVIEW
18 October2026. Scope: claims actually present in the frozen six-page paper.

FIR2012
Read the locally preserved author PDF text, pp33-34, plus the focused source
record. It explicitly discusses reversal overlap that doubles acceleration,
and waiting before the next opposite-direction request. The paper correctly
treats its reversal example as prior art and a regression case.
Local source: research/overnight/2026-10-18/fir-source-review-01/biagiotti2012.pdf
Primary URL:
https://web.ing.unimo.it/~lbiagiotti/Papers/FIR%20filters%20for%20online%20trajectory%20planning%20with%20time-%20and%20frequency-domain%20specifications.pdf

FIR2019
Checked the locally preserved source's abstract/rest-to-rest scope and institutional
record. The draft's narrow statement about time optimization under kinematic and
frequency constraints is supported. Its bibliography is incomplete but not false:
add Control Engineering Practice87,43-58, DOI10.1016/j.conengprac.2019.03.017.
https://iris.unimore.it/handle/11380/1175126

Ruckig
The official arXivv2 title, authors, RSS2021 venue and arbitrary initial-state
claim support the draft's short description. The draft does not claim a jerk-
minimization objective for the native method.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.04830v2

Reference governors
The publisher DOI was inaccessible to this tool. The authors' MERL technical
report was accessible and read in the introduction. It describes modifying
references to enforce future state/control constraints, including disturbance
and parameter uncertainty. The draft's broad prior-work statement is supported.
This is not an independent audit of every governor theorem.
https://merl.com/publications/docs/TR2016-102.pdf

ModelPlex
Read the author-hosted2014 article's abstract/introduction and checked title,
authors, venue and pages. It combines offline proofs with runtime model checks
and bounded deviations. The draft correctly does not claim it invented checked
execution or the model-to-plant distinction.
https://symbolaris.com/pub/modelplex.pdf

RTC and VLA-RAIL
Refreshed the specific arXivversions cited in the draft. RTC freezes committed
actions while forming later actions. VLA-RAIL combines successive action chunks
and uses polynomial smoothing. Their metadata matches the bibliography. The
paper only claims that these works retain/combine actions; this is supported.
No full-method reproduction or absence-of-prior-art claim is made here.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07339v2
https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24673v1

Industrial interfaces
FANUC's versioned official page discusses update rate, buffering and latency.
Yaskawa's official SMOOTH overview discusses a motion-filter setting and effects
on path and timing. These support the draft's interface-level comparison. They
do not reveal either vendor's complete internal algorithm. The paper states this.
https://fanuc-corporation.github.io/fanuc_driver_doc/v2.2.0/docs/fanuc_driver/how_to_configure_1khz_mode.html
https://knowledge.motoman.com/hc/en-us/articles/16313283895191-SMOOTH-Function-Overview

Novelty boundary
The paper's proposed value is a specialized Lean contract and reproducible
boundaries. Nothing in this review establishes novelty, physical relevance for
a specific robot, or publication acceptance. The cited techniques establish
that filtering, reversal waiting and checked execution are not new by themselves.
A submission must explain why this concrete formal combination is useful to
its target community, and supply an accessible artifact after owner approval.
