Uncertain actuator lag: exact segment certificate integration
Completed 2026-10-17. This note concerns the new UncertainLag module and its
twelve generated certificate modules, not the separately assembled execution.

Model and theorem
-----------------
The exact analytic held solution satisfies x'=v, v'=a, a'=r*(u-a).
The unknown rate r is held constant during the segment and is in [9/10,11/10].
The fixed feedback request is -x-3v-2a; its gains do not use r.
For every state z, held sector gain alpha in [2/5,1], and elapsed time
t in [0,1/5], nominal_energy_decrease proves

  V(heldSegment r t z (alpha*input z)) <= V(z) - (3/2)*t*||z||^2.

Here V(z)=z^T P z uses the same rational P as the prior travel theorem.
The companion nominal_contraction gives
  V(next) <= (1 - 2*t*(15/902))*V(z).
These bounds are relative to energy at the start of the held interval;
they do not assert pairwise monotonicity at every pair of interior times.

Exact semantics and certificate transfer
---------------------------------------
An exact exponential enclosure provides one remainder theta in [0,1] shared
by all coordinates and all commands for the given rate/time pair. The
polynomial solution is affine in theta and alpha and quadratic in r.
Its degree-two Bernstein rate coefficient at the middle index is
2*f(1) - (f(9/10)+f(11/10))/2, not an actual midpoint plant.
All twelve matrix identities are proved in Lean. Each generated certificate
then supplies the energy decrease bound at one combination of two sector
endpoints, two remainder endpoints, and three Bernstein rate coefficients.
Nested convexity transfers all twelve bounds to the exact exponential model.
No finite numerical grid is used in these proofs.

Saturation and perturbations
---------------------------
region_sector_bound proves V(z)<=12 implies (2/5)*|input z|<=1.
segment_sector_decomposition therefore applies the clamp sector theorem to
the actual clipped command. Active clipping remains possible in this region.
The exact held response is proved equal to the polynomial response enclosure
from UncertainResponse, yielding V(w*response(r,t))<=16*t^2*W^2 when |w|<=W.
The segment definition uses u=clip_1(input z)+w: w is a held command-equivalent
perturbation inside r*(u-a). An external additive jerk j outside this multiplier
requires w=j/r (and its corresponding bound), not w=j.
Any pre-clamp measured request error must first be converted using clipping
Lipschitz continuity; this module does not silently identify it with post-clamp
disturbance. Parent integration owns that conversion and global execution.

Scope and scaling
-----------------
This module proves the exact segment ODE, initial conditions, response bound,
and uniform energy decay; it does not itself define a sampled global trajectory.
A single unknown constant rate is directly compatible with the segment theorem.
Using independently selected rates on successive intervals additionally needs
the assembled execution/ODE statement and held-rate assumption there.
The normalized rate range corresponds to actual time constant
tau_actual/tau_nominal in [10/11,10/9], not a symmetric +/-10% time-constant range.
No hardware identification, calibration, or mathematical novelty is asserted.
