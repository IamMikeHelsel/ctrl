IsRecoveryExecution specifies a continuous three-coordinate trajectory with its
own initial state, exact held command -1 before T, and its own sampled clipped
feedback after T. There is one fixed rate r throughout, no state reset, and no
backup disturbance. Tail errors are held pre-clamp request error e and held
post-clamp command error d, both inside r*(command-acceleration).

The derived backup identity includes the endpoint. Shifting by T yields the
existing IsExecution without assumed equality to an analytic state. The generic
travel theorem requires a backup whole-path bound and an exact terminal energy
bound; these are explicit obligations for separate concrete certificates.
For r in [9/10,11/10], positive dwell lower bound, h<=1/5 and |e|+|d|<=1/90,
it proves |position(t)|<=2 for every t>=0.

An explicit piecewise execution satisfies this differential-equation contract
under r!=0, T>=0 and positive dwell, without energy or safety premises. Both
pieces agree at T in all coordinates. Any two executions with the same initial
state, fixed rate, timing and error sequences agree for every nonnegative time.
Thus the main result is not made vacuous by an uninhabited execution predicate.
