PhysicalRecoveryBox concrete wrapper (2026-10-17)

Three checked theorem closures use only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.
The build uses existing pinned artifacts; a separate fresh replay is coordinated
by the parent. See physical-box-manifest.json and physical-box-check.log.

The initial state must lie in the concrete normalized box:
 X/(U*tau0^2) in [99/100,101/100],
 V/(U*tau0) in [49/100,51/100],
 A/U in [-1/100,1/100].
Positive tau0,tau,U, actual fixed rate tau0/tau in [9/10,11/10], physical sampling
intervals h_n in [hmin,tau0/5] with hmin>0, and combined held tail command errors
|e_n|+|d_n|<=U/90 imply |X(s)|<=2U*tau0^2 for every s>=0, for every continuous
actual physical recovery execution. The backup holds exactly -U without errors
until 5tau0/4. Then the nominal-tau0 feedback uses its own sampled states, e_n
before clipping, d_n after clipping, both inside the actual actuator lag.

Backup whole-path safety and terminal entry are proved by imported concrete
certificates; neither is assumed by these theorem statements. The actual
physical terminal state, normalized using the fixed tau0 and U, has energy<=12.
An explicit safe execution exists under the same assumptions. No reset occurs.

This supplies mathematical ODE and travel guarantees for the stated model.
It does not identify a real robot, verify sensing/timing/hardware conformance,
allow errors during backup, or assert that the backup ends at rest.
