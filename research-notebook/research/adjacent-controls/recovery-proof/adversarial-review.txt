Publication interpretation of the proposed capability

A successful proof would establish a useful, bounded extension: verified
negative-command recovery from a nondegenerate box outside the previous invariant
set into that set, followed by indefinite robust sampled safety. The endpoint
need not be at rest. This is stronger than an isolated stopping-distance trace,
and correctly uses full-state terminal energy to prevent the v=0/negative-a trap.

It would still be a fixed-duration offline recovery policy from a small selected
region. It is not yet an arbitrary-request guard, an online backup-CBF filter,
a general stopping envelope, an optimal recoverable set, or a measured robot
safety result. The zero-error backup stage is materially weaker than the robust
tail and should appear in the headline contract, not only in limitations.

Closest inspected primary literature already supplies backup/tube safety under
disturbances (van Wijk et al., arxiv2409.07700) and sampled-data backup safety with
state uncertainty and specified input delay (Singletary et al., arxiv2005.06418).
Both differ in architecture/assumptions; neither comparison has been run here.
Therefore the distinguishing candidate is the exact checked recovery/terminal/
arbitrary-execution composition and usable artifact, not the invention of backup
control or intersample safety. A fixed-time recover-then-control construction is
established control reasoning. Absence of a found identical Lean theorem does
not establish scientific novelty.

For a stronger submission, expose the reusable recovery contract and this exact
model as an independently runnable benchmark; compare region coverage and proof
obligations against a matched baseline, with the same zero-error backup, fixed
rate interval, duration and tail semantics. A different disturbance budget or
missing intersample condition makes a purported superiority comparison invalid.

Remaining boundaries of the proved claim

The backup has zero command, measurement, disturbance and switch-time error.
The tail permits per-hold combined error |e_n|+|d_n|<=1/90 (physical U/90).
Those errors enter the acceleration command inside the lag; they are not
arbitrary external forces on position/velocity. The lag is uncertain but fixed
for the whole run. The theorem does not cover within-hold variation, reaction
latency before backup, uncertain state admission, a delayed handoff, finite-word
controller code, a changing target, multiple coupled axes, or hardware. These
are substantive model restrictions, not flaws in the current theorem.

The initial box has positive volume but is very small and selected offline.
The theorem extends the artifact's certified starting states; it does not
prove that the original feedback fails on this box, nor enlarge the original
controller's maximal safe set. It certifies a different two-stage policy from
additional states. It is not a generally applicable online safety filter.

Publication judgment

The local proof capability has advanced substantially: there is now a concrete
full-box recovery, terminal entry, arbitrary-ODE correspondence, explicit
existence, and indefinite robust sampled tail, including physical scaling.
That is a coherent formal-methods case study with reusable proof obligations.
It is stronger than the initial isolated diagnostic or a conditional handoff
lemma. No theorem blocker remains from this source review, subject to the
pending fresh-source replay and unchanged reviewed hashes.

It is still not a new control-theoretic recovery principle. The closest backup
CBF papers already establish related backup/invariant safety with assumptions
such as disturbances, state uncertainty and known delays that this backup
phase does not support. A matched comparison must retain identical model,
error placement, fixed-rate uncertainty, timing, intersample and terminal
conditions; comparing this exact backup against a more demanding robust method
without equalizing assumptions would be misleading.

Credible framing: a checked, inspectable recovery-to-invariant benchmark and
experience report, with honest model limits and reproducible certificate
construction. Weak framing: a novel robust braking shield, a superior recovery
controller, or deployment-ready robot safety. The present evidence supports
only the former. It may be useful for an ARCH-style benchmark/tool/experience
submission, but does not establish acceptance likelihood or strong research
novelty. Human review, independent reuse or matched external comparison, a
chosen redistribution license, and an explanation of why this selected box is
useful remain substantive publication work. If no external consumer or richer
use case materializes, keep the scope as a small formalization case study.
