Handoff meaning and remaining proof
The terminal full state is a valid initial state for the existing uncertain
travel theorem with the SAME fixed r in[.9,1.1]. After the switch, the original
combined held-error budget and positive-dwell/max-gap assumptions must hold.
A checked global theorem still needs the exact flow/interval obligations,
continuity of all three coordinates at T, a shifted sampling clock starting
at T, and correspondence to the piecewise backup/controller ODE. These are
correctly listed as assumptions to formalize; the script does not prove them.
There is no demonstrated robustness to errors during backup, changing r during
backup, delayed switching, implementation arithmetic or physical mismatch.

Do not call this a complete stop: terminal velocity can have either sign over
the box. It is a fixed negative-command recovery into a certified terminal
ellipsoid, not a proof of arrival at rest. The current model/status/NotClaimed
fields otherwise avoid overstatement. The README was added during review and
now records the analytic obligations, zero backup disturbance, and the
negative-command recovery interpretation; the missing-document note is resolved.
