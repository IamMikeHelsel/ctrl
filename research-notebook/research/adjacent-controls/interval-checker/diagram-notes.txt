Suggested Figure 1 caption:
Normalized one-axis carriage and control structure. The command u drives
first-order actuator lag, a'=r(u-a); acceleration and velocity determine
v'=a and x'=v. The backup holds u=-1 until T, then the sampled controller
uses the current state and holds clip(-x-3v-2a) between updates. Travel
limits x=+-2 are normalized model constraints. The cartoon is a possible
interpretation of the model, not a drawing of calibrated hardware.

Suggested Figure 2 caption:
Nominal trajectory from the Manim explanation: r=1, z(0)=(1,0.5,0),
backup duration T=1.25, subsequent sample hold h=0.1, and zero errors.
Orange shows the backup and teal the sampled feedback. Vertical dashed
lines mark handoff, the red lines mark position limits, and the teal
horizontal line marks V=12. V drops from 24.475 to about 10.092 at handoff.
The state is continuous and is not at rest: v(T) is about -0.037 and
a(T) about -0.713. This floating-point illustration does not establish the
all-box theorem; the theorem and its assumptions are checked separately.

Scope and visual choices:
- The nominal plot uses h=.1 to match the existing animation. The interval
  comparison family elsewhere uses h=.05. Do not describe them as the same
  experiment or use the image as evidence for the family bounds.
- The feedback bus represents the state tuple (x,v,a), not three electrical
  wires tied together. It is schematic software data flow.
- The controller box describes a time-triggered switch. It is not a claim
  that a physical brake engages or that v=0 is a stable terminal state.
- In the diagram r is the normalized actuator rate. No mass, force, motor
  current, physical distance, sensor architecture, friction, or hard-stop
  mechanics is inferred from the cartoon.
- The energy axis is the mathematical quadratic V=z^T P z, not mechanical
  energy in joules.
