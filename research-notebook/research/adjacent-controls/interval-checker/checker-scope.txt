IntervalChecker: reusable exact-rational recurrence containment (2026-10-17)

The checked generic layer defines rational intervals and sound addition,
negation, subtraction, four-corner multiplication and clipping to [-1,1].
It uses no floating-point acceptance, sorry, custom axiom or native_decide.
Empty intervals are representable; actual-state membership prevents their use
as inhabited starting boxes.

Coefficients.dt, halfSq, E, B, C enclose respectively h, h^2/2,
exp(-r*h), (1-exp(-r*h))/r and h/r-(1-exp(-r*h))/r^2. CoeffMem states these
real memberships explicitly. The generic theorem does not trust a rational
coefficient generator or assume that approximations are correct.

heldBox uses the exact factored state transition:
  x+h*v+C*a+(h^2/2-C)*u,
  v+B*a+(h-B)*u,
  E*a+(1-E)*u.
Its soundness theorem proves containment of UncertainLag.heldSolution.
commandBox encloses the actual own-state feedback clip_1(-x-3v-2a+e)+d.
Both errors are held command quantities; d is after clipping but inside lag.

trace_sound composes steps with bounds required only for n<N.
certificate_trace_sound instead accepts user-supplied rational boxes and checks
that each computed next box is contained in the supplied next box. This permits
outward rounding at each step. Subset checks are rational endpoint inequalities;
their orientation is outward, with no state membership assumption at future
steps. The actual state recurrence is derived inductively from the initial box.

The generic analytic recurrence permits per-step r,h values, including values
outside the physical scope. A physical use must impose fixed/held positive
rates and valid positive sample times. Endpoint containment alone is not an
intersample safety proof. No termination, energy or handoff claim is implicit.
In particular, a generic post-clamp error can make |u| exceed1; any path bound
using |a|<=1 must require d=0 or prove an appropriately wider acceleration bound.
