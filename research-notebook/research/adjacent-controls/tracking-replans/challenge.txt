Independent challenge: physical tracking through smooth replans
17 October 2026

Proposed closure
The new proof should connect the existing scalar command segments to the actual
uncertain actuator model. Smooth commanded position does not by itself imply
small physical tracking error. This is a useful missing interface to check.
It is not a complete VLA controller or a novelty result.

Exact error dynamics
Let q, q', q'' be a constant-jerk reference segment with q'''=j. Let actual
state be x,v,a and error e=(x-q,v-q',a-q''). The plant obeys
  x'=v, v'=a, a'=r(t)*(U+w-a).
Use U=q''+command(0,e)+kappa*j, with command(0,e)=-8e_x-6e_v-e_a/2.
Then on the open segment interior,
  e_x'=e_v,
  e_v'=e_a,
  e_a'=r(t)*(command(0,e)+w_eff-e_a),
  w_eff=w+(kappa-1/r(t))*j.
This algebra is correct when r is nonzero. The allowed r>=8 supplies that.
The intended reference is q, not a sensed proposal of q. Sensor and delay
errors must be included in a separately justified w budget.

The robust feedforward constant
For r in [8,12], the reciprocal interval is [1/12,1/8].
Nominal-rate feedforward kappa=1/10 gives max|kappa-1/r|=1/40.
The minimax constant is kappa=(1/12+1/8)/2=5/48. Its maximum residual is 1/48.
The two endpoint distances are equal; any constant kappa has at least one
endpoint distance >= half the reciprocal-interval width = 1/48. This proves
optimality only among constant coefficients for this specific residual metric.
It does not prove optimality of the controller or best physical tracking.
Without jerk feedforward, kappa=0 gives the larger residual 1/8.
Thus the jerk part of the sufficient disturbance budget improves by a factor
of six against no feedforward, or by a factor of 6/5 against 1/10.

Physical tube and authority
If |j|<=J, |w|<=W, and W+J/48<=2R, the old robust-hold invariant applies to
the error trajectory, provided the initial transformed error cube has radius R.
It gives |x-q|<=R, |v-q'|<=8R, and |a-q''|<=64R.
If |q''|<=A, the requested acceleration-target command has bound
  |U| <= A+24R+5J/48,
and the disturbed effective target has bound
  |U+w| <= A+24R+5J/48+W.
A saturation implementation must be shown inactive under its actual limit.
The error dynamics alone do not account for clipping, contact, joint coupling,
flexibility, moving obstacles or inaccessible task-space commands.

Finite replan boundaries
Matched reference position, velocity and acceleration preserve the error state
at a switch because the actual state is not reset. The error cube therefore
passes directly to the next segment. The next segment must satisfy the same
budget, or an explicitly checked compatible cube and authority contract.

A jump in reference jerk can make q''' undefined at the switch. The feedforward
command can jump too, so actual acceleration need not be differentiable there.
Do not treat the whole concatenation as a single globally classical trajectory
whose ODE holds at every interior time. Use a continuous trajectory on each
closed segment with the ODE required only on its open interior. Prove the
endpoint bounds by continuity, then induct over a finite segment sequence.
A finite sequence needs no minimum dwell for the invariant. This does not
establish an infinite schedule, Zeno execution, or an implemented scheduler.
The actual state and the reference must refer to the same execution time;
matching a predicted state at plan-generation time is insufficient.

Numerical scale is a material limitation
At J=10 m/s^3 and W=0, the required radius is at least:
  no feedforward: 0.625 m;
  nominal 1/10 feedforward: 0.125 m;
  minimax 5/48 feedforward: 0.1041667 m.
These are sufficient invariant-cube radii, not measured or exact peak errors.
A one-millimetre radius with W=0 permits only J<=0.096 m/s^3 under these gains.
The improvement is useful, but the fixed-rate example is not fast precision
holding. A tighter finite-horizon bound or faster verified plant may help.
For a constant residual and constant jerk, the steady error equation gives
|e_x|=|w_eff|/8. The invariant radius can be conservative even in this simple
case. This observation is not a smaller proved worst-case variable-input tube.

Time-scaled extension checked algebraically
For sigma>0 and an actual measured rate band [8*sigma,12*sigma], use
  command=-8*sigma^2*e_x-6*sigma*e_v-e_a/2,
  feedforward=(5/(48*sigma))*j.
Set tau=sigma*t and normalized errors E=(e_x,e_v/sigma,e_a/sigma^2).
The normalized error ODE has rate r/sigma in [8,12] and disturbance
(w+(5/(48*sigma)-1/r)*j)/sigma^2. Therefore the sufficient condition is
  W+J/(48*sigma) <= 2*sigma^2*R.
The transformed cube is
  (e_x, e_x+e_v/(4*sigma), e_x+e_v/(2*sigma)+e_a/(16*sigma^2)).
The bounds are |e_x|<=R, |e_v|<=8*sigma*R, |e_a|<=64*sigma^2*R,
and effective-target authority A+24*sigma^2*R+5J/(48*sigma)+W.
The required radius floor is W/(2*sigma^2)+J/(96*sigma^3).
The formulas are correct. Sigma scales the actual rate interval; increasing
gains alone on the original [8,12] plant does not satisfy this assumption.
This is a conditional pathway for faster verified hardware, not a free gain
change or measured robot result. Formal verification of this extension is
separate from this algebraic review.

Defensible scope for tonight
A source-replayed theorem for actual tracking on one segment, an exact endpoint
handoff, and finite sequence composition would close a real gap between the
existing command proof and physical holding proof. Include a fair no-feedforward
comparison with identical uncertainty and authority assumptions. Report the
radius and any rejected examples. An exact minimax residual lemma clarifies why
the feedforward constant was chosen. Do not call the sixfold residual reduction
a sixfold measured tracking improvement. The proof's contribution is the checked
composition; control-method novelty needs a separate prior-work comparison.

Further practical gaps
The most important remaining interface is finite computation/sensing delay:
a planned segment must begin at the state it will actually inherit, and the
state estimate must have a validated error set. The accepted trajectory must
retain a stop or hold reserve across missed updates. End-effector position and
contact constraints also need a robot dynamics and kinematics model. The
scalar tube is useful as a component, not an unqualified dexterous-arm result.

Reference jerk is not actual jerk
The reference bound |j|<=J does not imply |a'|<=J for the physical plant.
For example, at zero tracking error, w=0 and r=12, the proposed law gives
actual jerk r*kappa*j=(5/4)j. A reference at its positive jerk limit can
therefore exceed the same numerical physical jerk limit.
In error-cube coordinates z, command(0,e)-e_a=-8z0+24z1-24z2.
Thus an available physical bound on the open segment interior is
  |a'| <= 12*(56R+5J/48+W).
Under the sigma extension it becomes
  |a'| <= 12*sigma*(56*sigma^2*R+5J/(48*sigma)+W).
This is conservative and can be much larger than J. If the application has
a physical jerk limit, admit the reference using an actual-jerk certificate
or a tighter derived bound. Do not transfer the command jerk limit unchanged.

Two minimax criteria give different feedforward constants
The 5/48 coefficient is optimal for max|kappa-1/r|, the residual metric used by
the invariant-cube certificate. It is not universally optimal feedforward.
For max|r*kappa-1| over r in [8,12], the minimax coefficient is 1/10 and the
minimum is 1/5. The coefficient 5/48 instead gives maximum 1/4 in this metric.
At zero initial tracking error and constant reference jerk, initial actual-jerk
error is (r*kappa-1)j. Short-time position error starts as
(r*kappa-1)j*t^3/6. Constant-input steady position error is instead
(kappa-1/r)j/8, which matches the inverse-rate criterion. These facts explain
why different observation horizons and objectives can prefer different gains.
A fair numerical comparison should retain both 1/10 and 5/48. A smaller proved
cube is not proof of uniformly better finite-horizon tracking.

The numerical constants are consistent: reciprocal residual 1/48, physical

Meaningful negative verification cases
1. Change only the centered reciprocal conclusion from 1/48 to 1/96. At r=8,
|5/48-1/8|=1/48>1/96, so the tighter theorem is false.
2. Change only input's feedforward sign from +kappa*j to -kappa*j. Retain the
residual formula and error_trajectory statement. The ODE derivation must fail.
At zero error, r=8, kappa=5/48, j=1 and w=0, the wrong-sign error jerk is
8*(-5/48)-1=-11/6. The unchanged residual formula predicts
8*(5/48-1/8)=-1/6. The difference is -5/3. This checks the actual input/error
interface and is stronger than rejecting an unrelated false arithmetic fact.
