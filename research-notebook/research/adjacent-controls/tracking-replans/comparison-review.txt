SMOOTH PLANS, BOUNDED TRACKING
Independent comparison and publication review — 17 October 2026

Decision: finish the conditional tracking theorem and its comparison artifact.
Do not yet present this as a new robotics controller or a submission-ready
research paper. The proposed reduction is sound in outline and useful for our
proof chain. Its control mathematics is simple, and the inherited error tube
can be too wide for the earlier high-jerk example. Both facts belong in the result.

This review inspected the current recovery manuscript, SmoothReplan report and
proof scope, RobustHold statement, sampled handoff scope, and the proposed new
tracking equations. It does not assert that the proposed theorem has passed Lean.
The separate final verification record must establish that fact.

THE EXACT BRIDGE

The physical scalar plant is
  x'=v, v'=a, a'=r(t)(u+w-a),  8 <= r(t) <= 12.
On each open reference segment, q'=v_ref, v_ref'=a_ref, a_ref'=j.
Let e=(x-q, v-v_ref, a-a_ref) and choose
  u=a_ref - 8e_x - 6e_v - e_a/2 + kappa*j.
Direct substitution gives
  e_x'=e_v, e_v'=e_a,
  e_a'=r[-8e_x-6e_v-(3/2)e_a + w_eff],
  w_eff=w+(kappa-1/r)j.

This is the old holding error model with a moving reference. It is not a new
stability mechanism. Its value is that the tracking premise follows from the
actual plant rather than being supplied as an unexplained tracking-error bound.

If |w|<=W, |j|<=J, and delta bounds |kappa-1/r|, require
  W + delta*J <= 2R.
The inherited initial cube is
  |e_x|<=R,
  |e_x+e_v/4|<=R,
  |e_x+e_v/2+e_a/16|<=R.
Its invariance gives |x-q|<=R, |v-v_ref|<=8R, |a-a_ref|<=64R.
These are model bounds in the same fixed SI gains as RobustHold.

A physical position interval follows if the reference interval is reduced by R
at both ends. Matching q,v_ref,a_ref at the actual switch preserves the error
state and its cube. No physical coordinate is reset. The result should quantify
over a finite, chronologically ordered list of positive-duration segments.

Jerk can jump at a splice. Therefore the actual acceleration derivative need not
exist at the switch. Use a continuous physical state and an ODE on each open
segment, then finite induction. Do not silently apply a global classical-ODE
contract across every switch. This is also why reference jerk and actual jerk
must remain separate quantities.

THE RIGHT THREE-WAY COMPARISON

For the inherited cube, compare the same gains, W, J, initial cube, plant, and
reference sequence. Change only the constant jerk feedforward coefficient:

  Method                     kappa (s)  max |kappa-1/r| (s)
  Acceleration feedforward   0          1/8
  Nominal rate 10/s          1/10       1/40
  Reciprocal midpoint        5/48       1/48

The first row still feeds forward reference acceleration; it omits jerk
feedforward only. Calling it "no feedforward" would misdescribe the baseline.

The reciprocal midpoint is minimax for this one objective. Since
  1/r in [1/12,1/8],
its center is (1/12+1/8)/2=5/48 and its radius is 1/48.
For any kappa, the sum of the endpoint distances is at least 1/24, so their
maximum is at least 1/48. The endpoints supply a short exact optimality proof.
This is elementary interval minimax arithmetic, not a new synthesis method.

Do not call 5/48 globally optimal tracking. At zero initial error and w=0,
the initial acceleration-error derivative is
  e_a'=(r*kappa-1)j.
The different objective max_r |r*kappa-1| is minimized by kappa=1/10:
its endpoint value is 1/5. For kappa=5/48 the endpoint values are 1/6 and 1/4.
Thus the reciprocal midpoint improves this uniform certificate while giving a
larger worst initial acceleration-error derivative. The two objectives differ.
Keep this negative comparison; it prevents a false optimal-controller claim.

An oracle kappa(t)=1/r(t) removes the jerk mismatch algebraically. It is a useful
reference for the price of uncertainty, but it assumes access to the true rate.
It is not a fair deployable competitor when our controller has only an interval.

HOW CONSERVATIVE IS THE CERTIFICATE?

The smallest radius allowed by this disturbance inequality is
  R_min=(W+delta*J)/2.
It also needs to contain the initial transformed error. The following values
use W=0.0005 m/s^2. They are exact rational arithmetic converted to millimetres.
These are illustrative specifications, not measured robot performance.

  J (m/s^3)   R_min, k=0   R_min, k=1/10   R_min, k=5/48
  0.005        0.5625 mm    0.3125 mm       0.302083... mm
  0.05         3.375 mm     0.875 mm        0.770833... mm
  0.5         31.5 mm       6.5 mm          5.458333... mm
  5          312.75 mm     62.75 mm        52.333333... mm
  10         625.25 mm    125.25 mm       104.416666... mm

The earlier SmoothReplan example uses J=10 m/s^3 over 20 ms segments. Even with
W=0, this inherited reciprocal-midpoint tube requires R>=104.166... mm. It
cannot certify that example as a millimetre-precision motion. The radius is a
sufficient all-time invariant for arbitrary admitted jerk signs and initial
errors. It is not the actual error of a short pulse.

For R=1 mm and W=0.0005 m/s^2, reciprocal-midpoint admission needs J<=0.072
m/s^3. Nominal-rate feedforward permits J<=0.06, and omitting jerk feedforward
permits J<=0.012. These comparisons concern certificate admissibility. Failure
of the sufficient inequality does not prove unsafe tracking.

For constant r,j,w, a steady tracking-error solution has
  e_x=w_eff/8, e_v=e_a=0.
This gives an analytic sanity check: the inherited R=|w_eff|/2 bound is four
times the magnitude of this particular steady error. It is not proof that the
invariant is four times too wide for all initial states and switching signals.

IMMEDIATE IMPLEMENTATION LIMITS

1. The theorem uses actual continuous-state feedback. Existing sample-and-delay
   capture results do not automatically certify this tracking controller.
   Derive sensor, sample-age, command-hold, and delay errors into W, or state
   this implementation bridge as open. Do not reuse the VLA update rate as
   the low-level servo rate without an execution model.

2. If |a_ref|<=A_ref, the nominal command obeys
     |u| <= A_ref + 24R + |kappa|J.
   The effective target including w obeys
     |u+w| <= A_ref + 24R + |kappa|J + W.
   State which quantity the actuator clamps. A bounded requested command alone
   does not prove the saturated actuator satisfies the unclamped ODE.

3. A new reference must start from the previous executed reference state at the
   actual switch. Starting it at the measured physical state may reset the
   tracking error and break reference continuity. A stale candidate needs an
   explicit continuation or a new feasibility test at its actual start time.

4. Matching reference acceleration preserves reference jerk bounds through a
   finite history. It does not prove |a'|<=J for the physical axis. In fact,
   |a'|<=12(56R+|kappa|J+W) follows by combining the error terms in the cube
   coordinates, and can be much larger. Do not report planned jerk as measured
   or certified physical jerk.

5. A terminal constant reference can reuse the hold invariant when q'=q''=0
   and the error cube is admitted. Bounded tracking alone does not ensure a
   planned motion reaches that terminal state, or that a stop reserve exists
   from every intermediate state. A separate continuous path to the terminal
   hold is needed. No contact, collision, flexure, grasp or human tremor model
   is added by the tracking reduction.

CLOSEST PRIMARY WORK AND ITS CONSEQUENCE

[1] Lambrechts, Boerlage and Steinbuch, 2004/2005, higher-order trajectory
    planning and feedforward for motion systems. The primary paper discusses
    planned versus actual motion limits, model inversion, higher derivatives,
    and discretization. Feedforward tied to higher-order references is mature.
    https://skoge.folk.ntnu.no/prost/proceedings/acc04/Papers/0827_FrM03.2.pdf
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.conengprac.2004.02.010

[2] Chen et al., FaSTrack, 2021. A simplified planner is connected to a higher
    fidelity tracking model with a computed tracking-error bound and safety
    controller. Thus the planner-to-plant tube is also established. Our possible
    distinction is a small replayable Lean certificate and explicit splice
    semantics for this chosen uncertain-lag model, not the architecture.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.07039v2

[3] Kousik et al., Reachability-based Trajectory Design, 2018/2020. Tracking
    error, planning deadlines, and persistent feasibility appear together in
    the prior method, with simulation and hardware. A retained backup is not
    a new idea. Our stop/hold composition must be compared with that obligation.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.06746

[4] Holmes et al., ARMTD, 2020. Every planned arm trajectory includes braking
    if replanning fails. Section II.C explicitly uses kinematic trajectories
    and discusses tracking error and available methods for bounding it. It
    does not make our scalar proof a new full-arm collision certificate.
    https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.01591

[5] Berscheid and Kroeger, Ruckig, RSS 2021. General jerk-limited trajectory
    generation is broader than our constant-jerk segment certificates. Use
    Ruckig as a reference generator if comparing implementations; do not
    misrepresent it as a competing uncertain-plant tracking controller.
    https://roboticsproceedings.org/rss17/p015.pdf

[6] Bohrer et al., VeriPhy, PLDI 2018. Verified models, runtime checks, machine
    arithmetic, executable controllers, and a physical robot are connected in
    an established formal pipeline. A Lean theorem over real arithmetic is a
    narrower result. There is no basis to call our current package the first
    verified robotics controller or an end-to-end implementation proof.
    https://user-web-p-u02.wpi.edu/~rbohrer/pub/pldi18.pdf

RTC and Legato remain relevant to the proposal source, but this theorem does
not run or prove either policy. Their command continuation work is already
documented in ../vla-smooth-replanning/report.txt and ../steady-motion/.
No absence-of-prior-work claim follows from this bounded literature check.

FINITE DELIVERABLE TO FINISH TONIGHT

A defensible artifact is a verified case study called "Smooth Plans, Bounded
Tracking" with the subtitle "A Lean certificate for finite replanning under
uncertain actuator lag." Its deliverable is a checked model and honest tradeoff
table. It is not an announcement of dead-steady robot arms.

The minimum complete package is:

- One actual-plant-to-error theorem with explicit units and disturbance placement.
- Exact residual bounds for kappa=0, 1/10, and 5/48, including the limited minimax
  theorem and the competing initial-error objective.
- Finite matched replanning with unchanged physical state, global real-time
  position containment, and a nonempty trajectory example.
- A command-authority corollary and a reference travel margin. If saturation is
  not modeled, clearly restrict the result to an inactive-saturation regime.
- A closed reference sequence ending at a constant hold, with every transition
  covered, if it can be completed without assuming a safe path. Otherwise title
  the result tracking through finite replans and leave the terminal guarantee open.
- A pinned fresh build, standard-axiom audit, kernel replay, and a negative
  mutation that breaks a real bound. Publish the exact theorem statements and
  hashes in the local package; do not replace evidence with a theorem count.
- A fixed comparison over the same references and uncertainties. Include short
  jerk pulses, longer sustained segments, sign reversals, and a rate change.
  Keep the J=10 example as a visibly rejected precision specification.

For numerical evaluation, retain three fixed rates (8,10,12), a smooth varying
rate such as 10+2 sin(t), and a switch-adjacent change. Keep the same trajectories,
disturbances, initial states and authority for all coefficients. Report actual
peak error, endpoint error, command magnitude and the conservative tube. A
time-step refinement check is necessary. None of these traces proves all-time
uncertainty coverage. A validated finite-horizon error enclosure would give a
stronger useful comparator than simulation alone.

The fixed coefficient comparison should show both objectives. Do not retune
gains or choose durations separately to favor one coefficient. A difference in
certificate radius is a certification result; a difference in numerical error
is numerical evidence. Keep those columns separate.

GO / NO-GO

GO: finish and package the bridge as a local, fully checked technical result.
It closes a real gap between our command proof and actual conditional tracking.
An educational or formal-methods case study is plausible if the implementation
and negative tests are reusable. Acceptance at any venue remains unknown.

NO-GO: claim a novel general controller, publishable robotics performance, or
precision dexterous manipulation from this theorem alone. The algebra is too
small, the plant is narrow, and the uniform tube can be too conservative.

To justify a stronger research paper, show one of the following concrete gains:
a sound finite-horizon error checker that admits useful short high-jerk motions
when this invariant rejects them; a checked sampled/delayed implementation with
a useful error budget; or a full recurrent admission-and-stop reserve theorem
that keeps its hypotheses through execution. Compare against the established
methods above and quantify the cost of certification. This is a research gate,
not a promise that adding one feature will ensure publication.

Do not append the proposed tracking claim to the current recovery manuscript
without rewriting its scope. That draft centers on a different fixed-rate
normalized recovery example and contains historical open-comparison statements.
Its assumptions and claim map need reconciliation before a paper revision.

DECLARED NUMERICAL COMPARISON AND RESULTS

The accompanying comparison.py uses the actual three-state plant. Reference
segments use exact rational initial values and constant jerk; the numerical
integrator receives the analytic reference on each segment. Four signed jerk
segments [+J,-J,-J,+J] of equal duration h return reference velocity and
acceleration to zero, then hold the final reference for four seconds.

These profiles were fixed before inspecting outcomes:
  short_high_jerk: J=10 m/s^3, h=0.02 s (ties to the earlier segment scale);
  long_high_jerk:  J=10 m/s^3, h=0.25 s;
  slow_precision: J=0.05 m/s^3, h=0.5 s.
All methods receive the same profile within a case. Differences between profiles
are intentional stress cases, not evidence that changing J has no cost.

Compare sigma=1 and sigma=10 as different physical response families:
  r in [8 sigma,12 sigma]/s;
  feedback = -8 sigma^2 e_x - 6 sigma e_v - e_a/2;
  kappa = 0, 1/(10 sigma), or 5/(48 sigma);
  cube coordinates = [e_x,e_x+e_v/(4 sigma),
                       e_x+e_v/(2 sigma)+e_a/(16 sigma^2)];
  sufficient radius = (W+delta J)/(2 sigma^2).
The faster response band is an assumption, not a gain-only software upgrade.
Use the same physical reference histories and W for both families.

Test fixed normalized rates 8,10,12 and the smooth stress signal
  r(t)=sigma[10-2 tanh(4 cos(pi t/(2h)))].
It tends toward the slow endpoint during positive jerk and the fast endpoint
during negative jerk. It is deliberately aligned to create a residual bias for
the reciprocal-midpoint choice. It is a hand-chosen stress signal, not a proven
worst-case adversary. The mathematical rate contract supplies no rate-of-change
bound. This waveform must not be confused with a calibrated physical actuator.

Use W=0 and W=0.0005 m/s^2 with smooth disturbance
  w(t)=-W[1-exp(-20t)].
Start all runs with zero tracking error. Do not change disturbance sign or rate
trace separately for each coefficient. Run a refined integration with tighter
tolerance and a smaller maximum time step. Record numerical peak position
error, maximum transformed-coordinate error, endpoint error, input magnitude,
the sufficient radius, and refinement differences. Use SI units in JSON/CSV.
The plot may display millimetres for readability. Sampled peaks remain numerical
measurements and cannot certify all continuous times or all admitted signals.


COMPLETED NUMERICAL EVIDENCE

The experiment completed 144 cases, each with a coarse and refined integration
(288 integrations). All declared numerical consistency checks passed. The largest
coarse-to-refined position difference was 1.42e-10 m. This is a numerical
refinement result, not a validated error enclosure or all-time proof.

The two examples below retain a mixed outcome. For EACH coefficient, the entry
is the maximum sampled position error across the same four declared rate signals,
with W=0.0005 m/s^2 and zero initial error. Units in this table are millimetres.

Profile and response family         Acceleration FF   Nominal jerk FF   Midpoint FF
Short high jerk, sigma=1
  largest sampled position error        0.112446         1.883685          1.960176
  sufficient radius                   625.250000       125.250000        104.416667
Long high jerk, sigma=10
  largest sampled position error        0.156422         0.031760          0.026711
  sufficient radius                     0.627500         0.127500          0.106667

The short reference moves only 0.16 mm in 0.08 s. A rate signal aligned with
jerk can create a residual bias. In this family, both jerk-feedforward choices
have larger sampled peak error than the acceleration-feedforward baseline.
The long reference moves 312.5 mm in 1 s. In that faster-response family, both
jerk-feedforward choices improve this selected numerical measure. The midpoint
choice improves it slightly over the nominal coefficient. These cases do not
rank the methods for all inputs or all admissible rates.

The vector figure also shows fixed-r=12/s, zero-disturbance traces. There the
midpoint residual has 1.25 times the nominal residual. The linear error system
therefore gives 1.25 times the position error for the same reference. This check
makes the different minimax objectives visible rather than hiding a losing case.

The animation file is comparison-trace.json. It contains the SHORT sigma=10
reference, the smooth stress rate, and W=0.0005 m/s^2. It holds 201 samples per
coefficient, concentrated around the 80 ms motion. Each sample has t_s, q_m,
x_m, error_m and R_m. The midpoint radius is 0.1066667 mm; the final reference is
0.16 mm. Values between the saved numerical samples are linearly interpolated.
Use the stated analytic reference for exact reference geometry. This is one
illustrative trajectory, not a worst-case witness or a physical measurement.
