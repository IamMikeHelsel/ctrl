stopping.py reads the local numerical results.
Three curves show the 0.40 m/s example: a varying admitted response, the weak/slow
endpoint response, and a weaker target outside the declared contract. The curves
are numerical illustrations. They end at their respective first zero velocities.
The final card states the separate Lean certificate: first zero by 0.55 s,
position <=15 cm from the measured starting reference until that event.
It states that remaining stopped needs a separate guarantee, and that no
hardware validation or PL d/e claim follows from this example.
