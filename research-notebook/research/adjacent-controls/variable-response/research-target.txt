A stopping bound under changing response
Research target fixed before the numerical comparison, 2026-10-17

Question
Given bounded starting-state error, command delay, response speed and braking
strength, how far can an axis travel forward before it first reaches zero
velocity, even when its response changes during the maneuver?

Useful output
An admitted approach-speed/clearance pair, a stopping deadline, and explicit
rejection when the assumptions or margin are insufficient. All distances and
timing are measured from a specified stop request and reference position.

Current scope
One powered axis, x'=v, v'=a. During delay, acceleration has an upper bound.
After engagement, a'=r(t)*(b(t)-a), with a positive bounded response rate and a
bounded effective acceleration target. The weakest admitted target is negative.
Classical continuous trajectories, initial measurement boxes and finite horizon.
The effective target separates available braking strength from response speed.

First zero velocity is the stopping event for this work. Remaining stopped,
recovery of frictional grip, a stop after loss of power and a fault-tolerant
brake are separate requirements. No physical temperature law, vehicle, shoe,
or ISO 13849 Performance Level is established by this abstraction.

Conditions and history
The admitted response can change with time. The theorem only applies while
its declared response bounds hold. Temperature, load, wear or contact changes
need evidence that they stay inside those bounds, or an explicit new mode.
A recovered state at one instant does not prove that recovery will persist.

Illustrative numerical experiment, SI units, not measured hardware
Position estimate is zero with error +/-0.001 m.
Velocity estimate has error +/-0.01 m/s; use its upper end for clearance.
Initial/delay acceleration is at most 0.1 m/s^2, delay at most 0.05 s.
After engagement, r(t) is in [8,12]/s and b(t) in [-2,-1.2] m/s^2.
Reference clearance is 0.15 m. Fixed velocity-estimate cases: 0.30,0.40,0.50 m/s.
Compare instantaneous nominal braking, fixed nominal response, lag-only
uncertainty, and varying response plus varying strength on the SAME initial
error and delay assumptions. Weaker-model acceptance is not a valid robust
certificate for the richer plant. Also compare a simple conservative bound
that includes the same lag and strength assumptions.

Use an analytic upper curve for the full interval. Its endpoint alone is not
its maximum. Root finding and ODE simulation are numerical evidence unless a
separate Lean/rational certificate checks the claimed bound. Preselect smooth
varying-rate/target traces, a weak constant endpoint case, and one target outside
the stated bound to challenge model conformance. No parameter search for a
favorable trajectory. A speed threshold search is an output, not a change to
the declared model.

Advance gate
Close a new sound proof obligation, demonstrate a useful accepted/rejected
decision under stated uncertainty, and expose the physical evidence still
needed. A tighter bound alone is not proof of research novelty. Compare against
existing robust-control and verified-reachability methods before a paper claim.
