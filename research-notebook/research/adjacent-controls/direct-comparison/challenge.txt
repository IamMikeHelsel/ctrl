DIRECT-FEEDBACK COMPARISON — BOUNDED ADVERSARIAL CHALLENGE
17 October 2026. Independent AI-agent assessment; no human review implied.

Decision
A direct-feedback certificate is the strongest immediate comparison. The
current experiment already removes the claim that backup is needed for the
tested cases. Prove both policies under the same assumptions, then compare
certified region and verification effort. One more theorem on the same small
box does not, by itself, make the result a stronger research contribution.

Exact counterexample to the saturation shortcut
Take r=1 and z0=(1,1/2,0), which is inside B. During u=-1,
  x(t)=3t/2-t^2/2+exp(-t),
  v(t)=3/2-t-exp(-t),
  a(t)=-1+exp(-t).
Thus the unclipped feedback request is exactly
  k(t)=t^2/2+3t/2-5/2.
It first reaches -1 at (sqrt(21)-3)/2, approximately .791288.
For a .1-period sampled controller, every sample through .7 requests <=-1.
The direct execution therefore equals the backup through .8. At .8 it requests
-49/50, so its next command differs from the backup.

Let E=exp(-4/5)>0. The state at .8 is
  (22/25+E, 7/10-E, -1+E).
Exact rational expansion gives
  V = 61323/5000 + (997/625) E + (189/50) E^2 > 12.
The constant term alone is 12.2646. No exponential approximation is needed.
Numerical V is approximately 13.7445384.

This refutes the general shortcut "direct feedback remains exactly -1 until
entry into V<=12." It does NOT refute direct-feedback safety. It is also not
itself a new controls result. A Lean proof would close an interpretation issue.

Additional diagnostic, not a proof
Imported the existing experiment.py functions without running main or writing
results. Evaluated its 24 rate/corner combinations with the analytic held flow.
At the first sample that requests more than -1:
  period .05: all 24 have V>12; V range 12.76677--14.37833;
  period .10: all 24 have V>12; V range 12.20536--14.37833;
  period .20: 21 of 24 have V>12; V range 11.71345--13.76705.
The continuous request crosses -1 before entry in all tested combinations.
These floating-point results do not cover interior states or rates. The exact
central example above is sufficient to reject the proposed universal shortcut.
With nonzero post-clamp d during a prefix, even exact initial equality to the
-1 backup would fail. Match error assumptions explicitly.

Smallest fair formal comparison
Use the same full B, fixed real r in [.9,1.1], P, position limit and T=5/4.
For both policies set all prefix command/measurement/disturbance errors to zero.
Use direct feedback with period 1/20 for its first 25 holds. T then lies on both
sampling clocks. Backup remains the existing exact -1 command until T.
At T, both use the same admitted sampled-feedback contract: arbitrary positive
lower-bounded holds <=1/5 and combined held errors <=1/90. Carry all state
coordinates through T; do not reset acceleration or velocity.

For direct feedback, prove continuous position safety on [0,T] AND V(f(T))<=12
for the whole initial box and whole rate interval. Prove correspondence to the
actual sampled ODE and existence. Then apply the same indefinite-tail result.
Do not compare a nominal direct prefix with an error-tolerant backup prefix or
claim robustness from the zero-error experiments. If period 1/10 is used
instead, either choose a common aligned endpoint, such as T=13/10 and recertify
both policies, or explicitly compare different clock phases. The 1/20 choice
reuses the existing backup theorem with no such phase difference.

A direct prefix needs more than the backup endpoint formula. Saturation-release
times depend on rate and initial state. A checked branch decomposition or a
sound enclosure of clip is needed. Pointwise affine formulas do not justify
corner reduction across changing clamp branches. Natural interval propagation
can overestimate badly through repeated feedback. Failure of that certificate
method is not a counterexample to safety and must not be presented as one.

Strongest useful comparison within this model
After the shared-B proof, fix a small family BEFORE comparing methods, for
example boxes centered at (1,1/2,0), with halfwidths .01*lambda in each axis
for lambda in {1,2,4,8}. These boxes are nested; all three widths and the domain
volume are explicit. It is also acceptable to choose a different family for a
stated application, before seeing which method wins. Do not tune separate
families in favor of each policy. No claim of a maximal safe set is justified.

Use the same theorem target, available arithmetic, machine, pinned toolchain
and resource cap. Record:
- Which full boxes are certified, and their normalized volumes.
- Path and endpoint bounds actually exported by Lean.
- Number of state/rate subdivisions and certificate size.
- Certificate construction, Lean elaboration and kernel replay times separately.
- Proof-specific manual obligations and new trusted assumptions, if any.
- Each unsuccessful attempt as timeout, enclosure failure, or actual witness.

Keep empirical peak travel, terminal energy and command variation as secondary
behavior measures. They cannot establish certified region inclusion. Do not
use source-line or theorem count as a proxy for verification value. With only
two policies from this artifact, call this an internal comparison; it is not
an independent-tool evaluation.

Falsifiable publication gate
1. Technical pass: the complete direct prefix and both actual-execution
   compositions replay from fresh sources with only the permitted axioms;
   all quantifiers and error/timing assumptions match. One floating-point
   endpoint or an assumed terminal condition fails this gate.
2. Comparative pass: the fixed region family produces an auditable result in
   certified coverage or verification cost that a reader can use. A positive
   or negative result is acceptable. For example, direct control may certify
   the same/larger region with similar/lower effort; that defeats a backup
   necessity story and supports a limited method comparison instead.
3. Interpretation pass: state the concrete finding and its scope. A timeout
   is not unsafe behavior. A looser enclosure is not a smaller true safe set.
   More proof lemmas are not new control theory. Preserve the strong current
   result that direct feedback is benign in the numerical comparison.
4. External-value gate: before a strong research claim, obtain independent
   reuse/review or a matched existing-method comparison, or show that a stated
   realistic assumption changes the verified design. A local animation helps
   explain the result; it cannot supply this evidence.

Grade judgment, not acceptance probability
Adding the saturation counterexample and a direct certificate for B alone
leaves the work around B- as a small case study. A complete matched comparison
with a reproducible, transferable finding can justify considering B. It need
not favor backup. More lemmas or a polished animation alone do not justify the
increase. If both methods certify only the same selected box and no material
cost or assumption tradeoff is measured, keep the bounded experience-report
claim and stop escalating the novelty language. A stronger grade needs an
external-value result, not a claim that the baseline failed when it did not.

Scope of this pass
Read current recovery manuscript, existing experiment code/results and prior
experiment review. Used only standard-library analytic-flow calculations plus
exact Fraction polynomial arithmetic. No proof source, paper, or stored
experiment result was edited. No full build, new literature search or subagent
was used. The exact counterexample algebra is independently checked here but
is not yet claimed as a new kernel-checked theorem by this review.

Analytic basis
E=exp(-rh), B=int_0^h exp(-rs)ds and C=int_0^h(h-s)exp(-rs)ds
have series sum_n (-r)^n*h^(n+k)/(n+k)! for k=0,1,2 respectively.
For 0<=rh<=1 the absolute terms decrease. The partial sum through even n=12
is an upper bound; adding n=13 gives a lower bound. The endpoint monotonicity
in r is valid: E decreases, and B/C integrate decreasing exponentials against
nonnegative weights. The implementation uses lower at the larger rate and
upper at the smaller rate. It does not infer monotonicity of the full control
trajectory from monotonicity of these three coefficients.

The update formulas are the exact held solution:
  a_next=E*a+(1-E)*u,
  v_next=v+B*a+(h-B)*u,
  x_next=x+h*v+C*a+(h^2/2-C)*u.
The sample command uses interval arithmetic on -x-3v-2a and monotone clip.
This encloses feedback on the actual sampled state. Reusing independent
coefficient intervals at each step loses fixed-rate correlations, but that
loss enlarges the enclosure and is conservative.

Tick conversion rounds rational lower/upper endpoints outward. Multiplication
uses signed floor for its lower bound and signed ceiling for its upper bound;
this is correct for negative products too. Addition and negation are exact
on ticks. The Cartesian rational partitions cover all initial states and
rates, including boundaries. They do not just check original-box corners.

The energy calculation maximizes an exact convex quadratic on the current
state enclosure box. The exact LDL check proves P positive definite. Taking
its maximum over the eight enclosure vertices is valid. This is different
from assuming the nonlinear closed-loop trajectory is affine in its original
state, which would be false across clip branches.

The whole-hold path bound is also valid. Actual acceleration stays in [-1,1]
by induction: the initial acceleration is in that range, clipped commands are
in that range, and the exact actuator response is a convex combination of
a_start and u. For every s in [0,h], the displacement lies between
min(0,h*v_start)-h^2/2 and max(0,h*v_start)+h^2/2.
Outward interval products supply containing endpoints. This argument does not
require every propagated acceleration enclosure itself to lie in [-1,1].

Independent small checks
Compared 27 E/B/C rational bounds at r=.9,1,1.1 and h=.05,.1,.2 against 80-digit
Decimal evaluations. All contained the reference values. Checked 1,000 signed
interval products against exact Fraction endpoint products. All contained the
products. These are implementation spot checks, not a formal proof or a replay
of the full parameter covering. The mathematical reasoning above is the basis
for judging the algorithm sound; the Python implementation remains trusted.

The latest recorded run uses the matched period 1/20 for 25 steps, with 4^3
initial cells and 16 rate cells: 1,024 parameter boxes. Its full-box terminal
energy bound is approximately 11.46322514 and all whole-hold position checks
pass. The result identifies itself as an exact-rational prototype with no Lean
replay. That label is correct. The earlier period .1 with 13 steps is a useful
additional result but has T=1.3, so it is not the same endpoint as the existing
T=1.25 recovery theorem.

Next technical gates, in order
1. Prove a reusable Lean containment lemma for one sampled step, including
   coefficient bounds, monotone clamp and outward arithmetic. Prove the
   acceleration/path invariant separately. Do not import Python success as an
   axiom or assume the desired endpoint energy.
2. Check a small representative trace end to end and record proof size and
   replay cost before generating all 25,600 box-steps. If interval correlation
   loss or checker cost dominates, change the enclosure method and disclose
   that change. Do not treat a failed enclosure as a safety counterexample.
3. Check the complete closed covering and terminal energy, then relate the
   recurrence to arbitrary actual sampled ODE executions with existence.
   Compose the robust tail at the aligned sample T. The current prototype
   does not yet prove this composition.
4. Compare matched certified domains and measured verification work. The
   Python result now makes a broad claim that backup is needed even less
   credible. A Lean direct-prefix result would improve completeness and
   honesty, but a grade increase still needs the comparative-value gate above.

Best next bounded formal target
For the strongest reusable comparison, the missing generic one-step interval
containment/checker is the priority: it can turn either policy's trace into a
checked prefix and expose where bounds are lost. Pilot a small trace before
committing to the large family. Do not spend the remaining budget generating
many proof terms without a measured pilot cost.

For a cheaper narrow extension, lambda2 backup may reuse the long-segment
proof through a NEW initial-corner convexity lemma. Merely widening the current
whole-box LDL certificate is unlikely to pass: exact-rational estimates using
its32 rate cells give upper12.89764 with no initial split,12.39738 with2^3

To use that shortcut, prove that the held terminal flow is affine in the
initial state and that its quadratic energy is convex; then prove the closed
box reduces to its eight vertices. Also generalize the path bound and certify
the larger initial box. The existing concrete InitialBox theorem does not
already cover lambda2. This corner shortcut applies to constant-input backup;
it cannot be transferred across direct feedback's changing clamp branches.
A lambda2 backup theorem alone enlarges a selected certificate but does not
resolve the direct-comparison or publication-value gate.
