C01 — BLOCKER — State the engineering decision before the proof technique.
Anchor: publication/robust-manuscript/main.tex:18,21-28.
The abstract lists proof components but does not show a decision made possible
by the result. Define a user, a motion task, and the quantity they choose:
clearance, starting speed, information age, or actuator authority. A declared
academic benchmark is honest; it is not evidence of engineering demand.
Close with: a fixed task contract and a comparative result that matters to it.

C02 — BLOCKER — The current novelty sentence is too weak to carry a controls paper.
Anchor: robust-manuscript/main.tex:24,236-241 (same publication directory).
Lyapunov reasoning, saturation sectors, Bernstein certificates, backup sets and
intersample safety have prior art. Formal control envelopes and Lean control
foundations also exist. Identify the exact composition/interface delta and
evaluate it. Do not claim firstness from an incomplete library search.
Close with: an overlap table at statement/API level, plus a demonstrated benefit.

C03 — MAJOR — "Uncertain lag" must not suggest changing response during a run.
Anchor: publication/robust-manuscript/main.tex:32,57,241.
One unknown fixed r is materially different from r(t). Warm/cold repeated units
could motivate a fixed range, but warm-up during motion requires a different
model. Keep this distinction in the abstract and examples. No measured range
has been supplied for either interpretation.

C04 — BLOCKER — A certified region is not yet a useful operating region.
Anchor: publication/robust-manuscript/main.tex:41-69,239-241.
The full-state ellipsoid is justified mathematically. Its location, shape and
units are not tied to an engineering requirement. Show admitted/rejected states
in position, velocity and acceleration. State how those quantities are sensed.
A larger certificate alone does not demonstrate a larger true safe region.

C05 — MAJOR — Keep the direct-controller evidence visible.
Anchor: research/adjacent-controls/direct-comparison/README.txt;
        research/adjacent-controls/recovery-proof/README.txt.
Direct control succeeds in the recorded matched numerical tests. The rational
full-box prototype also passes. The scale-2 comparison is a difference between
sufficient enclosures; it does not show direct control is unsafe. Do not use
the recovery theorem to imply that a brake-first policy is needed.

C07 — BLOCKER — Do not merge theorem scopes in prose.
Anchor: publication/controls-team-review-20261018/NOVELTY-EVAL.txt;
        research/overnight/2026-10-18/controls/builder-05/CLAIMS.txt.
The sampled saturated fixed-rate theorem is not generalized by the continuous
changing-rate tracker. The latter has different gains, rate range and command
assumptions. A unified digital theorem needs an explicit common execution model.

C08 — BLOCKER — Detection and eventual handoff are missing from the stop story.
Anchor: research/adjacent-controls/variable-response/EventStop.lean;
        variable-response/sampled-handoff/Handoff.lean:14-17 and README.txt
        (both under research/adjacent-controls).
The stop event uses true velocity. The capture theorem starts from an admitted
reading. Neither proves that noisy samples eventually produce an admitted
reading while all intervening motion remains safe. Failure to switch can cause
rearward motion under continued braking. State and prove the detector's progress
condition; never assume the successful sample as the whole-operation conclusion.

C09 — MAJOR — Bounded position is not a precise stop.
Anchor: variable-response/sampled-handoff/README.txt and hold-scope.txt.
The illustrative hold interval is [0.0326,0.14365] m, centred at 0.088125 m
after a 0.14 m reading. It permits a substantial retreat. The exported cube
bounds allow speed 0.4442 m/s and acceleration 3.5536 m/s^2; these are coarse
bounds, not predicted motion. Do not describe this as precision standstill.
Choose terminal speed/acceleration requirements before advertising a stop.

C10 — BLOCKER — Continuous holding needs a digital implementation bridge.
Anchor: variable-response/sampled-handoff/RobustHold.lean:200-242;
        same folder/README.txt, "Holding model".
The proof uses actual continuous state. Measurement error, age, held commands
and drive error must share one explicit mismatch budget. The new age calculation
is only a screen. Its cube and mismatch premises require a noncircular invariant
argument. Switching and sample boundaries also need the right trajectory class.

C11 — MAJOR — "Reusable" currently means a candidate interface.
Anchor: publication/robust-manuscript/main.tex:234,239.
Generic Bernstein, convexity and perturbation lemmas can be reused. There is no
independent consumer on a different plant. Show exactly which proofs are reused
and which model identities remain manual. Do not equate type-level generality
or scaled copies with demonstrated reduction in verification effort.

C12 — MAJOR — Retain the trust boundary and make it testable.
Anchor: publication/robust-manuscript/main.tex:214-218.
The metadata is not a verified semantics parser. Real arithmetic is not a
finite-word controller. Model-mutation rejection is useful, but does not show
that the mutations represent frequent field failures. An implementation claim
needs checked timestamps, state ordering, units, command placement and rounding.

C13 — BLOCKER — Separate our grades.
Anchor: publication/controls-team-review-20261018/READ-ME.txt;
        research/adjacent-controls/direct-comparison/README.txt (B-minus).
Acceptance by a proof critic is evidence for the bounded proof. It is not a
publication grade. Replace any single global score with correctness, novelty,
comparative value and implementation evidence gates. Human review remains open.

C14 — SCOPE — A one-axis problem can be meaningful.
Anchor: proposed next paper, not an existing theorem.
Dimension alone is not the defect. A small plant can expose a useful sharp
trade, verify an important implementation gap, or enable reuse. Adding a Franka
model without a coupled proof would increase the claim gap. Broaden the supported
operation and evaluate it before broadening the robot.
