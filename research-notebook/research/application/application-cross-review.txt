Measured execution: soundness and nonvacuity
-------------------------------------------
1. segment uses the actual command clip(input(z)+e)+d. e enters before the
   clamp and d enters after it. The comparison with nominal clipped feedback
   is an exact equality, not an assumption that either request is unclipped.
   Clamp nonexpansiveness supplies the bound |effectiveDisturbance|<=|e|+|d|.

2. samples is recursively defined from this actual segment. effectiveErrors
   is subsequently computed from those already defined samples, so the
   equality bridge to TravelSafety is not circular and assumes no unproved
   recurrence. State-dependent effective disturbances are permitted by the
   existing universally quantified disturbance-sequence theorem.

3. The all-real-time theorem requires a positive minimum dwell time. It uses
   the clock selector's coverage theorem and bounds the elapsed time inside
   its containing interval. It is therefore stronger than a sample-only
   statement. The initial energy-region hypothesis is retained throughout.

4. The actual measured execution has explicit global continuity and interior
   ODE theorems. The ODE's held command is reconstructed as clip(input+e)+d;
   it does not silently become continuously recomputed feedback. The clamp
   bounds only its own output, not the output plus the post-clamp disturbance.

5. Nonvacuity is concrete: h_n=1/10, e_n=d_n=1/200, z0=[0,0,1] satisfy the
   hypotheses. Initial energy is 278/25<12, the erroneous request is -399/200,
   and the command clips to -1. active_example_travel certifies the whole
   resulting run. Thus both an actual clipping event and nonzero admitted
   request error/disturbance are present in a certified trajectory.

6. The separate-grid wrapper recomputes errors from its own recursive actual
   sample states. Its error budget dx/2+3*dv/2+da follows the controller gains
   [-1,-3,-2] and each ideal nearest-grid error <=half a grid step. Positive
   grid resolutions and the shared disturbance budget are explicit. An
   independent W>=0 premise is unnecessary because |d_n|<=W already entails
   it for any admissible execution.

TravelScope: correct counterexample and limited ablation meaning
---------------------------------------------------------------
The initial state [19/10,1,0] lies strictly within the position strip and
requests -49/10, so its held command is -1 with zero disturbance. At t=1/5,
position equals 32/25+exp(-1/5). The checked lower exponential bound already
gives position>=52/25>2. Its energy is 2322/25>12. This correctly refutes
replacing the ellipsoid hypothesis by a position-only initial bound. It does
not say every initial state outside the ellipsoid is unsafe or that the
ellipsoid is a maximal safe set.

The two additional inequalities correctly compare the scalar sufficient
Young budgets at fixed H=1/5, eta=5/451, W=1/100, R=12: C=9471/200 fails and
C=13 passes. They do not assert a physically unsafe trajectory for the failed
budget. Nor do these two arithmetic declarations themselves prove that the
coarse C is a valid response-energy bound. The refined C=13 has a separate
checked response-energy derivation in TravelDisturbance.

Paper wording boundaries (not source defects)
---------------------------------------------
- rounded_active_example uses positive grids but its initial state lies
  exactly on them, so its initial rounding error is zero. It is evidence of
  clipping with an admissible nonzero-resolution budget, not evidence of a
  nonzero realized rounding error at that sample. Use active_initial for the
  distinct abstract-error witness with nonzero e and d.
- roundedState has direct all-real-time safety and sample-agreement theorems.
  The explicit global continuity/interior-ODE transfer in this module is for
  state, the abstract held request-error execution. A theorem identifying
  roundedState with such an execution is not present in this snapshot. Do not
  describe that particular formal transfer as already delivered.
- The rounding is ideal real arithmetic and unlimited integer rounding. The
  proof does not include IEEE operations, finite registers, sensor overflow,
  delays, or continuously varying disturbances. e and d are held per interval.
- A failed sufficient budget is an inconclusive certificate calculation,
  not instability or a counterexample. Attribute the coarse/refined
  comparison to these fixed constants; it is not an optimality result.

No source change is required for the claims actually stated by these files.
