Execution uniqueness and arbitrary-solution travel safety
2026-10-17. New modules: ExecutionUniqueness and PhysicalExecutionUniqueness.

What is now proved
------------------
The travel guarantee is no longer restricted to the explicitly constructed
analytic trajectory. Any trajectory satisfying the independently stated ODE,
initial condition, sampled feedback, and continuity assumptions must equal it.
This is proved in normalized coordinates and separately transferred from the
physical-coordinate equations. Neither specification assumes the desired
equality, safety, or a uniqueness hypothesis.

Normalized specification
------------------------
For a fixed real rate r, define sample times T_n = sum_{k<n} h_k. IsExecution
requires a function f:R->R^3 that is continuous on [0,infinity), starts at z0,
and, on every open interval (T_n,T_{n+1}), satisfies

  x'=v, v'=a,
  a'=r*(clip_1(-x(T_n)-3*v(T_n)-2*a(T_n)+e_n)+d_n-a).

The sampled request uses f's own state at T_n, not the recursively constructed
sample sequence. Errors e_n and d_n are arbitrary held scalar sequences for
the uniqueness result. With r != 0 and h_n >= hmin > 0, execution_eq_state
proves equality to UncertainTravel.state at every nonnegative real time.
samples_eq_of_execution first proves agreement at all sample times by induction.
The positive dwell time gives interval coverage without finite-time accumulation.
There is no conclusion about negative time.

The reusable held_ode_unique theorem is stronger locally: it works for every
real r and held command u on any closed interval. Two continuous trajectories
satisfying x'=v, v'=a, a'=r*(u-a) on the open interior and agreeing at the left
endpoint agree everywhere on the closed interval. It does not require stability.
The r!=0 hypothesis enters only when connecting to our existing analytic formula,
which contains division by r.

Proof mechanism and endpoint assumptions
---------------------------------------
For two held solutions, exp(r*t)*(a_f(t)-a_g(t)) has zero interior derivative.
The scalar mean value theorem plus closed-interval continuity makes it constant;
the initial equality makes it zero. Velocity and position differences then have
zero derivative in turn. This avoids assuming derivatives at sample boundaries.
Continuity of all three coordinates at those boundaries remains essential.
The existing constructed trajectory is separately proved to satisfy IsExecution,
so the result is not a vacuous wrapper around inconsistent execution conditions.

Physical specification and exact transfer
----------------------------------------
For fixed positive nominal tau0, actual tau, and command magnitude U, the physical
IsExecution specifies a continuous f=(X,V,A) with its own sampled feedback:

  X'=V, V'=A,
  tau*A'=-A+clip_U(-X(T_n)/tau0^2-3*V(T_n)/tau0-2*A(T_n)+e_n)+d_n.

Both errors are in acceleration-command units. In particular, d is inside the
1/tau factor; an external additive jerk is a distinct model and is not covered
by this physical statement without the earlier appropriate conversion.
normalize_isExecution proves that z(t)=(X(tau0*t)/(U*tau0^2),
V(tau0*t)/(U*tau0),A(tau0*t)/U) meets the normalized specification with
r=tau0/tau, hnorm=h/tau0, enorm=e/U, dnorm=d/U. Clipping and vector-field
homogeneity are proved, not assumed. execution_eq_state then recovers equality
in physical coordinates, and execution_unique compares arbitrary physical
executions. constructed_isExecution also provides a physical existence witness.

Transferred safety statement
----------------------------
PhysicalExecutionUniqueness.execution_travel_limit proves |X(T)|<=2*U*tau0^2
for every T>=0 and every trajectory satisfying the physical specification if:
  * tau0>0, tau>0, U>0;
  * 9/10 <= tau0/tau <= 11/10, with fixed tau and tau0;
  * 0<hmin<=h_n<=tau0/5 for every sample;
  * |e_n|+|d_n|<=U/100 for every sample;
  * the initial normalized quadratic energy is <=12.
The corresponding normalized theorem admits the proved larger 1/90 combined
error budget. The physical theorem deliberately retains the original U/100
engineering contract. Nominal controller gains do not depend on actual tau.

Boundaries of the result
------------------------
This closes the constructed-formula versus arbitrary continuous classical
solution gap for the stated fixed-rate, held-error model. It does not establish
hardware calibration, disturbances varying within an interval, delays, sampled
measurement consistency beyond the specified error sequence, or time-varying tau.
Uniqueness compares trajectories with the same prescribed h, e, and d sequences;
it does not separately prove uniqueness for a sensor model that generates
different error sequences from different candidate trajectories.
No new mathematical novelty claim is made for affine ODE uniqueness. The value
is a checked connection from the physical ODE specification to the existing
certificate and all-time travel theorem.
