4. Manuscript against the actual contracts
No mismatch found in the reviewed main.tex:
- r is one fixed unknown value, not time varying or sample varying.
- All three state coordinates are continuous, including switches. Interior derivatives are pointwise classical derivatives; no derivative at a switch is required.
- Feedback uses f(t_n) itself. h,e,d are prescribed sequences; uniqueness compares the SAME sequences and does not claim state-dependent quantizer uniqueness.
- Combined error budget is |e_n|+|d_n|<=.01, not .01 per channel. Post-clamp d is inside r(u-a); an outside-rate jerk is excluded from this contract.
- Existence and correspondence need positive dwell and r!=0; the narrower physical theorem uses positive tau,tau0,U. The uncertainty box, ellipsoid, upper dwell bound and error budget are safety hypotheses, not hidden existence hypotheses.
- Manuscript's arbitrary-execution energy invariant is a valid direct composition of ExecutionUniqueness.execution_eq_state with UncertainTravel.state_invariant; it is not a separately named theorem in ExecutionUniqueness. Travel is directly exposed there. The weaker .01 budget follows from the formal1/90 result.
- Physical controller uses nominal tau0 while dynamics use actual tau. Its normalization and own-sample feedback commute as proved. Rate[.9,1.1] means tau/tau0 in[10/11,10/9]. The illustrative4cm,20ms,.02m/s^2 figures are correct and labeled illustrative.
- Active clipping example and position-only initial-condition warning are accurately limited. The energy decrease is relative to the sampled initial state, not pairwise monotonicity between interior times.
- No novelty or general synthesis claim is inferred from proof counts. Hardware, delay, varying lag, nonheld disturbances and uncertain quantizer execution are excluded.
