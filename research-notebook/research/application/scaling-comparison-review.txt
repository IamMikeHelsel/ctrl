PHYSICAL SCALING
With t=T/tau and (x,v,a)=(X/(U*tau^2),V/(U*tau),A/U), the normalized equations
x'=v, v'=a, a'=-a+u give X'=V, V'=A, tau*A'=-A+U*u. The factor 1/tau in the
chain rule is present in vectorField_rescale and both interior-ODE proofs.
The resulting physical sampled request is exactly
  q=-X/tau^2-3*V/tau-2*A.
Clamp homogeneity requires U>=0 and is used with U>0; the inverse changes of
units require tau>0 and U>0. Held request errors and drive disturbances scale
as e/U and d/U. Sampling periods scale as h/tau, and sampleTime_normalized
proves exact clock agreement. The physical measured execution therefore has
  tau*A'=-A+clip[-U,U](q_n+e_n)+d_n
on every open sampling interval, not merely a renamed position bound.
Continuity at updates and agreement with recursive samples are also checked.

The safety assumptions are the normalized FULL-STATE ellipsoid, a positive
uniform minimum period, h_n<=tau/5, and |e_n|+|d_n|<=U/100. They imply the
absolute position envelope |X(T)|<=2*U*tau^2 for every T>=0. This is neither a
bound on accumulated travel nor a consequence of position bounds alone.
The numerical example tau=0.1 seconds, U=2 m/s^2 yields 20 ms maximum period,
0.02 m/s^2 combined error budget and a 4 cm symmetric position envelope.
The example [0,0,2] has normalized state [0,0,1], request -4 and clamp -2.
All units and values are consistent.

Essential scope retained: d is an additive DRIVE/COMMAND disturbance before
the first-order actuator lag, after the clamp. It is not a mechanical force
or acceleration added directly to V'. U bounds the clipped command, not the
sum including d. tau is known and fixed; changing it changes controller gains
as well as the plant. This is not robustness to uncertain lag. Errors are held
per interval. No floating-point implementation, physical grid-normalization
corollary, arbitrary measurable disturbance, or ODE uniqueness result is claimed.
