Physical uncertain-lag bridge

Source: lean-controls/LeanControls/PhysicalUncertainTravel.lean

The controller and coordinate normalization use one fixed nominal constant tau0.
The actual plant uses a distinct, fixed positive tau. The normalized actuator
rate is r=tau0/tau. In physical coordinates the verified interior ODE is
  Xdot=V, Vdot=A,
  tau*Adot=-A+clip_U(-Xsample/tau0^2-3*Vsample/tau0-2*Asample+e_n)+d_n.
Thus the uncertainty result is not obtained by retuning the controller or
renormalizing each plant using its actual time constant.

Physical samples and all-time state are defined by the certified normalized
uncertain execution, with h/tau0, e/U, d/U and coordinates
(X/(U*tau0^2), V/(U*tau0), A/U). The checked bridge proves initial-state
agreement, sample agreement, continuity including sample boundaries, and the
actual-tau differential equation on every open sampling interval. It does not
claim differentiability at a switching time.

The travel theorem assumes tau0,tau,U>0, 0.9<=tau0/tau<=1.1, a strictly
positive common lower bound on sample spacing, h_n<=tau0/5, arbitrary held
errors with |e_n|+|d_n|<=U/100, and initial normalized quadratic energy<=12.
It concludes |X(T)|<=2*U*tau0^2 for every T>=0. No periodicity or fixed sample
spacing is required. The rate condition also implies
10/11<=tau/tau0<=10/9. Saturation may remain active.

Interpretation limits: tau is fixed over the trajectory; the proof does not
cover an arbitrarily time-varying actuator rate. d is a command-equivalent
held perturbation after clipping and before the actuator lag, not a separate
force added after the lag. Both errors are held during each sample interval.
The initial ellipsoid, delay-free held control, exact real arithmetic, plant
model, positive spacing, and physical calibration remain explicit assumptions.
This formal result is not hardware validation or a certified IEEE controller.
