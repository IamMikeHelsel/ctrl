EVERY-SEGMENT TRAJECTORY ENVELOPE

Both namespaces also provide sampled_segment_bound. For any h_n in
[hmin,1/2] with hmin>=0, and any sequence obeying the actual recurrence
  z_(n+1)=segment(h_n,z_n),
the theorem proves for every n and every 0<=t<=h_n:
  V(segment(t,z_n)) <= (1-hmin/M)^n V(z_0),
where M is the corresponding generated upper energy bound. Thus the energy
envelope covers every point in every segment, not merely sample endpoints.
hmin=0 gives a nonincreasing bound; positive hmin is required for geometric
decay. The theorem is indexed by sample and local time, rather than using a
single global real-time trajectory definition.
