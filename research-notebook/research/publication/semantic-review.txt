SEMANTIC AND PROOF-SCOPE REVIEW — 17 October 2026

Reviewed by an independent agent, without source edits. Scope: Quantized.lean,
Certificate.lean, BernsteinCertificate.lean, their relevant sampling/energy
definitions, pinned mathlib Round.lean, and the first version of
scripts/generate_interval_certificate.py. Certificate/Bernstein/generator were
still being developed. This is a statement/argument audit, not a fresh build or
an assertion that generated certificates have passed Lean.

RESULT

No logically invalid theorem statement or contradictory assumption set was
identified in the reviewed files. The strongest useful statements are exact-real
sample-time bounds. Several additional claims require explicit bridges. Most
notably: generated energy decrease is not yet a state-stability result without
positive definiteness; the present rounded-controller operating envelope does
not demonstrate behavior under meaningful actuator saturation; and a polynomial
transition must be tied to exact held plant semantics before being called ZOH.

1. QUANTIZATION AND HELD-INPUT SEMANTICS

quantize delta x = delta * round(x/delta) is ideal nearest-grid rounding of an
unbounded mathematical real to an unbounded integer. Pinned mathlib round breaks
halfway ties toward POSITIVE infinity: round(1/2)=1 and round(-1/2)=0. It is not
ties-to-even or ties-away-from-zero. rounding_error correctly requires delta>0.
An implementation theorem must reproduce this rule or prove the same error
bound for its own rule, including representable input range and overflow.

The command is computed once from the sampled x,v, then clipped once, then held.
The disturbance w_n is a constant acceleration over that same interval. For
a_n = input(delta,U,x_n,v_n)+w_n the model is
  x(t_n+s)=x_n+s*v_n+(s^2/2)*a_n,
  v(t_n+s)=v_n+s*a_n,  0<=s<=h_n.
The stated recurrence is exactly its endpoint. It does not model continuously
recomputed feedback, arbitrary measurable disturbances during a hold, input
delay, stale sensor readings, independent x/v sampling times, or actuator lag.
These are limitations of scope, not defects in the recurrence theorem.

Quantized.lean and Certificate.lean compose the endpoint algebra correctly.
SampledPD.lean proves derivatives for the nominal held segment. To claim the
new rounded/clipped/disturbed continuous segment itself is verified, connect its
initial conditions and derivatives and assemble it across sampling times.

2. SATURATION SCOPE — IMPORTANT FOR PAPER WORDING

clipping_error is a valid projection inequality: clipping cannot increase
distance to an in-range NOMINAL command. The hypothesis |u_nominal|<=U implies
U>=0, so omitting a separate U>=0 hypothesis there is not a loophole.
pd_quantized_step includes genuine clipping, and can apply even if a coarse
rounded request is clipped. It does not claim a contracting invariant region
under arbitrary coarse quantization.

The concrete all-time instance does not exercise clipping. With
  P=[[5,1],[1,1]], k=(-2,-3), k P^-1 k^T=37/4,
we have |u_nominal|^2 <= (37/4)V <= 37/40.
Adding the rounding bound 1/4000 gives a rounded request below 0.963, hence
strictly within the +/-1 actuator limit. This calculation is an independent
ordinary mathematical observation; it has not been added to Lean by this review.

In fact the generic invariant's current conservative feasibility conditions
also appear to exclude clipping. For any nonempty execution, R>=0 and W>=0.
Write r=1-hmin/6, D=5delta/2+W. Then r>=11/12, D>0, and the budget reads
  (1+theta)r R + (1+1/theta)*(54/25)D^2 <= R.
It forces R>0, (1+theta)r<1, and theta<1/11. Consequently
  D^2 < (25/7776)R <= (25/77760)U^2 < U^2/2500.
Meanwhile nominal |u|<=(97/100)U follows from (37/4)R<=37U^2/40.
Thus |rounded request| < (99/100)U. This derivation should be independently
checked/formalized before making it a theorem, but is enough to flag that
"we handle active saturation" is not supported by the current feasible family.

Acceptable wording: a local guarantee for a controller whose definition includes
clipping. For a substantive active-saturation benchmark, produce an admissible
initial condition and parameters where the clamp provably changes the command,
then prove the desired trajectory property. Tightening the actuator-region
bound from 10R<=U^2 to (37/4)R<=U^2 could make such examples possible, but that
change must be assessed and verified separately.

3. INVARIANCE, CONVERGENCE, AND EXECUTION EXISTENCE

safe_trajectory and pd_quantized_invariant conclude a bound only at n∈Nat.
They do not yet conclude the same bound at every real time between samples.
The older nominal intersample result concerns h<=1/6 and no rounded/disturbed
feedback; it cannot simply be cited for the newer h<=1/2 result.

The generic quadratic_trajectory theorem is appropriately conditional. A
nonnegative quadratic form may be degenerate (including Q=0). Therefore Q<=R
is an energy-sublevel invariant, not a bounded physical state region unless
coercivity and containment in the desired safety region are additionally proved.
Its epsilon ultimate-bound statement is correctly phrased: it does not assert
convergence of the disturbed state, convergence to the floor, or a zero limit.

R and W nonnegativity are implied in nonempty concrete executions by hinit,
energy_nonneg, and hw, although exposing them in an interface would make the
contract easier to read. example_parameters demonstrates the scalar inequalities
are feasible. It does not itself construct x/v trajectories. Recursion on the
deterministic rounded update can provide existence for every permitted h,w and
initial pair, and would remove a common source of reader uncertainty.

For physical all-time claims, additionally define t_n=sum_{k<n}h_k, require a
positive lower sampling bound or prove t_n tends to infinity, and glue the held
segments. hmin=0 is allowed in the invariance API; sample-index invariance alone
does not rule out zero steps or Zeno sampling. The concrete hmin=1/20 avoids
that issue once a real-time assembly theorem is provided.

4. BERNSTEIN/LDL INTERFACE AND TRUST BOUNDARY

The reviewed Bernstein interface is logically sound as a sufficient scheme:
nonnegative Bernstein weights preserve PSD, exact reconstruction identifies
the target matrix function, and an LDL identity with nonnegative diagonal is
a valid PSD witness. L need not be triangular/invertible for this direction.
Zero pivots may certify semidefiniteness; they do not certify strict positivity.
The interval theorem excludes degenerate endpoints via a<b. covered_psd is
actually valid for ANY index type and cover, not only a finite cover as its
comment suggests. None of these points creates an unsoundness gap.

The first generator validates P symmetry but not positive definiteness. Its
generated certified_decrease theorem correspondingly proves only
  V(A(h)x) <= V(x) - h*c*||x||^2.
For indefinite P, energy can decrease while state norms grow. Before advertising
stability, certify mI<=P<=MI with m>0 and link this to the state norm, then apply
the recurrence theorem. A theorem for the exact input polynomial A(h) says
nothing yet about whether A(h) is the plant's true held transition.

The generator, SymPy, rational LDL discovery, and certificate JSON should remain
outside the mathematical trust base: the emitted Lean identities and witness
inequalities must be checked from source. The input-to-model correspondence
remains a reviewed/formalized obligation. Rejecting a corrupted certificate is
a useful implementation test, but not a substitute for the general soundness
theorem. Build success and the axiom audit should be reported separately from
human semantic audit, clean-environment replay, and physical/model acceptance.

5. RECOMMENDED BENCHMARK SET

1) Existing double integrator, state (position,velocity), held acceleration
feedback -2x-3v. It is the transparent 2x2 baseline; exact transition degree2.

2) Jerk-controlled triple integrator, state (position,velocity,acceleration),
plant x'=v, v'=a, a'=u, held u=-(x+3v+3a). The exact transition is
  I+hN+(h^2/2)N^2 - [h^3/6,h^2/2,h]^T [1,3,3],
where N has ones on its superdiagonal. This exercises dimension3, degree3,
and a nontrivial 3x3 positive quadratic form; it is not a duplicated baseline.

3) Coupled planar double integrator, state (q1,q2,v1,v2), plant q'=v,v'=u,
with held u=-Kp*q-Kd*v,
  Kp=[[2,1/2],[1/2,1]], Kd=[[3,1/4],[1/4,2]].
The exact transition is
  [[I-(h^2/2)Kp, hI-(h^2/2)Kd],[-hKp,I-hKd]].
Both gains are SPD and do not commute, so they cannot be simultaneously
orthogonally diagonalized into two independent scalar loops. This is a useful
4x4 coupled certificate, not a direct sum padding exercise. Componentwise
actuator limits and different sensor scales would additionally exercise the
generic disturbance interface, once their bound is proved.

These three plants have nilpotent OPEN-LOOP state matrices, which is why exact
held transitions are finite polynomials. The closed-loop continuous matrix need
not be nilpotent. A damped oscillator, motor lag, or general mechanical
linearization has nonnilpotent open-loop dynamics: its ZOH transition contains
exp(Ah). A polynomial truncation is an approximation and needs a proved
remainder/error enclosure. Such a plant is a valuable later boundary test,
not a justified exact-polynomial benchmark today.
