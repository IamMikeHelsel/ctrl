Checked active-saturation recovery episode
=========================================
Source: lean-controls/LeanControls/SaturatedEntry.lean
Main theorem: LeanControls.SaturatedEntry.recovery_guarantee

Result
------
A genuine actively clipped trajectory is now connected to the existing robust invariant theorem. It starts at the single normalized state (x,v)=(1,0), outside the V<=0.1 local operating set. The first 26 sampling intervals are exactly h=0.1, with zero acceleration disturbance. At least the first four commands are actively clipped by the unit actuator limit. The rational trace enters V<=0.1 at sample 26. Every later sampling interval may vary arbitrarily in [0.05,0.5], and every later held acceleration disturbance may vary arbitrarily with |w|<=0.00025. The same rounded/clipped controller is used throughout.

The checked conclusions are:
  1. On commands n=0,1,2,3, the rounded request has magnitude >1 and the applied input equals -1.
  2. V(x_n,v_n)<=5 at every sample index n.
  3. V(x_(n+26),v_(n+26))<=0.1 for every n>=0.

The finite prefix lasts normalized time 2.6. The endpoint is
  x_26=148931/1000000,
  v_26=-7039/50000.
Its energy, by exact rational evaluation, is
  17757641969/200000000000 ~= 0.088788209845.
The first four rounded requests are exactly -2, -1.69, -1.36, and -1.01; all four are clipped to -1.

What the proof actually checks
-----------------------------
The controller uses mathematical nearest-grid rounding with delta=1/10000, with ties toward positive infinity as defined in mathlib. The state update is
  u_n=clip[-1,1](-2 quantize(delta,x_n)-3 quantize(delta,v_n)),
  x_(n+1)=x_n+h_n*v_n+(h_n^2/2)*(u_n+w_n),
  v_(n+1)=v_n+h_n*(u_n+w_n).

The source defines the actual recursive trajectory run(h,w), so this is not merely a claim about a potentially nonexistent sequence. An untrusted exact Python Fraction calculation proposed the 27 rational states. Lean checks each of the 26 transitions against the actual rounded, clipped formula, including explicit round_eq_iff proofs for the intermediate rational rounding decisions. The 26 step facts are private helpers, not 26 claimed mathematical research results.

run_prefix then proves by induction that any actual trajectory satisfying the prefix scheduling/disturbance hypotheses equals the checked rational witness through sample 26. prefix_bounded checks all 27 sample energies against 5, and prefix_entry checks the entry condition. tail_safe applies Quantized.safe_trajectory to the shifted actual run. recovery_guarantee composes the finite recovery episode and the infinite robust tail.

Scope
-----
This is one rigorously checked recovery episode, not a region-wide active-saturation stability theorem. The finite recovery phase has a fixed sample interval and zero disturbance. Timing jitter and disturbances are allowed only after sample 26. A neighborhood of initial states, a disturbed recovery prefix, and a general basin of attraction are not certified.

The reported V bounds are at sample instants. No continuous-time intersample bound for the recovery prefix is asserted. The arithmetic is over exact mathematical reals and ideal quantization; machine floating point, sensor implementation, physical parameter correspondence, and hardware behavior are not covered.

This result is consistent with SaturationBoundary.budget_forces_clipping_inactive: the finite prefix starts outside that local invariant budget and is checked separately. Once the trajectory reaches the robust set, the local theorem controls the infinite tail. This gives a truthful active-saturation example without pretending the original invariant certificate covered active clipping.

Publication relevance
---------------------
The contribution here is compositional verification of a concrete recovery episode followed by a robust infinite-time regime, including exact quantization and saturation semantics. It is not claimed as a new control-theoretic result or an open-problem solution. A stronger paper would parameterize and automate finite-entry certificates, cover a nontrivial set of initial conditions, and compare assumptions and verification cost with prior work.
