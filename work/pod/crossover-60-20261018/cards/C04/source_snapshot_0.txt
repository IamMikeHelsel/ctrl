CAN THE EXISTING HOLD CERTIFICATE SUPPORT DIGITAL FEEDBACK?
18 October 2026. New exact-arithmetic screen. Not a Lean or trajectory proof.

Purpose
Test a cheap candidate bridge before starting more formalization. Use the
existing changing-response hold law and its illustrative sensor bounds. Do not
retune gains or expand the accepted cube after seeing the result.

Inherited continuous model, SI units
  x'=v, v'=a, a'=r(t)(k+w-a),   8<=r<=12 per second
  k=-8(x-c)-6v-a/2
  z1=x-c, z2=x-c+v/4, z3=x-c+v/2+a/16
  |zi|<=R,  R=2221/40000 m=0.055525 m
The existing hold theorem admits total target mismatch |w|<=W=2R=0.11105 m/s^2.
The gains in these expressions carry SI units; these are not dimensionless laws.

Candidate digital implementation
At time t the command uses a noisy reading at time s<=t. Its total information
age is t-s, including sensing, computation, transport and holding time. The
measurement errors are bounded by ex=.001 m, ev=.01 m/s, ea=.05 m/s^2.
The implemented command is k(measured state at s), held until the next update.
Add a separate physical target error d. For a fixed reference c, the mismatch
relative to continuous feedback is
  w_total(t)=k(measured state at s)-k(actual state at t)+d(t).

Exact calculation
Measurement error costs M=8ex+6ev+ea/2=0.093 m/s^2.
Using v=4(z2-z1), a=16(z1-2z2+z3), differentiate the actual k:
  k'=(4r-64)z1+(160-12r)z2+(12r-96)z3-r*w_total/2.
Conditional on the cube and |w_total|<=2R, its maximum absolute value is
104R=5.7746 m/s^3. The expression is affine in each variable separately;
the absolute value is bounded by its maximum over the 32 endpoint vertices.
age_budget.py checks all vertices with Fraction arithmetic and checks the
identity independently through the original physical-coordinate derivatives.

For piecewise differentiable trajectories with the required continuity and
finite switching on each bounded interval, integrating this conditional slope
gives the candidate sufficient budget
  M + (104R)*total_information_age + max|d| <= 2R.

What passed
Exact expression and endpoint arithmetic. A 1 ms candidate has nonzero budget
slack even with the separate physical error. The measured command magnitude is
bounded by 24R+M=1.4256 m/s^2 if its actual sampled state is in the cube. With
0.01 physical error this is below the illustrative authority of 2 m/s^2.
Whether saturation is inactive throughout the full operation still needs proof.

What has NOT passed
The bound assumes both the cube and the total mismatch condition. It does not
prove their preservation under the digital controller. Prove a bootstrap/first-
exit or one-step implication without assuming the conclusion. Specify initial
command/history, a fresh first reading, finite sampling, switch regularity and
existence. The old theorem requires classical derivatives; sampled commands
need a suitable piecewise execution contract before it can be reused.

No full braking prefix, eventual accepted detector sample, or initial capture
bridge was checked here. The previous one-time capture delay <=5 ms is not a
bound on every later sample age. Its theorem is not contradicted by this screen.

A failed budget only rejects this sufficient proof route at that age. A sharper
analysis, another tube, or another gain set might work. No unsafe trajectory was
found or claimed. Sensor errors and rates remain illustrative, not measured.
