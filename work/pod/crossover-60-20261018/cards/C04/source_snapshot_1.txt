Result
AdaptiveAdmission.delayed_sampled_hold replaces the old fixed-position
reading with an evaluable measured guard. For the actual first reading m,
choose the center ONCE as c=m_x+m_v/4+m_a/32. Keep that center thereafter.
Require |m_v|<=.02 m/s and |m_a|<=1.25 m/s^2. Position is unrestricted by
this guard. The theorem derives admission from the original sensor box and
the pending-command ODE; it assumes no endpoint cube or aggregate mismatch.

The interface retains one continuous physical state, the original reading
timestamp zero, pending command -1.21 m/s^2, startup D<=.5 ms, total age
<=1 ms, rates in [8,12]/s, the old sensor bounds, clipping, and target error.
The command switches on the right at D. It keeps the old command on the left.
The reading at D is still m with stamp zero. Startup consumes its age budget.
