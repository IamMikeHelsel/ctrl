## Safe reuse and remaining bridge

An accepted action remains accepted under a verified nonempty refinement of the world set
with the same action and safety predicate. Expansion, inconsistent data, changed shared-error
structure, or changed intervention dynamics needs rechecking. A future decision receipt must
bind the model, observation constraints, error-sharing semantics, action, and state/time/horizon.
Matching hashes alone does not prove that an old physical safety claim remains applicable.
No receipt implementation or dynamic bridge was integrated in this review.
