## Enumeration and integer faithfulness

For reduced w=p/q>0, the quantity p²+4Nq² is an integer. If it is a rational square then its root s is an integer; congruence modulo4 forces s and p to have the same parity. Thus a=(s−p)/2 and b=(s+p)/2 are positive integers with ab=Nq² and b−a=p. Conversely every positive factor pair a<b of Nq² gives such a square. This establishes the positive per-denominator factor enumeration used by extend_6x4.py. The numerator range is finite,1≤p≤Nq²−1, with no independent arbitrary numerator cutoff.
