## Minimal-intervention meaning

Scalar clipping minimizes intervention relative to the supplied nominal action in the declared Euclidean joint-velocity metric. Changing coordinate units or using a torque/energy metric changes that optimization; no coordinate-free or physical-energy minimum is claimed. It does not decide which nominal behavior is task-relevant. If nominal null action is a posture-centering feedback, the optimal clip still recenters whenever feasible. The intended behavior needs a task-only nominal law, or explicitly authorized secondary action, and a proof that safe fiber deviations leave it unchanged. At rest, torque0 throughout a safe fiber is a valid concrete positive. With retained null velocity, braking may be necessary even though task position and task velocity are exact. Correcting that unsafe motion is consistent with minimal intervention.
