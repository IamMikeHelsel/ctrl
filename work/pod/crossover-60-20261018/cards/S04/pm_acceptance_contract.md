# Acceptance contract emerging from S04

AI-written research proposal; no integration. The root and three inherited-model roles
used independent files. Exact runtime IDs unavailable. Inventor completed the initial
three-transfer report, then a further audit experiment turn was interrupted by a
platform safety flag. Its available files are evidence to check, not accepted proof.

The trusted consumer owns the question and policy. A producer may supply candidates,
proof witnesses and convenient labels; it may not choose which obligations exist.
For this source, the fixed approved model hash and fixed checker semantics form one
contract, not three independently acceptable current file hashes.

Acceptance has four logically different parts:

1. **Semantic cover:** every original world belongs to at least one checked branch.
   This is not a disjointness requirement: d=q=0 legitimately belongs to both sign
   branches. The source interval [1/5,3/10] is nonempty. With an empty lambda interval,
   the zero-d polyhedron can survive although the original existential model is empty.
2. **Local evidence:** every required branch has a checked bound or a checked empty
   certificate. Endpoint witnesses establish attained extrema; a vertex catalogue need
   not list every vertex when dual bounds and attainment witnesses are checked.
3. **Identity:** the exact bytes hashed are the bytes parsed/compiled and summarized.
   Capturing bytes is insufficient if a mutable mapping is looked up again later or a
   mutable certificate is hashed after parsing. The repaired root prototype captures
   plain mappings once, requires exact immutable bytes, uses the captured checker,
   checks its hard-coded model identity, and verifies arithmetic again.
4. **Use context:** mathematical validity for the same fixed model is reusable.
   'This attempt completed', 'these measurements are current', and 'this robot state
   satisfies the model' are additional facts requiring separate trusted inputs.
   A producer-controlled timestamp or nonce cannot establish those facts.

A saved JSON `status=verified` is not independently checkable proof. A consumer either
rechecks the certificate, or trusts an authenticated verifier attestation with the exact
subject and policy. A trusted request epoch can reject an old invocation receipt only
if its writer/integrity is also trusted. The worker's failed process return must not be
silently replaced by a previous file's success status. For immutable mathematics,
rejecting all old artifacts wastes sound evidence; use content and policy validity.

Hashes bind bytes under their ordinary collision-resistance assumption. They do not
bind descriptive prose to mathematics, make a regex a Lean parser, or make a build
log an authenticated kernel statement. The Lean kernel correctly reports assumptions
in the negative fixture; the external parsing policy must account for every record.
For arbitrary untrusted Lean source, source execution and stdout provenance need a
separate isolation design. A duplicate-aware parser only repairs the demonstrated
collision of records, not every possible hostile host/compiler scenario.

The small immutable-source wrapper is a positive prototype, not a production sandbox.
Python/runtime, approved expected checker/model semantics, private temporary storage,
and trusted in-process invocation remain in its trust base. It does not solve general
model-to-physics correspondence, producer execution isolation, atomic multi-file builds,
or physical measurement freshness.
