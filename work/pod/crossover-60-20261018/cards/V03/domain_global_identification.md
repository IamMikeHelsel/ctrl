# Exact sufficient identification for the rank-eight V03 fixture

AI-written mathematical derivation. This is an exact sufficient result for the specified
ideal fixture, not a full characterization of two-view identifiability. The numerical rank count
is checked by Fraction elimination. The fixed-data matrix-kernel line is additionally
proved directly from11 expanded incidence equations in domain_linear_identification.lean;
that proof does not need a trusted rank computation.

## Fixture and assumptions

The first nine pairs have x=(u,v,1), y=(u+1,v,1), u,v∈{0,1/2,1}.
Add the two pairs

    (1/4,1/4,1) -> (3/4,1/4,1),
    (1/4,3/4,1) -> (3/4,3/4,1).

They are generated by R=I,t=e1, with scene depths1 for the first nine and2 for the last
two. All first-image rays are distinct, as are all second-image rays. The exact design
matrix A has row(y_i*x_j) flattened row-major. `domain_rank_gate.py` checks rank(A)=8
and A*vec(E_A)=0, where

    E_A=[[0,0,0],[0,0,-1],[0,1,0]]=[e1]cross.

Every matrix satisfying all incidence equations is alpha E_A. This follows from
rank-nullity, but domain_linear_identification.lean also proves it directly: linarith
forces all entries zero except f12=-f21. The11 scalar constraints are explicit expansions
of the listed ray pairs. Since E_A is nonzero and fits them, its span is the entire kernel.

A competing physical reconstruction must have real R,t and scene depths d1_i,d2_i
such that R is orthogonal with determinant+1, all depths are strictly positive, and

    d2_i*y_i = R*(d1_i*x_i)+t

for every pair. Rays are calibrated, normalized with third coordinate1, and correspond
to the same static rigid scene across the two images. The competing scene is otherwise
unknown. No fixed scene plane or known metric target is assumed.

## Excluding zero translation using the actual fixture

If t=0, then each target ray is proportional to R times its source ray. Orthogonality
therefore preserves the squared angular cosine between any two rays. Use two grid pairs:

    x_a=(0,0,1), y_a=(1,0,1),
    x_b=(1,0,1), y_b=(2,0,1).

Their squared angular cosines are respectively

    (x_a dot x_b)^2 / ((x_a dot x_a)(x_b dot x_b)) = 1/2,
    (y_a dot y_b)^2 / ((y_a dot y_a)(y_b dot y_b)) = 9/10.

These are unequal. Nonzero proportionality factors cancel in this invariant. Thus no
zero-baseline proper rotation explains even these two pairs. Alternatively, a theorem
may assume nonzero t explicitly; the fixture supplies a direct way to discharge it.

## Identifying rotation and translation direction

The existing epipolar implication gives [t]cross R in ker(A). Since t is nonzero and
R is invertible, this matrix is nonzero. Hence

    [t]cross R = alpha [e1]cross, alpha !=0.

The left-null identity t^T[t]cross=0 implies t^T[e1]cross=0. Writing t=(tx,ty,tz)
shows ty=tz=0, so t=tau e1 with tau!=0. Divide by tau and put k=alpha/tau:

    [e1]cross R = k [e1]cross.

Left multiplication by [e1]cross selects minus row3 and row2 of R. Comparing entries
forces row2=k e2^T and row3=k e3^T. Row orthogonality gives k^2=1 and forces row1 to
be(r,0,0). The determinant is r*k^2=r, so det(R)=1 forces r=1. Therefore

    R=diag(1,k,k), k∈{+1,-1}.

For any normalized-ray pair, the third-coordinate motion equation is d2=k*d1.
Strictly positive depths imply k>0, so k=1 and R=I. For a pair of horizontal disparity
p>0, the first coordinate now gives

    tau = d1*p >0.

Every pair in the fixture has p=1 or p=1/2. Consequently every competing admissible
pose is exactly

    R=I, t=tau e1, tau>0,
    d1_i=d2_i=tau/p_i.

The reconstruction is unique up to positive global scene/translation scale. Setting
tau=1 is a gauge convention, not a measured baseline or a claim of metric recovery.

## Why each gate matters

- Rank8 identifies the nonzero matrix line; it does not itself enforce proper rotation,
  cheirality, or nonzero translation.
- The zero-baseline exclusion prevents the vacuous E=0 solution from escaping the
  nonzero kernel-line argument.
- Proper rotation, including determinant+1, excludes reflections.
- Positive depths exclude the half-turn branch and determine translation sign.
- Scale remains unobservable from these images alone.
- Exact rank can be poorly conditioned under noisy data. No robustness margin or
  uncertainty-aware uniqueness theorem is established here.

The nine planar pairs alone have rank6 and admit the two physically valid poses already
exhibited. Adding the distinct critical nonplanar point raises rank to7 but preserves
both. The specified two separating off-plane observations raise rank to8 and support the
sufficient argument above. Thus number of observations, nonplanarity and local rank
language must not substitute for the actual global observation-map analysis.

`domain_planar_poses.lean`, `domain_offplane.lean`, and
`domain_nonplanar_ambiguity.lean` kernel-check the ambiguity and discrimination examples.
`domain_cheirality.lean` checks the final scalar/coordinate pruning for the supplied

argument above. The fixed-data matrix-line portion is now proved in
`domain_linear_identification.lean` without relying on numerical rank. No standalone approved Challenge or complete certified estimator is
