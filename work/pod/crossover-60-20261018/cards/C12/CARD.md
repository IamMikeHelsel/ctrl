Question: What minimal varying-rate and effective-target model captures meaningful stopping variability without disguising reduced braking strength as slower response?

Obstacle: No calibrated mechanism or data validates a particular rate/target uncertainty set.

Proposed cheap test: Construct equal-time-constant models with different steady acceleration and test the saved clearance contract on both; then identify the missing authority premise.
