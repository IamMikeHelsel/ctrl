## Contract boundaries and failure cases

- Native replan inputs, exported reference one-sided jets, and physical actuator state must remain separate records. Resetting actual state invalidates the retained-state jump formula.
- A boundary needs exact execution order and both sides. A right-continuous value alone cannot certify jump mass. Native-side selection is a separate rule that finite q/v/a tests do not prove.
- Zero-duration and superseded phases remain provenance; positive executed phases cannot be removed by a tolerance. Distinct exact times may share the same floating timestamp.
- The identity Δactual_jerk=r Δu assumes the same rate, disturbance and actual acceleration on both sides. Changed rate/disturbance requires extra terms.
- A jerk jump has distributional snap; reporting finite piecewise snap or sampled higher derivatives does not bound its impulse.
- Source mechanical work, squared jerk and command variation have different units/objectives. Small trajectory error or energy does not bound total variation.
- Candidate-phase completeness requires a trusted native export contract or independent completeness certificate. A checker cannot recover deliberately unreported events from samples alone.
