## Primary-source check

[SE-Sync](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07386) provides a relaxation, noise-regime guarantees and an a posteriori certification route; it does not assert unconditional tightness for every inconsistent or adversarial graph. The [corrigendum](https://david-m-rosen.github.io/files/SESync_correction.pdf) repairs the proof of Theorem12, whose statement remains correct: the prior constrained singular-value optimization mishandled binding nonnegativity constraints. The repaired argument bounds squared norm using the maximum singular value. Neither the survey's broad phrasing nor this correction justifies a blanket claim that pose-graph optimization is always exact or that SE-Sync is invalid.
