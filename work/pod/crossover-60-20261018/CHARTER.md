## Ten-minute protocol

Start only after all three role assignments are dispatched. Record real UTC timestamps and
role/model identities. Minutes 0–2 recover exact question and existing obstacle; 2–5 generate
three different transfers, at least one surprising; 5–8 derive or test; 8–10 independent critique
and PM gate. These are guides; substantive research must total at least 600 elapsed seconds.
Parallel workers do not multiply elapsed time. Never fill time with idle sleeps or claim setup,
inventory, interruption, scheduling gaps or unrelated work as research. Stop the active segment
when research stops; resume with a fresh segment. A gap over 120 seconds between activity
records requires explicit explanation or exclusion. Longer work is reported honestly.

Play: reverse an assumption, preserve a different invariant, change the representation, try
the dual, or bring a failure pattern from another field. Each analogy must specify objects,
mapping, assumptions, and a falsifier. If the first idea fails early, use remaining time to
test alternatives or bound the obstruction. Never weaken the original question silently.

Each role writes an independently attributable note. Inventor supplies at least three ideas;
critic challenges the actual experiment and source assumptions, not just a summary. Root
records GO/HOLD/NO-GO and a next test. GO can mean 'worth a bounded test', not 'discovered'.
HOLD names missing evidence/resources. NO-GO closes a route; it does not settle the problem.
