## F4 (02:28): why Lagrange's 4×4 family is rich, and its 2-parameter deformation (curves wave 3)
- For three quartic columns, E: Y² = Π(X + C_j) over Q(x) is an elliptic K3 surface (12 I₂ fibres,
  full 2-torsion). Polynomial rows are x(2P) for sections of height exactly 1. The 7 halved rows
  span the lattice A₃(½) ⊕ ⟨1⟩, which has exactly 14 norm-1 vectors, hence 7 rows (matches the
  complete enumeration). Closure growth 1 + 2·3^k comes from collinear-3×3 completions on
  successive triple curves. Richness criterion: three rows whose sections pair to ±½ on both the
  row-triple and column-triple curves.

  is a general-type surface. A search over ~1.7e9 pairs to height 200 finds only Lagrange (a, b) =
  (5, 3) outside the degenerate curve ab = ±9.
- Neither smooth-lane 6×4 class has a 4×4 subgrid in Lagrange's polynomial set (`member.py`).
